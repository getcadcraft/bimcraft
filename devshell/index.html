                    Working…

                    Open a building model.

                    Choose an IFC, RVT, RFA or DWG file, or a whole project folder, from your computer. Or drag either onto this page: a dragged folder opens without a permission prompt. Or start a new building and draw it here. It all stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

                    • IFC

                      The full model in 3D. Orbit it, turn storeys on and off, cut it with a section plane, and click any element to see its properties and quantities. Change names, tags and property values, then save a new IFC; your original stays as it was.

                    • RVT and RFA

                      A file card: the release it was saved in, project information, the preview picture and linked files. The 3D model inside an RVT is in an undocumented format, so to see it in 3D, export IFC from the app that made it (File, Export, IFC) and open the .ifc here.

                    • DWG and DXF

                      The drawing in 2D: model space and every sheet, pan and zoom, layers on and off, with live coordinates. Switch model space to 3D to orbit round its elevations, thickness, 3D faces, meshes and blocks. Print view shows it black on white with its line weights. Linked drawings and title-block pictures are found in the open folder. AutoCAD 2000 to 2018 formats.

                    • A folder

                      Every model in a project folder at once: numbered backups grouped with their model, linked files checked against the folder, and incomplete files flagged. Only the few kilobytes each card needs are read, so a network drive is fine.

                    When a model opens

                    Rooms and spaces are see-through boxes that can hide the walls behind them, so they start hidden. The Spaces button still turns them on and off for the open model.

                    Edge lines give the drafting look but cost memory. "Small models only" draws them for models under 300,000 triangles. Above 1.5 million triangles they are always off.

                    Check against IDS

                    Off: the IFC keeps only a reference and the IDS stays in this browser (a client's IDS may be confidential). On: the IDS travels inside the IFC to everyone you send it to.

                    Drawings open in

                    3D draws a large drawing much faster. Sheets, Print view and the equipment list stay on the tabs above the drawing. A drawing with nothing in model space, or a browser that can't show 3D, opens in 2D.

                    Opening view

                    The camera a model opens with. Fit looks at the whole model from a corner.

                    A building turned in its site

                    When a building's walls run at an angle to the model's axes (as models on survey or shared coordinates arrive), "Square to the building" shows it square: Fit, Top, Front, Side, the view cube and new sections follow its walls. The compass still points to the model's north.

                    Placing library items

                    When placing or pasting starts, the item's back snaps to the nearest wall. Tab or the Wall snap button turns it off for the moment.

                    Corner indicator

                    In the corner of the 3D views: a view cube to click a side to look from, an X/Y/Z axis icon, or nothing. The 2D drawing view has none.

                    mm

                    Location

                    The system Measure › Location gives latitudes and longitudes in. The file's own map system and datum are always shown beside them.

                    Your name on issues

                    Goes on the issues (BCF) you make. Signed in, your account's name is used unless you type another here. Reset leaves it as it is.

                      Layout

                      Workspace

                      From add-ons and files

                      A layout is a .ccworkspace.json file: data only, read as untrusted. It arranges the same building blocks in every app; each app fills them with its own tools. Drop a .ccworkspace.json file here to import it.

                      Every app that has the workspace you pick opens in it; the others keep their toolbar and panels. Your toolbar style, menus and keys stay as they are.

                      Work environments. Save your whole setup under a name (the layout, menu style and keys, toolbar, panels and My tools, for every app) and switch between setups. The theme and the language stay as they are.

                      Lost a toolbar, a menu or a panel?

                      Puts the toolbar (order, lock and buttons), the panels, the screen mode and the menu style back to how BimCraft starts. Your theme, language, sizes and BimCraft's own settings stay as they are.

                      Theme

                      The theme applies to every CadCraft app and the site.

                      Language

                      The language applies to every CadCraft app. The app reloads to change it.

                      Units

                      Global settings: each app can have its own measurement settings and defaults too.

                      Switch a unit on to see it in the unit lists. The star marks the default for new drawings, measures and items. These choices are the same in every app in this browser.

                      Loading the units…

                      A file keeps its own unit (a drawing's units, a project's feet or meters), and that unit stays in its list even when it is turned off here.

                      Hardware specs

                      Sharing your hardware specs allows us to build more efficient engines. The information is aggregated and not associated with your account: the graphics card, processor cores, memory and screen size, the browser and system family, and the app — no files, no name, no address.

                      Full-screen crosshair

                      Two thin lines through the pointer across the whole drawing or view, to check that things line up (CURSORSIZE 100). Not with a finger.

                      First-time tours

                      Off: no app starts its tour by itself. Each app's own settings can still turn its tour off, or take it again.

                      Screen

                      Browsers allow full screen only after a click or key press, so it starts on your first click in the drawing.

                      Toolbar

                      Toolbar rows

                      Wrap shows every group. 2 rows and 1 row keep the drawing taller; scroll the toolbar sideways for the rest.

                      On a touch screen (swipe the toolbar for more, or let it wrap)

                      Arrange: drag tools within their group, and drag a group by its name.

                      Right-click the toolbar (or long-press it) for Arrange, Lock and Reset.

                      Pen and touch

                      Fingers on the drawing, once you use a pen here

                      The pen's side button, its eraser end and the finger taps are set in Input devices, where they can run any command.

                      With Only move the view, one finger on the drawing does nothing and two fingers move and zoom it. These choices are for this device, in every app. A pen is noticed the first time you use one here.

                      Sizes and panels

                      The bars, toolbars, panels and dialogs of BimCraft, bigger or smaller; the drawing keeps its own zoom. Panel text size works on top of it.

                      Compact tightens the row heights, panel padding, toolbar buttons and panel text of BimCraft, so more fits on the screen. A touch screen keeps its large targets either way.

                      You can also drag a panel's inner edge. Double-click the edge to put it back.

                      Panel widths apply when the panels sit beside the drawing (wider windows).

                      Panels

                      Drag a panel by its name to dock it left, right or at the bottom, or drop it over the drawing to float it. A new window can go on your other screen. Reset layout puts every panel back where it started.

                      Add-ons

                        Add-ons add features to BimCraft. Switching one off takes effect at once; some need a reload to go completely.

                        Developer mode is on: add-ons that are not signed can load. Only use add-ons you trust.
