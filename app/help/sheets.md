## What you'll do

Make a drawing set from the model: the plan of each level, the north, east, south and west elevations, a building section for every section line, the door, window, room and lumber schedules, and an elevation of each framed wall. The set is drawn again from the model each time you open it, so it always matches what you built.

## Draw a section line

1. On a level, open {{ui:Build}} and choose {{ui:Section line}}.
2. Click where the line starts and where it ends. The short line at its middle shows the side the section looks toward: the left of the line, going from the first click to the second.
3. The line shows on the plan with its heads and its letter (A, then B, and so on).

Pick the line to change it in **Properties**: its letter, **Flip the view** to look the other way, and **Look as far as** to stop the view a set distance behind the cut (0 shows everything behind it). Press {{key:Delete}} to remove it.

## Open the sheets

1. Click {{ui:Sheets}} in the model bar (beside {{ui:Print}}).
2. The sheets are listed on the left: **A-101** and on for plans, **A-201** for elevations, **A-301** for sections, **A-401** for schedules and **S-101** for wall framing. Click one to see it.
3. Choose the **Paper**: Letter, Tabloid (11 × 17), Arch D (24 × 36), A3 or A1. The paper keeps its standard name and its size is written in the {{ui:Units}} you chose, for example *Letter · 8.5 × 11 in* or *A3 · 297 × 420 mm*.
4. Click {{ui:Download the set as PDF}}: one page per sheet.

## What is on the sheets

- **Plans** show walls, doors, windows, rooms with their areas, dimensions and section lines, at the largest standard scale that fits: 1/4" = 1'-0" or smaller (1:50 or smaller in millimetres).
- **Elevations** are drawn from the 3D model with hidden lines removed, two to a sheet, all at the same scale, with a ground line under them. The roof, the foundations and the ground show as they are modeled.
- **Sections** fill what the cut passes through (walls, floors, the roof) and draw what is seen beyond it.
- **Elevations and sections carry their notes:** a mark for each level with its name and height on the right, the height between levels on the left, and on an elevation the building's width under it and a tag (D1, W1) on each door and window in a wall facing you. For a room's own walls see [Interior elevations](topic:interior-elevations).
- **Schedules** are the same tables as {{ui:Schedules}} in the model bar.
- **Framing** shows each framed wall's studs, plates, headers and sills as seen from its outside, so a crew can lay it out. See [Framing walls, floors and roofs](topic:framing).

Each sheet has a title block with its number, its title, the file's name and the date.

:::tip
Sheets come from the model: to change a drawing, change the model and open {{ui:Sheets}} again.
:::

:::note
Elevations and sections show lines and fills only: dimensions, level marks and notes on them are not built yet.
:::
