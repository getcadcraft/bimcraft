## What you'll do

Find out what your place asks for, and have BimCraft use it: the steps to get a permit, what to hand in and in what format, how drawings are to look, and the standard notes to put on them. One pack holds it, next to the building rules, and you choose it once.

A pack is a guide. The authority and the registered professional decide what is required. Every pack says how far it can be trusted: **unchecked** (nobody confirmed it), **draft** (written from published pages, not yet reviewed by a professional) or **reviewed**. A made-up teaching pack says MADE-UP everywhere.

## Steps

1. Add the Building regulations add-on and open {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:Building regulations}}.
2. Tick the pack for your place. Open it: {{ui:Also holds}} lists what it carries.
3. In the permit package choose its list: the steps, the format and the drawings to hand in appear. See [The permit package](topic:permit-package).
4. In a sheet, {{ui:Standard notes}} places the pack's notes. See [Sheets](topic:sheets).
5. In {{ui:Annotation sizes}}, {{ui:Use a regulation pack's sizes}} takes the pack's text heights. See [Draw at full size, annotate by scale](topic:annotation-sizes).

## What a pack can hold

- **Approval process:** the steps in order, who does each, what is handed in, the documents. A fee or a time frame appears only with the page it was read from and the date it was read.
- **Submission format:** sheet sizes, orientation, file formats, copies, file names, title block fields, and the drawings (with scales) and papers a submission needs. A missing item is a note in Problems, never a stop.
- **Drawing conventions:** text heights on paper, line weights, a pen table, and marks such as a north point or a scale bar. Some are checked as rules.
- **Standard notes:** notes by discipline, in the pack's own words, each with its clause.

## Good to know

- A city pack builds on its country's pack: its own steps, drawings and notes win, the country's stay.
- Anything not read from an official page is marked as a sample or unverified in the pack and where it is shown.
- You can write a pack for your place and add it in {{ui:Upload a regulations file}}; it is always shown as yours, never as reviewed.
