## Waxa aad samayn doontid

Ku hay doorashooyinka mugga dhismaha ee fikrad mid ah moodel kali ah halkii faylal kala gaar ah: dhowr mug dhismaha oo wada jira, mid ka mid ah **doorashada hadda jirta**, kuwa kale waa la kaydiyaa barbardhig ahaan.

Doorasho mugga dhismaha waa mug dhismaha: qaab-dhismaha kor loo qaadayo sare, iyo tiro dabaq. Ma aha darbiyo weli. Waxaad kaydin kartaa intii aad rabto, oo lagu kooxayn karaa **set-ka doorashada** (doorashooyinka hal set-ka ka tirsan waa kala beddelaad).

## Kaydi doorasho

1. Fur {{ui:Barnaamijka qolalka ilaa qaabka dhismaha}}, qor barnaamijka qolalka iyo bannaanka, kadibna dooro {{ui:Doorashooyinka raadinta}}.
2. Dooro mid ka mid ah doorashooyinka la liistay.
3. Hoos {{ui:Doorashooyinka mugga dhismaha ee moodelka}}, qor magac {{ui:Set-ka doorashada}} haddii aad rabto, kadibna dooro {{ui:Hay doorashada la doortay}}.

Doorashadu waxay ka muuqataa moodelka sida mug dhismaha iyo liiska, iyada oo dabaqyadeeda, bannaanka dhismaha, aagga sagxadda guud iyo mugga. Waxba lama dhisin. Mid kale isla siyaabaha kaydi.

Doorashada koowaad ee set-ka waa doorashada hadda jirta. Tallaabo kasta waa hal tallaabo oo ka noqod.

## Doorashada hadda jirta

Mid ka mid ah doorashooyin kasta set kasta waa midka hadda jira. Taasi waxay leedahay laba micne:

- **Dabaqyadeeda waxay noqdaan aagagga sagxadda dhismaha.** Doorashada hadda jirta waxay leedahay meel kasta oo dabaq kasta oo qaadda aagga sagxadda guud ee dabaqaas, sidaas darteed wax kasta oo akhriya aagagga dabaqyada wuxuu arkaa doorashada firfircoon oo keliya.
- **Waa la hubiyaa goobta.** Haddii ay ka baxdo xadka goobta, khadka dhismaha ama xuquuqda gudbinta, digniin magaceeda ku qoran ayaa ka muuqda {{ui:Dhibaatooyin}}. Digniin keliya ayaa: waxba lama diido. Doorashooyinka aan hadda jirin lama hubiyo.

Calaamee {{ui:Ka dhig mid hadda jira}} doorasho kale si aad u beddesho.

{{ui:Qari doorashooyinka aan hadda jirin}} wuxuu qariyaa kuwa kale aragga 3D. Taasi browser-kan oo keliya ayaa, faylka lama kaydiyo.

## Mugga dhismaha ModelCraft

ModelCraft, {{ui:Dhoofi}} › {{ui:Mugga dhismaha ilaa BimCraft…}} wuxuu u diraa kooxda ama component kasta sida doorasho mid ah. BimCraft wuxuu furaa faylka isagoo liistaa doorashooyinka.

Si aad ugu soo geliso moodel horey u furan, dooro {{ui:Ku dar doorashooyinka mugga faylka ka}} oo dooro faylka `.ifc`. Mugagiisa waxay soo galaan sida doorashooyinka set, hal tallaabo oo ka noqod, iyagoo ku taagan meesha lagu sawiray. Fayl mugga dhismaha oo CivilCraft ah wuxuu u shaqeeyaa isla sidaas.

Doorasho lagu calaamadiyay "(shape simplified)" (qaabka la fudaydiyay) ma ahayn bloog toosan oo ModelCraft ku jirtay (way dib u dhigtaa, way dalool tahay ama ma xirna). Mugga halkan waa khadka aasaaska ee lagu kor qaaday sarreeyaha; aagagga dabaqyadeeda iyo mugga waa kuwa laga qiyaasay jidhka adag laftiisa.

## Horumari doorashada la doortay

Dooro {{ui:Horumari}} doorasho. Waxay noqotaa heer (lagu daray tirada dabaqyadeeda), darbiyo ku wareegsan khadka aasaaska dabaq kasta, iyo sagxad dabaq kasta, hal tallaabo oo ka noqod. Doorashadu waxay ka baxdaa liiska; doorashooyinka kale waxay sii joogaan sidii ay ahaayeen. Saqaf weli ma jiro.

{{ui:Magac cusub}} wuxuu beddelaa magaca doorashada, {{ui:Ka saar}} wuxuu ka saaraa moodelka. Marka la saaro doorashada hadda jirta, doorashada kale ee koowaad ee set-ka ayaa hadda jirta noqonaysa.

## Waxa aad arki doontid

- Doorasho kasta oo la kaydiyay oo ah bloog aragga 3D iyo safka liiska.
- "(hadda jira)" dhinaca mid ka mid ah doorashooyin kasta set kasta.
- Digniin {{ui:Dhibaatooyin}} marka doorashada hadda jirta ay gudbiso xadka goobta, khadka dhismaha ama xuquuqda gudbinta.

## Waxa ku habboon inaad ogaato

- Doorasho moodelka IFC waa building element proxy oo leh guddiga sifooyinka `CadCraft_SiteMass` (set-kiisa doorashada, magaca doorashada iyo haddii ay hadda jirto ayaa ku jira guddigaas). Dabaqyada doorashada hadda jirta waa spaces nooca `MassingFloor`. Barnaamijyada kale waxay akhriyaan proxy-ga sida element guud.
- Kaydinta doorasho weligeed ma kordhiso heerarka; horumarinta ayaa kordhisa.
- Caawiye ayaa liistada doorashooyinka kaydsan, doorasho kaydin, mid ka dhig hadda jirta, magac beddel, horumari ama ka saar.
