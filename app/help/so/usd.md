{{ui:USD}} qalabka sare wuxuu qoraa waxa furan oo dhan (dhammaan moodeelada, kuwa federated ah ee meesha ku dhigtay) hal fayl USD ah.

## Dhoofin

1. Dooro {{ui:USD}} qalabka sare.
2. Dooro {{ui:Text (.usda)}}, {{ui:Binary (.usdc)}} ama {{ui:Package (.usdz)}}.
3. Faylku wuu soo degayaa. Sadarka xaaladda wuxuu sheegayaa inta walxood gashay.

## Waxa ku jira faylka

| Moodeelka IFC | Faylka USD |
|---|---|
| Goobta, dhismaha, dabaqyada | Kooxo ku jira midkood midka kale, dabaqyada sida ay u kala horreeyaan sarreynta |
| Walax kasta | Hal prim oo hoos taagan dabaqeeda iyo takhasuskeeda (Architecture, Structure, MEP, Furnishing, Site, Spaces, Other), oo geometry-geeda ahaan mesh |
| Class, GlobalId, magac, nooc, calaamad | Sifooyin la magacaabay `ifc:class`, `ifc:globalId`, `ifc:name` iyo kuwa kale |
| Habaynta sifooyinka iyo tirooyinka | Sifo kasta; dherer, bed iyo mug waxay ku jiraan mitir, mitir laba jibbaaran iyo mitir saddex jibbaaran |
| Midabada | Agabka (oo leh hufnaan) |
| Spaces | Waa la hayaa, lagu calaamadeeyay hagayaal si rendarradu u dhaafaan |

Geometry-gu wuxuu ku yaal isku-xidhka IFC faylka. Qaabaynta waxa raacaysa habka qoran ee CadCraft ee USD ee dhismayaasha.
