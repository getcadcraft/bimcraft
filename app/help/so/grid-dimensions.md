## Waxa aad samayn doontid

Ku dhig masaafada u dhexaysa khadadka shabakadda iyo cabbirka guud ee shabakadda qorshayaasha, gadaasha goobooyinka, si xaashiyada laga sameeyo ay cabbirrada qaadaan. Waxaad u baahan tahay shabakad marka hore: eeg [Shabakadaha iyo tiirarka](topic:grids-and-columns).

## Tallaabooyinka

1. Muuji qorshaha dabaq oo guji {{ui:Shabakad}} si aad qalabka shabakadda u shido. Safka doorashooyinka shabakadda ayaa muuqda.
2. Calaamee {{ui:Cabbirrada}}. Masaafada u dhexaysa khadadka lambarka leh ee deriska ah iyo khadadka xarfaha leh ee deriska ah, iyo cabbirka guud labada jihood, ayaa lagu sawiraa qorshaha.
3. Dooro dabaq kale: cabbirrada isla markaana waxay muuqdaan qorshihiisa.
4. Dhaqaaq khad, magacee mid, ama ku dar mid cusub. Cabbirrada way raacaan shabakadda.
5. Ka saar calaamadda {{ui:Cabbirrada}} si aad uga qariso mar kale.

## Xusuusin

- Isbeddel kasta waa hal tallaabo oo ka noqod: {{key:Mod+Z}} ayaa ka noqdiya.
- Dejintu waxay lagu kaydiyaa IFC-ga shabakadda ay weheliso, qorshayaasha xaashiyada lagu dhigo waxay qaadaan cabbirrada.
- Haddii moodeelka aanu lahayn shabakad aad beddeli karto (shabakad ka timid fayl barnaamij kale, oo halkan laga beddeli karin), sadarka xaaladda wuxuu leeyahay "Ma jiro shabakad la beddeli karo."
