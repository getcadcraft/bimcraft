## Waxa aad samayn doontid

Ka arag moodelka meeshiisa: khariidadda waddooyinka goobta ama sawirka hawada ee dhulka hoostiisa.

## Tallaabooyinka

1. Fur moodel IFC oo xambaarta meeshiisa khariidadda (beddel khariidad, IfcMapConversion, oo leh nidaam isku-dhafka mashruuc ah). Soo dhoofiyayaasha ayaa qoraa marka isku-dhafka mashruuca (barta sahanka, isku-dhafka la wadaago) la dejiyo.
2. Dooro {{ui:Muuji}} › {{ui:Lakabyada khariidadda…}}. Wada-hadalku wuxuu sheegaa nidaamka moodelka, tusaale ahaan EPSG:28356. Moodel aan lahayn beddel khariidad ma lahaan karo lakabyada khariidadda: wada-hadalku wuu sheegaa.
3. Shid {{ui:Lakabyada khariidadda ee internetka ka yimid}}. Ilaa markaas waxba lagama codsado server kasta; marka uu furan yahay, server-yada aad ku darto waxay arkaan aagga ku wareegsan moodelka iyo cinwaanka internetkaaga, moodelkaaga ma arkaan.
4. {{ui:Ku dar lakab}} wuxuu dooran karaa tiles (cinwaan leh `{z}`, `{x}` iyo `{y}`), adeeg WMS, WMTS ama ArcGIS ({{ui:Hel lakabyada}}, kadibna dooro mid), ama sawir leh faylkiisa adduunka.
5. Xir wada-hadalka. Khariiddu waxay ku jirtaa dhulka, sare hoosta dhibcaha ugu hooseeya moodelka, oo dusha moodelka ka weyn, iyo sidoo kale wareega ku wareegsan.

:::note
Khariiddu waa shaashadda kaliya; kuma kaydsanto faylka IFC. Liiska lakabyada waxaa lagu kaydiyaa browser-kan. Isticmaal kaliya adeegyada khariidadda oo shuruudahoodu oggolaanayo.
:::
