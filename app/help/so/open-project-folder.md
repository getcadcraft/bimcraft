## Waxa aad samayn doontid

Fur galka mashruuca oo dhan, sida kii laguu siiyay shaqo, oo ka saar BimCraft: maxay yihiin moodelada, kuwee nuqullo keydka ah, maxay isku xidhan yihiin, faylasha khasaaray, iyo faylka aad u badan tahay inaad furto.

Kaliya dhowr kilobytes oo ay qaadato kaarta moodel kasta ayaa la akhriyaa, sidaas darteed galka ku yaal darawal shabakad ama gal cloud-ka la isku waafajiyey waa fiican yahay.

## Tallaabooyinka

:::chromium
1. Riix {{ui:Open a folder}} bidix (ama dhexda shaashadda madhan).
2. Dooro galka mashruuca daaqadda furta oo riix **Select** (ama **Upload** ama **Open**, iyadoo ku xidhan nidaamkaaga).
3. Riix **View files** markii browser-ku weydiiyo "Let site view files?". Tani waxay kaliya u oggolaanaysaa tabka inuu akhriyo galka; wax lagu soo rarin maayo.
4. Sug inta xariga xaaladdu tiriyo: "Galka la liisanayo: … fayl", kadibna "Faylasha Revit la hubinayo: … ka mid ah …".
:::

:::firefox
1. Riix {{ui:Open a folder}} bidix (ama dhexda shaashadda madhan).
2. Dooro galka mashruuca daaqadda furta oo xaqiiji. Firefox ayaa laga yaabaa inuu weydiiyo inaad xaqiijiso in boggu akhrin karo faylasha galka; way kombiyuutarkaaga ku sii jiraan.
3. Sug inta xariga xaaladdu yidhaahdo "Galka la akhrinayo: … fayl", kadibna "Faylasha Revit la hubinayo: … ka mid ah …".
:::

:::safari
1. Riix {{ui:Open a folder}} bidix (ama dhexda shaashadda madhan).
2. Dooro galka mashruuca daaqadda furta oo riix **Upload**. Erayga kor ku xusan ha ka dhigin: waxba kama bixi Mac-kaaga, faylasha waxaa loo gudbiyaa tabka oo keliya.
3. Sug inta xariga xaaladdu yidhaahdo "Galka la akhrinayo: … fayl", kadibna "Faylasha Revit la hubinayo: … ka mid ah …".
:::

Waxaad sidoo kale **jiidi galka** {{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=maareeyaha faylalkaaga}} ilaa bogga. Galka la jiiday wuxuu furmaa iyada oo aan ruqsad weydiin lagu sameynin browser kasta.

## Waxa aad arki doontid

Warbixinta galka ayaa buuxisa dhexda:

- Magaca galka, tirinta moodelada, sawirrada CAD, moodelada IFC iyo faylasha, iyo wadarta cabbirka.
- Ogaysiisyo casaan ama casaan-yar haddii faylasha qaarkood aan dhammaystirnayn ama faylal isku xidhan qaarkood ka maqan yihiin galka.
- Sanduuqa {{ui:Start here}}: faylka BimCraft soo jeediyo inaad furto, iyo sababaha.
- **Moodelada**: hal safka moodel kasta, nuqullada keydkiisa oo hoos ku kooxaysan.
- **Faylasha la xidhay iyo kuwa tixraacan**, **Moodelada IFC**, **Sawirrada CAD** iyo **Dhinac la dhigay**, haddii galku leeyahay.

Bidix, qaybta {{ui:Folder}} ayaa soo koobaysa; **Dhibaatooyinka** wuxuu leeyahay "Midna" marka nuqul kasta uu caafimaad qabo.

## Isticmaal sanduuqa Start here

Sanduuqu wuxuu magac u bixiyaa hal fayl oo wuxuu sababta sheegaa, tusaale ahaan:

- "Shaqada ugu dambeysay ee la kaydiyay galkan (Mar 3, 2025)."
- "*Consultant.rvt* waxa lagu xidhay moodelada kale, sidaas darteed waa asal, ma aha faylka laga wax beddelayo."
- "*Tower.rvt* wuxuu u egyahay doorashada ugu fiican, laakiin waa khasaaray; nuqulkiisii ugu dambeeyay waa …"
- "Tani waa moodel dhexe: Revit-ka, ku fur adigoo isticmaalaya "Create New Local" oo ka shaqee nuqulka maxalliga ah."

1. Akhri sababaha.
2. Riix {{ui:Open its card}} si aad u akhrido kaartida faylkaas, ama {{ui:Show it in the list}} si aad safkiisa u hesho **Moodelada**.
3. Hubi qofka ugu dambeeyay ee shaqeeyay mashruuca ka hor intaadan isku halleyn. Sanduuqu wuu sidaas sheegaa: waa hagaajin ku salaysan magacyada, taariikhaha iyo xidhiidhyada.

## Fur faylasha warbixinta

1. Riix magaca moodel si aad u furto kaartidiisa. Kaartidu wuxuu hubiyaa xidhiidh kasta galka.
2. Riix {{ui:Back to}} iyo magaca galka sare ee kaartida (ama {{ui:Back to the folder}} bidix) si aad ugu noqoto.
3. Riix faylka IFC kasta hoos **Moodelada IFC** si aad 3D ugu furto.
4. Riix DWG ama DXF kasta hoos **Sawirrada CAD** si aad 2D ugu furto. Sawirrada xidhan iyo sawirrada waxaa galka laga heli doonaa si toos ah.

## Mar dambe ku fur galka

:::chromium
Galalka aad ku furtay {{ui:Open a folder}} ama aad jiidday waxaa bidix hoosta ku liisan {{ui:Recent folders}}. Riix {{ui:Reopen}} iyo magaca galka si aad mar kale u furto adigoon dooran. Markii browser-ka dib loo bilaabo, Chrome ama Edge mar kale ayay weydiin doonaan; dooro "Allow on every visit" si ay weydiinta u joojiyaan. Liistaasi waxay ku kaydsantahay browser-kan oo keliya, mana loo diro meel kale. Riix × si aad galka u illowdo.
:::

:::firefox safari
Browser-kani ma xasuusan karo galalka intii u dhexeysay booqashooyinka. Dooro galka mar kale {{ui:Open a folder}}, ama jiid gudaha.
:::

## Khaladaadka caadiga ah

- **Galka oo aad weyn doortay.** Ka badan 20,000 fayl: "Galkan wuxuu leeyahay in ka badan 20,000 fayl. Dooro gal yar, sida mashruuc keliya." Dooro galka mashruuca, ma aha darawalka oo dhan.
- **Doorashada waalidka mashruucyo badan.** Sanduuqa {{ui:Start here}} markaas wuxuu dooranayaa dhex mashruucyada. Dooro hal mashruuc.
- **Galalka Revit ee u gaarka ah oo loo arko inay la dhiibteen.** Galalka `*_backup`, `*_log` iyo `Revit_temp` waa kuwo nadaafad ah oo ku liisan **Dhinac la dhigay**, ma aha kuwo la akhriyo.
- **Moodel laga rajeeyo inuu 3D ka furmo kaartidiisa.** Faylka mashruucu waa kaarti keliya. Fur dhoofinta IFC-giisa.
- **Taleefannada iyo tablet-yada.** Browser-yo badan oo moobaylka ah ma dooran karaan galalka. Isticmaal kombiyuutar.

Eeg [Hubi galka nuqullada khaldan, xidhiidhyada maqan iyo hadhaaga](topic:check-folder-health) iyo [Warbixinta galka: doorarka, xaaladaha iyo xidhiidhyada](topic:folder-roles).
