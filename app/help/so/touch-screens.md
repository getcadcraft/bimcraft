BimCraft wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa farahaaga iyo sidoo kale mouse-ka. Markii ugu horreysay ee aad shaashadda taabato, badhamada, tabsyada iyo safafka liistada ayaa koraya ilaa cabbirka farta.

## Moodelka 3D

| Si aad u | Samee tan |
|---|---|
| Wareeg | Jiid hal farta |
| Weynee | Ku qabo laba farta isku dhowaysa ama isku fogaysa |
| Guuri | Jiid laba farta oo wada jira |
| Dooro walax | Taabo |
| Ku dar walax doorashada, ama ka saar | Riix oo hay nus ilbiriqsi (waa isku mid {{key:Ctrl}}+riix) |
| Waxba ha dooran | Taabo meel bannaan |

Ku qabashada iyo jiidista laba farood marna ma dooran wax, xitaa haddii farta ay ka kor kacdo walax.

## Naqshadaha (DWG iyo DXF)

| Si aad u | Samee tan |
|---|---|
| Guuri | Jiid hal farta |
| Weynee | Ku qabo; barta ka dhex-dhexaysa farahaaga ayaa meeshaa ku sii jirta |
| Muuji xaashida oo dhan | Laba jeer taabo, ama taabo {{ui:Fit}} |

Muuqaalka {{ui:3D}} ee naqshadda, gesture-yada moodelka ayaa dhacaya: hal farta wareeg, laba farood ku qabo oo guuri, taabo ayaa dooro, riix muddo dheer ayaa ku dari doona doorashada.

## Soco

Taabo {{ui:Walk}}, kadibna taabo dhulka aad rabto inaad taagnaatid. Safka badhamada fallaadhaha ayaa ka muuqda bar-ka socodka:

- Hay fallaadha si aad ugu socoto hore, dib, ama tallaabo dhinac ah.
- Haddii {{ui:Gravity}} la damiyay, fallaadhaha labajibbaaran ayaa kor iyo hoos u kaca.
- Jiid hal farta moodelka si aad u eegto wareeg.
- Taabo {{ui:Done}} si aad u joojiso.

## Shayada maktabadda ee la dhigo

Taabo meesha shayga la geynayo. {{ui:Turn 90°}} oo ku yaal bar-ka dhigidda ayaa leexiya (waa furaha {{key:R}} kiiboodhka), {{ui:Wall snap}} ayaa dhabarka u dhufanaya darbiga ugu dhow, {{ui:Stop placing}} ayaa dhammeeya.

## Cabbir

Taabo barta koowaad, kadibna tan labaad. Barta ayaa ku dhufata geeska iyo xuduudaha ku dhow taabashadaada.

:::tip
Kiiboodh oo ku xidhan tablet-ka, furaha kasta wuxuu u shaqeeyaa sida kombiyuutarka. Eeg [Furayaasha gaaban](topic:keyboard).
:::
