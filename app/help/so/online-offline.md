BimCraft wuxuu akhrinayaa faylalkaaga gudaha tab-ka browser-ka. Furista, eegista, wax ka beddelka iyo kaydinta faylalkaaga weligood ma diraan meel, online ama offline. [Shaqaynta offline](common:offline) waxay sharraxaysaa nuqullada offline ee dhammaan app-yada; boggan waxa uu daboolayaa waxa gaar u ah BimCraft.

## Waxa BimCraft ka soo rarta goobta

| Qayb | Goorta ay soo raraan haddii aanu nuqul offline ahayn | Cabbirka |
|---|---|---|
| App-ka laftiisa, oo ay ku jiraan muuqaalka 3D iyo koodka IFC engine-ka | Markaad furto BimCraft | Dhowr megabytes |
| Qaybta WebAssembly ee IFC engine-ka | Faylka IFC ee ugu horreeya ee aad furto tab-ka: "Bilaabaya IFC engine-ka…" | Ku dhawaad 1.6 MB |

Sawirada DWG iyo DXF, faylalka mashruuca iyo qoyska, iyo galalka ma u baahna wax dheeraad ah: akhristooda waa qayb ka ah app-ka.

## Kaydi BimCraft si uu offline uga shaqeeyo

1. Fur BimCraft inta aad online tahay.
2. Guji {{ui:Kaydi khadka ka baxsan}} baarka app-ka ee sare.
3. Eeg badhanka sida uu u kordhayo: tusaale ahaan "Kaydinaya 40 ee 212". Nuqulka BimCraft wuxuu ku dhawaad yahay 11 MB, sababtoo ah wuxuu ku jiraa IFC engine-ka.
4. Sug inta {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}} ay timaado.

Marka ay tibaaxayso {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}}, moodelada IFC, sawirada DWG iyo DXF, faylalka mashruuca, galalka iyo caawimaadan oo dhan waxay ku furmaan xidhiidh la'aan. Ma u baahnid inaad marka hore furto IFC ama DWG si aad akhristayaasha "u kululeeyso".

Si aad u cusboonaysiiso ama u saartid nuqulka, guji {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}} oo dooro {{ui:Mar kale kaydi (hel nooca ugu dambeeya)}} ama {{ui:Ka saar nuqulka khadka ka baxsan}}.

## Waxa badhanku sheegayo

| Badhanka | Macnaha |
|---|---|
| {{ui:Kaydi khadka ka baxsan}} | Weli nuqul offline ah ma jiro browser-kan. Guji si aad mid u kaydiso |
| "Kaydinaya *n* ee *m*" | Waa la kaydinayaa; tab-ka u hay furan ilaa uu dhammaado |
| {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}} | La kaydiyay. Ku dhaw si aad u aragto goorta; guji si aad u hesho menu-ga |
| {{ui:Lama kaydin karin}} | Faylal qaar ma soo degin. Hubi xidhiidhkaaga oo guji si aad mar kale u isku daydo. Nuqulka ugu dambeeya ee wanaagsan, haddii uu jiro, waa la hayaa |
| {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan lama heli karo}} | Daaqaddan ma hayn karto nuqul offline ah (daaqad gaar ah ama kombiyuutar la maamulo). Eeg [Shaqaynta offline](common:offline) |

:::offline
Waxaad offline tahay.

- Haddii badhanku yidhi {{ui:Khadka ka baxsan diyaar}}, nooc kasta oo fayl ah ayaa sida caadiga ah u furmaya.
- Haddii aanu yidhaahdin, waxa shaqeeya kaliya waxa tab-kan hore u soo rartay: akhrin aan waligiis ku soo rarnayn tab-kan ma bilaabmi karo, furistii nooca faylkaas waxay muujinaysaa qalad ka yimid browser-ka safka xaaladda. Ha dib u cusboonaysiin tab-ka ilaa aad mar kale online tahay.
:::

## Waxa aan weligiis xidhiidh u baahnayn

Markii BimCraft la furo tab-ka, waxyaabahan waxay offline ku shaqeeyaan xitaa nuqul la kaydiyay la'aantood:

- Akhrinta faylalka mashruuca iyo qoyska iyo hubinta galalka.
- Doorashada walxaha, akhrinta sifooyinka iyo wax ka beddelka qiyamka IFC moodel hore u furan.
- {{ui:U kaydi sida IFC}} iyo {{ui:Soo deji CSV}}: labaduba waa soo dejin lagu sameeyo tab-ka.

## Weli ma diyaar aha

- **Kaydinta cloud-ka.** Faylalku waxay ku kaydsamaan kombiyuutarkaaga oo keliya.

Eeg [Faylalkaagu meeye ayay tagaan](common:your-files).
