## Waxaad samaynaysaa

Ku xidh socodyada qalabka si moodeelku u noqdo hal nidaam isku xidhan: dhoofinta mishiinka ilaa tuubada ka baxda, iyo luuqa diffuser-ka ilaa tuubada laanta. Kadibna ka dhig BimCraft inuu liisto waxa aan isku xidhnayn.

## Ka bilow dekad

1. Dooro {{ui:Tuubo}} ama {{ui:Tuubada hawada}}.
2. Dhaqaaji markaad ku dhowdahay dekad qalab (goobaha yaryar; dekad furan waxaa lagu muujiyaa midabka digniinta). Mu'ashirku wuu ku xidhmaa wuxuuna sheegaa **dekad saaran**.
3. Guji: socodku wuxuu ka bilaabmaa dekadda dhererkeeda oo **qaadaa cabbirkeeda iyo nidaamkeeda** (tuubadu sidoo kale qaadaa agabka caadiga ah ee nidaamka). Status line-ku wuxuu sheegaa waxa qaaday.
4. Sii sawirka sida caadiga ah. Socod ku dhammaada dekad kale ayaa dhamaadkaas sidoo kale xidha.

Dhamaadka socodka dekad saaran wuxuu **ku xirnaan** yahay: dhaqaaji ama wareeji qalabka (Sifooyinka), dhamaadka socodka ayaa raacaa; wax ka beddel socodka, dhamaadkaas wuxuu sii joogaa dekadda. Geeska kale ee socodka waxay sii joogsadaan meesha ay joogaan, sidaas darteed dhaqdhaqaaq weyn kadib, cagta koowaad ayaa laga yaari karaa dekadda xagal ah: dhaqaaji geeska xiga sidoo kale.

## Hubi xidhiidhka

Fur {{ui:Dhismaha}} oo guji {{ui:Hubi xidhiidhka}}. Liiska hoosta bar-ka wuxuu muujinayaa:

| Xariiq | Macnaheeda |
|---|---|
| A port is not connected | Qalab ayaa leh xidhiidh aan wax lagu sawirin |
| Nothing on its end | Socod ayaa ku dhammaada hawada |
| Joined ports of different sizes | Socod cabbir kale ah ayaa ku kulma dekad ama socod kale oo aan reducer lahayn (cabbirka magaca tuubada, qaabka iyo dhinacyada tuubada hawada) |
| Joined ports of different systems | Tusaale ahaan tuubada soo celinta oo ku xidhan diffuser-ka soo bixinta |
| A pipe joined to a duct | Laba xidhiidh oo nooc kala duwan ah ayaa isku kulma |
| No equipment in its network supplies it | Midna ma leh qalab soo bixinaya, dhowr ha ahaadeen socodyada ay ku xirnaan. Biyo-dhaqidda, biyaha roobka iyo hawo-qaadka looma weydiinayo: biyahoodu waxay ka yimaadaan qalabka |
| Fixed to a port of equipment that is not in the model any more | Qalabka waa la tirtiray; mar kale sawir dhamaadka ama tirtir socodka |

Khaladaadku waa kuwa marka hore yimaadda. Guji xariiq si aad u doorato waxa uu magacaabo. {{ui:Xir}} wuxuu qariyaa liiska. Hubintu waxay akhrisaa moodeelka sida la sawiray: waxay hubisaa xidhiidhyada iyo cabbirrada, ma hubiso socodka, cadaadiska ama isku-dheelitirka.
