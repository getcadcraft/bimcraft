## What you'll do

Draw a size that is not the usual one, and see where BimCraft tells you about it.

## Steps

1. Draw or change the element as you want it: for example a stair 400 mm wide.
2. BimCraft makes it as asked.
3. Open {{ui:Problems}} in the top bar. Under **Drawing advice** there is a warning that names the element, the size you gave and the usual range.
4. Click the warning to select the element. Change the size if it was a slip, or leave it: a warning can be accepted, with or without a reason.

## What is advice

These used to be refused. They are made now, each with a warning:

- **Stairs**: narrower than 500 mm or wider than 5 m; risers outside 100 to 300 mm; treads outside 150 to 600 mm.
- **Spiral stairs**: a post outside 40 to 400 mm; treads under 100 mm deep on the walk line.
- **Columns**: under 50 mm across or under 300 mm tall.
- **Ramps**: under 300 mm wide.
- **Doors and windows**: under 100 mm wide or tall.
- **Grid lines**: under 100 mm long.
- **Walls**: a wall left under 50 mm long, for example by a trim.
- **Levels**: under 1 m or over 100 m tall (new building, split levels, a level's height).
- **Dormers**: outside 600 mm to 12 m wide, a front wall outside 300 mm to 4 m, an overhang over 1 m, walls outside 50 to 400 mm.
- **Building pads**: side slopes outside 1:1 to 10:1.

## What is still refused

Only what cannot be built or found, each with a message that says why:

- A size under 10 mm (a wall under 1 mm): there is nothing to build.
- A dormer not wider than twice its wall thickness: its two side walls would meet.
- A building pad with side slopes steeper than 0.1:1 (that is a retaining wall) or flatter than 100:1 (the grading would run on without end).
- Something that does not fit where you put it, an outline that crosses itself, or an element that is no longer in the model.

## Good to know

- The advice is worked out from the model, so it is there again when you open the file later, and it goes when the size is put right.
- Model checks, regulation packs and IDS checks also only list what they find. Nothing in {{ui:Problems}} stops you drawing or saving.
- The usual ranges are general practice, not a building code. For a code, add the Building regulations add-on and pick your place.
