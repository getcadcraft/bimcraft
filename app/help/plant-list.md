## What you'll do

Put plants in a building model. The {{ui:Plants}} source of the Library lists about 400 plants written by CadCraft (CC0), each with a plan symbol and its size at maturity.

## Steps

1. Open {{ui:Library}} on the right and choose {{ui:Plants}} as the source.
2. Search or pick a plant, then place it as you place any Library item.

## What you should see

- The list comes from the Library when you are online, so plants added there show up. Offline, or if the Library cannot be reached, BimCraft shows the plant list it carries with it.
- Each plant keeps its name, size and licence with it.
