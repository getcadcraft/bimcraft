## Okuzokwenza

Yenza amakhophi amaningi ento owe wayibeka kakade: itafula lesibili lokusebenzela, umugqa wamasinki. Lesi sihloko sikhuluma ngezinto zomtapo ezibekiwe, izakhi ezibhalwe yi-BimCraft emodelini: enye yazo ingamanamathiselwa kunoma iyiphi imodeli, ngisho nakwenye ithebhu. Izindonga, iminyango kanye nezinye izakhi zonke zikopishwa, zisikwe, zinamathiselwe futhi zisulwe ngendlela efanayo ngaphakathi kwemodeli yazo: bona [Susa, sika, kopisha namathisele izakhi zesakhiwo](topic:edit-elements).

Imiyalo itholakala kwibha yamathuluzi ngaphezulu kwemodeli: {{ui:Hlehlisa}}, {{ui:Phinda wenze}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}} kanye {{ui:Okuningi}}, imenyu enezinto ezi-{{ui:Sika}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}, {{ui:Phinda}}, {{ui:Susa}}, {{ui:Khetha konke}} kanye {{ui:Khetha lutho}}. Umyalo ongenakwenza okuthile manje uba mpunga; hambisa iphoyinti phezu kwawo ukuze ufunde ukuthi kungani, noma uwucofe futhi umugqa wesimo uzokutshela.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC enento yomtapo, noma beka enye kuqala. Bona [Beka izinto zomtapo nezingxenye zokufakelwa](topic:place-from-library).
2. Cofa into ku-3D view. Izimfanelo zayo zibonisa ibhokisi elithi **Into yomtapo**.
3. Cofa {{ui:Kopisha}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+C}}.
4. Cofa {{ui:Namathisela}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+V}}. Ibha yokubeka iyavula, futhi ibhokisi elibonakalayo lilandela iphoyinti, lijikiswe njengoba kunjalo.
5. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuze uyijikise ngama-90°, {{key:Tab}} ukuze ihlanganise emuva kwayo kudonga, noma uthayipha X no-Y kwibha yokubeka.
6. Cofa lapho ikhophi lihamba khona. Ukucofa ngakunye kubeka ikhophi elilodwa; cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze umise.
7. Cofa {{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}} ukuze ulande ikhophi elihlelwe lemodeli.

## Namathisela endaweni

{{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}} noma {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} unamathisela ngaphandle kokucofa, ku-X ne-Y efanayo nangokujikijela okufanayo:

- **Emodelini efanayo, esitezini esifanayo**: izinto ezimbili endaweni eyodwa azisizi, ngakho ikhophi lihamba eduze nomthombo, ububanzi bento eyodwa kuphezu kwe-100 mm ngobude bayo. Namathisela endaweni futhi kusuka kwikhophi elisha ukuze wenze umugqa.
- **Esitezini esihlukile noma kwenye imodeli**: i-X, i-Y nokujikijela okufanayo ngokuphelele. I-BimCraft isebenzisa isitezi esinegama elifanayo; uma singekho, kuthathwa eyeduze kakhulu ngobude. Umugqa wesimo uyatshela ukuthi yiyiphi.

## Phinda

{{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Phinda}} noma {{key:Mod+D}} ikopisha into ekhethiwe bese iqala ukuyinamathisela ngokushesha: isinyathelo esisodwa esikhundleni sezimbili.

## Kwenye imodeli noma kwenye ithebhu

Okukopishwe kugcinwa kulesi siphequluli, ngakho ungavula enye imodeli ye-IFC, noma i-BimCraft kwenye ithebhu, bese unamathisela khona. Lowo modeli udinga into yomtapo ngokwayo: i-BimCraft iyayibhekile uma into ibekiwe, ishintshwe noma ibuyekezwe kule thebhu. Into ebekwe enkathini yangaphambili ingenamathiselwa kuphela emodelini yayo; kwenye imodeli, yibeke kusuka kuthebhu ye-{{ui:Umtapo}}.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Ikhophi emodelini, likhethiwe, linento efanayo yomtapo, ilayisense, idatha yezinsimbi, inani kanye nenothi njengomthombo, **kanye nenombolo elandelayo ekhululekile yento**. Shintsha inombolo kuMaphrophathi, ngaphansi kwe-`CadCraft_Placement`.
- "Kubekiwe: *into* (into *n*)" ohlwini lwezinguquko. {{key:Mod+Z}} iyayihlehlisa; {{key:Mod+Y}} iyibuyisela futhi.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Amakhophi ahlanganyela uhlobo lomthombo, ngakho onke asebenzisa isethi eyodwa yejometri, njengalapho ubeka into efanayo kabili.
- {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no-{{ui:Phinda wenze}} bamboza zonke izinguquko: izinani ohlelile, ukubekwa, ukunamathisela, ukushintshwa nokubuyekezwa. Phinda wenze ibuyisela ukubekwa endaweni efanayo; inguquko entsha isula lokho okungenziwa ukuphinda.
- Kumbonakalo we-2D wedwebo akukho okungakhethwa, ngakho akukho okungakopishwa. Sebenzisa {{ui:Hlela ku-CadCraft}} ukuze uguqule idwebo, uma likhona.

## Iziphazamiso ezivamile

- **{{ui:Namathisela}} uthi izakhi zavela kwenye imodeli.** Okokugcina owakopisha kwakuyisakhi sesakhiwo, noma izakhi eziningi: lezo zinamathiselwa emodelini yazo kuphela. Khetha into eyodwa yomtapo ebekiwe bese uyikopisha.
- **{{ui:Namathisela}} uthi ikhophi lavela kwenye imodeli ngaphandle kwento yomtapo.** Beka into kusuka kuthebhu ye-{{ui:Umtapo}} kule thebhu kanye, bese uyikopisha futhi uyinamathisele.
- **{{key:Mod+C}} ikopisha umbhalo wekhasi esikhundleni.** Umbhalo wawukhethiwe ekhasini. Cofa imodeli kuqala.
