Ingxenye ethi {{ui:Hazard playback}} yephaneli elisekhohlo idlala ingozi kumodeli ye-IFC evulekile. Akukho okushintshwa kumodeli.

:::warning
Lezi yimodeli **elula zokubona ukuthi ingozi yenza ini kulolu hlelo lwesakhiwo**, hhayi ucwaningo lwezikhukhula, ukuhlolwa kwekhodi yomoya noma ukuklanywa kwesakhiwo. Umphumela ngamunye ubhala izimo zawo ezithathiwe.
:::

## Izikhukhula

Setha izinga eliphezulu lamanzi phezu kwesitezi esiphansi kakhulu nemizuzu yokukhuphuka, yokubamba nokwehla. Amanzi angena egumbini ngomgodi lapho ingcezu yawo yakudlula khona, nezindawo ezixhumene nezigumbi ezimanzi ngemigodi engaphansi kwamanzi zimi ezingeni elifanayo (quasi-static). Ithebhu ibonisa ukuthi yiliphi igumbi eliqala ukumanzisa nokujula kwalo; ipulani liyawudlala. Thayipha inani elikhulu kakhulu lamanzi igumbi elingawathatha, nezigumbi ezidlula kulona zibe yizexwayiso. **Ikusasa lesimo sezulu:** layisha ifayela lakho lesimo (JSON noma CSV enonyaka, ukukhuphuka kwezinga lolwandle nesici sezikhukhula) bese ukhetha unyaka. Noma vula okwengeziwe kwe-Climate futures bese ukhetha isimo nonyaka: izikhukhula zisebenza kabili, okwamanje kanye nokuzayo ngasohlangothini (bheka "Climate futures: 2050 and 2080 flood, heat and wildfire"). Izinhlobo ethi Wildfire exposure kanye ne-Heat: future weather file nazo zikuleli luhlu.

## Umoya

Ingcindezi ilandela ½ρV², nesivinini esikhuphuka ngokuphakama; amandla esitezi ngasinye yingcindezi leyo ephindwe ngesici sokuhlasela, izici ezimbili zengcindezi, ububanzi nobude besitezi. Uthola ukugoqa, umzuzu wokugingqika kanye nokunyakaza.

## Ukuzamazama komhlaba

Isakhiwo se-shear: ubunzima ngasinye phansi (indawo esakheke kuyo × amakhilogremu ngemitha yesikwele, owawusetha), ukuqina okufanayo kuzo zonke izitezi okulinganiswa ngesikhathi sokuqala owasinikeza (isikhathi sesigamu sesibili ngesitezi uma usishiya singabhalwanga, futhi sikutshela lokho), ukuncipha, nokunyakaza komhlaba okwenziwe (isigameko esisodwa noma umsindo ohlungiwe) ngesivinini esiphezulu osikhethayo. Umphumela uba ukuhamba kwesitezi ngasinye, izivinini zamafloor nesisindo esiphansi; ukuphakama kuyidlala ukunyakaza, kudwetshwe kukhulu kunokuba kunjalo. Thayipha isilinganiso sokuhamba osivumelayo, nezitezi ezidlula kuso zibe yizexwayiso. Kuyisimo se-elastic: akukho okuvuma noma okuqhekeka.

## Ukuqina kwezitezi kusuka ku-Analysis frame

Ukuze uthole ukuzamazama noma ukunyakaza komoya, khetha **The Analysis frame** njengomthombo wokuqina esikhundleni sokuthayipha isikhathi. Isitezi ngasinye siphushwa ecaleni ngokulandelana, ifreyimu (amabhimu, izinsika, izinsimbi zokuqinisa, namaslebhu) iyaxazululwa, futhi ukuqina kwezitezi konke kusebenza ndawonye kuyaqhuba ukusebenza. Isisindo yisisindo sezinto ezingokwazo kanye nomthwalo wesitezi ngamitha yesikwele owawusetha ipulani ngalinye lesitezi. Isikhathi sangempela sokuqala siyabonakala. Kuyisimo se-elastic: akukho okuvuma, nokujikeleza akumelwa. Ngomoya, amandla esitezi asasebenzisa lezi ezithathwe ngaphambili (iphrofayili yamandla, isici sakho sokuhlasela kanye nezici zengcindezi ezimisiwe, hhayi ikhodi yomoya; ukunyakaza kuphuma kufreyimu kuphela, ngokuxazulula ukuqina okuphelele kwefreyimu ngalawa mandla). Imodeli idinga i-IFC enefreyimu ethebhu Yokuhlaziya engayifunda.

## Amayunithi

Ukujula kwamanzi, ukunyakaza nokuhamba ngesitezi, izibalo nezindawo kubonakala ngokusho kwe-{{ui:Units}} oyikhethile. Amandla (kN), izikhathi nokusheshisa kugcina amayunithi azo, futhi inani olithayipha kubhokisi elibhalwe ngamamitha lihlala lingamamitha. Okufanayo kwimiphumela yokuhlanganiswa kwezindawo, ukukhanya kwemini, ukushisa nokupholisa kanye nokufaka izinto: ubude, izindawo nevolumu kulandela ukukhetha kwakho; ingcindezi, ukugeleza, izinga lokushisa, amandla nokusebenza kwawo akuguqulwa.
