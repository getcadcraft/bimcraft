## Okuzokwenza

Bona okushintshile phakathi kwemodeli onayo nowathunyelwe kuwe: yimaphi amazinto amasha, ayasusiwe, ayahanjiswa noma athola amanani ezici amasha.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula inguqulo entsha, bese uchofoza {{ui:Engeza imodeli…}} bese ukhetha eyendala. Bona [Bona amamodeli e-IFC amaningi ndawonye](topic:several-models).
2. Ku-{{ui:Amamodeli}}, vula {{ui:Qhathanisa izinguqulo}}.
3. Khetha {{ui:Inguqulo endala}} kanye {{ui:Inguqulo entsha}}.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Qhathanisa}}.
5. Chofoza into ohlwini ukuze uyikhethe ubone izici zayo. Chofoza {{ui:Landa i-CSV}} ukuze uthole konke okushintshile, isici ngesici.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Sula}} ukuze ubuyele embonweni ojwayelekile.

## Okufanele ukubone

- **Izakhi ezengeziwe** zibe obhakabhaka, zivela enguqulweni entsha.
- **Izakhi ezisusiwe** zibe bomvu, zivela enguqulweni endala.
- **Izakhi ezishintshiwe** zibe okuphuzi okunsundu, zivela enguqulweni entsha, zishela ukuthi yini eshintshile: izici, ukuhamba nokuthi kukhulu kangakanani, noma umumo omusha.
- Konke okufanayo kubonakala kukhanye.

## Okuhle ukukwazi

- Izakhi zihlanganiswa ngama-GlobalId awo. Uhlelo olunika izakhi ama-GlobalId amasha ekukhishweni ngakunye lenza ukuthi zonke izakhi zibonakale zisusiwe futhi zengeziwe kabusha.
- Izindawo zifaniswa lapho amamodeli ehlala khona esigcawini. Uma enye inguqulo yahanjiswa ngenhloso, yilinganise kuqala ngethuluzi {{ui:Hambisa}} ku-{{ui:Amamodeli}}.
- I-GlobalId esetshenziswe kabili kumodeli eyodwa ishiywe ngaphandle kokuqhathaniswa. [Hlola imodeli ngezinkinga ezivamile](topic:model-checks) iyazithola.
