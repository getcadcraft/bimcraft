## Okuzokwenza

Shintsha umqondo wakho ngento ngaphandle kokuyihambisa futhi, bese ugcina izinto zomtapo kumodeli zisesikhathini.

## Shintsha into eyodwa

1. Chofoza into yomtapo ebekiwe emodelini.
2. Ku-{{ui:Izici}}, chofoza {{ui:Shintsha into…}} ebhokisini i-**Library item**. Ithebhu {{ui:Umtapo}} iyavuleka.
3. Thola into entsha bese uchofoza ikhadi layo.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Shintsha … ibe lolu}}.

Into igcina indawo yayo, ukujika kwayo, isitezi, inombolo yento, inani nezinqaku. Uhlobo lwayo, isimo nedatha yayo kushintsha kube yinto entsha. Izinto ezibekwe ngendlela efanayo zabelana ngohlobo olufanayo, njengalapho uzibeka.

Into enezinhlobo ezihlukahlukene (bheka [Beka futhi ushintshe into yomtapo enezinhlobo](topic:library-variants)) ibala ezinye izinhlobo zayo phezulu kwethebhu {{ui:Umtapo}} lapho uyishintsha: ukuchofoza kukodwa kwalowo nalowo.

Uma into entsha iwuhlobo oluhlukile lwento, isibonelo isinki esikhundleni setafula, ikilasi le-IFC lento liyashintsha lifane nalo (lapha lisuka kwisakhiwo sefenisha lisiye kwisiphelo sokuhlanza). Ihlala iyinto efanayo, ne-GlobalId efanayo, ngakho izixhumanisi kuyo ziyasebenza. {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisa ikilasi elidala kanye nento endala.

## Hlola izibuyekezo

1. Chofoza {{ui:Izibuyekezo}} kumbhalo wamathuluzi (noma {{ui:Hlola izibuyekezo}} ebhokisini yento yomtapo).
2. Funda uhlu: yonke into yomtapo emodelini ngenguqulo yayo emodelini, inguqulo entsha kakhulu nesimo: **Up to date**, **Newer version**, **Withdrawn** noma **Not found**. Futhi itshela ukuthi yini eshintshile (ijometri, idatha, ilayisense noma ukulungiswa).
3. Chofoza {{ui:Buyekeza}} esihlanganeni, noma {{ui:Buyekeza konke}}.

## Okufanele ukubone

- "Buyekezwe *n* izibeka. Izibeka, izinombolo zento, izinani nezinqaku ziyagcinwa."
- Imigqa ethi "Buyekezwe: *into*" kuhlu lwezinguquko. {{key:Mod+Z}} iyahlehlisa ngayinye.

## Okufanele wazi

- Kuphela izinto ezivela emtapweni ziyahlolwa. Izinto ezivela emafayelini azinayo inombolo yomtapo.
- Uma ilayisense ishintsha, ikhophi osuyibekile igcina ilayisense eyayo kuze kube yilapho uyibuyekeza.
- Into **Withdrawn** ayinikezwa ukubekwa okusha. Eyayo ngenxa yamalungelo okushicilela ayikwazi ukulandwa; yishintshe nge-{{ui:Shintsha into…}}.
- Inhlobo ebekiwe igcina ukuba yinhlobo yayo uma inguqulo entsha yento inayo; uma ingenayo, ithatha inhlobo ezenzakalelayo yento.
- Ukuhlola kuthumela izinombolo zezinto nezinguqulo kuphela, akuthumeli imodeli yakho.
