## Okuzokwenza

Thola iphrojekthi elondolozwe njenge-`.pln` (iphrojekthi), `.pla` (i-archive nelayibrari yayo), `.tpl` (ithempulethi) noma `.bpn` (ukusekelwa) ukuze ungenise ku-BimCraft. Lawa ngamafayela e-Archicad angokwawo.

## Okuvulwa njengamanje

- **Amafayela ephrojekthi awavuli okwamanje.** Ifomethi yawo ayikashicilelwa, futhi i-BimCraft ayiwafundi okwamanje. Uma uvula elilodwa, i-BimCraft iyasho lokho futhi ikukhombise lapha.
- **I-BimCraft ihlola amabhithi okuqala efayela.** Amaphrojekthi e-Archicad kusukela enguqulweni 17 aqala ngesignature efanayo emfushane, ngakho i-BimCraft ingakutshela ngokuqinisekile ukuthi ifayela liphrojekthi ye-Archicad. Ifayela elinegama le-Archicad elingaqali ngaleyo ndlela mhlawumbe liqanjwe kabusha, lonakele, noma livela enguqulweni endala. Isignature ayihlukanisi iphrojekthi, i-archive nethempulethi.
- **Ukuthunyelwa kwe-IFC kuvula ngokugcwele**: imodeli ku-3D, izitezi, izindawo njengezikhala, idatha yezinto, amasethi ezakhiwo kanye nokuhlukaniswa, nakuyo yonke amathuluzi e-BimCraft.
- **Ukuthunyelwa kwe-DWG kuvula** ku-view ye-2D, nezendlela zayo nezakhiwo (layouts), nakwe-{{ui:3D}}. Ukuze uhlele, sebenzisa {{ui:Hlela ku-CadCraft}}.

Noma yisa iphrojekthi yonke njenge-zip eyodwa, ne-views, izakhiwo namaqembu wezakhiwo: bheka [Vula ibhandi leprojekthi](topic:open-project-bundle).

## Ukuthumela i-IFC (imodeli)

1. Vula iphrojekthi ku-Archicad.
2. Khetha **File > Save As**, bese ukhetha **IFC** njengohlobo lwefayela.
3. Khetha isiguquli se-IFC (igama le-Archicad lesethi yezilungiselelo zokuthumela i-IFC). Eyodwa ye-**IFC4 Reference View** noma **IFC2X3 Coordination View 2.0** ifanele i-BimCraft. I-IFC4X3 nayo iyavula.
4. Khetha okuzothunyelwa: iphrojekthi yonke, noma izinto ezibonakala kuphela kumazitezi obukele wona.
5. Londoloza, bese uchofoza {{ui:Khetha ifayela}} ku-BimCraft bese ukhetha ifayela `.ifc`.

Hlola lezi ezilungiselelweni zesiguquli ngaphambi kokuthumela:

- **Izindawo**: zithumele, ukuze amagumbi afike njengezikhala. Chofoza {{ui:Izindawo}} ku-BimCraft ukuze uzibone.
- **Izici nokuhlukaniswa**: zithumele, ukuze zibonakale ku-{{ui:Izici}} futhi zisetshenziswe ku-{{ui:Thola}}, {{ui:Umbala}} no-{{ui:Amanani aphelele}}.
- **`.ifc` evamile**, hhayi `.ifczip` noma `.ifcxml`. I-BimCraft ivula i-`.ifc` evamile kuphela.

## Ukuthumela i-DWG (imidwebo)

1. Vula ipulani, isigaba noma i-layout ofuna.
2. Khetha **File > Save As**, bese ukhetha **DWG** njengohlobo lwefayela.
3. Khetha isiguquli se-DXF-DWG, bese uyalondoloza.
4. Ukuze uthole iseti yonke yamashidi, londoloza ama-layout ku-Layout Book, noma uwapapashe njenge-DWG.
5. Vula i-`.dwg` ku-BimCraft. Imidwebo exhunywe ngokuthunyelwa ivulwa nayo uma isefolda efanayo; bheka [Imidwebo nezithombe ezixhunyiwe](topic:dwg-linked-files).

## Ukuxazulula izinkinga

- **"… iyiphrojekthi ye-Archicad: amabhithi ayo okuqala ayasho lokho."** Thumela i-IFC noma i-DWG njengoba kuchaziwe ngenhla, bese uvula leyo fayela.
- **"… iqanjwe njengefayela le-Archicad kodwa ayiqali njalo."** Bheka ukuthi ifike kuphi: mhlawumbe iyolunye uhlobo lwefayela olunegama elisha (zama ukulivula ngesandiso sangempela), ikhophi engakaqedi, noma iphrojekthi evela enguqulweni engaphansi kwe-17. Yivule ku-Archicad uma ukwazi, bese uthumela i-IFC noma i-DWG.
- **Imodeli ivula ngaphandle kwamagumbi.** Izindawo azithunyelwanga. Zivule kusiguquli se-IFC bese uthumela futhi.
- **Izimbobo azinqunywanga.** I-BimCraft ikutshela lokho ngaphansi kwe-**Akukadwetshwa okwamanje** ephaneli ye-{{ui:File}}. Thumela futhi; uma kuhlala, vula imodeli ngohlelo olwayenza bese uhlola izimbobo lapho.
- **Umbhalo ku-DWG ubukeka ngokwehlukile.** Ukuthunyelwa kungagcina amafonti e-BimCraft engenawo; i-BimCraft idweba umbhalo ngefonti elula ye-sans-serif.

Bheka futhi [Thola ifayela lephrojekthi ku-3D nge-IFC](topic:rvt-to-ifc) kanye [Okufundwa yi-BimCraft kusuka kuyo ifomethi ngayinye](topic:file-formats).
