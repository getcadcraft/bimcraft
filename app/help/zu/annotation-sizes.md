## Okuzokwenza

Setha ukuthi amagama emidwebo yakho aphakama kangakanani ephepheni, kanye ngesakhiwo. Awulokothi ulinganise imodeli futhi awulokothi uguqule usayizi wombhalo ngesikali: i-BimCraft ibala usayizi ngokuphakama kephepha nesikali sedwetshwe.

## Indlela esebenza ngayo

- Imodeli isayizi ogcwele. Udonga olunobude be-10 m luyi-10 000 mm ubude kuzo zonke izikali.
- Ukuchazwa kunokuphakama **kwephepha**: ukuphakama kohlamvu olukhulu ekhasini elishicilelwe, ngamamilimitha.
- Ngesikali se-1:100 umbhalo ongu-2.5 mm uba ngu-250 mm ukuphakama emodelini; ku-1:50 uba ngu-125 mm. Ephepheni ungu-2.5 mm kokubili.
- Izimpawu zepulani (zikagesi, zepayipi) zidwetshwa ngamamilimitha ephepheni futhi zilandela umthetho ofanayo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula i-project browser. Ngaphansi kwesihloko {{ui:Sheets}} chofoza {{ui:Annotation sizes…}}.
2. Thayipha ukuphakama ngamamilimitha ezinhlobeni ofuna ukuzisetha: {{ui:Notes and labels}}, {{ui:Dimension text}}, {{ui:Tags and level names}}, {{ui:Room names}}, {{ui:Grid bubble letters}}, {{ui:View titles on sheets}} and {{ui:Schedule text}}. Ibhokisi elingenalutho ligcina usayizi we-BimCraft, obonakala ngegrey.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Save}}. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa ongasihlehlisa.

Zonke amashidi, isethi yokudweba, i-PDF yepulani kanye nokubukeka kohlangothi nezigaba zisebenzisa lolu sayizi kusukela manje. Igebe phakathi komugqa wesilinganiso nombhalo wawo, usayizi webhamuza legridi nokuphakama komugqa wohlu kukhula kanye nombhalo wabo.

## Ipulani enesikali sayo

Nika ipulani isikali sayo kuluhlu lokubuka (ku-{{ui:Scale}}; bona [Ukubuka namashidi](topic:views-and-sheets)). Khona:

- ekuboneni, amagama amagumbi, amathegi, umbhalo wezilinganiso namabhamuza egridi adwetshwa ngosayizi wawo ophrintiwe ngokumelene nemodeli, ngakho okubonayo kufana nalokho ishidi lizokukhombisa;
- izimpawu zepulani namathegi awo zilinganiswa ngalesi sikali. Shintsha isikali sisuke ku-1:100 siye ku-1:50 zibe ngamahhafu emodelini futhi zifane ephepheni.

Ipulani engenaso isikali sayo igcina umbhalo wayo wesikrini osungiwe, nezimpawu zayo zilinganiswa ku-1:50 (1/4" = 1'-0" ngamayunithi ezinyawo nezintshi), njengakuqala.

## Usayizi wehhovisi lakho

Ngesengezo i-Default Policies ivulekile, setha amabanga ombhalo wehhovisi ku-Izilungiselelo › Global › Default Policies › {{ui:Usayizi wezichasiso ephepheni}}. Isakhiwo esisha sithatha lawo mabanga lapho uchofoza Create. Esakhiweni osesiqalile, ingxoxo inayo {{ui:Sebenzisa usayizi wehhovisi lami}}. Bona [Default Policies](topic:default-policies).

## Usayizi wephakheji yemithetho

Iphakheji yemithetho inganikeza amabanga ombhalo aphakanyiswa yindawo yayo (i-add-on yeBuilding regulations, izivumelwano zokudweba). Thikha iphakheji ku-Izilungiselelo › Building regulations, bese ku-ingxoxo uchofoza {{ui:Sebenzisa usayizi wephakheji yemithetho}}: amabhokisi ayagcwala, uwahlole bese uyagcina. Usayizi iphakheji elinikeza ngamapoyinti ungusayizi wefonti, futhi uguqulwa ube ukuphakama kohlamvu olukhulu, ngakho hlola. Akukho okusethwayo kuze ulondoloze.

## Okuzokubona

- Ukuphakama kombhalo okufanayo ngamamilimitha ishidi elingu-1:100 nelingu-1:50, kuyilapho ipulani ngokwayo ingaphindwe kabili ku-1:50.
- Usayizi uhamba nesakhiwo: ugcinwa kufayela lakhe le-IFC.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Usayizi we-BimCraft ngokwawo wukhona ayewusebenzisa amashidi ayo kuze kube manje (cishe ohlamvu olukhulu abangu-1.5 mm wombhalo wezilinganiso). Awuyona imigomo yokudweba: setha owehhovisi lakho.
- Ukuphakama kufanele kube phakathi kuka-0.5 no-50 mm.
- Umbhalo wesihloko sephepha nezincazelo zokhiye azihlangene nalokhu: zigcina usayizi wazo.
