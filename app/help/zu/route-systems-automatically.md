## Okuzokwenza

Vumela i-BimCraft yakhe inethiwekhi yemigqa phakathi kwezixhumi ezikhona kimodeli, izungeze izindonga nemishayo, bese uyihlola ngezinombolo oziboná.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli nokuhlola ukuxabana. Ngaphansi kwe-**Qondisa amapayipi omoya ngokuzenzakalelayo** khetha isiphephisi somoya nezinsiza zaso, noma ngaphansi kwe-**Qondisa ezinye izinhlelo ngokuzenzakalelayo** khetha uhlelo.
2. **Ukukhipha amanzi isanitari ngamandla adonsayo.** Thayipha indawo yesikhala somgodi nezindawo zesitaki uma unazo. Thika **Qondisa zonke izitezi ngesikhathi esisodwa** ukuthatha zonke izitezi ezinezifakelo: imifudlana yesitezi ngasinye ihlangana nesitaki ezingeni layo, futhi isitaki sigijima kusukela phezu kwepayipi eliphakeme kakhulu kuya esiseni sayo. Cindezela {{ui:Qondisa}}.
3. **Itrapu kuya ophephumulweni.** Ipaneli ikhomba ibanga kusukela kwitrapu ngayinye kuya kumbobo wayo wokuphefumula. Thayipha ibanga elikhulu kakhulu ikhodi yakho elivumela ku-**Ibanga elikhulu kusukela etrapini kuya ophephumulweni**; noma yisiphi isifakelo esidlula lapho sikhonjiswa njengesingenziwe, nobukhulu obudlula ngamamilimitha. Thika **Phefumula ngesitaki** uma izifakelo zingenawo amakhonkco okuphefumula azo: ibanga-ke likalwa kuya esitakini. I-BimCraft ayinathebula lekhodi kulokhu: wena uthayipha inombolo.
4. **Amanzi abandayo nashisayo.** Khetha umthombo, uthayipha uxinzelelo emthonjeni nalo olidingayo esifakelweni. Thika **Amanzi ashisayo**, khetha ixhumi elishisayo lesishisi, bese uthika **Isiyingi sokubuyisela** ukuze uphelise umugqa wokubuyisa ekhonjini le-**hot-return** lesishisi, noma kunoma yiliphi ixhumi okukhethayo (leli liphela ngo-150 mm ngaphansi, livulekile, ukuze uwuxhumanise wena).
5. **Ukuhlukanisa, ukuhlukaniswa kabaningi kanye ne-VRF.** Khetha iyunithi yangaphandle, thika iyunithi yangaphakathi, bese uthayipha amandla ngayinye. Thayipha imikhawulo esuka kudatha yomkhiqizi wakho. Thayipha **ubude olulinganayo** bokugoba nekhithi yesihlukanisi (amanani omkhiqizi), bese ubude obude nobuphelele buhlolwa ngobude obulinganayo.
6. Cindezela {{ui:Qondisa}}. Ipaneli ikhomba ukuthi mingaki imigqa edwebiwe, nanoma yini engakwazanga ukuyenza nesizathu sayo. {{ui:Hlehlisa}} ibuyisela konke ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

## Okufanele ukubone

Amapayipi anamaqhina, amathi nezinciphisi; imifudlana ewela okungenani ukuthambeka okuncane kakhulu kusayizi wayo; izinti zokuphefumula ezifakelweni; imigqa ye-refrigerant njengepheya lolwelo negesi eguquka ngendlela efanayo kuwo wonke amakhona, nekhithi yesihlukanisi emakiwe lapho igatsha liphuma kumgogodla.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Lapho impompo yomfudlana ihlangana nomunye umfudlana, imodeli idweba ithi. Ipulani ibiza lezo zindawo ngokuthi amaxhumano e-wye angu-45 amadigri ukuze ushintshe isifakelo.
- Amanzi ashisayo asebenzisa ixhumi lokushisa lesifakelo uma umndeni waso unalo, kanti uma ungenalo asebenzise ixhumi elingu-100 mm eceleni kwelibandayo.
- Imigqa ye-refrigerant iqala phakathi kwezimbobo ezimbili zeyunithi ngayinye. Uma izimbobo zeyunithi zimi zisondelene kakhulu ukuze kugobe, ipheya ihlala iphakathi kwazo.
- Inethiwekhi yamanzi isebenzisa ijika lesidingo nezinombolo zokumelana ezikhonjiswa ezibalweni; zishintshe ngezekhodi yakho ngaphambi kokuthembela kuzingcindezelo.
- Izindlu ezinkulu zingathatha isigamu sesikhathi noma ngaphezulu, ikakhulu inethiwekhi eshisayo.
- Lesi yisisiza sokudweba. Umuntu oqeqeshiwe uhlola usayizi, ukuthambeka, izimbobo zokuphefumula nezingcindezelo ngokumelana nekhodi esebenzayo.
