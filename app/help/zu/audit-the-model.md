## Okuzokwenza

Bheka imodeli yonke ngesikhathi esisodwa ngaphambi kokuyikhipha. Ukuhlola kugijima izinhlu zemithetho ezilungele bese kubeka okutholakele ku-{{ui:Problems}} njengeseluleko. Akukho okuvinjelwayo: ulungisa okutholakele, noma uyakwamukela ngesizathu, njengezinye izinkinga.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula {{ui:Model checks}} › {{ui:Audit}} kwiphaneli yesobunxele, noma {{ui:Tools}} › {{ui:Audit the model…}} (uthayipha: `AUDIT`).
2. Khetha {{ui:Project stage}}. Yisigaba esifanayo nasemininingwaneni yemidwebo.
3. Khetha izinhlu zemithetho ozozigijima.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Run the audit}}.
5. Funda umugqa ngamunye wezinhlu zemithetho, bese uvula {{ui:Open the Problems list}}. Ukuchofoza okutholakele kukhetha into yakho.
6. {{ui:The check report}} ilanda umbiko: konke okutholakele, ukuthi ubani wamukela ini nangani, nezinhlu zemithetho ezigijimile.

## Izinhlu zemithetho

- **Amagumbi angaqanjwanga**: igumbi elingenagama, noma elisabizwa ngalokho uhlelo lwalibiza ngakho ("Room 3").
- **Amathegi alahlekile**: umnyango noma iwindi elingenaso isibonakaliso, igumbi elingenanombolo, into kagesi engenalo ithegi.
- **Izinto ngaphansi kwe-LOD yesigaba**: udonga ngalunye, iphansi, isiling, uphahla, umnyango newindi ngalinye elinge-LOD ingaphansi kwalokho isigaba esikucelayo. Into ifunda ithegi lohlobo lwayo le-LOD; into eqinisekiswe endaweni ingu-LOD 500. Izinto ezingenathegi zibalwa emnweni munye, ngoba azikwazi ukuhlolwa.
- **Ukungqubuzana phakathi kwezizinda**: izinto zezizinda ezimbili (ukwakheka, isakhiwo, imishini, ipayipi, ugesi, ngeklasi ye-IFC) ezingqubuzanayo. Uphahla olusekela izindonga zalo alubalwa.
- **Ixhumano le-LOD 350**: wonke umnyango newindi ahlala endaweni evulekile edongeni lawo, ukuvuleka okulingana ngaphakathi kodonga, izivulo ezimbili azigqumi, ukuvuleka okubanzi kunomkhawulo kunelintel noma ikhanda (into phezu kwalo, noma inothi emnyangweni noma ewindini), nepayipi, umtapo womoya noma i-cable tray edlula udonga noma iphansi kunokuvuleka khona. Okutholakele kubalwa ngamapheya ezizinda.
- **Izinto ezibonakele ezingahlolwanga yimuntu**: izindonga, iminyango, amawindi namagumbi afundwe kusuka kwi-PDF, kusikena noma emdwebweni, okusengakaqinisekiswa. Bona [Maka izinto njengezihloliwe noma ezi-qinisekiswe endaweni](topic:verification).

## I-LOD esicelwa yisigaba ngasinye

Vula {{ui:The LOD each stage asks for}} ukuze ubone noma uguqule ithebula.

:::note
Ithebula nobubanzi belintel kungamanani okuqala e-CadCraft. Awathathwanga esilinganisweni; iphrojekthi isetha i-LOD ngesigaba ngasinye kuhlelo lwayo lokusebenza kwe-BIM. Yisethe ngokwakho. Ihhovisi lingasetha ithebula kubo bonke ku-Izilungiselelo › Default Policies › Audit; ushintsho esigabeni se-Audit ligcinwa kule kompyutha.
:::

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ilintel lingabhalwa esikhundleni sokuyimodela: khetha umnyango noma iwindi bese ugcwalisa {{ui:Lintel or header note}} ngaphansi kwesihloko {{ui:Verification}} ku-Izakhiwo.
- Ipayipi elidlula kuvulo langempela alidluli ngaphezulu kwesipha futhi alibikwa. Elimodelwe liqonda udonga lidlule kulo libikwa.
- Ukuhlolwa kokungqubuzana kuyayeka emva kwemizuzwana embalwa emodelini enkulu kakhulu futhi kuyasho lokho. Ukuhlolwa okuphelele kwezinhlu ezimbili ozikhethile sebenzisa [Thola ukungqubuzana phakathi kwezinto](topic:clash-check).
- Gijima ukuhlola kabusha uma ushintsha imodeli. Okutholakele okwamukelwe kuhlala kwamukelwe uma izinombolo zabo zingashintshi.
- Ifunda imodeli. Ayithathelwa indawo ukubuyekezwa kokuhlelwa ngabantu abanesibopho.
