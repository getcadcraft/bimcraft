Lokhu yikho okushintshe ku-BimCraft 0.9, kuqhathaniswa ne-0.8.1. Izindikimba ezimbili ezinkulu yithuluzi lodonga langempela nemibono eziziphatha ngendlela abantu abavela kuzinhlelo ezinkulu ze-BIM abayilindele. Ezinye zifika ngakho: ukukhipha i-Collada, abasizi abangabeka izinsiza zikagesi, Scan to BIM, nokusebenza komoya, ukuzamazama nezakhi ze-finite-element kuthebhu ye-{{ui:Ukuhlaziya}}.

## Okusha

### Izindonga

- **Dweba izimo ezigcwele.** Ku-{{ui:Dweba}}, vula {{ui:Izimo}} (noma khetha isimo ohlwini lwezimo kumugqa wedonga) ukuze uthole irektangele, irektangele egqunyiwe, ipholigoni elinezinhlangothi ezi-3 kuya kwezi-64, i-arc ngesikhungo, i-arc ethintayo, izindonga ezikhethwe emigqeni, noma izindonga ezizungeze indawo evaliwe ocindezela phakathi kwayo. Irektangele inezindonga ezine namakhona amane ahlanganisiwe kanye nesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bona [Izimo zezindonga, ukuhlukanisa, ukweluleka nokukhiya](topic:wall-tools).
- **Beka ngaphambi kokudweba, shintsha ngemva.** Umugqa wedonga unezinketho ezithi {{ui:Ukuhlukana}} no-{{ui:Hlanganisa}} (khipha ukukhetha ukuze udwebe izindonga ezimi ngokwazo). Ngemva kwalokho sebenzisa {{ui:Ukuhlukana}}, {{ui:Ukweluleka ngebhokisi}} no-{{ui:Linganisa futhi ukhiye}} kumadonga adwetshiwe. Udonga oluhanjisiwe noma oluweluleke luhlala lunawo amasango, amawindi nezilinganiso zawo. Yonke iresiphi iyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- **Sika izindonga.** {{ui:Hlukanisa}}, {{ui:Igebe}}, {{ui:Izimpelo ezikhululekile}}, {{ui:Susa ingxenye}} no-{{ui:Hlanganisa kabusha}} zisika udonga ngokuchofoza noma lapho olunye udonga lulisika khona. Ukusika esangweni kuyenqatshwa. Bona [Izimo zezindonga, ukuhlukanisa, ukweluleka nokukhiya](topic:wall-tools).
- **Khiya izindonga emigqeni yegridi.** Khetha udonga oluhambisana nomugqa wegridi bese uthika {{ui:Khiya egridini}} ku-{{ui:Izici}}. Ngethuluzi legridi livuliwe, thayipha ibanga ku-{{ui:Hambisa ngo}} komugqa okhethiwe, bese izindonga ezikhiyiwe zilandela ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- **Umuthi udonga olulodwa.** Ku-{{ui:Izici}} zodonga olulodwa ungaxhumanisa phezulu kwalo noma phansi kwalo kuphahla, iphansi noma ulingqo, uhlele umugqa wokuphakama kwalo, uwuthambekise, noma usike ukuvulwa kodonga ngaphandle kwesango noma iwindi. Bona [Imo yodonga](topic:wall-shape).
- **Ubude buchaza ukuthi yiliphi icala elihlala.** {{ui:Khula kusukela}} ichaza ukuthi ukuqala, isiphetho noma isikhungo kuyahlala lapho ushintsha ubude.
- **Ubude nezilinganiso zephlani ziyavumelana.** {{ui:Linganisa kusukela}} (umugqa wesikhungo, ubuso bangaphakathi noma ubuso bangaphandle) iqhuba kokubili inkambu yobude nezilinganiso zodonga epulanini. Iqala emugqeni wesikhungo, ngakho izilinganiso zodonga epulanini manje zifunda umugqa wesikhungo lapho zazifunda ubuso bangaphandle ngaphambili. Yishintshe ku-Umugqa wodonga, ku-{{ui:Izici}} noma ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:BimCraft}} › {{ui:Izindonga}}.

### Amazinga, amagridi nokulinganisa

- **Engeza izinga ngaphansi.** {{ui:Engeza izinga ngaphansi}} kuphaneli le-{{ui:Amazinga}} yengeza izinga ngaphansi kolisezansi, lide njengalo, ngokukhwezwa okungeyona into engeyinhle (Level -1, Level -2). Akukho okunye okunyakazayo. Manje {{ui:Engeza amazinga…}} yamukela ubude obungelona eyinhle. Ukuthayipha LEVEL, LEVELS, STOREY noma STOREYS emugqeni womyalo kuvula {{ui:Engeza amazinga…}}, futhi kukhona inkinobho ye-{{ui:Amazinga}} emenyini nasemathebhini e-ribbon. Bona [Izindawo, izakhiwo namazinga](topic:site-building-and-levels).
- **Izilinganiso zegridi epulanini.** Ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Igridi}} livuliwe, thika {{ui:Izilinganiso}} ukuze udwebe ibanga phakathi kwemigqa eseduze kanye nosayizi ojwayelekile ngaphandle kwamabhamuza kuwo wonke epulani lenhla ngayinye. Bona [Linganisa igridi](topic:grid-dimensions).
- **Linganisa epulanini.** {{ui:Linganisa}} eqenjini le-{{ui:Dweba}} inikeza ibanga nomehluko we-X ne-Y phakathi kwezinkinobho ezimbili. Akukho okulondolozwayo emodelini. Bona [Linganisa epulanini](topic:measure-on-the-plan).
- **Shintsha amakholomu amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa.** Khetha amakholomu kanye nodonga, bese uhlu lubonisa uhlobo ngalunye nge-{{ui:Gcina lezi kuphela}}. Inkinobho eyodwa igcina amakholomu futhi ikhombise izinkambu azo ezabelanayo.
- **Iyunithi eyodwa yokubonisa yonke indawo.** Izixhumanisi, izilinganiso, izigaba, izikali zamashidi, izinkinga nezikhwama zethebhu yefreyimu zilandela iyunithi oyikhethayo. Ukuphuma kugcina i-SI.

### Ukuhlaziya

- **Idatha yomoya nokuzamazama yesayithi.** Ku-{{ui:Ukuhlaziya}}: imithwalo yomoya ngokwe-ASCE 7-10 noma 7-16 yesakhiwo esiqinile esivaliwe, nenani lamandla kuwo wonke ungqimba njengamacala emithwalo, kanye nokuthola idatha yesayithi yokuzamazama nge-USGS nesipekthra sokuklama.
- **Ukulandelela kwesakhiwo nezakhi ze-finite.** Ukuhlolwa kwekhonkrithi ngokwe-ACI 318-19, izinkosi ezilinganayo zokuzamazama ngokwe-ASCE 7-22, ukuthuthuka kwezitezi, amafulowa nezindonga njengamapuleti, nokuhlanganiswa kwemithwalo ngokwe-ASCE 7 ne-EN 1990. Injini yethu yakhe ye-finite-element yenza ama-shell, ama-tetrahedron nokuhlaziya kwemodi. Bona [Hlola izakhi zekhonkrithi futhi wenze imithwalo yokuzamazama](topic:concrete-and-seismic) no-[Ukuhlaziya kohlaka](topic:frame-analysis).

## Ngaphambili: 0.8 no-0.8.1

Lokhu yikho okushintshe ku-BimCraft 0.8 (2026-11-01) ne-0.8.1 (2026-11-02), kuqhathaniswa ne-0.7. Manje i-BimCraft ingaphendula imibuzo ngesakhiwo futhi iyihlole, ngabantu, umsindo, amanzi, umoya nokuzamazama, futhi iyidwebe ngamathuluzi amaningi; i-0.8.1 ibukeka njengokulungiswa.

### Okusha

**Hlola isakhiwo**

- **Abantu esakhiweni.** Isigaba se-{{ui:Abantu esakhiweni}} sepaneli elingakwesobunxele sihamba nokuhlehla ngesikhathi sokuphuma noma ukufika kwansuku zonke ngamagumbi, iminyango nezitebhisi zakho. Ubona izindlela lapho isakhiwo sincipha, ukuhamba okude kakhulu ukuya ekuphumeni kusuka kugumbi ngalinye nokuthi isiphi sokugcina sithatha isikhathi esingakanani. Thika {{ui:Vala}} ku-door ukubona ukuthi yini eshintsha, bese uthika {{ui:Bonisa abantu kumbono we-3D}} ukubukela abahambi. Imiphumela ifika ku-Izinkinga. Kuyimodeli yokubhekisa ngokubanzi yezinqumo zokuklama, hhayi isitifiketi sokuphepha komlilo. Bona [Hambisa abantu esakhiweni](topic:people-in-the-building).
- **Ukuzwakala kwegumbi, nokulizwa.** Isigaba se-{{ui:Ukuzwakala kwegumbi}} sinikeza isikhathi sokuhlanganisa kugumbi ngalinye ngebhendi ye-octave (Sabine no-Eyring) kusuka kusayizi walo nezinhlobo zokuqeda oziqokile, futhi ngenhloso yakho yakho ithi ukumuncwa okwanele ukuze kuhlangabezane nayo. {{ui:Yidlale kuleligumbi}} idlala izwi, ukushaya izandla, izinothi ezikhishwe noma ifayela lomsindo lakho ngomthelela wokuphendula okubalwe ngegumbi. Izinombolo zokumuncwa ziwukuchaza kwendabuko okushicilelwe kwe-typical values ukuze ushintshe. Bona [Ukuzwakala kwegumbi](topic:room-acoustics).
- **Ukudlala ukugeleza kwamanzi, umoya nokuzamazama.** {{ui:Ukudlala kwengozi}} idlala amanzi akhuphuka ngamagumbi, ingcindezi yomoya ezitezini ngayinye nokuzamazama okushukumisa izitezi, phezu kwepulani noma kwenkulumo ngesilayidi sesikhathi. Amamodeli alula nokuqagela kwawo kuphaneli; hhayi ukuhlola kwekhodi. Bona [Dlala ukugeleza kwamanzi, umoya noma ukuzamazama](topic:hazard-playback).
- **Okuyogcina kuguga kumashumi amahlanu eminyaka.** I-{{ui:Umbono wempilo yonke}} idwesela amawindi, iminyango, ukuqedwa nophahla, ngokuphila kwenkonzo ngayinye, iminyaka lapho zishintshwa khona, futhi uma uthayipha inani ngeyunithi, inani ngonyaka nenani lamanje. {{ui:Londoloza uhlelo (CSV)}} ilibhala. Izikhathi zenkonzo ziyizinombolo zokuhlela. Bona [Bona okuguga eminyakeni engamashumi amahlanu](topic:life-cycle-view).
- **Izikhathi zekhonkrithi nezokuzamazama.** Izigaba ze-{{ui:Izakhi zekhonkrithi}} ne-{{ui:Imithwalo yokuzamazama}} zethebhu ye-{{ui:Ukuhlaziya}} zihlola izinsika nezingqi zekhonkrithi ngokwe-ACI 318-19 zenze amandla ancintisana ngokwe-ASCE 7-22 emasongweni eqinile, nokuthuthuka kwezitezi. Okwenziwa injiniyela ukuhlola. Bona [Hlola izakhi zekhonkrithi futhi wenze imithwalo yokuzamazama](topic:concrete-and-seismic).

**Hlola nokubika**

- **Hlola nge-IDS.** Layisha ifayela le-`.ids` umklienti akunike lona bese ubona zonke izakhi ezingahlangabezani nalo ku-Izinkinga, ngaphambi kokulethela i-IFC. Vula {{ui:Hlola nge-IDS}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ukuhlola imodeli}}. Bona [Hlola imodeli ngokwe-IDS yeklayenti](topic:check-against-ids).
- **Amathegi namashejuli.** Chaza amathegi akho, uwabeke ezakhi eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa futhi wenze amashejuli amakholomu, izihlungi namaqembu awafunda; amathegi agcinwa ku-IFC. Bona [Amathegi namashejuli](topic:tags-and-schedules).
- **Amadokhumenti emodelini.** {{ui:Amadokhumenti kusuka kumodeli}} igcwalisa ithemplethi yezinkomba kusuka ku-IFC evulekile bese iyigcina njenge-PDF, idokhumenti ye-Word noma i-Markdown. Bona [Amadokhumenti kusuka kumodeli](topic:documents-from-the-model).
- **Qhathanisa i-scan nemodeli.** Ngepoyinti lefu elivulekile, {{ui:Qhathanisa}} ihlola ngalinye isakhi njengesakhiwe, esakhiwe ngokuphelele, esingekho noma esingafinyelelwe, futhi ikhombisa amaphesenti akhiwe. Bona [Qhathanisa i-scan nemodeli](topic:compare-scan-with-model).
- **Ikhabhoni nezindleko.** I-Carbon pane ifaka ikhabhoni eyenziwe ngokusebenzisa itheyibhu yezinto ongayihlela, ne-Cost pane isakaza izintengo ngesheduli ye-4D.
