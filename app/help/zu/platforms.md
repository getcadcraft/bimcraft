I-BimCraft isebenza kumaguqulo amanje e-Chrome, i-Edge, i-Firefox ne-Safari, ku-Mac, Windows nasekuLinux. Ukubuka kwe-3D kudinga i-WebGL, okuyinto esekelwa yisiphequluli ngasinye samanje. Bheka [Izinsiphequluli nezikhompyutha](common:browsers) ukuze uthole okudingwa yi-apps ngokuvamile.

## Ukuvula ifolda

| Isiphequluli | {{ui:Vula ifolda}} yenza lokhu | Ukuhudula ifolda ngaphakathi | {{ui:Amafolda akamuva}} |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chrome, Edge | Isikhethi sefolda sesiphequluli, bese "Vumela isayithi ukubona amafayela?": chofoza **View files** | Ivuleka ngaphandle kokubuza | Yebo, kwamafolda akhethiwe noma adonselwe ngaphakathi |
| Firefox | Isikhethi sefolda sesistimu | Ivuleka ngaphandle kokubuza | Cha |
| Safari | Isikhethi sefolda sesistimu, nenkinobho **Upload** (akukho okulayishwayo) | Ivuleka ngaphandle kokubuza | Cha |

Ngemuva kwesigameko: i-Chrome ne-Edge zisebenzisa isikhethi se-File System Access futhi zingakhumbula ifolda. I-Firefox ne-Safari zisebenzisa isikhethi sefolda sakudala, esinikeza wonke amafayela efolda ngesikhathi esisodwa futhi asikwazi ukukhunjulwa. Ifolda edonselwe ngaphakathi ifundwa ngesinengiso ngasinye kuwo wonke amasiphequluli.

:::chromium
I-Brave kanye nezinye izisiphequluli ze-Chromium ezithile zivala isikhethi sefolda. I-BimCraft-ke isebenzisa isikhethi sefolda sesistimu, futhi isiphequluli singabuza ukuthi "Layisha amafayela *n* kule sayithi?". Akukho okulayishwayo; amafayela aya kuphela ethebhini. Ukuhudula ifolda ngaphakathi kugwema lombuzo.
:::

## Mac, Windows nasekuLinux

- Izinqamuleli ezibhalwe {{key:Mod+S}} kanye {{key:Mod+Z}} ziyi-Command ku-Mac futhi ziyi-Ctrl ku-Windows nasekuLinux.
- {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} (izindlela zesikrini) iyi-Control kuwo wonke amapulatifomu, kuhlanganise ne-Mac.
- Izingxoxo zamafayela kanye nefolda yokulanda zeqembu lesistimu yakho.

## Imouse, i-trackpad nokuthinta

| Okokufaka | Ukubuka kwe-3D | Umdwebo we-2D |
|---|---|---|
| Mouse | Ukudonsa kwesokunxele kuzungeza, ukudonsa kwesokudla kushelelisa, insimbi yevili isondeza, ukuchofoza kukhetha | Noma iyiphi indonga ishelelisa, insimbi yevili isondeza, ukuchofoza kabili kulinganisa |
| Trackpad | Ukuchofoza-nokudonsa kuzungeza; {{key:Shift}} noma {{key:Mod}} kanye nokudonsa kushelelisa; ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili noma ukucindezela kusondeza | Ukuchofoza-nokudonsa kushelelisa; ukuskrola ngeminwe emibili noma ukucindezela kusondeza |
| Ukuthinta | Umunwe owodwa uzungeza, iminwe emibili icindezela futhi ishelelise, ukuthinta kukhetha | Umunwe owodwa ushelelisa; asikho ukucindezela kokusondeza okwamanje; thinta {{ui:Lwanele}} |

Ku-trackpad ye-Mac, ukuchofoza ngokwesokudla kuyinkinobho yeminwe emibili (noma ukuchofoza nge-Control). Bheka [Izinkinobho, ama-mouse nama-trackpad](common:keys).

Amathebhulethi namafoni angavula amafayela awodwa abuke kuwo, kodwa isikrini sincane, izisiphequluli eziningi zeselula azikwazi ukukhetha amafolda, futhi amamodeli amakhulu angangeneki enkumbulweni yawo. Ikhompyutha isebenza kangcono kakhulu.

## Amafayela amakhulu nenkumbulo

Konke i-BimCraft eyakufunda kuhlala enkumbulweni yethebhu yesiphequluli. Ithebhu ingasebenzisa kangakanani incike kakhulu esiphequluleni nasekhompyutheni yakho, kunokuba incike ku-BimCraft.

| Ifayela | Okufundwa kangakanani | Okulindeleke |
|---|---|---|
| IFC | Ifayela lonke, kanye ne-geometry ye-3D eyakhiwe kulo | Ubukhulu bungancika ekhompyutheni nesiphequluli; imodeli enamamegabhithi ayamakhulu ingaba ngcono kakhulu kulaptop. Ngaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-1.5 zama-triangle, imiphetho iyacishwa |
| Amafayela ephrojekthi nomndeni | Cishe i-40 KB kuya ku-100 KB, noma ubukhulu bungaba yini | Ngisho namamodeli amakhulu amaphrojekthi avula ngokushesha, nasedrayivu yenethiwekhi |
| DWG kanye DXF | Ifayela lonke | Imidwebo emikhulu kakhulu ibonisa "This drawing is very large; only part of it is shown." |
| Amafolda | Uhlu lwamafayela, kanye nama-kilobytes ambalwa ngemodeli ngayinye | Kufika kumafayela angu-20,000 |

Uma ithebhu iphelelwa yinkumbulo, isiphequluli sibonisa ikhasi laso lephutha ("Aw, Snap!" ku-Chrome, njengesibonelo). Vala amanye amathebhu, layisha kabusha i-BimCraft bese uzama futhi, noma sebenzisa ikhompyutha enenkumbulo eningi.
