## Okufanele ukwenze

Qinisekisa ukuthi amasevisi ayahambisana nesakhiwo: yini engena esakhiweni, yini edlula eduze kakhulu nakho, nokuthi amadonga namasilabhu adinga izimbobo kuphi. Vula {{ui:Ukuhlola ukungqubuzana}} ephaneli yesokunxele; isigaba **Amapayipi nemisele yomoya kanye nesakhiwo** sisendaweni evamile yokuhlola.

## Ukungqubuzana okuqinile

Uhlu lwamasethi amabili manje lunikeza futhi **Amapayipi nemisele yomoya**, **Amadonga**, **Amasilabhu namaphahla**, **Amabhimu**, **Amakholomu** kanye **Nesakhiwo** (zonke ezine), kanye nenani lezinto ngayinye. Chofoza {{ui:Amapayipi nemisele yomoya × isakhiwo}} ukukhetha okokuqala nokokugcina bese uqalisa ukuhlola; noma khetha noma yiluphi uhlobo lwemibili wena bese uchofoza {{ui:Qalisa}}. Bona [Hlola ukungqubuzana](topic:clash-check).

## Amagebe

Ezinye izinsiza zidinga indawo ezizungezile: ukugoqa kwazo, nendawo yokusebenza noma yeleve.

1. Nikeza ipayipi noma umsele womoya ubukhulu be-**Insulation** kuZici (akukho okudwetshwa ngakho).
2. Thayipha i-**Igebe elizungeze amapayipi nemisele yomoya** (mm) ku-Ukuhlola ukungqubuzana.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Hlola amabanga}}: yonke ingxenye esondele kudonga, kusilabhu, kubhimu noma kukholomu kunokugoqwa kwayo kuphezu kwegebe ibhalwa kanye nebanga elinalo nelifunayo. Chofoza umugqa ukubona izingxenye ezimbili.

Ingxenye edlula ngodonga noma ngesilabhu iyimbobo, hhayi ibanga, futhi ibhalwa lapho esikhundleni salokho.

## Izimbobo ezidingekayo

Chofoza {{ui:Izimbobo ezidingekayo}}. Lapho umugqa omaphakathi wentambo udlula udonga noma isilabhu, i-BimCraft ibhala imbobo edingekayo: ejikelezile ipayipi noma umsele ojikelezile, ngaphandle kwalokho iyisikwele, ilingane nosayizi wangaphandle wentambo kanye nokugoqwa kwayo okuphindwe kabili kanye negebe eliphindwe kabili, ibekwe maphakathi lapho intambo idlula khona. Chofoza umugqa ukubona intambo nodonga noma isilabhu.

- {{ui:Izimbobo njenge-CSV}}: umugqa owodwa ngembobo ngayinye onentambo, usayizi wayo nokugoqwa kwayo, usayizi wembobo, into edlula kuyo ne-GlobalId yayo, izinga, izinga-X, Y no-Z zamaphakathi, nokuthi intambo idlula kangakanani.
- {{ui:Izimbobo njenge-BCF izindaba}}: indaba eyodwa ngembobo ngayinye, nekhamera ebheka kuyo nentambo nento ekhethiwe, ye-programu yonjiniyela wesakhiwo.

:::note
Akukho okusikwayo: izimbobo luhlu lwabantu abazilinganisela. Igebe ngelakho ukulithayipha; akukho khodi etshela ukuthi imbobo kufanele ibe kanjani inkulu. Amabhimu namakholomu ngukungqubuzana, hhayi izimbobo: ukuthi ipayipi lingadlula kwelinye yisinqumo somenjiniyela wesakhiwo.
:::
