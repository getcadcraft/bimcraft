## Okuzokwenza

Dizayina elinye isakhi sesakhiwo njengengxenye: lithumele ku-PartCraft, ulishintshe khona (sika, engeza, bhoboza, lulame ubukhulu), bese ubuyisela isimo esisha emodelini.

## Izinyathelo

1. Khetha isakhi esisodwa emodelini: isakhi esijwayelekile, isisetshenziswa, ikholomu, ibhimu, irelingi noma impahla.
2. Khetha {{ui:Izixhumanisi zengxenye}} kwithuluzi bese {{ui:Thumela ku-PartCraft}}. I-PartCraft ivula ithebhu entsha nesakhi njengengxenye. Izakhi ezingaphakathi kwe-3 m zidwetshwe zingwevu eduze kwaso ukuze uzidizayinele ngapha. Azikwazi ukushintshwa lapho.
3. Shintsha ingxenye ku-PartCraft. Ku-PartCraft khetha {{ui:Ifayela}} › {{ui:Thumela}} › {{ui:Londoloza ubuyele ku-BimCraft}}. Ungalondoloza kabuyela njengoba ufuna.
4. Emuva ku-BimCraft inkinobho ye-{{ui:Izixhumanisi zengxenye}} ibonisa inani, nekhadi libhala isakhi njengo-{{ui:Kushintshwe ku-PartCraft}} nokushintsha kobukhulu nevolumu ngamazwi.
5. Khetha {{ui:Bonisa}} ukuze uyibheke, bese {{ui:Amukela}} ukuze isakhi sithole isimo esisha, noma {{ui:Gcina okwami}} ukuze sishiye njengoba sasinjalo.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Ukwamukela kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Isakhi sigcina indawo yaso, i-GlobalId, uhlobo, izinto nezici. Kushintshwa umzimba waso kuphela.
- Isakhi sithola isethi yezici {{ui:CadCraft_PartLink}} enesixhumanisi, isimo sayo nenguqulo.
- {{ui:Thumela futhi}} ivula lesi sakhi ku-PartCraft futhi, ngalesi sixhumanisi.

## Okufanele wazi

- Izindonga, amafloro, uphahla, iminyango, amawindi, izitebhisi, amaramp nezikhala kwakhiwa amathuluzi wesakhiwo kusuka emugqeni nasezindaweni ezizungezile futhi kwakhiwe kabusha ngemuva kwawo wonke ushintsho. Umzimba ovela kwenye i-app ubhalwa phezulu, ngakho lezi zinqatshwa nesizathu.
- Isimo sibuya njenge-mesh (i-IFC tessellation), hhayi njengezici zengxenye. Umlando wengxenye uhlala ku-PartCraft.
- Uma isakhi siguquke emodelini emva kokuthi usithumele, ikhadi lisho lokho ngaphambi kokuthi wamukele: ukwamukela kushintsha lezo zinguquko.
- Akukho okulayishwa. Lezi zinhlelo zimbili ziyimikhasi yesayithi eyodwa futhi zidlulisa isakhi phakathi kwazo kulesi sisiphequluli. Zombili kumele zivulwe esiphequluleni esifanayo.
- Isakhi kumele sibe yisimo esiqinile ku-PartCraft. Isakhi esinezikhala ezinkulu ezivulekile siyenqatshwa khona nenombolo yemiphetho evulekile.
- Ukuze wamukele, yenza imodeli ebamba lesi sakhi ibe ngeyamanje.
