Vula i-IFC, vula ibhulu lamaphoyinti ngaseceleni (bona [Amabhulu wamaphoyinti](topic:point-clouds)) bese uyibeka emodelini. Isigaba {{ui:Qhathanisa ukuskena nemodeli}} sivela ngakwesokudla lapho ibhulu livulekile.

## Okwenzayo

Chofoza {{ui:Qhathanisa}}. Isakhi ngasinye semodeli sihlolwa ngobuso baso, ngoba yilokho umskeni akubonayo. Izinhlangothi eziyisithupha zebhokisi lisikiwe zibe amaseli angaba ngu-20 cm; iseli libalwa uma iphoyinti lokuskena lisesikhaleni sakho se-{{ui:Ukubekezelela}} (50 mm ekuqaleni) kusuka ebusweni. Ubuso obukhulu obuhle obubalwayo, ngakho udonga oluskenwe ngohlangothi olulodwa alujezisi ngenxa yolunye uhlangothi lwalo.

- **Yakhiwe**: okungenani ama-40 % ebusweni ayabonakala.
- **Ingxenye**: 10 % kuya kuma-40 %.
- **Ilahlekile**: ngaphansi kwalokho, kodwa ukuskena kuyifinyelela isakhi (amaphoyinti angaphakathi kwe-1.5 m kuso).
- **Akufinyelelwe**: ukuskena akuyizanga lapho, ngakho akukho okushiwo ngayo.

Kwezakhiwe nezingxenye zakhiwe, ukuskena kunika nesilinganiso samaphoyinti okuskena kusuka ebusweni bemodeli, futhi kushilo ukuthi isakhi sisendaweni yaso (ngaphakathi kokubekezelela), eduze (ngaphakathi kwezikhathi ezintathu) noma kude (ngaphezulu). Ibona kuphela ukungafani kuze kube yizikhathi ezinhlanu zokubekezelela; isakhi esakhiwe kude nendawo yaso ngaphezu kwalokho sifunda njengesilahlekile.

Isifinyezo sinika iphesenti elakhiwe phakathi kwezakhi ezifinyelelwe ukuskena, ngenani nangevolumu. Izakhi eziyingxenye ziyabalwa ngokwahlukana futhi azengezwa kwezakhiwe.

## Ngokumelene nohlelo lwe-4D

Lapho uhlelo lwe-4D lulayishiwe (bona [Uhlelo lwe-4D](topic:4d-schedule)), umphumela uphinde usebenzise usuku osulisethile ku-slider ye-4D: izakhi ezifanele ngalolo suku ezingabonakali njengezakhiwe yi-scan ziyi-**ezemuva kohlelo**; izakhi ezingakafiki ezakhiwe ziyi-**ezisemberi**. Izakhi ezemuva kohlelo zishiwo ku-{{ui:Izinkinga}}, futhi ithebula lingahlungwa lube lezo kuphela. Ngaphandle kohlelo akukho okushiwo ngokuthi ziemuva noma zisemberi.

## Ithebula nemodeli

Chofoza umugqa ukuze ukhethe into. {{ui:Dweba imodeli ngemibala}} ipenda izakhi zibe luhlaza (zakhiwe), zibe mthubi (ingxenye), zibe bomvu (ziyalahlekile) noma zibe igrey (azifinyelelwe); {{ui:Imibala yayo}} iyazibuyisela. {{ui:Londoloza i-CSV}} ibhala ithebula. {{ui:Maka izakhi ezifanayo njengezihlolwe endaweni}} imaka zonke izakhi ezibonwe njengezakhiwe futhi zisendaweni yokubekezelela njengezihlolwe endaweni (LOD 500, indlela scan) ngesinyathelo esisodwa, osungasihlehlisa. Bona [Maka izakhi njengezihloliwe noma zihlolwe endaweni](topic:verification).

:::warning
Lokhu kuqhathanisa ibhokisi lezakhi ngayinye namaphoyinti okuskena ngaphakathi kokubekezelela. **Akuyona invo, akukhishwa kwezinto nezisekelo zokukhokha.** Into efihliwe ngemuva kwento eskenwe (udonga ngemuva kwesidwaba, isilabhu ngaphansi kokuqeda kwendlela) yabelana ngobuso bayo futhi ingafundeka njengakhiwe; isakhi esigobile noma esithambekile sinebhokisi elikhudlwana kunomzimba waso futhi singafundeka njengengxenye noma njengesilahlekile. Bheka uhlu ngaphambi kokuthatha isinyathelo. Amazwana emvume nezimpawu zenqubekelaphambili azikho lapha: zihlala ezikholomini ozigcina kuzo.
:::

Umsizi angasebenzisa ukuqhathanisa okufanayo ngethuluzi {{ui:Qhathanisa ukuskena nemodeli}}; ngeke ishintshe lutho emodelini.
