## Okuzokwenza

Guqula imodeli ibe uhlu lwezinto nesilinganiso ku-TakeoffCraft. I-BimCraft idweba ipulani lalo lonke izinga ngesikali esijwayelekile, imaka kuwo wonke amanani, bese ifaka ukubala amanani ngaphakathi kwe-PDF. I-TakeoffCraft ilinganisa izimaki ngokwayo, ngakho inombolo ngayinye ingahlolwa ephepheni.

## Thumela

1. Chofoza {{ui:Ukubala amanani}} kubha yemodeli (ngasese kwe-{{ui:Amashidi}}).
2. I-TakeoffCraft ivula ithebhu entsha ngamapulani. Lapho ibuza, khetha ukuvula ukubala amanani iyitholile ku-PDF.
3. Engeza amanani akho etafuleni lamanani le-TakeoffCraft; izinhlanganisela sezinikeza kakade izimpahla.

:::note
Uma i-TakeoffCraft ingatholakali lapha, inkinobho ilanda i-PDF esikhundleni salokho. Yivule ku-TakeoffCraft kamuva: izothola ukubala amanani ngaphakathi kwayo.
:::

## Okulinganiswayo

- **Izindonga** ngohlobo nobude: imigqa yazo emaphakathi njengezingxenye, nobude bodonga kulesi sinto, ukuze i-TakeoffCraft inikeze ubude kanye nendawo yodonga. Yonke ingxenye iphethe iminyango namawindi alo, ngakho i-TakeoffCraft iwasusa endaweni yodonga kanye, ngomthetho ofanayo nohlu lwe-BimCraft: bobabili bavumelana ngokuphelele, ngokususiwe nangokusele. Idatha yokubala amanani yohlobo lodonga (bona [Izinhlanganisela zezindonga](topic:wall-assemblies)) ifika njengomugqa owodwa onentengo kulesi sinto sohlobo. Ngaphansi kwendlela yokulinganisa engeyona eyejwayelekile (bona [Khetha indlela yokulinganisa](topic:plan-schedules-and-pdf#choose-a-measurement-method)), ukubala amanani kuthwala indlela, yonke ingxenye ithwala lokho okususwa yizixhumanisi ebusweni bodonga, futhi i-TakeoffCraft ilinganisa ngendlela efanayo iyibize ngegama. Uhlobo lodonga lapho iyunithi inobude nobude, lunikeza izingxenye ezimbili ezinamanani: izinyunithi zokuyala kanye nevolumu ye-mortar, kubhidwa uma izinga lohlobo liyisilinganiso ngeyunithi, ngamayunithi angu-1000 noma ngama-m³ e-mortar. Inani elifika ngokuphelele ku-nombolo ephelele lingaba yiyunithi eyodwa ngaphezulu ku-TakeoffCraft, ngoba ukuthunyelwa kuzungeza izikhundla ephepheni.
- **Uphahla**: indiza ngayinye yophahla njengendawo enokuphakama kwayo (i-TakeoffCraft inikeza indawo ephethwe), imiphezulu, izinhlangothi nezigodi (izinhlangothi nezigodi ngokuphakama kwazo), imiphetho yokuphuma nemiphetho esemaceleni.
- **Izisekelo**: izinsika zesisekelo nezindonga zesisekelo njengemigqa ezungeze iziphakathi zazo, namaslabhu njengendawo enobukhulu bawo, ukuze ukhonkolo uphume ngevolumu.
- **Iminyango namawindi** ibalwa ngohlobo, nokuqedwa kwewindi.
- **Amakhabethi asesisekelweni** ngobude bezinhlangothi zawo ezingaphambili.
- **Amadivayisi kagesi** abalwa ngohlobo.
- **Indawo yephansi** yalo lonke izinga, isuka emakamelweni alo.
- **Uhlu lokusika izinkuni** kusuka ekwakhiweni, lubalwe ephepheni lalo (ichashazi elilodwa ngalinye).

Lapho iphakheji lomsebenzi linenhlanganisela efanayo — izindonga zangaphandle nangaphakathi, amaslabhu, izisekelo, izinsika zesisekelo, amashingles, ukuphela kophahla nezigodi, imiphetho yokuphuma, iminyango yangaphakathi, ukuqedwa kwamawindi, amakhabethi asesisekelweni, amasokhethi, iziswitshi, izibani, amabhokisi okuxhuma nephaneli — into ifika ngezimpahla zayo nezifomula zayo. Amaphakheji awafiki ngezintengo futhi awakahlolwa umbali wezindleko.

:::tip
Amapulani enziwa kusuka emodelini njalo lapho uchofoza, ngakho ngemva kokushintsha, chofoza kuphinde {{ui:Ukubala amanani}}.
:::
