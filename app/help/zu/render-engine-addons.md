I-add-on (Izilungiselelo › Okwengeziwe) ingangeza injini kuhlu lwe-Injini engxoxweni {{ui:Renda}}. Umbhalo ogcwele wababhali be-add-on useceleni 16 wesipesifikheshini se-add-on; ngokufushane:

- I-add-on ibhalisa injini enesamba esiqala ngesayo, igama, izilungiselelo ezingaphoqelekile, **noma** uhlelo lokuqhuba **noma** umsebenzi we-JavaScript odweba isithombe.
- Injini eqhuba uhlelo idinga imvume **`engine.run`**, ebonakala ngaphambi kokufaka nakuZilungiselelo. Isebenza kuStudio kuphela; kuwebhu ibonakala njengefayela okufanele silondolozwe.
- I-add-on iqamba uhlelo (igama lefayela elilula kuphela: hhayi i-shell), iskripthi **esingaphakathi kwe-add-on esayiniwe**, nezimpikiswano ezakhiwe ngokubamba izindawo nemigqa elula. I-Studio iyathola uhlelo noma icele ukuthi uyibhekisele kanye.
- Okokuqala lapho i-add-on iqhuba uhlelo, **i-Studio ikubuza engxoxweni yayo**: yiyiphi i-add-on, yiluphi uhlelo, nokuthi iskripthi singenza noma yini uhlelo olungayenza. Yivumele kuphela i-add-on oyithembayo. Impendulo yakho iyakhunjulwa ngale add-on nalolu hlelo.
- Ipakethe (isimo njenge-USD nefayela lezilungiselelo, kanye nefayela le-glTF uma i-add-on iyicela) neskripthi le-add-on kungena kufolda yesikhashana emisha; uhlelo luqhuba lapho, isithombe sibuya sibhalwe ngegama leinjini ye-add-on.

:::note
Ama-add-on angangeza injini zokurenda kuphela kusukela ku-API ye-add-on 1.4. Isibonelo esisebenzayo sesikhali somyalo esivulekile sitholakala endaweni yaso yokugcina; asikaqhutshwa kuleyo renda.
:::
