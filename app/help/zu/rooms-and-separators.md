## Okuzokwenziwa

Qamba amagumbi odonga abawenza, bese uhlukanisa igumbi elivulekile libe ngamabili ngaphandle kodonga.

## Lapho kukhona amathuluzi

- Izakhiwo ze-Ribbon: {{ui:Architecture}} › **Igumbi nendawo** › {{ui:Room}} ne-{{ui:Room Separator}}.
- Isakhiwo sakudala: ibha ye-Draw › imenyu ye-{{ui:Rooms}}.
- Noma yisiphi isitayela semenyu: imiyalo ibizwa ngokuthi Room ne-Room Separator; ibha yemiyalo iyayithola.

## Izinyathelo

1. Dweba izindonga. Yonke ilufu elivaliwe lezindonga seligumbi ngokwalo: i-BimCraft iyenza, iyibalele futhi inikeze indawo yayo ngenkathi udweba.
2. Khetha {{ui:Room}}. Hambisa phezu kwe-plan: igumbi elingaphansi kwesikhombi liyakhanya ngendawo, umkhawulo nevolumu yalo.
3. Ukuze uqambe amagumbi ngokushesha, thayipha {{ui:Name}} ne-{{ui:Number}} ohlwini kuqala, bese uchofoza egumbini. Inombolo ikhuphuka ngu-1 egumbini elilandelayo (101, 102; A-09, A-10).
4. Noma chofoza nje igumbi: liyakhethwa, futhi **Properties** inegama, inombolo, indawo, ubude bomkhawulo, ivolumu nethegi laleligumbi.
5. Ukuze uhlukanise igumbi elivulekile, khetha {{ui:Room Separator}} bese uchofoza amaphoyinti amabili: odongeni kuya kodonga, noma kodonga kuya kwesinye isihlukanisi. Qhubeka uchofoza ukwenza uchungechunge; i-Enter, i-Esc noma ukuchofoza kabili kuyiqeda.

## Okwenziwa yisihlukanisi

- Simisa amagumbi njengodonga olungenabukhulu. Izindawo zamagumbi amabili zifinyelela ngokuphelele egumbini ebeliwukuba yilo.
- Igumbi elibamba iphakathi lendala ligcina igama nenombolo yalo. Elinye lisha.
- Isihlukanisi esima endaweni evulekile, singathintani nakuthile ngesinye sesiphetho, asihlukanisi lutho. Sinwebe futhi kuzokwenza.
- Ingxenye yesihlukanisi esiphakathi kodonga iyanganakwa, ngakho ungabophela emigqeni yasemaphakathini wezindonga.
- Khetha isihlukanisi bese ucindezela Delete ukusisusa: amagumbi amabili abuyela kube munye. I-Hlehlisa iyasibuyisela.

## Uma kungekho gumbi lapho uchofoza

Umugqa wesimo uyasitshela: izindonga azivali lapho. Vala igebe, noma dweba isihlukanisi kulo. Akukho lutho olwenqatshwa; kungolje nje akukho gumbi elivaliwe.

## Ku-IFC

Igumbi liyi-IfcSpace nenombolo yalo (Name), igama lalo (LongName) kanye ne-Qto_SpaceBaseQuantities: NetFloorArea, NetPerimeter, Height, NetVolume. Ivolumu iyindawo ephindaphindwe ngokuphakama kuya kulelo zinga elingenhla.

Isihlukanisi segumbi liyi-IfcVirtualElement, uhlobo lwe-IFC lomkhawulo ongakakhiwe. Linomugqa futhi alinawo umzimba, ngakho olunye uhlelo alufundi odonga, futhi alubali lutho ngakho.

## Akukakwakhiwa okwamanje

- Izihlukanisi ezigoqayo.
- Igumbi elilandela uphahla oluthambekile: ivolumu ihlala iyindawo ephindaphindwe ngokuphakama kwezinga.
- Ukuxhumana komkhawulo wesikhala (IfcRelSpaceBoundary) akubhalwa, kwezindonga noma zezihlukanisi.
