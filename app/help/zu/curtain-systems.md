## Okuzokwenza

Yenza udonga lwengilazi lwamafasiteli phakathi kwemiqamelo, shintsha lapho imigqa yegridi yalo ikhona, futhi ukhethe imigqa eyothwala umqamelo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula **Dweba** bese uvula imenyu ye-**Build** (ku-ribbon: ithebhu ye-Architecture).
2. Khetha **Curtain System**. Emgqeni walo setha phezulu (izinga elilandelayo, noma ukuphakama), umugqa njalo ngobude obuthile kuwo, umugqa njalo ngokuphakama okuthile (shiya ukungenalutho uma kungekho), ukuthi uzoba nemiqamelo yini, nephrofayili yomqamelo.
3. Cofa lapho uqala khona nalapho uphela khona, njengoba udweba udonga. I-Shift igcina kulingene.
4. Ukuze ushintshe igridi, khetha **Curtain Grid** bese ucofa uhlelo:
   - **Engeza umugqa**: umugqa oma-ngqo uyavela lapho ucofa khona. Ngomugqa wezinga, bhala ukuphakama kwawo ku-**At** kuqala.
   - **Hambisa umugqa**: cofa umugqa, bese ucofa lapho uyohamba khona.
   - **Susa umugqa**: cofa umugqa.
   - **Setha ngebanga**: bhala ibanga ku-**Every**, bese ucofa uhlelo.
5. Ukuze ushintshe imiqamelo, khetha **Mullion**, khetha okusebenza kuwo (umugqa oma-ngqo ocofa kuwo, umugqa wezinga ngokuphakama, umphetho, noma yonke imigqa), khetha **Place mullions** noma **Remove mullions** neprofayili, bese ucofa uhlelo.
6. Khetha uhlelo ukuze ubone konke ku-**Properties**: wonke umugqa wegridi ngendawo yawo, uphawu lomqamelo wawo nenkinobho yokuwususa; ibanga; umphetho; iprofayili; ubukhulu bepaneli.

## Ngobuso

**By Face** yenza uhlelo lwekhethini ebusweni obusicaba obucofa kuwo ku-3D view. Umugqa wayo unezilungiselelo zegridi nemiqamelo ezifanayo.

- Lusebenza kunoma yiluphi ubuso obusicaba bunoma yiliphi element elibonwa yi-3D view: ubuningi, udonga, ifloor, uphahla, insika, into ebekiwe. Ubuso bungaba buma-ngqo, bethambekile noma buyisicaba.
- Uhlelo luthatha umbhalo wobuso. I-gable noma enye imumo engavamile iyasika amapaneli nemiqamelo ukuze ilingane.
- Lungenza **udonga** esikhundleni, ngaphansi kwesisekelo sobuso obuma-ngqo, siphakame njengobuso. Udonga lapha lusoze lume ngqo, ngakho ubuso obuthambekile noma obuyisicaba abunakukwazi.
- Alwenzi uphahla. Uphahla ku-BimCraft lubalwa kusuka ocingweni lwezinga nasekuthambekeni, hhayi kubuso bodwa.
- Ubuso obugoqekile kuyizindawo eziningi ezincane ezisicaba: ukucofa kukodwa kuthatha olunye lwazo.
- Ubuso olunezimbobo: uhlelo luhlanganisa umbhalo wangaphandle kwebuso.

## Ukukalwa

- **Gross area** ingumbhalo wohlelo.
- **Glass area** ingamapaneli njengoba enziwe: ipaneli ngalinye lilahlekelwa uhhafu wobubanzi bomqamelo ohlangothini olunomqamelo wegridi, nobubanzi bonke kumqamelo womphetho.
- Umqamelo oma-ngqo uhamba kusuka komunye umphetho kuya komunye. Umqamelo wezinga uyama kuwo wonke umqamelo oma-ngqo nasemphethweni. Imiqamelo yomphetho ilele ngaphakathi kombhalo; lapho ezimbili zihlangana, eyethe kakhulu iyadlula. Amahlanganiso anqunywa acaleke, awagoqwa.
- **Schedules** inohlu lwe-**Curtain systems** nalezi zinombolo uma imodeli inalo.

## Ku-IFC

I-IfcCurtainWall esitezini sayo, eyakhiwe yi-IfcPlate (ipaneli yekhethini) ngalinye ngepaneli ngalinye, ne-IfcMember (umqamelo) ngayinye ngocezu lomqamelo ngalunye, ngalunye nesimo, impahla kanye nezinani zesisekelo zalo.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Noma yisiphi usayizi senziwa njengoba ucela. Umbhalo ongenayo indawo kuphela uyenqatshwa, ngoba akukho okungakwenziwa.
- Uhlelo odwetshwe njengodonga ugcina ukuphakama okwadwetshwa ngakho. Uma uhambisa izinga kamuva, setha ukuphakama kwalo futhi ku-**Properties**.
- Ithuluzi le-Move alikahambisi uhlelo lwekhethini. Lisuse bese uyadweba futhi, noma ushintshe igridi yalo endaweni.
- Undo ibuyisela ngayinye inyathelo: uhlelo lonke, ushintsho olulodwa legridi, noma ushintsho olulodwa lomqamelo.
