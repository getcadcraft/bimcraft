{{ui:Penda}} ku-toolbar ivula umtapo wempahla yokwakha ngaphezu kwe-3D view: ibhulokhi nezitoko, ukugoqa, izinhlanga zophahla, izinkuni, itshe, ithayela, ukhonkolo, umhlabathi, ingilazi nensimbi.

## Penda izakhi

1. Chofoza {{ui:Penda}}.
2. Chofoza impahla yokwakha.
3. Chofoza izakhi ku-3D view. Ukuchofoza ngakunye kupenda leyo nto ochofoze kuyo.

Ukuze upende izinto eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa, zikhethe kuqala (chofoza, {{key:Ctrl}}+chofoza, noma [sesha](topic:find)), bese uchofoza impahla yokwakha: yonke izakhi ezikhethiwe ithola yona ngesinyathelo esisodwa.

Into ibonisa umbala wempahla yokwakha ku-3D view ngokushesha. Ukuthungwa kwayo kuvela ku-[isithombe esinemibala](topic:render). {{key:Esc}} noma {{ui:Vala}} kuyeka ukupenda.

## Penda uhlangothi olulodwa

Khetha {{ui:Uhlangothi oluchofozwe kuphela}}, bese uchofoza uhlangothi ofuna: ingaphandle lodonga ngokugoqa kanye nangaphakathi ngesinye isiqedo, phezulu kwephansi ngezinkuni nangaphansi kulushiywe njengoba kunjalo. Yonke enye ingxenye yento igcina impahla yayo.

Uhlangothi litholakala ngezinqondiso zento ngokwayo, ngakho lihlala luhlangothi olufanayo uma uhambisa noma ujikeleza into.

Ukupenda amaphansi igumbi ngegumbi, nika igumbi ngalinye iphansi lalo ({{ui:Dweba}} › {{ui:Iphansi}} › **Lolu gumbi**, bheka [Amagumbi, amaphansi, amasilingi namaphahla](topic:floors-and-roofs)), bese upenda phezulu kwephansi ngalinye.

## Susa impahla yokwakha

Chofoza iswishi enemigqa ({{ui:Akukho mpahla yokwakha}}), bese uchofoza into. Yonke impahla yayo, kubandakanya nezinhlangothi, iyasuswa.

## Ku-IFC

Ukupenda kushintsha i-IFC (isinyathelo esisodwa ngasinye, {{key:Ctrl+Z}} iyakuhlehlisa) futhi kugcina ku-IFC elula:

- ngayinye into epenjiwe ithola isethi yezakhiwo ethi `CadCraft_Render` ebiza impahla yayo, nepropathi eyodwa ngohlangothi ngalunye olupenjiwe;
- umzimba wayo uthola isitayela sendawo ngombala wempahla yokwakha, ukuze ezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM zibonise umbala. Ku-IFC4 isitayela siphinde sibize nokuthungwa.

Uma i-BimCraft iyibumba kabusha udonga owulipentile (njengalapho uhambisa ukuphela kwawo), udonga ugcina impahla yalo nombala walo, ku-BimCraft, ezithombeni nasezinhlelweni zabanye.

:::note
Ukuthungwa kudwetshwa yi-BimCraft ngokwayo: kusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi futhi kukhululekile ukusetshenziswa noma yini (CC0). Ukuthungwa kwezithombe zakho akukakwakhiwa.
:::

## Ukuthungwa ku-3D view

Udonga webhulokhi ophenjiwe ubonisa amabhulokhi alo, iphansi lezinkuni libonisa amapuleti alo, ngosayizi wangempela, uma nje sekukhona upende. Khipha ithikhi ku-**Bonisa ukuthungwa ku-3D view** kuphalethi lokupenda ukuze ubone imibala eqinile. Imodeli enkulu kakhulu iyayivala ngokwayo futhi iyasho lokho. Umbala ngepropathi uhlala ubonisa imibala yawo. Ukuthungwa kudwetshwa yiprogramu, ngakho akukho okulandiwayo, futhi izithombe ezinemibala kanye nefayela le-glTF zinakho kunoma iyiphi indlela.
