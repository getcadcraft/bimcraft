{{ui:Renda}} inento ethi **Injini** ngaphezulu kwe-{{ui:Indlela}}. Izinjini zethu ezimbili zikhona njalo futhi zisebenza noma yikuphi; ama-add-on angangeza ezinye, futhi ku-CadCraft Studio uhlu luphinde lube nezinhlelo ezithatha isimo.

| Injini | Yenzani | Lapho |
|---|---|---|
| Eyakhelwe: Ngokushesha | Isithombe esisheshayo: ukukhanya kwemini, izithunzi ezithambile, izimo zengilazi nensimbi, emasekhondeni | Iwebhu ne-Studio |
| Eyakhelwe: Isilandeli sendlela | Ukukhanya okugxumayo phakathi kwezindawo, ekhadini lezithombe noma kuprosesa, uhlu lokurenda kanye namanye amakhompyutha kunethiwekhi yakho | Iwebhu ne-Studio |
| Injini ye-add-on | Iyadweba futhi ibuyisele isithombe: bona [Dweba isithombe ngolunye uhlelo](topic:render-other-program) | Iwebhu (injini ebhalwe nge-JavaScript) noma i-Studio (injini eqhuba uhlelo) |

| Vula ku… | Ilondoloza isimo bese ilivula kuhlelo; akukho okubuyela: bona [Vula isimo kolunye uhlelo](topic:render-open-in) | Iwebhu ilondoloza ifayela |

## Ukuthi ukukhetha kuziphatha kanjani

- Ukukhetha i-{{ui:Ngokushesha}} noma isilandeli sendlela kushintsha {{ui:Indlela}} nakho, nangokuphambene. Akukho okunye okushintsha engxoxweni kwabantu abangakaze basebenzise enye injini.
- Injini engatholakali ihlala ohlwini nesizathu (“akutholakalanga”, “idinga inguqulo entsha”); awukwazi ukuyikhetha. Kuwebhu, injini esebenza kuStudio kuphela ibonisa “ilondoloza ifayela”: {{ui:Renda}} ilondoloza isimo nezilungiselelo zaso kufayela oliyisa kuleyo nhlelo.
- Ukukhetha kugcinwa kanye nezilungiselelo zokurenda zefayela, futhi injini oyikhethe ekugcineni iyinjini yokuqala yamafayela angenayo ukukhetha kwawo.
- Isithombe esivela kolunye uhlelo sibonisa ukuthi yiluphi uhlelo olwenze lona nenguqulo yalo, engxoxweni nasohlwini lokurenda. Izithembiso zekhasi le-[Renda](topic:render) zihlanganisa **izinjini zethu** kuphela: olunye uhlelo luyakhanyisa, luthinte futhi lube ngamasampula ngendlela yalo, ngakho izithombe ezimbili zesimo esifanayo zifana ngekhamera, ilanga, izibani nokuvezwa, kodwa hhayi ngepikseli.

## Okwenziwa yisimo yiyo yonke injini

Isimo siya kolunye uhlelo njenge-**ipakethe**: imodeli njengefayela le-USD (ifayela elifanayo [ouligcina ngokuthi Londoloza njenge-USD](topic:usd)), kanye nefayela lezilungiselelo elibamba okungathenjwa ngakho umlethi we-USD: amanani ezinto zokwakha ngokunembile, izibani ezivulekile ngamandla nemaphrofayili wokukhanya, ilanga, isibhakabhaka, ukuvezwa nekhamera njengoba ingxoxo ingayidweba. Akukho lapho okulayishwa.

## Uhlu lokurenda

Isithombe esivela kolunye uhlelo singalinda kuhlu lokurenda njengezethu ([bona uhlu lokurenda](topic:render#the-render-queue)); umsebenzi wawo ukhumbula ukuthi iyiphi injini eyiyo.
