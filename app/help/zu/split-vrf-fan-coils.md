## Okuzokwenza

Faka iyunithi zohlelo lwe-split, lwe-multi-split noma lwe-VRF kanye ne-fan coil zalo emodeli, bese uzixhumanisa ngezethi yemigqa: umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi ngekhopha le-ACR, ahlanganiswe nezimbobo zeyunithi. Iyunithi zisebenza njengazo zonke ezinye izinto ze-[MEP equipment with ports](topic:mep-equipment): ibha efanayo, {{ui:Izici}}, izimbobo kanye nezixhumanisi ([connections](topic:mep-connections)).

:::note
Lo ungumsizi wokudweba nokuhlanganisa. Iyunithi ziyizimo ezijwayelekile ezilingana ngamanani owabhalayo, hhayi imikhiqizo yomkhiqizi othile. Usayizi wamapayipi azo uvela etafuleni lezinxaneli **ezijwayelekile zesibonelo** zezinsiza ze-R410A ne-R32, hhayi kumkhiqizi othile: incwadi yokufaka yomkhiqizi weyunithi yona inquma usayizi wangempela, futhi imigqa ende noma umehluko omkhulu wobude uyawushintsha. Shintsha itafula, noma bhala usayizi, ukuze ufanane neyunithi oyisebenzisayo.
:::

## Beka iyunithi

1. Vula {{ui:Dweba}}, vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinsiza}}.
2. Khetha uhlobo bese usetha amanani alo:

| Uhlobo | Ukusetha | Izixhumi zayo |
|---|---|---|
| Iyunithi yangaphakathi ye-split, odongeni | amandla, umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi, ubude, ubujule, ukuphakama, usayizi wokuphuphuthekisa | isiphephisi soketshezi nesegesi, ukukhipha ukuphuphuthekisa (ngakwesokudla, eduze kodonga) |
| Iyunithi yangaphakathi ye-split, ye-cassette | amandla, umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi, ipanele, umzimba, ukuphakama, usayizi wokuphuphuthekisa | isiphephisi soketshezi nesegesi, ukukhipha ukuphuphuthekisa (ngakolunye uhlangothi, eduze kwaphezulu) |
| Iyunithi yangaphakathi ye-split, enombobo | amandla, umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi, ubude, ububanzi, ukuphakama, izimbobo zokunikeza nokubuyisa, usayizi wokuphuphuthekisa | umbobo wokunikeza ophumayo, umbobo wokubuyisa ongenayo, isiphephisi soketshezi nesegesi, ukukhipha ukuphuphuthekisa |
| Iyunithi yangaphandle ye-split | amandla, umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi, ububanzi, ubujule, ukuphakama | isiphephisi soketshezi nesegesi (ngakwesokudla) |
| Iyunithi yangaphandle ye-VRF | amandla, umugqa woketshezi nomugqa wegesi, ububanzi, ubujule, ukuphakama | isiphephisi soketshezi nesegesi (phambili, phansi) |
| Ukuxhumana kwe-refnet | umugqa woketshezi noma wegesi, umthwalo kwelinye ngalinye igatsha, ipayipi ekugcineni ngakunye | ukungena, igatsha A, igatsha B |
| Iyunithi ye-fan coil | ama-2-pipe noma ama-4-pipe, ubude, ububanzi, ukuphakama, izimbobo zokunikeza nokubuyisa, ipayipi nesistimu ye-coil, ipayipi lokushisisa, usayizi wokuphuphuthekisa | umbobo wokunikeza ophumayo, umbobo wokubuyisa ongenayo, i-coil ngenayo nephumayo, ukushisisa ngenayo nephumayo (ama-4-pipe), ukukhipha ukuphuphuthekisa |

3. Setha **Ku-**: ukuphakama kwesisekelo seyunithi ngaphezu kwezinga. Isisekelo seyunithi yodonga yisiphansi sayo (njalo ngo-2100 mm); eseyilingi isibhamu sayo ngaphakathi kophahla; eye-refnet ukuphakama komugqa wepayipi wayo.
4. Chofoza lapho iyoya khona ({{key:R}} iyijikisa ngama-90°).

**Amandla** ayizinga lokupholisa elibekiwe leyunithi ngo-kW, kanye no-Btu/h eduze kwawo. Uma **Umugqa woketshezi** nes **Umugqa wegesi** ushiywe ku-**Kusuka etafuleni**, ukuxhumana kweyunithi kuthatha usayizi wetafula kulawo mandla; khetha usayizi wetyubhu ukuze usebenzise weyunithi yakho. Ukungena kwe-refnet kulinganiswa ngamagatsha womabili ndawonye, nelingi ligatsha ngomthwalo waleligatsha.

Izixhumi zesiphephisi zisebenza zombili izindlela (ipompo lokushisa lisebenza nakweyiphi indlela). Ukukhipha ukuphuphuthekisa kungusayizi wepayipi njenganoma yiliphi idrenishi.

## Dweba isethi yemigqa

1. Khetha izinto ezimbili ({{key:Shift}}+chofoza eyesibili): iyunithi yangaphandle neyunithi yangaphakathi (i-single split), noma iyunithi yangaphandle ne-refnet, i-refnet neyunithi yangaphakathi, noma ama-refnet amabili (VRF).
2. Vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Isethi yemigqa}}.

BimCraft idweba imigqa yoketshezi negesi phakathi kwezimbobo zazo ezikhululekile, iphuma kwisimbobo ngasinye iye ebangeni elifanayo elingaphezulu, ngekhopha le-ACR (i-coil ethambile kuya ku-1-5/8 in, ityubhu ehlanganisiwe ngaphezulu kwalo), njengesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}. I-refnet ithatha umugqa wayo kuphela: ingena ivela ngakolwangaphandle, iphume ilunge egatsheni elibheka iziyunithi zangaphakathi. Umugqa ngamunye uthatha usayizi wokugcina lawo okuwusangane (iyunithi yangaphakathi, noma ukungena kwe-refnet); uma isimbobo sokugcina esinye sinosayizi ohlukile, i-{{ui:Hlola ukuxhumana}} iyawudweba: yilapho okudingeka khona i-reducer. Iziyunithi zangaphakathi ezimbili zidinga i-refnet phakathi kwazo.

Hambisa imigqa ngemuva njengalanoma yimuphi umugqa wepayipi; iziphetho zawo zihlala ezimbobeni. Imigqa phakathi kwezitezi idwetshwa nge-{{ui:Ipayipi}}: ipayipi eliqalwa esimbobeni sesiphephisi lithatha isistimu yalo, ikhopha le-ACR nosayizi walo.

## Kufayela le-IFC

Iyunithi zangaphakathi nezangaphandle zezinhlelo ze-split ne-VRF ziyi-IfcUnitaryEquipment ngohlobo i-SPLITSYSTEM, nohlobo lwento lusho ukuthi yiyiphi ("Split indoor unit, wall-mounted", "Split indoor unit, ceiling cassette", "Split indoor unit, ducted", "Split outdoor unit", "VRF outdoor unit"). I-fan coil ingu-IfcUnitaryEquipment ohlobo i-AIRHANDLER nohlobo lwento "Fan coil unit": i-IFC4 ayinahlobo lwe-fan coil, futhi ifeni, i-coil nefilthara yegumbi elilodwa yileyo i-AIRHANDLER echaza. Ukuxhumana kwe-refnet ngu-IfcPipeFitting ohlobo i-JUNCTION ("Refnet joint"). Amafayela e-IFC2X3 athola i-IfcEnergyConversionDevice ne-IfcFlowFitting ngohlobo olufanayo.

Izimbobo zesiphephisi nemigqa ziseziphephisini i-REFRIGERATION (enye yemigqa yoketshezi, enye yemigqa yegesi), nokuphuphuthekisa kusesistimini ye-DRAINAGE. Imigqa ye-ACR iqanjwa ngobubanzi bangaphandle. Itafula lesethi yemigqa liyisethi yezakhiwo i-CadCraft_HvacLineSets kuprojekthi.
