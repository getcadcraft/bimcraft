## Beka isithombe

Vula {{ui:Draw}}, uye ezingeni, bese ukhetha {{ui:Picture}} kumenyu {{ui:Build}}. Bese {{ui:Insert picture…}} bese ukhetha ifayela le-PNG, JPEG noma WebP. Lifundwa kusiphequluli sakho futhi aliyi kulayishwa. Livela ngaphansi kwepulani, lilingana nombono, futhi ithuluzi lithi awusasikalisi. Konke oyidwetshayo kuhlala phezulu kwalo.

## Setha isikali

Khetha {{ui:Scale}}, bese uchofoza amaphoyinti amabili esithombeni okwaziwayo ibanga langempela phakathi kwawo (imikhawulo yodonga ezindaweni ezisikeniwe epulanini, ubukhulu obubhalwe esikisela), bese uthayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Isithombe siyakhula noma sincipha sizungeze iphoyinti lokuqala owalichofoze. {{key:Esc}} iyakhansela.

## Namathisela indawo ekopishiwe

Ku-MarkupCraft, {{ui:Copy area}} ikopisha ingxenye yeshidi le-PDF njengesithombe. Ngemuva ku-BimCraft, lapho ipulani lezinga likhombisa, cindezela {{key:Ctrl+V}} (noma {{ui:Paste}} kumenyu {{ui:Edit}}): indawo ifika epulanini njengesithombe, ibekwe maphakathi lapho kukhomba khona isikhombi (noma maphakathi nombono). Uma ishidi linesikali sokudweba, ifika ngobukhulu bayo bangempela, ngakho udonga oluselwayo lubude njengodonga lwakho. Uma lingenalo, ifika ngosayizi wephepha futhi i-{{ui:Scale}} iyaqala, ukuze ukwazi ukuchofoza amaphoyinti amabili bese uthayipha ibanga lawo. Indawo ekopishwe iqala ngokuqina okwakhopishelwa ngakho. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa futhi igcinwa njenganoma yisiphi esinye isithombe.

{{ui:Paste}} uthatha okokugcina okopishile: indawo ekopishiwe noma into okopishe ku-BimCraft. {{key:Ctrl+V}} iphinda inamathisele noma yisiphi esinye isithombe se-PNG, JPEG noma WebP esikhumbulweni sakho, sibekwe njengaku-{{ui:Insert picture…}} futhi singasikalisiwe. Indawo ekopishiwe ikhunjulwa amahora ayisithupha.

## Hambisa, jikeleza, fiphaza, khiya

{{ui:Move}} uthatha iphoyinti lesisekelo esithombeni bese kuthi indawo okufanele iye kuyo. {{ui:Turn}} uthatha i-engeli ngamadigri, ngokuzungeza phakathi kwesithombe. {{ui:Opacity}} kuyisona esifiphaza ukuze imigqa yakho ibonakale. {{ui:Lock}} kuyigcina isingahambi ngenkathi udweba; isithombe esikhiyiwe usakwazi ukushintsha ukuqina kwaso nokusifihla. Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo sokuhlehlisa.

## Izithombe eziningi, amazinga amaningi

Isithombe siyeyesinqubo esabekwe kuso, futhi sibonakala epulanini yalelo zinga nakwepulani yesiling. Chofoza isithombe ukuze usisebenze, noma ukhethe kuhlu. {{ui:Hide}} siyasivala ngaphandle kokusisusa; {{ui:Remove}} (noma {{key:Delete}}) siyasikhipha emodelini.

## Izithombe ku-3D view

Yonke isithombe futhi simi ku-3D view njengendiza eliyisicaba: isithombe sepulani sihlezi phezu kwezinga layo, isithombe sendiza ephakeme sime phezu komugqa wayo wesigaba. Sidwetshwa phezu kwemodeli ngaphandle kokuyifihla, futhi imodeli ifihla ingxenye yesithombe ebehlala ngemuva kwayo. Izithombe ezigqumekile ziyahlelwa ngobubanzi. Ukuqina, Fihla, Khiya noSusa kusebenza ngendlela efanayo, futhi {{ui:Show in 3D}} ivala zonke iziphandla ku-3D view kuphela. Izinga elilodwa linamanye ama-8 wezithombe kuphela, ukuze imibono isheshe.

## Isithombe endizeni eme

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Place on}} khetha {{ui:A vertical plane (section line)}}, bese {{ui:Insert picture…}}. Chofoza amaphethelo amabili womugqa epulanini (ubuso bodonga bunamathela), noma khetha umugqa wesigaba oqanjiwe wezinga ku-{{ui:Over a section line}} ngaphandle kokuchofoza. Isithombe sime phezu kwezinga phezu komugqa, sibe bubanzi obufanayo nomugqa. Setha i-{{ui:Width}} yaso, indawo yaso {{ui:Along the line}} nokuphakama kwaso {{ui:Height above level}} ngokuthayipha; {{ui:Turn}} siyasijikeleza endizeni yaso. Isithombe esime asidwetshwa ngaphansi kwepulani; ipulani likhombisa umugqa waso lapho usisebenza. I-BimCraft ayinayo iwindi lesigaba se-2D elisebenzisanayo: izigaba nezibonakalo zidwetshwa emashidini, futhi izithombe azishicilelwa kulezo zibonakalo zamashidi okwamanje.

## Ukushicilela

{{ui:Print view}} epulanini ibhala izithombe zezinga kwi-PDF ngaphansi komdwebo, ngesikali, ngokuphendulwa nokuqina kwazo, kuvinjelwe ngaphakathi kweshidi; isithombe esifihliwe sihlala ngaphandle. I-JPEG ingena njengoba ikhona; ezinye izithombe zehliswa zibe ngu-200 dpi kuqala ngaphambi kweshidi.

## Kugcinwe eduze kwemodeli

Amabhithi esithombe awabhalwa embhalweni we-IFC. Imodeli ibhekisa kwesinye ngasinye ngegama (i-IfcDocumentReference efana ne-pictures/<id>.png) futhi amabhithi ahamba esethini yeprojekthi. Sebenzisa {{ui:Save as bundle…}} (noma {{ui:Save as IFC}} bese ukhetha lokho): uthola i-.ccbundle eyodwa enemodeli nezithombe zayo; i-IFC ihlale incane futhi ukuhlela kusheshe. Vula i-.ccbundle bese izithombe zibuya.

I-.ifc elula ayikwazi ukugcina isithombe ngaphandle kokukhula kakhulu. Lapho uyigcina, i-BimCraft ithi izithombe ezingaki zigcinwe ngaphandle kwayo futhi inikeze isethi, noma i-IFC elula efaka izithombe kuze kufike ku-512 KB futhi ishiye ezinye. I-.ifc elula evulwe ngaphandle kwesethi yayo ikhombisa lezo zithombe njengezingekho futhi uhlu lwezinkinga luzibhala; vula i-.ccbundle abagcinwe kuyo. Amafayela agcinwe yinguqulo yokuqala, lapho isithombe sisemngceleni we-IFC, asavulwa, futhi ukugcinwa kwesethi kuhambisa izithombe zawo ngaphandle. Isithombe sigcinwa njengoba sinjalo kuze kube ngu-6 MB; esikhulu sehliswa usayizi kuqala, futhi uyatshelwa. Ezinye izinhlelo ezivula imodeli ziyazinaka izithombe. Isithombe ongakasisikalisi sibhalwa ohlwini lwezinkinga njengesaziso.

## Ezigatsheni nasezibonakalweni zamashidi

Isithombe endizeni eme (khetha {{ui:Vertical}} kuphaneli yesithombe bese uchofoza amaphethelo amabili womugqa, noma usibeke phezu komugqa wesigaba oqanjiwe) sishicilelwa kwizibonakalo zamashidi ezibheke kuso ngqo. Isigaba sibonisa isithombe esime emugqeni wasso wesigaba, noma esibekwe phezu kwaloyo mugqa wesigaba ngegama. Ukubukeka kohlangothi kubonisa wonke izithombe ezimile endizeni ezibheke kuso. Isithombe sidwetshwa ngaphansi kwemigqa yombono, ngesikali sombono, sigcinwe ngaphakathi komngcele wombono, futhi sigcina ukuqina kwaso. Vula {{ui:Sheets…}} ukuze ubone; i-PDF yeshidi iphethe njengesithombe. Ukubukwa ngasemuva isithombe sibonakala sijikiwe, njengakwi-3D. Izithombe ezifihliwe ziyashiywa.
