## Okuzokwenza

Vula ipane ethi **Solar PV**. Khetha uhlangothi lophahla (ukuthambeka nokubheka kwalo kufundwa emodelini) noma wenze uhlelo lwasemhlabathini ngokuthambeka nokubheka; izimodyuli zifakwa ngaphakathi zishiyiwe emaphethelweni futhi zibalwe, zinikeze amandla e-DC ngo-kWp. Ukufaka uhlelo kubhala kumodeli njengezinsiza zamasola, ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

## Ukufiphala, isivuno nezintambo

- **Ukufiphala** kuthumela umugqa kusuka kwimodyuli ngayinye ukuya elangeni ngezinsuku ezijwayelekile zenyanga ngayinye, ngokumelene nejiyomethri yemodeli, ngakho izakhiwo eziseduze, izindonga eziphezulu nemigqa ephambili iyabalwa. Kuncishiswa kuphela ukukhanya kwelanga okuqondile, futhi uhlelo olunesiqephu esifiphele lulahlekelwa ngamandla ngokulingana.
- **Isivuno** sinikeza ama-kWh ngenyanga nangonyaka, i-kWh ngayinye kWp, nesilinganiso sokusebenza. Ilanga livela ezinanini zakho zangenyanga ezingaphezulu kwendawo ebhalwe ngokuqondile uma uzithayipha. Ngaphandle kwazo ipane isebenzisa umkhawulo ophezulu wesibhakabhaka esicacile futhi iyakusho lokho. **Landa isimo sezulu esivela ku-NASA POWER** ngenkinobho ethisekhweni lesivuno: ithumela kuphela ububanzi nobude besayithi, kanye, futhi igcina impendulo kulesi sikhombisi (ihlolwe kusiphequluli ngo-2026-10-30). PVGIS ayivumeli ikhasi lewebhu ukufunda impendulo yayo, ngakho ithebhu linikeza ixhumanisi elivula idatha yayo yenyanga ngayinye yesayithi kuthebhu elisha; namathisela lokho okubonisayo ebhokisini bese izinyanga eziyishumi nambili zigcwaliswa.
- **Izintambo** zihlola izimodyuli ngentambo ngayinye ngokuya ngewindi le-voltage ye-inverter ngosuku olubanda kakhulu nolushisa kakhulu, nezintambo ezixhunyiwe ngokufanayo ngamandla kwayo.
- **One-line** yenza idatha yomdwebo (uhlelo, combiner, disconnect, inverter, ipanele, umthombo kagesi) nobukhulu bocingo nofuse ngezinto ozithayipha. Iyidatha ne-SVG; i-Electrical add-on ye-CadCraft ingayibeka njengezimpawu.

Imodyuli, i-inverter, ukulahlekelwa nezinkomba zocingo okuwukho i-BimCraft ngezibonelo ezijwayelekile, futhi zibhalwe ngokuthi zinjani emiphumeni yonke. Thayipha idatha yomkhiqizi nezinani lekhodi ozidizayinela kuzo, bese umuntu obhekele lokho ubuyekeza idizayini.
