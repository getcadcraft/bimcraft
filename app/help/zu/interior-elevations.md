## Okuzokwenza

Khetha igumbi bese uthola ishidi elilodwa elibonisa izindonga zalo ezine njengoba uzibona usemi ngaphakathi. Lenzelwe amakhishi, izindlu zokugezela nanoma yiliphi igumbi lapho udinga khona ukubona amakhabethe, iminyango namawindi ngodonga ngalunye.

## Yenza ishidi

1. Khetha igumbi. Ku-pulani ne-{{ui:Draw}} ivuliwe, chofoza phakathi kwegumbi. Ku-3D view noma ku-model tree, khetha indawo (i-IfcSpace).
2. Ku-**Izici**, chofoza {{ui:Interior elevations…}}.
3. Ingxoxo ivula ishidi.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Download PDF}} ukuze ulilondoloze. Ifayela libizwa ngokuthi "<file> — Interior elevations — <room>.pdf" futhi liyi-vector PDF.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Close}} uma usuqedile.

## Okubonakala ishidi

Ishidi linemibono emine, emibili ngemibili, ngesikali esisodwa: isikali esijwayelekile esikhulu kakhulu esilingana. Yi-**North wall**, **East wall**, **South wall** kanye ne-**West wall**. Ibhulokhi yesihloko iphethe inombolo yeshidi A-501, isihloko "Ukubukeka kwangaphakathi — " nenombolo negama legumbi, iphrojekthi nosuku.

Ngayinye imbono inqunywe phakathi kwegumbi ibheke udonga oluseceleni. Izindonga eziseceleni, kanye nalokho okusikiwe kuzo njengewindi, zibonakala njengepoché enombala ogrey ezungeze umbono. Umbono uyeka kancane ngaphesheya kobuso bodonga, ngakho akukho okubonakala ngemuva kwalo.

Ngayinye imbono iqukethe:

- ububanzi begumbi ngaphansi kwayo, kusuka ebusweni bangaphakathi kuya ebusweni bangaphakathi;
- ukuphakama kwegumbi ngasohlangothini, kusuka phansi kuya ezingeni elingaphezulu;
- ithegi elisikwele kunoma yiluphi umnyango nethegi le-hexagon kunoma yiliphi iwindi kuleyo ndonga, nenombolo yalo (D1, W1 njll.);
- ithegi lesiyingi nenombolo yento kunoma yiyiphi into yomtapo ebekwe phambi kwalowo donga: amakhabethe, izixhobo nezinsiza.

:::note
Imibono yenziwa kusuka kumodeli njalo lapho uvula ingxoxo, ngakho ihlala ifana nayo. Hambisa ikhabethe, bese uvula ingxoxo futhi.
:::

## Kumashidi akho

Udonga ngalunye lwegumbi ngalinye nalo ingumbono ongawubeka ishidi oyenzile: kumbonisi wephrojekthi, chofoza {{ui:New sheet}}, bese ngaphansi kwe-**Ukubukeka kwangaphakathi** wengeza "101 Ikhishi — North wall" nezinye. Lifika nezilinganiso namathegi afanayo.

## Akukakwakhiwa

Ukubukeka kwangaphakathi kusethi yemizobo ekhiqizwe (kuvela ku-**Izici** zegumbi noma emashidini akho), amagumbi angewona ama-rectangle amaningi (umbono wenziwa ngesikwele ohlangothini ngalunye lwebhokisi legumbi), ukudweba, amanothi noma izilinganiso zakho kuwo, nokukhetha izindonga ezizobonakala.
