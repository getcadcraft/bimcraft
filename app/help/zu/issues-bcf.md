## Okuzokwenza

Chofoza ngqo enkingeni emodelini bese ugcina umbono oyibonisayo, bese uyidlulisela ngefayela le-BCF, ifomethi ezinye izinsiza zokuhlanganisa ezifunda nezibhala kuyo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC bese usetha umbono: zungeza kuyo, khetha izakhi ezihileleke, fihla okuvimbelayo, uyisike ngendiza yesigaba noma ngebhokisi.
2. Chofoza ithebhu ethi {{ui:Issues}} ngakwesokudla, bese {{ui:New issue from this view}}.
3. Bhala isihloko, nombono uma ufuna; khetha isimo; nika igama lakho noma i-e-mail (uma ungenile, igama lo-akhawunti yakho liyagcwaliswa; bona [Izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft](topic:settings#your-name-on-issues)). Chofoza {{ui:Create issue}}.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Show}} enkingeni ukuze ubuyisele umbono wayo.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza i-BCF}} ukulanda zonke izinkinga njengefayela elilodwa le-`.bcf`.
6. Ukuze ubone izinkinga ezenziwe omunye umuntu, chofoza {{ui:Open BCF…}} bese ukhetha ifayela lakhe le-`.bcf` noma le-`.bcfzip`.

## Okubonakalayo

- Inkinga ngayinye ohlwini ngesithombe sombono wayo, isihloko, isimo, umbhali nosuku.
- {{ui:Show}} ibuyisela ikhamera, izakhi ezikhethiwe, okwafihlwa (noma izinto kuphela ezibonisiwe) kanye nesigaba. Izakhi zitholakala nge-GlobalId yazo kuzo zonke izimodeli ezivuliwe; umugqa wesimo uyatshela uma ezinye zingekho.
- Ipulani yesigaba noma ibhokisi evela kwenye insiza iboniswa uma ihambisana nama-akhisi emodeli; amapulani athambekile noma angeziwe abuya njengevolumu yesigaba (kuze kube ngamapulani ayi-6). Inkinga igcina ibhokisi lokuqala levolumu enamabhokisi amaningi.

## Okuwusizo ukukwazi

- Ifayela liyi-BCF 2.1 futhi ligcina umbono ngokuhlelwa kwemodeli, ngakho ezinye izinsiza zibeka ikhamera endaweni efanayo.
- Izinkinga zihlala kule thebhu uze uzigcine. Ukuvula enye imodeli kugcina uhlu; vula imodeli izinkinga ezihlobene nayo ngaphambi kokuzibonisa.
- Ikhamera ye-parallel (orthogonal) evela kwenye insiza iboniswa ngekhamera ye-perspective yalo mbonisi, ibuyiselwe emuva ukuze ingxenye efanayo yemodeli igcwalise umbono.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Ukubonisa kuhambise ikhamera endaweni engavamile.** Inkinga yenziwa kwenye imodeli noma ngamanye ama-coordinates. Vula imodeli efanele kuqala.
- **Ifayela alivuleki.** Alisona i-BCF zip, noma lonakele; umugqa wesimo uchaza ngani.
