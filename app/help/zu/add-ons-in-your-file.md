## Lokhu kwenza ini

Uma okwengeziwe kubhala emodelini (ithrasi, umugqa wepayipi), i-BimCraft iqopha okwengeziwe nenguqulo yakho ku-IFC project, ku-property set ebizwa ngokuthi CadCraft_AddOns. Irekhodi libhalwa ngesinyathelo esifanayo sokuhlehlisa njengoshintsho lwalokho kwengeziwe, ngakho ukuhlehlisa lolo shintsho nalo kususa irekhodi. Ezinye izinhlelo zilinaka i-property set; okwadwetshwa yilokho kwengeziwe kuhlala kufayela noma kunjalo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula ifayela le-IFC okwasiza okwengeziwe ukulenza.
2. Uma okwengeziwe okusisebenzisile kungavuliwe, kukhona inothi phezu kwemodeli elitshela ukuthi yiziphi, nenkinobho yokuzengeza kwi-akhawunti yakho yoMtapo.
3. Zengeza, noma vala inothi. Imodeli iyavuleka kokubili.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **Akukho nothi elivela.** Awungeni ku-akhawunti yakho kule thebhu, noma ifayela alinarekhodi (lenziwe ngaphambi kwamarekhodi, noma ngohlelo olunye).
- **Uhlu lwezinguquko lubhala "undefined".** Kulungisiwe: amalebula manje akhombisa igama lokwengeziwe.
