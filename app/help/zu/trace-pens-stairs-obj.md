## Landela izinga

Khetha {{ui:Landela}} kumenyu {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}}, bese ukhetha ombono kuhlu: ephlanini lelinye izinga, ephlanini lophahla lwangaphakathi lezinga, noma isibizo sesigaba (esisikwe ngomkhawulo waso). Izindonga, izinsika namaphansi alo kubonakala ngamachashaza ngaphansi kwephlani ozwela kulo. Yihambise ngokuchofoza iphoyinti lesisekelo esisethenjwa bese uchofoza lapho leliphoyinti kufanele liye khona, noma uthayiphe X, Y nengeneli (iyajikeleza ngaphakathi kwayo). {{ui:Setha kabusha}} iyabuyisela, {{ui:Fihla}} iyayisusa. Yisethenjwa kuphela: akukho okuyikhethayo, ayiphrintwa futhi ayigcinwa ku-IFC. I-BimCraft iyakhumbula kusiphequluli lesi ukuthi ulandele kanjani izinga ngalinye.

## Amapeni ngombono

Khetha {{ui:Amapeni}}. Chaza ukuthi ipeni ilani: zonke izindonga, zonke iminyango, zonke amawindi, uhlobo lwento ekhethiwe, noma izinto ezikhethiwe. Khetha ububanzi, umugqa (oqinile, onamachashaza, onamaphoyinti) nombala, bese {{ui:Sebenzisa}}. Ushintsho lusebenza kuphlani lwalelizinga kuphela. Ukukhetha kunqoba uhlobo, nohlobo lunqoba isigaba sonke. {{ui:Sula konke}} iyakususa. Zigcinwa kulesi siphequluli. Thikha **Imibala yamapeni ku-3D** ukuze upeyinte amapeni emibala emodelini kwi-3D view; ububanzi noma ukudashwa kumugqa kuwuhlobo lomugqa, ngakho imibala kuphela ibonakala lapho; susa ithikhi ukuze ubuyele emuva. {{ui:Phrinta}} iwadweba kwi-PDF yephlani: ububanzi buskala kusuka kusikrini (ipeni elizenzakalelayo lodonga elingu-1.75 amapixel ngu-0.5 mm), izindawo zokudasha zihlala zidashi, nombala uphrinta ngenani lawo lesihloko sephepha.

## Isitebhisi esivela esketshini

Khetha {{ui:Dweba isitebhisi}} bese uchofoza amakhona womugqa wokuhamba, isinyathelo esiphansi kuqala. Umugqa owodwa uyisitebhisi esiqondile, imigqa emibili ehlangana ngengeneli eqondile iyi-L, nemigqa emithathu (phezulu, phambili, emuva) iyi-U. Cindezela {{key:L}} ukudweba isiteshi sokuphumula: chofoza amakhona alo bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}; uhlangothi lwalo olufushane luyibeka ububanzi besitebhisi. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukubeka isitebhisi. Inyathelo ngalinye libalwa ngobude bomugqa wakho nenani lezinyathelo ezidingwa ukuphakama. Ngemva kwalokho kuyisitebhisi esijwayelekile: shintsha kuIzici, bese umgodi wesitebhisi nezibambelelo ziyalandela.

Thikha **Yenza ibe umndeni** kumugqa wethuluzi (noma cindezela {{key:F}}) ngaphambi kwe-{{key:Enter}} ukuze ubeke isitebhisi njengomndeni (isitebhisi esiqondile, i-L noma i-U). Ifika njengento eyodwa bese ivula kumhleli womndeni nobubanzi bayo, inyathelo, ukuphakama okuphelele, izinyathelo zendiza ngayinye nebanga le-U njengamapharamitha, ukuze wengeze izilinganiso, uphawu lwephlani noma ama-hotspot. Izinyathelo nokuphakama kwezinyathelo kulandela kuwo. Alinazo izibambelelo noma umgodi wesitebhisi: lezo zingezesitebhisi esijwayelekile.

## Vula i-OBJ

Vula ifayela le-`.obj` njengakunoma iyiphi enye ifayela. I-OBJ alinayo izinyunithi, ngakho i-BimCraft ifunda amamitha nge-Y ephezulu, njengoba izinhlelo eziningi zilibhala. Yonke into noma iqembu iba yinto eyodwa yemeshi ezingeni elilodwa, bese ungayibona, uyilinganise, uyisike, uyibeke emodelini futhi uyigcine njenge-IFC. Imigqa, amajika nezindawo ziyashiywa, futhi umugqa wesimo uyakutshela lokho.

Into eyiphrizimu ngokuphelele (imeshi evaliwe enezindawo ezimbili ezilingana ngokuphelele nezinhlangothi ezingqondile kuzo: ifulegi, ipositi, ibhulokhi lodonga, ipuleti ngezimbobo) igcinwa ku-IFC njengevolumu eqinile yakwa-IFC enesakhiwo sayo nobujula, hhayi ohlu lwezinxantathu. Ezinye izinhleloziyifunda njengevolumu eqinile, ayikhulu, futhi ubujula nesakhiwo sayo kungahlelwa kamuva. Umugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi zingaki izinto ezigcinwe ngale ndlela. Okungeyona iphrizimu ngokuqinisekile (isimo esincipha, imeshi lapho ikhona elilodwa lishintshwe, imeshi enegebe) kuhlala kungezinxantathu. Ifayela le-STEP lifundwa ngendlela efanayo.
