## Okuzokwenza

Dweba imikhawulo yohlelo lwendawo, beka izindawo ngaphakathi kwayo, bese ufunda izinhlanganisela.

## Lapho kukhona amathuluzi

- Izakhiwo zeribhoni: {{ui:Architecture}} › **Igumbi nendawo** › {{ui:Area}} kanye {{ui:Area Boundary}}.
- Isakhiwo sakudala: ibha yokudweba › imenyu {{ui:Rooms}}.

## Izinyathelo

1. Khetha {{ui:Area Boundary}}. Ku-row khetha {{ui:Scheme}}: Indawo ephelele yesakhiwo noma Ethengiswayo. Uhlelo ngalunye lunemikhawulo yalo.
2. Khetha indlela yokwenza imigqa ku-{{ui:Make by}}:
   - **Dweba**: chofoza iphoyinti ngelinye.
   - **Khetha izindonga: umugqa wephakathi**: chofoza udonga, umugqa waphakathi walo ube umkhawulo.
   - **Khetha izindonga: ubuso obuchofoziwe**: chofoza udonga ohlangothini ofuna ukuba khona; lobo buso bube umkhawulo. Ye-indawo ephelele yesakhiwo chofoza ngaphandle kwezindonga zangaphandle.
3. Khetha {{ui:Area}}. Khetha uhlelo olufanayo, thayipha igama, inombolo noma uhlobo uma ufisa, bese uchofoza ngaphakathi kwemikhawulo.
4. Indawo ilinganiswa masinyane. Yikhethe ukuze uguqule igama, inombolo noma uhlobo ku-Izakhiwo.

## Okulandelayo

- Umkhawulo owenziwe ngokukhetha udonga uyalandela leyo ndonga: hambisa udonga bese indawo ilinganiswa futhi ngesinyathelo esifanayo.
- Umkhawulo owudwetshile uhlala lapho wawudwetshe khona.
- Izinhlangothi zezindonga ezihlangana ekhoneni nazo zihlangana khona, ngakho izindonga ezine ezikhethiwe zivala indawo ngaphandle kwezikhala.
- Imikhawulo yezindawo ibonakala epulanini uma isithuluzi segumbi noma sendawo sisesandleni sakho, ukuze ingavimbi ipulani esikhathini esisele.

## Uma imikhawulo ingavali

Indawo isabekwa. Ibhala **Akuvalekile** futhi ayilinganisi lutho uze imikhawulo ivale yonke. Lesi iseluleko: akukho okunqatshelwayo.

## Uhlu

Ipaneli yesobunxele › {{ui:Schedules}} › **Izindawo**: uhlelo, inombolo, igama, uhlobo, izinga, indawo nesibungu, nesamba. Ukuchofoza umugqa kukhetha indawo. {{ui:Download CSV}} inikeza ithebula elifanayo.

## Ku-IFC

Indawo iyi-IfcSpace engenayo umzimba: PredefinedType GFA ohlelweni lwendawo ephelele yesakhiwo, USERDEFINED ne-ObjectType Rentable ethengiswayo. Iphethe GrossFloorArea ne-GrossPerimeter, kanti ayinayo indawo yenethi, ngakho ingadidekiswa negumbi. Umkhawulo uyi-IfcVirtualElement.

## Akukakwakhiwa

- Izinhlelo zakho (kukhona Gross building ne-Rentable kuphela).
- Ukugcwalisa ngemibala ngohlobo lwendawo.
- Imithetho ebeka umkhawulo ngokuzenzakalelayo, njengokuthi "kuze kufike umugqa weglasi".
