## Okuzokwenza

Nika isakhiwo isisekelo esihambisana nezindonga zaso. BimCraft ilandela ngaphandle kwezindonga, ilinganisa izisekelo ngohlobo lodonga nokuthi izitezi zingaki ezimi phezu kwazo, futhi yenza isisekelo kabusha uma izindonga zishintsha.

## Faka isisekelo

1. Esitezini lezindonga ezimi kuso (ngokuvamile okokuqala), vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Isisekelo}}.
2. Khetha uhlobo:
   - **Ipulati ezansi emhlabeni**: ipulati lekhonkolo elingu-4" elinephezulu lingumgogodla, nomphetho ogqoliwe ngaphansi kwezindonga.
   - **Izindonga zesisekelo nepulati**: izindonga zesisekelo kuze kufike emgogodleni ezisekelweni, nepulati ngaphakathi kwazo.
   - **Crawlspace**: izindonga zesisekelo ezima ngaphansi kwesakhiwo samaphansi, ezisekelweni, ngaphandle kwepulati.
   - **Ibhasimende**: izindonga zesisekelo ezigcwele ukuphakama konke ezisekelweni nepulati lebhasimende, ngaphansi kwesakhiwo samaphansi.
3. Phakathi kwesakhiwo phawula: izisekelo zibonakala zidashiwe nosayizi wazo. Chofoza.

Ukuchofoza isakhiwo esinesisekelo sesivele sikhetha lesi, ukuze ushintshe esinye kunokusho esinye.

## Shintsha

Khetha isisekelo (chofoza phakathi kwesakhiwo nge-{{ui:Isisekelo}}, noma chofoza noma iyiphi ingxenye yaso kumbono we-3D noma kumuthi wemodeli) bese ushintsha ku-**Izici**: uhlobo, ububanzi nokujula kwezisekelo, ukuthi phansi kangakanani ukuhlangana kwaso, ubukhulu bodonga, ukuthi izindonga zimi kangakanani ngaphansi kwephansi, ukuphakama kwebhasimende nobukhulu bepulati. Yonke inguquko iyisinyathelo esisodwa se-undo.

Izinkulumo ezimaka **auto** zivela ezindongeni: izisekelo ezingu-12", 15" noma 23" ububanzi zesitezi esisodwa, ezimbili noma ezintathu ezisekelweni nasezindongeni zesisekelo; 18", 22" noma 27" ngaphansi kwe-crawlspace noma ibhasimende, nobubanzi obengeziwe nge-4" ngaphansi kwezindonga zebhilikhi noma zekhonkolo. Thayipha usayizi ukuze usethe owakho; thayipha `0` ukubuyela ku-auto.

:::warning
Usayizi ozenzekelayo ungamanani ajwayelekile wezindlu ezilula zokwakha, hhayi ukuklama. Umklami noma unjiniyela uhlola izisekelo, ukujula nezindonga ngokomhlabathi, ukujula kokuqandisa nemithwalo.
:::

## Okugciniwe

Isisekelo sigcinwa njengendlela ye-IFC element assembly ("Foundation") eyakhiwe ngesisekelo (noma ukuthambeka kwepulati ezansi emhlabeni), udonga lwesisekelo nepulati njengoba uhlobo lwaso lufuna, konke kukhonkolo, ngayinye inezinanikelo zayo: ubude, ububanzi, ukuphakama, izindawo nevolumu, ilungele ukubala amanani.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Hambisa, engeza noma sula izindonga, isisekelo ngaphansi kwazo siyenziwa kabusha; dweba izindonga esitezini eliphezulu, bese izisekelo ezingaphansi ziyanda ngesitezi esengeziwe.
- {{key:Delete}} isusa isisekelo esikhethiwe nazo zonke izingxenye zaso.
- Uma umhlabathi wesayithi usemodeli (bona [Umhlabathi wesayithi, isiza sesakhiwo nokufakwa kwendlela](topic:terrain)), izindonga zesisekelo ne-crawlspace nezisekelo zehla nawo ngezinyathelo ze-8".
- Izisekelo ngaphansi kwezindonga zangaphakathi azikakwenziwa.
