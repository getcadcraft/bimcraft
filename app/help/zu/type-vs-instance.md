## Okuzokwenza

Setha umkhiqizi ohlobweni lomnyango ukuze zonke iminyango yalolohlobo ziwubonise, bese unikeza omunye umnyango isilinganiso sawo somlilo ngaphandle kokuthinta eminye. Lokhu kubonisa umehluko phakathi kwezici zohlobo nezezibonelo ku-BimCraft.

## Uhlobo noma isibonelo?

- **Isibonelo** siyeka into eyodwa: ithegi yayo, imaki yayo, igumbi layo, isilinganiso sayo somlilo.
- **Isici sohlobo** siyeka uhlobo okwabelwana ngalo izinto eziningi: umkhiqizi, imodeli, ububanzi bohlobo lomnyango.

Kuphaneli {{ui:Izici}}, amasethi avela ohlobweni anesithi " (type)" emva kwegama lawo nomugqa othi "Isici sohlobo: ushintsho lapha lusebenza kuzo zonke izinto zalolohlobo." Yonke esinye isethi siyeka into ngokwayo. **Uhlobo** ebhlokini lokuqala lubonisa igama lohlobo.

## Izinyathelo: shintsha uhlobo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC bese uchofoza umnyango.
2. Qaphela **Uhlobo** lwawo ebhlokini lokuqala lephaneli {{ui:Izici}}, isibonelo "Single-Flush 900 x 2100".
3. Skrola kusethi igama layo eliphela ngo" (type)".
4. Chofoza inani ofuna ukulishintsha, isibonelo **Umkhiqizi**.
5. Thayipha inani elisha bese uchofoza {{key:Enter}}.
6. Bheka uhlu {{ui:Izinguquko}}: ukungena kuphethwe nge-" (type)".
7. Chofoza omunye umnyango walolohlobo.
8. Hlola inani elifanayo: nalo lisabonisa inani elisha.

## Izinyathelo: shintsha into eyodwa kuphela

1. Chofoza umnyango oyodwa.
2. Vula isethi engenalo "(type)" egameni layo, isibonelo `Pset_DoorCommon`.
3. Chofoza inani, isibonelo **FireRating**, thayipha inani elisha bese uchofoza {{key:Enter}}.
4. Chofoza umnyango oseduze nawo, owalolohlobo olufanayo.
5. Hlola inani elifanayo: lisabonisa inani elidala.

Noma uma umthumeli wedatha ewabelana ngaleso sethi phakathi kwezicabha zombili, i-BimCraft inikeza umnyango wokuqala ikhophi yawo ngaphambi kokuwushintsha. Bheka [Kungani ushintsho lwakho lushintsha into eyodwa kuphela](topic:edit-ifc-properties#why-your-edit-changes-only-one-element).

## Gcina

1. Chofoza {{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}} noma cindezela {{key:Mod+S}}.
2. Vula ikhophi egciniwe ku-BimCraft, noma kunoma yisiphi isibuki se-IFC, ukuze uhlole zombili izinguquko.

## Imiphumela ejwayelekile

- **Akuwona wonke amamodeli anohlobo.** Abanye abathumeli ababhali izinto zohlobo, noma babeka yonke into esicabheni. Ngakho akukho amasethi "(type)", futhi isicabha ngasinye kumele sishintshwe ngokwaso.
- **Inani elifanayo livela esethini yohlobo nesethini yesibonelo.** Abanye abathumeli babhala ipharamitha endaweni ezimbili. Ezinye izinhlelo zingafunda noma iyiphi; shintsha zombili uma udinga ukuthi zivumelane.
- **Ukulindela ukukhetha "zonke izinto zalolohlobo" uzihlele ndawonye.** Akukho ukukhetha okuningi kule nguqulo. Isici sohlobo yindlela yokushintsha izinto eziningi ngesikhathi esisodwa.
