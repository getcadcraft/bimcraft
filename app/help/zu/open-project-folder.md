## Okuzokwenza

Vula ifolda yonke yeprojekthi, njengaleyo onikezwe yona womsebenzi, bese uvumela i-BimCraft iyihlele: yiziphi izimodeli ezikhona, yiziphi ezikopi ezingezikhulu, yini exhuma kuyini, yiziphi amafayela alimele, nokuthi yiyiphi ifayela okungenzeka ufuna ukulivula.

Kufundwa kuphela amakhilobhayithi ambalwa anesidingo ikhadi ngalinye lemodeli, ngakho ifolda kudrayivu yenethiwekhi noma kufolda yefu ehlanganisiwe iyamukeleka.

## Izinyathelo

:::chromium
1. Chofoza {{ui:Vula ifolda}} ngakwesobunxele (noma phakathi kwesikrini esingenalutho).
2. Khetha ifolda yeprojekthi kuwindi elivulayo bese uchofoza **Khetha** (noma **Layisha** noma **Vula**, kuye ngesistimu yakho).
3. Chofoza **Bona amafayela** lapho isiphequluli sibuza "Vumela isayithi ukubona amafayela?". Lokhu kuvumela ithebhu ifunde ifolda kuphela; akukho okulayishwayo.
4. Linda umugqa wesimo ubala: "Ukubala ifolda: … amafayela", bese "Ukuhlola amafayela e-Revit: … kwezingu-…".
:::

:::firefox
1. Chofoza {{ui:Vula ifolda}} ngakwesobunxele (noma phakathi kwesikrini esingenalutho).
2. Khetha ifolda yeprojekthi kuwindi elivulayo bese uyaqinisekisa. I-Firefox ingase ikubuze ukuthi uqinisekise ukuthi ikhasi lingafunda amafayela efolda; asahlale ekhompyutheni yakho.
3. Linda umugqa wesimo uthi "Ukufunda ifolda: … amafayela", bese "Ukuhlola amafayela e-Revit: … kwezingu-…".
:::

:::safari
1. Chofoza {{ui:Vula ifolda}} ngakwesobunxele (noma phakathi kwesikrini esingenalutho).
2. Khetha ifolda yeprojekthi kuwindi elivulayo bese uchofoza **Layisha**. Nakuba igama lithi kanjalo, akukho okuphuma ku-Mac yakho: amafayela anikezwa ithebhu kuphela.
3. Linda umugqa wesimo uthi "Ukufunda ifolda: … amafayela", bese "Ukuhlola amafayela e-Revit: … kwezingu-…".
:::

Ungaphinda **uhudule ifolda** isuka ku-{{pick:mac=Finder|windows=File Explorer|linux=isiphathi-fayela sakho}} uyibeke ekhasini. Ifolda ehudulwe ivula ngaphandle kwesimemezelo sokuvuma kuwo wonke amasiphequluli.

## Okuzobona

Umbiko wefolda ugcwalisa maphakathi:

- Igama lefolda, izinani zamamodeli, imidwebo ye-CAD, amamodeli e-IFC namafayela, nosayizi wonke.
- Amanothi abomvu noma aphuzi uma amafayela ethile engaphelele noma izixhumanisi ezithile zingekho kufolda.
- Ibhokisi le-{{ui:Qala lapha}}: ifayela leBimCraft elisikisela ukuthi uvule, nezizathu zalo.
- **Amamodeli**: umugqa owodwa ngemodeli, nezikhulu zayo zihlelwe ngaphansi kwayo.
- **Amafayela axhunyiwe nakhonjiwe**, **Amamodeli e-IFC**, **Imidwebo ye-CAD** kanye **Ibekelwe eceleni**, uma kukhona kufolda.

Ngakwesobunxele, uphaneli {{ui:Ifolda}} uyifingqa; **Izinkinga** zithi "Akukho" uma yonke ikopi iphilile.

## Sebenzisa ibhokisi le-Qala lapha

Ibhokisi libiza ifayela eyodwa bese lichaza ngani, isibonelo:

- "Umsebenzi osanda ukugcinwa kule folda (Mar 3, 2025)."
- "*Consultant.rvt* ixhunyiwe kwamanye amamodeli, ngakho iyingemuva, hhayi ifayela okufanele uyihlele."
- "*Tower.rvt* ibonakala njengokukhetha okuvelele, kodwa ilimele; ikopi yayo yokugcina esihle isuka ku …"
- "Lena imodeli emaphakathi: ku-Revit, yivule ngo-"Create New Local" bese usebenza ekopini yasendaweni."

1. Funda izizathu.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Vula ikhadi layo}} ukuze ufunde ikhadi lalelo fayela, noma {{ui:Yibonise ohlwini}} ukuze uthole umugqa wayo ku-**Amamodeli**.
3. Hlola nomuntu owasebenza ekugcineni eprojekthini ngaphambi kokuthembela kuyo. Ibhokisi nalo liyasho lokho: kuyisibikezelo esiqagelwe ngamagama, usuku nezixhumanisi.

## Vula amafayela kumbiko

1. Chofoza igama lemodeli ukuze uvule ikhadi layo. Ikhadi lihlola ngayinye ixhumanisi ngefolda.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Buyela ku}} negama lefolda phezulu kwekhadi (noma {{ui:Buyela kwifolda}} ngakwesobunxele) ukuze ubuyele.
3. Chofoza noma iliphi ifayela le-IFC ngaphansi kwe-**Amamodeli e-IFC** ukuze liveze ku-3D.
4. Chofoza noma iliphi i-DWG noma i-DXF ngaphansi kwe-**Imidwebo ye-CAD** ukuze liveze ku-2D. Izixhumanisi zalo nezithombe zitholakala ngokuzenzakalelayo kufolda.

## Vula kabusha ifolda kamuva

:::chromium
Amafolda avulwe nge-{{ui:Vula ifolda}} noma ngokuhudula alondolozwa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Amafolda akamuva}} ngakwesobunxele. Chofoza {{ui:Vula futhi}} negama lefolda ukuze uyivule futhi ngaphandle kokuyikhetha. Ngemuva kokuqalisa kabusha isiphequluli, i-Chrome noma i-Edge ibuza kanye futhi; khetha "Vumela njalo" ukuze unqamule lokho. Uhlu lugcinwa kulesi siphequluli kuphela, akuthunyelwa noyedwa. Chofoza × ukuze ukhohlwe ifolda.
:::

:::firefox safari
Lesi siphequluli asikwazi ukukhumbula amafolda phakathi kokuvakashelwa. Khetha ifolda futhi nge-{{ui:Vula ifolda}}, noma yihudule ngenhla.
:::

## Izinephutha ezivamile

- **Ukukhetha ifolda enkulu kakhulu.** Ngaphezu kwamafayela angu-20,000: "Le folda inamafayela angaphezu kwezingu-20,000. Khetha ifolda encane, njengeyodwa yeprojekthi." Khetha ifolda yeprojekthi, hhayi idrayivu yonke.
- **Ukukhetha umzali wamaprojekthi amaningi.** Ibhokisi le-{{ui:Qala lapha}} lizokhetha phakathi kwamaprojekthi. Khetha eyodwa.
- **Amafolda e-Revit ebukeka ehlukaniswe.** Amafolda `*_backup`, `*_log` nelethi `Revit_temp` ayizinto zokugcina endaweni ehlanzekile futhi ahlelwa ngaphansi kwe-**Ibekelwe eceleni**, awafundwa.
- **Ukulindela imodeli ukuthi ivule ku-3D ekhadini layo.** Ifayela leprojekthi liyikhadi kuphela. Vula i-IFC yalo ekhishwe.
- **Amafoni namathebulethi.** Amasiphequluli amaningi wefoni awakwazi ukukhetha amafolda. Sebenzisa ikhompyutha.
