## Okuzokwenza

Bheka indawo yemodeli yomdwebo ngo-3D. Imigqa nama-polyline ahlala ngamaphakamo awo wangempela, izinto ezinobukhulu zimi njengezindonga, futhi ama-3D faces, ama-mesh nama-3D block abonakala njengezindawo ongazungeza kuzo. Amashidi asahlala ngo-2D.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula i-DWG noma i-DXF. Bona [Bona umdwebo we-DWG noma we-DXF](topic:view-dwg). Ivulwa ngo-3D ngaphandle uma {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:BimCraft}} › **Amadwebo avula ku** isho {{ui:Model (2D)}}.
2. Kusukela ku-2D, chofoza {{ui:3D}} kwibha ephezu komdwebo, noma cindezela {{key:3}}.
3. Linda isikhashana okokuqala: umugqa wesimo uthi "Kwakha umdwebo nge-3D…", bese ubonisa inani lemigqa nezindawo okubonisa.
4. Zungeza, hudula futhi usondeze (bona ithebula elingezansi).
5. Chofoza umbono kwibha yesibili: {{ui:Iso}}, {{ui:Top}}, {{ui:Bottom}}, {{ui:Front}}, {{ui:Back}}, {{ui:Left}} noma {{ui:Right}}.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Perspective}} ukuze uguqule phakathi komzimba nombono ohambisanayo.
7. Chofoza {{ui:Extents}} noma cindezela {{key:F}} ukubona umdwebo wonke futhi.
8. Chofoza {{ui:3D}} futhi, cindezela {{key:3}}, noma chofoza {{ui:Model}} noma ithebhu yeshidi ukuze ubuyele ku-2D.

## Okufanele ubone

- Indawo yemodeli nge-3D, Z iphezulu, ngemibala yomdwebo ngokwawo. Izindawo zilula kancane kunemiphetho yazo.
- Izingqimba ozivala ku-{{ui:Layers}} ziyanyamalala nasebukweni be-3D.
- Ngakwesokudla: inani lemigqa nezindawo, ubukhulu bomdwebo ngo-X, Y no-Z, **Kubukwa kukhona kuphela kwi-2D** no-**Akukadwetshwa**.

## Yisike ngendiza yesigaba

Chofoza {{ui:Section}} kwibha ye-3D, noma cindezela {{key:X}}. Ibha ngaphansi kwayo isebenza njengoba kwenzeka kumamodeli e-IFC (bona [Sika imodeli ye-IFC ngendiza yesigaba](topic:section-plane)): sika kusuka ku-{{ui:Top}}, {{ui:Front}} noma {{ui:Side}}, hudula noma uthayipha i-offset — ngeyunithi yomdwebo ngokwawo lapha — futhi {{ui:Flip}} ukugcina olunye uhlangothi. Lokho indiza elisikayo alikwazi ukukhethwa. Ukubuyela ku-2D kususa ukusika.

## Bona izici zento

Chofoza into ku-3D. Iyashintsha ibe yimibala yokukhetha futhi {{ui:Properties}} ikhombisa okutshiwo yifayela ngayo: uhlobo lwayo, ingqimba, umbala, uhlobo lomugqa nesibambo, bese idatha yayo, njengokuqala, ukuphela nobude komugqa, isikhungo nesiphambano sendilinga, amakhona e-3D face, noma igama leblokhi, iphoyinti lokufaka, isikali, ukujikeka nezinani zezici zeblokhi.

- Ireferensi yeblokhi ikhethwa njengento eyodwa, nezibhokisi ezizikwe ngaphakathi kwayo nazo.
- Ukuchofoza okuseduze kwemigqa embalwa ngamapikseli kukhetha umugqa.
- Bamba {{key:Mod}} bese uchofoza ukuze ukhethe izinto eziningi, noma ukhiphe eyodwa; {{ui:Properties}} bese iyazibala ngohlobo.
- {{key:Esc}}, noma ukuchofoza endaweni engenalutho, kususa ukukhetha.
- Izinto ezisezingqimbeni ozivalile azikwazi ukukhethwa.

## Yini edwetshwe ku-3D

| Emdwebweni | Ku-3D |
|---|---|
| Imigqa, ama-polyline ka-3D | Ngamaphakamo awo wangempela |
| Ama-polyline, imijikelezo, amajika, ama-ellipse | Endizeni yawo, ngokuphakama kwawo, ngokuthambeka uma umdwebo uthambekisa |
| Ubukhulu emigqeni, kumapolyline, emijikelezweni nasemajikeni | Izindonga zalobo bude; imijikelezo ivaliwe phezulu nasezansi |
| Ama-3D faces, ama-polyface mesh, ama-polygon mesh | Izindawo ezinemthunzi nemiphetho yazo ebonakalayo |
| Amablokhi | Abekwe, ajikelwe futhi akhulisiwe nge-3D, namablokhi asehlelwe ngaphakathi |
| Ama-hatch | Imiphetho yawo |
| Imigqa elula ye-2D | Ibhalwe, lapho yadwetshwa khona |

## Kubukwa kukhona kuphela kwi-2D

Umbhalo, umbhalo wemigqa eminingi nezici ezibonakalayo zamablokhi zidwetshwa nge-3D, endizeni yazo, ngamaphakamo azo, futhi ukuchofoza kuzo kuzikhethe. Imigqa ebanzi ye-polyline ibonisa ububanzi bayo njengebhande elilingene. Ama-multileader, izithombe nemifanekiso yama-hatch ibalwa ngaphansi kwe-**Kubukwa kukhona kuphela kwi-2D**: zibheke ethebhu ye-{{ui:Model}} noma emashidini.

## Akukadwetshwa

Izindawo nezindawo ezigcinwe njengemizimba ye-ACIS (ama-3D solids, imizimba, izifunda nezindawo) nama-subdivision mesh zidweshwa ngaphansi kwe-**Akukadwetshwa** nenani lazo. Ziyadlulwa, azinakucatshangwa.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Konke kubonakala kulinganile.** Umdwebo ungowe-2D: imigqa yawo ayinamaphakamo. Chofoza {{ui:Front}} ukuze uhlole; umdwebo olinganile ubonakala njengomugqa.
- **Imodeli eqinile ayibonisi lutho.** Izinto ezenziwe ngomhlobisi weqoqo ziyizimbiza ze-ACIS, zidweshwa ngaphansi kwe-**Akukadwetshwa**. Hambisa imodeli njenge-IFC noma njengama-3D faces esikhundleni salokhu kusuka kuhlelo olwayenza.
- **Umdwebo uyiqoqo elincane ngaphakathi.** Into ekude emoduleni, njengekhophi endala yepulani, yenza okuphelele kube kukhulu. Sondeza ngeskrola lapho ofuna ukubheka.
