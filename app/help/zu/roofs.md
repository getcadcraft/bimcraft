## Okuzokwenziwa

Yakha uphahla ngokusuka kodonga abaphansi kwalo. Udonga ngalunye unokusethwa kwalo, ngakho uphahla olulodwa lungaba ne-hip ngaphambili, amagabhula emaphethelweni futhi lume lufinyelele odongeni olude ngemuva. Esivandekeni noma kunoma yiluphi uhlangothi uphahla lokuzenzakalela olungakwazi ukukwenza, dweba indawo yophahla ngamakhona ayo.

## Faka uphahla

1. Esiqeshini esinezindonga zophahla, chofoza {{ui:Roof}}.
2. Thayipha i-**Pitch** (`6/12`, noma amadigri afana njengo `30`) ne-**Overhang**.
3. Khetha ilungiselelo: **Uphahla lwe-hip**, **Amagabhula ezinhlangothini ezimfushane**, **Amagabhula ezinhlangothini ezinde**, noma **Ishedi** (uhlangothi oseduze nesikhombi yiwo ophansi; ezinye izinhlangothi ziyimiphetho yamagabhula).
4. Shiya **I-fascia ne-soffit** ichofoziwe uma ufuna imiphetho ebhokisiwe nebhodi le-fascia, noma yikhiphe uma ufuna umphetho wophahla ongenalutho.
5. Chofoza ngaphakathi esakhiweni: iphethelo, umgogodla, ama-hip nezigodi zibonakala ku-plan. Chofoza.

Noma yisiphi isakhiwo siyasebenza: izimo ze-L, T, U nezimo ezinamaphuzu, amakhona agxumile nezinqena. Ama-hip, imigogodla nezigodi zitholakala lapho izindawo zophahla zihlangana.

## Udonga ngalunye ngesikhathi

Khetha uphahla (esiqeshini esingaphezulu kwezindonga, chofoza ngaphakathi komgca walo) bese ubheka ngaphansi kwe-**Each wall** ku-**Properties**. Ukuphakamisa umugqa kumaka leso sidonga ku-plan.

- **Pitched**: udonga unendawo yophahla ethambekile phezu kwalo. Thayipha ukuthambeka kwalo, njengo `12/12` ukuze kube nemiphetho emile kuphahla olungu `6/12`.
- **Gable end**: uphahla luyeka ngomphetho oqondile phezu kwalo dongo, udonga ugcwaliswa kuze kufike ophahleni, bese uphahla lugoqa ngaphaya kwalo nge-**Gable overhang**.
- **None (stops flush)**: uphahla luyeka odongeni ngaphandle kokuphuphuma nangaphandle komphetho wegabhula, lapho uphahla lumelana nodonga oluphakeme.

Uhlu lwe-**Ends** phezulu lusetha zonke izindonga ngesikhathi esisodwa; **Set per wall** iyavela uma izindonga sezingahambisani nelungiselelo elithile.

## Shintsha uphahla owuvele wabeka

Khetha uphahla bese **Properties** ikhombisa izilungiselelo ezifanayo nalezo ezisethweni lethuluzi lophahla: **Pitch**, **Overhang**, **Gable overhang**, **Plate height**, **Fascia height**, **Boxed eaves** ne-**Thickness**. Noma yisiphi isishintsho sibonakala ngokushesha ku-plan nasekubukeni kwe-3D, ngezinhlolo ezifanayo nalezo zethuluzi (ukuthambeka okungu-80 degrees noma ngaphezulu kuyenqatshwa, kanjalo nesilungiselelo esingeke senze uphahla). Thayipha inani bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}, noma uphume ebhokisini.

Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuHlehlisa. Izishintsho eziningi ebhokisini elifanayo ngokulandelana (thayipha `6/12`, bese `7/12`, bese `8/12`) ziyahlanganiswa zibe isinyathelo esisodwa, ngakho i-{{key:Ctrl}}+{{key:Z}} eyodwa ibuyisela okwakukhona ngaphambi kolandelano; ukushintsha ibhokisi elihlukile kuqala isinyathelo esisha.

## Ukuphakama kwe-plate, i-fascia ne-soffit

- **Plate height** yindawo lapho izindawo zophahla zihlangana ngaphandle kwezindonga, zikalwa kusukela ephansini lesiLinga. Yishiye ingenalutho ukuze uphahla uhlale phezu kwezindonga; thayipha inani elikhulu uma ufuna isithende esiphakanyisiwe.
- **Fascia height** yibhodi elingaphansi kwemiphetho nasemagabhuleni. Thayipha `0` uma ingekho.
- **Boxed eaves** ifaka i-soffit elinganayo ngaphansi komphetho ngamunye, kusuka odongeni kuya emuva le-fascia.

Yonke indawo ihlangana nodonga wayo ngokuphakama kwe-plate, ngakho izindonga ezinokuthambeka okuhlukile zigcina umugqa we-plate munye, bese imiphetho yazo iphela ngezinkomba ezahlukile, njengoba kwakhiwa.

## Dweba indawo yophahla

1. Esiqeshini lapho izindonga zendawo ezihlezi khona, vula {{ui:Build}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Roof plane}}.
2. Thayipha i-**Pitch** kanye ne-**Eave height** uma ingesiyo phezulu kwezindonga.
3. Chofoza amakhona. Uhlangothi lokuqala oludwetshwe luyiphethelo; indawo ikhuphuka kulo iye ekwakheni kwalo kwonke.
4. Chofoza ikhona lokuqala futhi, phinda ukuchofoza kabili, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuqeda. {{key:Esc}} ulahla amakhona.

Indawo yophahla ayilandeli izindonga. Yikhethe esiqeshini esingenhla (chofoza ngaphakathi kwayo) ukuze ushintshe ukuthambeka kwayo, ukuphakama kwephethelo noma ubukhulu, noma cindezela {{key:Delete}} ukuyisusa.

## Engeza ifasitela lophahla

1. Bonisa isiLinga uphahla olusisekelo sawo.
2. Vula {{ui:Build}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Dormer}}.
3. Esihlanganweni sezinketho khetha **Dormer kind** (**Gable** noma **Shed**) bese usetha **Width**, **Front wall** (ukuphakama kodonga lwangaphambili kusuka ophahleni olungenhla kuya kumphetho wefasitela), **Pitch** ne-**Overhang**.
4. Chofoza ophahleni lapho kuzoba khona phakathi kwangaphambili kwefasitela.

I-BimCraft yakha odongeni lwangaphambili, izindonga ezimbili zohlangothi nophahla lwefasitela, bese iyilandisa iye emuva kuze ihlangane nophahla. Ifasitela legabhula linezindawo ezimbili ezihlangana emgogodleni. Ifasitela leshedi linendawo eyodwa, okufanele ibe ngaphansi kophahla kakhulu. Uphahla ngaphansi kwefasitela luyasikwa. Ifasitela lilandela uphahla uma izindonga zinyakaziswa.

Chofoza ukuze ushintshe uhlobo, ububanzi, udonga lwangaphambili, ukuthambeka noma ukuphuphuma ku-**Properties**. {{key:Delete}} iyasusa futhi ivale uphahla futhi. Kumele ifanele ngasohlangothini olulodwa lophahla, ngaphandle kwama-hip, izigodi nomgogodla, futhi ama-roof akhiwa ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Roof}} kuphela athatha amafasitela, hhayi izindawo zophahla ezidwetshwe ngesandla. Akusakhiwe okwamanje: iwindi ngaphambili kwefasitela namafasitela ezindawo zophahla ezidwetshwe ngesandla.

## Okulondolozwayo

Uphahla lutholakala njengophahla lwe-IFC lwesilebhu esisodwa ngendawo ngayinye, izindonga zamagabhula, ilunga elilodwa le-fascia nesembozo esisodwa se-soffit. Uphahla luphethe ubude bomgogodla, ama-hip, izigodi, iziphethelo nama-rake, nezindawo zalo, ukuze kubalwe amanani. Indawo yophahla iwuphahla we-IFC olunesilebhu esisodwa.

:::tip
Hlehlisa ibuyisela noma yiliphi ushintsho lophahla ngesinyathelo esisodwa, kuhlanganise nelungiselelo elishintsha wonke amadonga.
:::
