## Okuzokwenza

Qedela isakhiwo sesakhiwo. Amagumbi avela ngokwawo kusuka ezindongeni; amaphansi, amasilingi nophahla kuthatha ukuchofoza kanye ngakunye. Ngoba atholakala kusuka ezindongeni, alandela uma izindonga zinyakaza.

## Amagumbi

Yonke indawo evalwe izindonga ezidwetshiwe iyigumbi. Livela ngokushesha lapho udonga lokugcina seluwuvalile, linenombolo (101, 102 … esitezini sokuqala, 201 … esilandelayo) negama (**Igumbi 1**, **Igumbi 2** …), nendawo yalo ibonakala phakathi kwalo kupulani.

- Uma {{ui:Khetha}} evuliwe, chofoza phakathi kwegumbi ukuze ulikhethe. Ku-**Izici** shintsha **Igama** (isibonelo `Ikhishi`) ne-**Inombolo**; indawo nomjikelezo kuyabonakala.
- Susa udonga phakathi kwamagumbi amabili bese zibe elilodwa; dweba udonga udlule igumbi bese libe amabili. Igumbiligcina igama nenombolo yalo lapho izindonga ezilizungezile zinyakaza.
- Igumbi alikwazi ukususwa ngokwalo: liyisikhala izindonga ezakha.

Izindawo zilinganiswa ngaphakathi kobuso bezindonga. Amagumbi agcinwa njengezikhala ze-IFC ne-indawo yephansi, umjikelezo, ukuphakama nevolumu, ukuze amashidi azifunde (bona [Engeza izinani, uhlele izinto futhi uhlele izikhala](topic:quantity-totals)).

## Amaphansi

1. Chofoza {{ui:Iphansi}}.
2. Khetha **Lesi sigumbi** noma **Wonke umakhiwo**, **Uhlobo** (iphansi lokhuni noma islebhu lekhonkolo) nobukhulu bayo.
3. Chofoza phakathi kwegumbi (noma noma yiliphi iphoyinti phakathi komakhiwo). Okuzomboza iphansi kuqaqwa nendawo yalo.
4. Chofoza. Phezulu kwephansi kuyiphansi lesitezi; liyehla ngobukhulu balo. Iphansi lomakhiwo wonke lifinyelela ubuso bangaphandle bezindonga.

## Izilingi

1. Chofoza {{ui:Isilingi}}.
2. Khetha **Lesi sigumbi** noma **Wonke umakhiwo**, bese **Ukuphakama** kwawo phezu kwephansi.
3. Phakathi phawula bese uchofoza.
4. Ukuze uyibone isuka ezansi, nogridi nezibani ezisesilingini, shintsha ipulani ibe yipulani yesilingi: bona [Imibono, izinhlobo zokubuka namashidi akho](topic:views-and-sheets).

## Uphahla

1. Esitezini lezindonga ezithwala uphahla, chofoza {{ui:Uphahla}}.
2. Thayipha **Ukuthambeka** njengokukhuphuka ngaphezu kuka-12 (`6/12`) noma ngamadigri (`30`), kanye **Ukweqa**.
3. Khetha **Ukuphela**: **Uphahla oluyisikhwama** (zonke izinhlangothi ziyathambeka), **Amagable ezinhlangothini ezimfushane** noma **Amagable ezinhlangothini ezinde** (isakhiwo esinezinhlangothi ezine), noma **Ishedi**.
4. Phakathi kwesakhiwo: ukweqa nomphetho ophezulu kubonakala ePulani. Chofoza.

Uphahla lumi phezu kwezindonga, esitezini eliphezulu kwazo, nomphetho wawo ongaphansi uhlangana nobuso bangaphandle bezindonga. Ukuphela kwamagable kugcwaliswa udonga lwegable olunobukhulu obulingana nodonga oluphansi. Isakhiwo esinesimo se-L, T noma U sithola uphahla oluyisikhwama lapho amaphiko ahlangana ezikhaleni.

## Shintsha

Khetha iphansi, isilingi noma uphahla (kumbono we-3D, kumuthi wemodeli, noma uphahla ngokuchofoza phakathi kwesakhiwo sawo esitezini eliphezulu) bese ulishintsha ku-**Izici**: uhlobo lwephansi, ubukhulu nokuthi liphakama kangakanani phezu kwesitezi; uhlobo lwesilingi, ukuphakama, ubukhulu kanye **Ipharetheni** (igridi isilingi esiyidweba ePulani yesilingi: lutho, 600 × 600, 600 × 1200, 2' × 2' noma 2' × 4'); uhlobo lophahla, ukuthambeka, ukweqa, ukuphela nobukhulu. Yonke inguquko iyisinyathelo esisodwa se-Undo.

## Nikeza iphansi, isilingi noma uphahla uhlobo ngezendlalelo

Iphansi, isilingi nophahla kungathatha **uhlobo**, njengodonga: ukwakheka okunegama lwezendlalelo, ngayinye inempahla yayo nobukhulu bayo bangempela.

- **Lapho uyibeka.** Amathuluzi Iphansi, Isilingi, Uphahla kanye ne-Draw floor anolu hlu **Uhlobo** esihlokweni sawo. **Awukho uhlobo** ibeka njengakuqala, ngobukhulu bayo. Uhlobo luyibeka ngobukhulu bezendlalelo zalo ndawonye.
- **Kamuva.** Khetha iphansi, isilingi noma uphahla bese ukhetha **Uhlobo** ku-**Izici**. Into ihlala iyinto efanayo: indawo yayo, igumbi nedatha yayo kuyagcinwa, akukho okuphinde kudwetshwe. Kushintsha kuphela uhlobo, ubukhulu nempahla. Yileyo ndlela into eyisikethi iba ngayo eyakhiwe ngokugcwele: shintsha uhlobo lwayo lwejwayelekile ube uhlobo langempela.
- **Hlela uhlobo…** ivula umhleli wohlobo kulolo hlobo: izendlalelo kusuka **phezulu** kuya phansi, umgogodla, umsebenzi wendlalelo ngayinye, nethegi ye-LOD yohlobo. Ukulondoloza kushintsha zonke izinto zohlobo esinyathelweni esisodwa, futhi i-Undo eyodwa iyabuyisela. Ukushintsha uhlobo lokuqala kugcina ikhophi njengohlobo lwakho.
- Ngenkathi into inohlobo, ubukhulu bayo bungohlobo futhi abukwazi ukuthayiphwa. Khetha olunye uhlobo, noma **Awukho uhlobo**, ukuze ushintshe.

Uhlobo lokuqala luyisisodwa **jikelele** ngalinye (indlalelo eyodwa yempahla ejwayelekile, ethegiwe LOD 200) kanye nezimbili ezinezendlalelo (ezithegiwe LOD 300): iphansi lamapulangwe okhuni nephansi lekhonkolo elinesendlalelo sokulinganisa; uphahla lokhuni nophahla lekhonkolo oluyisicaba; nesilingi sebhodi lejipsamu nesilingi samathayili asingiwe. Lawa amanani okuqala, hhayi ukwakheka okuhloliwe: hlola izendlalelo ngaphambi kokuwathembela.

Uhlu **Izendlalelo: indawo nevolumu** lunikeza zonke izendlalelo zawo zonke izinhlobo ezisebenzayo; bona [Uhlu, ubukhulu nePDF](topic:plan-schedules-and-pdf). Uhlobo lwephansi, lophahla nelesilingi luhamba phakathi kwamaphrojekthi ephakethe lohlobo noma njengento ye-Library; bona [Amaphakethe ohlobo](topic:type-packs).

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ukuhamba, ukungeza noma ukususa izindonga kwenza kabusha amagumbi, amaphansi, amasilingi nophahla phezu kwazo.
- Uphahla luvumelana nanoma yimuphi umumo. Ukuze unikeze udonga ngalunye ukuthambeka, igable noma ukuphela okuyishedi, isiqeshana esiphakanyisiwe, i-fascia ne-soffit, noma ukudweba indiza eyodwa yophahla, bona [Uphahla: ukuthambeka kodonga ngalunye, amagable, amashedi namapuleni ophahla](topic:roofs).
- {{key:Delete}} isusa iphansi, isilingi noma uphahla oluqokiwe.
