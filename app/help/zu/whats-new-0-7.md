Lokhu yikho okushintshile ku-BimCraft 0.7, kuqhathaniswa ne-0.6. Uhlu luhlelwe ngezindawo, okubaluleke kakhulu phezulu.

## Okusha

### Akha isakhiwo

- **Izindonga ezidlula kwezitezi eziningi.** Uhlu lwobude lwethuluzi le-Wall lunikeza **Up to** noma yisiphi isitezi esiphezulu, njengoba i-Properties yenza kudonga oluyikhethile. Amapulani ezitezi eziphakathi ibonisa udonga okhanyayo, amagumbi lapho avala khona, futhi izinmnyango nezifasiteli zingafakwa kuwo kusuka kuleyo mapulani; i-**Runs through** ku-Properties iqamba izitezi edlula kuzo. Bheka [Shintsha udonga, umnyango noma ifasitela](topic:change-a-wall-or-door#change-a-wall).
- **Copy this level.** {{ui:Copy this level…}} ePhaneli Yezitezi idweba futhi izindonga zesitezi, nezinmnyango nezifasiteli zalo, nezindawo nezilingo zalo esitezini esinye noma esisha phezulu, kwisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa; amagumbi avela ngokwawo. Bheka [Draw walls](topic:draw-walls#levels).
- **Izindonga kusuka kwi-PDF noma kwiskena.** {{ui:Build}} › {{ui:From a drawing…}} manje ithatha i-PDF (umsebenzi wemigqa noma iskena ngaphakathi kuyo) noma ipulani eskeniwe (PNG, JPEG, TIFF, amakhasi amaningi): ikhasi ngalinye libe yizindonga, izinmnyango, amafasitela namagumbi esitezi, nekhasi lihlala ngaphansi kwepulani yalo. Ukulandela akusebenzisi unethiwekhi noma imodeli eqeqeshiwe, futhi umphumela ungokwemigqa yakho: hlola isitezi ngasinye ngekhasi lakhe. Bheka [Izindonga kusuka kumasethi wamapulani](topic:walls-from-plans).
- **Izakhiwo zakho zodonga.** {{ui:Wall assemblies…}} yakha uhlobo lodonga ngesendlalelo ngesendlalelo, ngengaphakathi, ingaphakathi lomoya nemembrane, nombono onezilinganiso; {{ui:Material library…}} igcina izinto zephrojekthi ku-IFC; {{ui:Annotate}} › {{ui:Reference dimension}} ilinganisa kubuso nasemigqeni yengaphakathi bese ilandela izindonga uma zishintsha. Ngomnikela womphakathi, u-Doc (drsurfbum). Bheka [Izakhiwo zodonga nezilinganiso zokubhekisela](topic:wall-assemblies).
- **Izinkambu zokubonisa nezilawuli zombono.** {{ui:Units}} ethulini lokusebenza ibonisa ubude ngamafiti namainshi, amainshi, amafiti ayinhlamvu, amamilimitha, amasentimitha noma amamitha, ezilinganisweni, ezinhlwini, ezindaweni, ezilinganisweni nasezigatshaneni. {{ui:Plan}}, {{ui:Split}} kanye {{ui:3D}} zisethulini lokusebenza, ihlukaniso lingadonswa (noma lihanjiswe ngekhibhodi), {{ui:Shaded}}, {{ui:Edges}} kanye {{ui:Wireframe}} zisecaleni kwekhyubu yombono, futhi ukuthambeka kophahla, i-fascia kanye ne-soffit kushintsha uphahla olukhethiwe ngokushesha. Ngomnikela womphakathi, u-Joel (yisraelk). Bheka [Draw walls](topic:draw-walls#levels).
- **Izinsika ezinezikhala.** {{ui:Framing}} ingabeka ama-joists aqinisiwe (ahlelweni) kanye nezinkuni ezisawiwe, ikhethe uhlu olucwebe kakhulu nokujula okufinyelela ibanga, i-16" noma i-24" ngokuphakathi, endleleni noma yiyiphi; zingena ku-IFC nohlu lokusika. Isincomo esivela kwithebhula elishicilelwe somkhiqizi, hhayi ubunjiniyela. Futhi ngoJoel. Bheka [Framing walls, floors and roofs](topic:framing).
- **Izinsiza ezengeziwe zikagesi.** {{ui:Electrical}} yengeza amasokhethi edatha, eselula nevidiyo-iudiyo, iswishi yokukhetha, isikhathimeli, ithemostethi, izikhulumi zentuthu nokushisa, isiteshi sokuma, isiren, ifeni, imitha yamandla kanye nepanel encane, ngayinye ingumkhakha wayo we-IFC. Bheka [The electrical plan](topic:electrical-plan).

### Imindeni

- **Imindeni yenza okuningi.** Amapharamitha angaba izinombolo eziphelele, umbhalo, impahla, ipeni noma ukugcwalisa; umndeni ungadweba isimbonakalo sawo sepulani, uphindaphinde isinqo, uphawule izingxenye zemininingwane emincane, uphathe imindeni yakho (umlenze etafuleni), futhi ube nezinhlamvu ozidonsayo, nakwinto ebekwe epulanini. Usayizi uvela kwithebhula le-CSV, namapharamitha amaphawulwe ngokuthi **Per element** avumela izinmnyango ezimbili zosayizi ofanayo ukuthi zihluke. Bheka [Edit a family](topic:family-editor#plan-symbol) kanye [Families and their sizes](topic:families#sizes-from-a-table).

### Amapulazi, izinkanyezi nezinsiza

- **Izinsiza ezinezixhumanisi.** {{ui:Build}} › {{ui:Equipment}} ibeka i-air handler jikelele, ifeni yomugqa, ipompo, i-boiler, isishisi samanzi, iyunithi yokugcina, i-diffuser, igrille noma ithangi, ilinganiswe ngamanani owathayiphayo, nezixhumanisi zayo zepayipi nomoya njengezixhunyo. Ngayinye igcinwa njengomkhakha wayo we-IFC nezixhunyo ze-IFC. Bheka [MEP equipment with ports](topic:mep-equipment).
- **Imigqa exhunyiwe.** Ipayipi noma umoya oqalwa kuxhunyo uthatha usayizi nohlelo lwalo futhi uhlale uxhunyiwe kuwo: hambisa ipompo bese ipayipi lalo liyalandela. {{ui:Check connections}} ibala izixhunyo ezivulekile, iziphetho ezikhululekile, ukungafani kosayizi nohlelo, nemigqa engenalutho engenayo ukunikezwa. Bheka [Connect pipes and ducts to equipment](topic:mep-connections).
- **Amapayipi nemimoya ngokumelene nesakhiwo.** I-{{ui:Clash check}} inamasethi alungiselelwe kanye {{ui:Pipes and ducts × structure}} ngokuchofoza kunye; {{ui:Check clearances}} isebenzisa ukugoqwa kwemigqa ngayinye kanye negebe owalithayiphayo; {{ui:Openings needed}} ibala lapho imigqa edlula ezindongeni nezilele, njengeCSV nezinkinga ze-BCF. Akukho okusikwayo. Bheka [Check pipes and ducts against the structure](topic:mep-against-structure).
- **Izinkanyezi zamapayipi ensimbi.** Umugqa wensimbi usikwa ube yizinkanyezi ezinenombolo ngobude obude kakhulu kanye ku-{{ui:Field weld}} ozibekayo; {{ui:Spool sheets (PDF)}} inikeza ikhasi ngalinye lenkanyezi ngombono wayo we-isometric, izinde zokusika, izinto ezixhumayo, ukuwelda nobuhlu bempahla, futhi {{ui:Duct cut list}} ibala izingcezu zomoya ezigqamile. Bheka [Steel pipe spools and spool sheets](topic:pipe-spools).

Lezi yizinsiza zokudweba nokuhlela, zilinganiswe ngunjiniyela: azikho izindlela zokugeleza, izingcindezi noma ukulinganisa.

### Ukuphatha isakhiwo

- **Amadokhumenti ngempahla ngayinye.** Ithebhu ye-{{ui:Assets}} ixhumanisa imanuwali, izaziso zomshwalense, izithombe nemidwebo nempahla ngegama lefayela eliseduze ne-IFC noma ikheli lewebhu; kuphela ixhumo olungena ku-IFC. Bheka [Documents](topic:assets-and-maintenance#documents).
- **Ispredishithi ye-COBie.** {{ui:Save as COBie (.xlsx)}} ibhala incwadi yokudlulisa yomsebenzi ye-COBie 2.4, futhi incwadi ye-COBie efundwa ngokubuyela ibala konke ukushintsha ngaphambi kwesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Apply}}. Ayikaze idlule kumhloli we-COBie okwamanje. Bheka [COBie spreadsheet](topic:assets-and-maintenance#cobie-spreadsheet).
- **Izifundo zezinzwa nokudlula kwedatha ephilayo.** Vula i-CSV yezifundo, noma {{ui:Connect}} ngqo kusiphequluli kumabhrokha wakho we-MQTT noma ikheli lesistimu yakho yesakhiwo esiphendulayo: ipaseli ngayinye ibonisa isifundo sayo sakamuva nechati, ikhanyiswe emodelini, futhi iphawulwa uma ingaphandle kwemikhawulo elondolozwe ku-IFC. Izifundo zihlala ethebhini; amaphasiwedi awagcinwa lutho. Bheka [Sensor readings](topic:assets-and-maintenance#sensor-readings) kanye [Live readings](topic:assets-and-maintenance#live-readings).

### Ukukhanya kwemini namandla

- **Isibhakabhaka nelanga ezindizeni ngayinye.** Ithebhu ye-{{ui:Daylight}} inika ifasitela ngalinye isibhakabhaka sawo esiphezulu nento yokuvimbela, ilandela ilanga phakathi kosuku nge-{{ui:Sun through the windows on a date}}, futhi isebenzise i-{{ui:Daylight factor on a grid}} kuwo wonke igumbi, idwetshwe epulanini layo. Bheka [Vertical sky component of each window](topic:daylight-factor#vertical-sky-component-of-each-window).
- **Izidingo zokushisa nokupholisa.** {{ui:Heating and cooling loads}} ibala ukulahleka kokushisa kwegumbi ngalinye, ukushisa kwalo ngonyaka kusuka ezinsukwini zedigri, nokupholisa kwalo okuphezulu, kusuka kokubopha emodelini nakwi-U-value yento ngayinye; i-U-value oyithayiphayo lapho ibhalwa ku-IFC. Bheka [Estimate heating and cooling loads](topic:heating-and-cooling-loads).

Kokubili kuyizinsiza zokuklama ngezindlela ezishicilelwe, hhayi izibalo zokuhambisana, futhi yonke ipaneli iyasho lokho.

### Iskena

- **Hlanganisa amaskena amaningi.** {{ui:Register scans…}} (esiqaleni kwephaneli, noma ku-{{ui:Show}}) ibeka amaskena esakhiweni esisodwa ngamaphuzu ahlanganisiwe akhethiwe noma ngamasethi athayiphiwe, ilungise ngeICP ukusuka phuzu kuye phuzu bese ukusuka phuzu kuya endizeni, ilondoloze ukuhlanganiswa njengefayela elincane leprojekthi, futhi ibhale ifu elihlanganisiwe njengefayela elilodwa le-LAS noma i-E57. Bheka [Register several scans into one](topic:register-scans).

### Izithombe nombono ophilayo

- **Umbono ophilayo.** {{ui:Live view}} ethulini lokusebenza ibonisa imodeli ngendlela yesikhathi sangempela eduze kombono we-3D, ilandele wona nazo zonke izinguquko, ne-Walk, isikhathi sosuku nenyanga; {{ui:Live view in a new window}} iyibeka esikrinini sesibili. Bheka [The live view](topic:live-view).
- **Ukulandelela indlela ngekhadi lezithombe.** Ngo-WebGPU, {{ui:Render}} › {{ui:High quality (path traced)}} isebenzisa umlandelelo we-CadCraft ekhadini lezithombe, ingamisa futhi {{ui:Resume}}, futhi inciphisa umsindo ekugcineni; ngaphandle kwe-WebGPU isebenzisa umlandelelo we-WebGL njengakuqala. Bheka [High quality](topic:render#high-quality).
- **Okwengeziwe kwengxoxo yokuRenda** (ekhadini lezithombe, ngakho idinga i-WebGPU): isibhakabhaka esiphysical nezithombe zesibhakabhaka, amaprofayili okukhanya e-IES, ikhamera nge-f-stop, ukujula kwenkundla, ukubona okubili, ukulinganisa umbala, izinkulungwane ezingu-360° ezibonakalayo, ibanga lokukhanya lesithombe esiqediwe, ukuxuba ukukhanya, izakhi zokurenda ngemva kokurenda, ukuqhuma, intuthu nama-caustics, ukugcina njenge-PNG enebhithi eyi-16, EXR noma ifayela lokurenda, nohlu lokurenda. Bheka [Skies and light profiles on the graphics card](topic:render#skies-and-light-profiles-on-the-graphics-card) kanye [The render queue](topic:render#the-render-queue).
- **Umshini wakho.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:BimCraft}} › {{ui:Rendering}} › {{ui:Your machine}} ibonisa amagrafikhi, amakhodi eprosesa nenkumbulo esiphequluli sivumela uhlelo ukuthi lukusebenzise, nezinga lapho ukurenda kuqala khona. Bheka [What your machine renders with](topic:your-machine).

### Amafayela nezinye izinhlelo

- **Amafayela e-STEP ayavuleka.** Ifayela `.step` noma `.stp` lengxenye, lesisetshenziswa noma lomhlinzeki (AP203, AP214, AP242) livula njengemodeli encane ye-IFC, ngamunye umzimba oyinto oyihlola, ohlolwayo, olinganiswayo nolondolozwayo njenge-IFC. Imibala namagama ezingxenye zesakhiwo awakafundwa okwamanje. Bheka [Files BimCraft opens and saves](topic:files#what-it-opens).
- **Ukuthumela kwe-USD.** {{ui:USD}} ethulini lokusebenza ibhala imodeli evuliwe njenge-`.usda`, `.usdc` noma `.usdz`, ihlelwe ngendawo, isakhiwo, isitezi nendawo yokusebenza, nayo yonke into ine-IFC class, i-GlobalId nezakhiwo. Bheka [Export the model as USD](topic:usd).
- **Umthamo weCivilCraft.** Umthamo wesakhiwo ovela kumbono wesayithi we-CivilCraft ubonisa izinombolo zokufunda kuma-Properties, futhi {{ui:Develop this mass}} yakha izindonga zazo zonke izitezi nofudu kusuka kuwo kwisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bheka [Develop a building mass from a site study](topic:develop-a-mass).
- **Izinto zomtapo ezinezinhlobo.** Into eza ngezinhlobo eziningi inokukhetha kwe-{{ui:Variant}} kuthebhu ye-{{ui:Library}}; into ebekiwe igcina uhlobo lwayo ku-IFC, {{ui:Swap item…}} iqamba ezinye izinhlobo, nokukhopisha nokubuyekeza kuyagcina. Bheka [Place and swap a library item that has variants](topic:library-variants).
- **Amaphakheji wephrojekthi esikhwameni.** {{ui:Open a folder}} ibala amafayela `.ccbundle.zip` ngaphansi kuka-{{ui:Project bundles}}, futhi isakhiwo ngasinye ephakheji sinakho **Open in …**: i-PDF iya ku-MarkupCraft, i-DWG iya ku-CadCraft, ethebhini elisha. Bheka [Open a layout](topic:open-project-bundle#open-a-layout).

### Abasizi be-AI nezengeziwe

- **Okwengeziwe kubasizi be-AI.** Umsizi manje angathola amagama ezinto nokusetha izakhiwo, ahambise futhi asuse izinto, ashintshe izindonga, iminyango namafasitela, akhiphe i-USD, arende isithombe noma asebenzise uhlu lokurenda; ngayinye iyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Bheka [Let an AI assistant work with your model](topic:assistants).
- **Izengezo zingakha emodelini.** I-add-on API 1.1 ivumela isengezo ukwenza ushintsho emodelini njengesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa (lihamba ku-Live TeamWork), ukufunda imodeli, nokufunda imigqa yamapayipi nomoya, izixhunyo, izinsiza kanye nezinkanyezi.
- **Isengezo se-Plant** (sivaliwe kuze kube yilapho usivula ku-{{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Add-ons}}): izici zamapayipi ezihlolwa ngemigqa, imidwebo ye-isometric yomugqa njenge-SVG ne-DXF nohlu lwezinto, izinsiza zokucubungula ezijwayelekile ezinezinzipho ezibekwe emodelini, umelalumanzi phakathi kwezixhunyo ezimbili, kanye nokuhlolwa kokuhlelwa kwendawo. Isiza sokudweba, hhayi ukugunyazwa kwezinjiniyela; amazwi aso ngesiNgisi kuphela.

## Kushintshiwe

- **Indlu yokuqala kwilaputhobhu.** Esikrinini esincane njengesilaputhobhu i-BimCraft iqala ngepulani kuphela, ibha yokudweba iba iphaneli emincane enepulani elingene eduze kwayo, ipulani nombono we-3D kuyabuya lapho umdwebo uphuma kuwo, umbono we-3D wakha indlu yonke ephaneli emincane, futhi izinmnyango namafasitela kukhethwa ngaphambi kwemigqa yesilinganiso.
- **Ukugcina isithombe.** **Save PNG** ekhadini lezithombe manje ngu-{{ui:Save}}, nge-{{ui:Save as}} eduze kwayo kwezinye izinhlobo. Bheka [Save the picture](topic:render#save-the-picture).
- **Ithebhu yokuhlaziya** inebhulokhi ye-{{ui:Design checks}}: yenza izinhlanganisela zemithwalo ye-ASCE 7 noma i-EN 1990 ngamacala akho futhi ihlola ngayinye yesakhiwo sensimbi esigqwayiwe (i-W, S, IPE, HEA noma HEB ngegama lephrofayili nosayizi) ngokwe-AISC 360 noma i-EN 1993-1-1, ibonise ukuthi yisiphi isabelo sokukhawuleza esisetshenzisiwe. Isisekelo kuze kube yilapho unjiniyela ebhekisisa; izakhiwo ezibotshiwe nezinye izigaba zithi "not checked". Bheka [Design checks](topic:frame-analysis#design-checks).

## Kulungisiwe

- Ukuphinda ukwenza ngemva kwesinyathelo esidale usayizi omusha womndeni kwehluleka ("no size"). Yonke isinyathelo manje senza i-IFC GlobalIds efanayo uma kuphinda kwenziwe, ngakho ukuphinda kusebenza kumindeni nakokunye konke ukushintsha.
- Lapho abantu ababili belondoloza umndeni ofanayo (esikhathini se-Live TeamWork noma ngamawindi amabili), ukugcinwa kwesibili kwashintsha okokuqala ngokuthula. Manje kuyenqaba bese kuchaza ukuthi ungabona kanjani ushintsho lwabo bese wenze olwakho kabusha.
- Imodeli ngamafiti esizwe sase-US yayifundwa njengo-0.3048 m ngefiti esikhundleni se-0.3048006096 m, okwakunyakazisa zonke ama-IFC anendawo emhlabeni ngamafiti esizwe sase-US ebhaleni.
- Umhleli womndeni: ukuvala akusayikhipha ukugxila kwekhibhodi ekhasini, i-Save yakhe isalokhu ibonakala ku-foni, nezinkambu zakhe azisenzi ifoni ilingane.

## Kuzo zonke izinhlelo

- **Izinkomba zobukhulu.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Units}} inohlu olulodwa lobude, indawo, ivolumu, i-engeli nokuthambeka, ngosayizi abaqondile ezichazweni zamayunithi (ifiti lesizwe sase-US liyiyunithi yalo). Vala amayunithi ongawasebenzisi awaphume ohlwini, futhi uphawule elilodwa usebenzisa okusha okuqala kulo. Ifayela ligcina iyunithi yalo njalo. Ku-BimCraft uhlu lwe-{{ui:Units}} ethulini lokusebenza ilandela amaswishi, isiqalo sisetha amayunithi okubonisa kuze kube yilapho ukhetha ngokuthi ethulini (ukukhetha kwakho kwangaphambili kuyagcinwa), futhi amabhokisi obude wamathuluzi okudweba amukela ukuthi yiyiphi iyunithi, njengo-`3.5 m` noma `10 US ft`. Bheka [Units, typed values and the unit lists](common:unit-lists).
- **Ukwakheka okujulile nezimpaneli ezizihlelayo.** {{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Layout density}} › {{ui:Compact}} ilungisa okwengeziwe esikrinini (izikrini zokuthinta nofoni zigcina izinkinobho zazo ezinkulu). Ngayinye iphaneli yecala inebha yesihloko elincane: yidonsele ukuze uyifake noma uyihlukanise, uyilahlele ebhareni lephaneli yomunye ukuze yabelane ngefreyimu njengamathebhu, uyiphinde ukuze ihambe ilale ngasemaphethweni, noma uyifihle bese uyibuyisela emaphethweni. Bheka [Arrange the panels](common:arrange-panels#make-the-layout-denser).
- **Amabhokisi ayahamba.** Donsa ibhokisi ngebha yalo yesihloko ukuze ulisuse endleleni yomdwebo; liyabuya lapho wawuliyeka khona. Chofoza kabili ibha yesihloko ukuze ulibeke phakathi futhi. Bheka [Move a dialog](common:arrange-panels#move-a-dialog).
- **Izindlela zabasizi be-AI.** Izicelo eziyishumi ezisebenzayo zomsizi wakho (hlela umdwebo, hlela isethi, hlola imidwebo, buza imodeli imibuzo, bala izinto ngokuphelele, zama inguqulo yengxenye…), ngayinye nezithuluzi ezibiza nemvume edingayo, futhi ngayinye iphindwa yizivivinyo zethu. Izinhlelo azinayo i-AI. Bheka [Recipes for AI assistants](common:assistants-recipes).
- **Izilimi.** **Português (Portugal)** kusha ku-{{ui:Settings}} › {{ui:General}} › {{ui:Language}}. Yona, **Português (Brasil)**, **Español (México)** kanye **Bahasa Indonesia** manje zinawo wonke amagama ezinhlelo, njengemisebenzi engakabhekwa umuntu wendabuko. Izihloko zosizo zisesiNgisi, kanye nezinye ezisesiSpanish.
- **CadCraft Studio** isho ukuthi kungani ukuhlolwa kwesibuyekezo kwehlulekile (akukho fayela lesistimu yakho okwamanje, akukho inethiwekhi, isiginesha embi) esikhundleni se-"Done" nje, futhi iyeka ukubuyisa ikhadi le-What's new oliliwe uze kube khona inguqulo entsha.

## Akusakho kule nguqulo okwamanje

- **0.7 ayisayinwanga ngekhodi.** Ukusayina, kanye nephakheji le-Microsoft Store, kwadlulela ku-0.8 ngoba ukugunyazwa kwesitifiketi akukafiki. Hlola i-SHA-256 eseceleni kwefayela ngalinye ku-getcadcraft.com/download. Ku-Windows, uma i-SmartScreen imisa isifakeli, khetha {{ui:More info}}, bese {{ui:Run anyway}}. Ku-Mac, okokuqala, chofoza ngokudla i-CadCraft Studio bese ukhetha {{ui:Open}}. I-CadCraft Studio ayikakabi esitolo sezinhlelo, futhi akukakhiwa ukuthi ngokwakhiwa kwe-iPad noma i-iPhone.
- Amalisti wama-engeli nokuthambeka ku-{{ui:Units}} ayagcinwa, kodwa akukho bhokisi elilifundayo okwamanje.
- Ukulandelela indlela ekhadini lezithombe, izengezo ze-Render dialog kanye nohlu lokurenda kudinga i-WebGPU (ngaphandle kwayo, ikhwalithi ephezulu isebenzisa umlandelelo we-WebGL); umbono ophilayo usebenzisa i-WebGPU lapho isiphequluli sayo, kwesingenayo i-WebGL 2. Ziye zahlolwa lapha kuphela kumsizi wegrafikhi wesofthiwe, hhayi okwamanje kumakhadi egrafikhi angempela. Umbono ophilayo awunayo ukusakaza noma ukwabelana ku-BimCraft (ye-ModelCraft inakho).
- Ukurenda uhambo olurekhodiwe njengevidiyo akwakhiwe ku-BimCraft.
- Izinhlolo zoyilo: i-I-shape ngegama layo le-IFC lephrofayili elingeyona usayizi we-W, S, IPE, HEA noma HEB (noma usayizi wayo uhluke kuwo) ithathwa njengewelded futhi ibuyela "not checked", njengamathubhu, ama-angles, amachanneli, izingxenye ezincane kakhulu nokujikeka. Akukho unjiniyela owazihlolisisa okwamanje.
- Izinto ezidwetshiwe azikwazi ukukopishwa zodwa, zijikelezwe, zibhalwe ngasese noma zihlelwe ngamakhophi, futhi akukho udonga oluhlukanisiwe, ithuluzi lombhalo noma udonga lwekhethini. (Isitezi sonke singakopishwa.) Udonga oludlula udonga oluvela kwesitezi esiphansi esitezini esiphezulu alufakwa emzimbeni we-3D walowo wudonga, futhi izinkundla zikhombisa izimbobo zesitezi esiphezulu ngaphansi kwesitezi sodonga lwazo.
- Imindeni: umtapo wemindeni ohlelwe phakathi kwamaprojekthi awukakwakhiwa. Imindeni nezitezi ezikopishiwe azikaze zivulwe ezinhlelweni zeBIM ezinye.
- MEP: izinsiza zijwayelekile (azikho idatha yabakhiqizi); amatee nezinciphisi ezinkanyezini zensimbi zijwayelekile, azivela etafuleni le-ASME B16.9; izixhumi zempahla yomtapo ezibekwe ngethebhu ye-{{ui:Library}} azibhalwa ku-IFC; akukho okuhlolwe unjiniyela.
- Isengezo se-Plant asikwazi okwamanje ukudweba umugqa oqondisiwe emodelini, ukubalela izinkanyezi kumidwebo yazo ye-isometric, noma ukuhlola ukuhlelwa kwezinsiza ezibekwe kumtapo.
- Izitshalo zomtapo wezitshalo we-CivilCraft azikwazi ukubekwa ethebhini le-{{ui:Library}} le-BimCraft okwamanje.
- Amandla: akukho ukulinganisa ngamahora, akukho indlela ye-ISO 52016-1 neyamafayela ezulu. Amasethi: akukho ukuphila kwedijithali okugcina izifundo noma okuthumela izexwayiso lapho i-BimCraft ivaliwe.
- Ukuhlanganiswa kweskena kuhlolwe kumaskena okuhlola, akuhlolwanga kusethi yangempela yeskena lenhlangano; amafayela e-E57 avula kuze kube ngamapointi ayizigidi eziyisi-6 kanye no-512 MB.
- Amaphakheji wephrojekthi avulwa ku-BimCraft kuphela (ezinye izinhlelo zithatha isakhiwo nge-**Open in …**), futhi amaphakheji nekhithi yezinhlelo ezimbili ziye zahlolwa ngezinhlelo ezilingisa, hhayi okwamanje ne-Archicad noma i-Revit ngokwazo.
