## Okuzokwenza

Guqula uhlu lwezingqubuzano lube yizinqumo. Isigaba ngasinye (isakhiwo, imishini, ipulamba, kagesi, ukwakhiwa) siphakamisa izixazululo zezinto zaso kuphela, yonke iphakamiso ihlolwa ekhophini lemodeli, wena ukhetha.

## Izinyathelo

1. Qala wenze ukuhlola kokungqubuzana. Bona [Hlola ukungqubuzana](topic:clash-check) kanye [Hlola amapayipi nemisele ngokumelene nesakhiwo](topic:mep-against-structure).
2. Ngaphansi kwemiphumela, chofoza {{ui:Phakamisa izixazululo}}. Ipaneli ethi **Izixazululo eziphakanyisiwe** ivula ecaleni kwemodeli.
3. Kungokungqubuzana ngakunye uzobona iziphakamiso zesigaba ngasinye, eziphezulu kakhulu phezulu. Umugqa ngamunye uchaza ukuthi yenzani nokuhwebelana kwayo: noma ixazulula ukungqubuzana, ingakhi kangakanani okusha, yephula yiphi imithetho, ingeza kangakanani umugqa noma ihamba kangakanani, izinhlanganisela ezingakanani, nesilinganiso sezindleko.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Show}} ukubona izinto ku-3D. Chofoza {{ui:Accept}} kwisixazululo ofuna (eyodwa ngokungqubuzana ngakunye), noma {{ui:Yenqaba}}.
5. Umugqa ongaphansi uchaza ukuthi zonke izixazululo ozamukele zenza ndawonye yini: ukungqubuzana kangakanani okuxazululiwe, kangakanani okusele, nokuthi kukhona okusha okungqubuzanayo yini.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Sebenzisa izixazululo ezamukelwe (isinyathelo sokuhlehlisa esisodwa)}}. Ukuhlola kokungqubuzana kuqhubeka futhi. {{ui:Undo}} ibuyisela zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Yini esigaba ngasinye esingayenza

- **Imishini** (imisele, amapayipi okushisa nokupholisa): zungeza phezulu noma ngaphansi ngahlangothi olugoqayo, phakamisa noma yehlisa wonke umugqa, uwususe eceleni, noma wenze umsele onqamunxeni ube mncane ngobude kodwa indawo ifane.
- **Ipulamba** (amanzi, ukukhipha, igesi, umlilo): imihlahlandlela efanayo, ngaphandle kokuthi ipayipi lokukhipha elisebenza ngodonsela womhlaba alikaze linikezwe ukuzungezwa; lingahanjiswa njengoyunithi, ligcine ukwehla kwalo.
- **Kagesi** (amathreyi nemisele yocingo): imihlahlandlela efanayo; ithreyi kufanele ligcine i-150 mm ecacile phezulu kwalo ukuze kubekwe ucingo.
- **Isakhiwo**: hambisa insika (kufika ku-300 mm), noma ivulo esiphakathini kwebhimu noma esilebeni ngomugqa odlula. Konke ukulungiswa kwesakhiwo kuthi umhlahlandlela wesakhiwo uyasayina.
- **Ukwakhiwa**: isileve odongeni ngomugqa odlula, noma hambisa udonga okwaziwa ukuthi alwethwe umthwalo.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Izixazululo eziphula umthetho ziyamakwa: eziseduze kakhulu nengaphezu kwe-25 mm yegebe kwenye into, ngaphansi komgoqo wokuphakama ongu-2,100 mm, ivulo esiphakathini kwebhimu elikhulu kuno-40 % wokujula kwebhimu noma eliseduze kakhulu nokuphela kwalo, nebhimu eliyoshintsha ibanga.
- Izixazululo ezingenziwa yi-BimCraft zisetshenziswa kumodeli: imigqa odwebe ngamathuluzi e-Pipe ne-Duct nezinsika ozidwebile. Ezinye zithi **Iseluleko kuphela**: ukuzamukela kwazo kwengeza inkinga ethebhini le-Issues.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Izixazululo zivela ezimthethweni ezakhelwe ku-app; ayisetshenziswa i-AI kuzo.
- Uma uxhuma umsizi (bona [Vumela umsizi we-AI asebenze nemodeli yakho](topic:assistants)), angafunda ukungqubuzana futhi angangeza iziphakamiso zakhe zesigaba esisodwa kupaneli evulekile. Zihlolwa ngendlela efanayo, zimakwe ngegama lomsizi, futhi akukho okushintshwa kuze kube yilapho uyamukela futhi usebenzisa.
- Ikhophi ihlolwa ngamabhokisi azungeze isinto ngasinye nomgoqo wesinto ngasinye womugqa, ngakho umugqa othambekile ungabonisa ukungqubuzana okungase kungatholakali ekuhloleni kokungqubuzana. Phinda wenze ukuhlola kokungqubuzana ngemva kokusebenzisa: yiyo ekugcineni.
- Imikhawulo (umugqa ongakanani ongahamba, ukuphakama kokuzungeza, igebe) iyizinkambiso ezizenzakalelayo ezibhalwe kupaneli.
