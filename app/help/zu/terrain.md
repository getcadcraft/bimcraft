## Okuzokwenza

Beka indlu endaweni yayo. Umhlaba uba yingxenye yemodeli: uboniswa ku-3D, usikwa ezigabeni nasekubukeni kohlangothi, namakhonthuwa ephlanini. Isisekelo sesakhiwo sisikwa ngaphansi kwendlu, imigwaqo yokungena nezindlela zabahambi ziyilandela, nezisekelo zehla nayo.

## Letha umhlaba

1. Vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Umhlaba}}.
2. Noma:
   - **Vula ifayela lokuhlola…**: amaphoyinti okuhlola (PNEZD, PENZD noma empumalanga, enyakatho nokuphakama kuphela; ahlukaniswe ngekhoma, ithebhu noma isikhala), amakhonthuwa ku-DXF noma DWG (imigqa eyi-polyline ngokuphakama kwayo, imigqa eyi-polyline ye-3D), ukuskena kwe-LAS noma LAZ (amaphoyinti omhlaba agcinwe, anciphisiwe abe cishe elilodwa ngesikwele samitha ngasinye), i-GeoTIFF noma igridi yokuphakama ye-ASCII, noma **umphezulu we-LandXML** njengalowo ukhishwa yi-CivilCraft (amaphoyinti nezinxantathu zawo ziyagcinwa; uma ifayela linamaphezulu amaningi, i-BimCraft iyabuza ukuthi yiyiphi umhlaba; izinyunithi zayo zivela kulo fayela). Khetha izinyunithi zefayela kuqala (i-LandXML ichaza zayo).
   - Noma uthayipe **Ukuphakama kwendawo** (kusuka ephansi lalelizinga, ngakho `-1'` kuyinyawo eyodwa ngaphansi kwalo) bese uchofoza amaphoyinti ephlanini, ushintshe ukuphakama bese uchofoza ezinye, bese uchofoza **Yenza umhlaba**. Amaphoyinti amathathu ayanele ukuqala.

Izixhumanisi zokuhlola ezikude nemodeli (state plane, UTM) ziyahanjiswa zifike endlini, nokuphakama kusethwe ukuze umhlaba endlini ube cishe ngu-8" ngaphansi kwephansi lokuqala. **Phakamisa umhlaba** ku-**Izici** iyalungisa lokho ngalo nanoma yisiphi isilinganiso.

## Isisekelo sesakhiwo

1. Khetha {{ui:Isisekelo sesakhiwo}}.
2. Setha **Umda** ozungeze indlu, ukuthi **isisekelo sihlala kangakanani ngaphansi kwephansi** (8" ngaphansi kwesilabhu, ngaphezulu ngaphansi kwe-crawlspace) nezimpendulo **Izimpendulo** eziya emhlabeni osele (`2:1` = izinyawo ezimbili ngaphandle ngayo yonke inyawo ephakanyiswa noma yehliswa).
3. Chofoza phakathi kwendlu bese uchofoza.

Umhlaba ulinganiswa ngaphansi kwendlu nakuze kube umda, bese uthambeka waya ezintabeni (ukumba) nasezansi ohlangothini oluphansi (ukugcwalisa) kuze kuhlangane nomhlaba okhona. **Izici** zomhlaba nezesisekelo zinikeza amavolumu okumba nokugcwalisa. Hambisa izindonga, isisekelo siyalandela.

## Imigwaqo yokungena, izindlela nezindawo zangaphandle

Khetha {{ui:Ukufaka ipavumende}}, khetha **Indlela yabahambi**, **Umgwaqo wokungena** noma **Ipatiyo**, bese uchofoza amakhona. Chofoza ikhona lokuqala futhi, chofoza kabili noma cindezela {{key:Enter}} ukuze uqede. Ipavumende ilandela izinga eliqediwe; shintsha uhlobo lwayo noma ukujula kwayo ku-**Izici**.

## Izisekelo zilandela izinga

Lapho umhlaba usemodeli, izindonga zesisekelo nezisekelo zesikhala ngaphansi kwendlu ziwulandela. Ngaseduze kwazo zonke izindonga, isisekelo sihlala ngokujula kwaso ngaphansi komhlaba ongaphandle, sinyuke ngezinyathelo ezigcwele ezingu-8" lapho umhlaba wehla (bona [Izisekelo ngaphansi kwezindonga](topic:foundations)).

## Yinikeze ku-CivilCraft

**I-LandXML ye-CivilCraft** (kumugqa we-Umhlaba, noma ku-**Izici** zomhlaba) ilanda umhlaba okhona nezinga eliqediwe njengefayela le-LandXML. Vula ku-CivilCraft ukuze uthole ukulinganisa umhlaba okuqotho, ukudonsa amanzi nemisebenzi yokumba.

## Okufanele wazi

- Umhlaba udinga imodeli ye-IFC4, njengakho konke okwakhiwa okusha.
- Amakhonthuwa abonakala ephlanini lezinga lomhlaba ngobude obusethwe ku-**Izici** zomhlaba; wonke wesihlanu uba mkhulu.
- Umhlaba ugcinwa njengento ye-IFC yomhlaba (indawo evaliwe ehlukaniswe ngezinxantathu, ukuze izigaba ziyisike); isisekelo nepavumende zigcinwa nawo. Susa umhlaba ukuze uqale kabusha — isisekelo sawo nepavumende zihamba nawo.
