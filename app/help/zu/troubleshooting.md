Imilayezo ivela emugqeni wesimo ongaphansi kwesiteji, ibe mibomvu lapho okuthile kuhlulekile. Thola umlayezo ongezansi.

## Ukuvula amafayela

**"*name* akuyona ifayela le-IFC, RVT, RFA, DWG noma DXF."**
I-BimCraft ayilibonanga ifayela. Hlola isandiso nokuthi ifayela alilingene. Ama-PDF, izithombe namafayela ehhovisi awavuleki.

**".ifczip ayisasekeli okwamanje. Gcina noma ukhiphe imodeli njenge-.ifc elula."** (futhi ku-`.ifcxml`)
Khipha i-`.ifczip` (ingumzipho ojwayelekile oqukethe i-`.ifc`) noma khipha i-IFC elula.

**"… yifayela lephrojekthi i-BimCraft engakafundi."**
Ifayela le-`.pln`, `.pla`, `.tpl` noma `.bpn`. Khipha i-IFC noma i-DWG kuwo bese uvula lokho: bona [Vula amafayela ephrojekthi .pln nama-.pla](topic:pln-project-files).

**"Le fayela le-IFC alikwazanga ukufundwa."**
Ifayela le-IFC lonakele noma akuyona i-IFC eqinile. Liyivule kuhleli wombhalo: ifayela le-IFC langempela liqala nge-`ISO-10303-21;`. Liphinde likhiphe.

**"Leli fayela alipheli: liyi… MB, kodwa uhlu lwalo lwalo lukhomba idatha okungenani… MB ngaphakathi. …"**
Iphrojekthi noma ifayela lomndeni lusikiwe lapho lukopishwa, lulayishwa noma lusatshalaliswa. Ifayela le-cloud-drive elithi "ku-inthanethi kuphela" libukeka kanjalo. Ifayela elivulwa lodwa liyavuleka, lingaphezulu kwalo lo mlayezo ngaphezulu kwekhadi nokuthi "Ikhombisa okutholakele; kopisha okuyisisekelo futhi ukuze uthole imodeli egcwele": ikhadi likhombisa kuphela izingxenye ezinayo zonke izinhlamvu zazo kuleli fayela futhi zidwebe ezisele ngaphansi kwethi **Akusekho kwifayela**. Yenza ifayela litholakale ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kwidrayivu yakho yefu, noma kopisha okuyisisekelo futhi, bese uyavula kabusha. Efolda, i-BimCraft ikhombisa isipele esisha esiphelele.

**"… Nohlu lwezinto zefayela nalo lusemgodini ongekho, ngakho akukho okungavulwa kuwo."**
Ifayela lasikwa ngakusenesikhathi kangangokuthi nohlu lwalo lwezinto alukho, ngakho akukho okungabuyiselwa kule kopi. Kopisha okuyisisekelo futhi uyivule kabusha.

**"Lo fayela le-compound alinayo i-BasicFileInfo stream, ngakho alisiyo ifayela le-Revit."**
Ifayela liwuhlobo lwe-compound lolunye uhlobo (amadokhumenti amadala ehhovisi asebenzisa isitsha esifanayo). Hlola ukuthi ukhethe ifayela elifanele.

**"Sisasebenza kowedlule…"**
I-BimCraft isasebenza ngefayela noma ngefolda yangaphambilini. Linda umugqa wesimo uqede, bese uzama futhi.

**Isikhethi sefayela sifiphaza ifayela lami.**
Isikhethi sinikeza kuphela `.ifc`, `.rvt`, `.rfa`, `.rte`, `.rft`, `.dwg` nama-`.dxf`. Hudula amanye amafayela ekhasini, bese i-BimCraft iyakutshela ukuthi ingawafunda yini.

## Ukuvula amafolda

**"Lo folda unamafayela angaphezu kuka-20,000. Khetha ifolda encane, njengephrojekthi eyodwa."**
Ukhethe ifolda ephezulu kakhulu, njengedrayivu yonke. Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi eyodwa.

**"Isiphequluli asinikezanga *name* ukufinyelela. Yikhethe futhi ngothi Vula ifolda."**
Uchofoze {{ui:Vula futhi}} kwifolda yakamuva futhi imvume yesiphequluli yenqatshwe noma iphelelwe yisikhathi. Khetha ifolda futhi, noma hudula uyingenise.

**Isiphequluli sithi izo "upload" amafayela.**
Lokho yisichazo sesiphequluli sokudlulisela amafayela ekhasini. I-BimCraft ayiwathumeli noma kuphi. Hudula ifolda uyingenise ukuze uphephe lombuzo.

**Akukho okwenzekayo kwifoni noma ku-ithebhulethi.**
Izinsiphequluli eziningi zeselula azikwazi ukukhetha amafolda. Vula amafayela ngamanye, noma sebenzisa ikhompyutha.

## Ingxenye yokuqala yenjini ye-IFC

**Umugqa wesimo uhlala ku-"Kuqalisa injini ye-IFC…", noma ukhombisa iphutha elifana no-"Failed to fetch".**
Injini ye-IFC ayikwazanga ukulandwa. Ungase ungabi ku-inthanethi, noma i-firewall noma isivimbi somkhiqizo siyivimbela.

:::offline
Awuku-inthanethi. Uma inkinobho yebha yohlelo ingathi {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}, injini ye-IFC ayikaze igcinwe kulesi siphequluli. Xhuma futhi, vule ifayela kabusha, bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} ukuze isebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngesikhathi esilandelayo. Bona [Ku-inthanethi nangaphandle](topic:online-offline).
:::

:::online
Layisha kabusha ikhasi bese uzama futhi. Kunethiwekhi yomsebenzi, cela ithimba lakho le-IT ukuthi livume amafayela `.wasm` wesayithi.
:::

## Ukulondoloza ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi

**Inkinobho ithi {{ui:Akukwazanga ukulondoloza}}.**
Amanye amafayela awazange alandwe, ngokuvamile ngoba ukuxhumana kuphuke. Hlola ukuxhumana bese uchofoza inkinobho futhi. Ikhophi yangaphambilini engaphandle kwe-inthanethi iyagcinwa kuze kube yikhophi entsha iphelele.

**Inkinobho ithi {{ui:Ayikho ingaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}.**
Leli windi alikwazi ukugcina ikhophi engaphandle kwe-inthanethi, isibonelo iwindi lokuzimela noma ikhompyutha yomsebenzi elawulwayo. Bona [Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](common:offline).

**Inkinobho ihlala ku-"Ilondoloza *n* kwe-*m*" isikhathi eside.**
Ikhophi ye-BimCraft icishe ibe ngu-11 MB, ingxenye enkulu iyinjini ye-IFC. Gcina ithebhu livulekile kuze kuthi {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}}.

**I-BimCraft ivulwe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kodwa ibukeka idlule isikhathi.**
Ikhophi yakho elondoloziwe ivela kuvakashelo lwangaphambilini. Uma uku-inthanethi, chofoza {{ui:Ilungele ukusetshenziswa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Londoloza futhi (thola inguqulo entsha kakhulu)}}.

## Ukubuka kwe-3D

**Imodeli ayisendaweni engilindele, noma imincane kakhulu.**
Cindezela {{key:F}} noma chofoza {{ui:Lwanele}}. Lwanele ihlala ifaka konke okusemodelini, okuhlanganisa nezitezi ongazisusanga nezinto ezizulazulayo ezikude nesakhiwo, ngakho isakhiwo singabonakala sincane. Sondeza kusuka lapho.

**Ukuchofoza akukhethi lutho.**
Bamba imbewu inganyakazi lapho uchofoza: ukuhudula okuncane kuzungeza. Izinto ezitezini ezingasasiwe azikwazi ukukhethwa. Uma {{ui:Izindawo}} zivuliwe, izindawo ezibonakalayo zikhethwa ngaphambi kwalokho okungaphakathi kwazo.

**Inkinobho {{ui:Imiphetho}} ibonakala imnyama: "Imiphetho ivaliwe kumamodeli amakhulu kakhulu".**
Imodeli inezinxantathu ezingaphezu kwezigidi ezingu-1.5. Imiphetho ishiyelwe ukuze kulondolozwe inkumbulo.

**Ithebhu liba lihamba kancane, lishone, noma likhombise ikhasi lokuwa lesiphequluli.**
Imodeli inkulu kakhulu enkumbulweni esiphequluli sinikeza ithebhu. Vala amanye amathebhu bese ulayisha kabusha. Khipha i-IFC encane: isakhiwo esisodwa, umkhakha owodwa noma izitezi ezimbalwa. Bona [Izinsiphequluli, amadivayisi nenkumbulo](topic:platforms).

**Ukubuka kwe-3D kuhlala kungenalutho.**
I-WebGL ingase ivalwe noma ivinjelwe. Ezilungiselelweni zesiphequluli, vula ukusheshisa kwe-hardware (noma ukusheshisa kwegrafikhi) bese uqala kabusha isiphequluli.

## Ukuhlela i-IFC

**"Sebenzisa inombolo."** Inani linani elilula. Thayipha inombolo elula ngaphandle kwamayunithi, njengo `2.4`.

**"Sebenzisa inombolo ephelele."** Inani liyisibalo noma inombolo ephelele. Susa izinhlamvu zedesimali.

**"Izici zenani elilodwa kuphela ezingahlelwa."** Uhlu, izinhlobo nezilinganiso ziyafundeka kuphela.

**{{key:Mod+S}} ivula iwindi "Londoloza ikhasi" lesiphequluli.**
Akukho okungagcinwanga, ngakho i-BimCraft ishiye ukhiye kwisiphequluli. Vala leli windi.

**{{key:Mod+Z}} akwenzi lutho.**
Lisebenza kuphela uma unezinguquko ezingagcinwanga futhi awubhali ensimini. Ngemva kokulondoloza, akukho okusalwe okungahlehliswa.

**Uhlu {{ui:Izinguquko}} lusabonisa ukuhlelwa okulahliwe lapho uvula idwebo noma ifolda.**
Uma uphendula umbuzo othi "Ubalahle?" nge-OK bese uvula okungewona imodeli ye-IFC, ukuhlelwa okudala kugcinwa enkumbulweni futhi {{ui:Londoloza njenge-IFC}} ingazigcina. Ukuqala kabusha ngempela, vula i-IFC yokuqala futhi, noma layisha ikhasi kabusha.

**Ukuhlela kwami kushintshe izinto eziningi kunalezo engizikhethile.**
Ushintshe isici esethini emaka "(uhlobo)". Izici zohlobo zisebenza kuzo zonke izinto zalolo hlobo. Chofoza {{ui:Hlehlisa}}. Bona [Shintsha isici sohlobo kuzo zonke izinto zohlobo](topic:type-vs-instance).

## Amadwebo

**"*name* akukwazanga ukufundwa. Mhlawumbe yonakele, noma igcinwe ngefomethi umfundi angakayisekeli (…)."**
Gcina idwebo kusuka kuhlelo olwenza ngalo njenge-DWG endala (2018 noma ngaphambili) noma njenge-DXF, bese uzama futhi.

**"Leli dwebo alinalutho olubonisayo."** noma **"*name* ngudwebo olungenalutho: alinayo imigqa, umbhalo noma amabhulokhi."**
Idwebo lingenalutho, noma linalokho i-BimCraft engakadwebi (bona **Akusadwetshwa** ngakwesokudla).

**"Leli dwebo likhulu kakhulu; kubonakala ingxenye yalo kuphela."**
I-BimCraft iyeka ukudweba ukuze isasabeleke. Vala izingqimba ongazidingi, noma vula amashidi odingayo kusuka kwidwebo elincane.

**Izingxenye zeshidi zingasekho.**
Imidwebo exhunyiwe ingase ingasekho: bheka ngaphansi kwe-**Imidwebo exhunyiwe** ngakwesokudla. Bona [Thola imidwebo nezithombe exhunyiwe](topic:dwg-linked-files). Izingqimba nazo zingase zivalwe: chofoza {{ui:Konke kuvuliwe}}.

**Isithombe sibonakala njengohlaka olungenalutho.**
Ifayela lakhe alitholakalanga, noma kuyi-TIFF ("TIFF ayikwazi ukuboniswa kusiphequluli"). Engeza ikhophi ye-PNG noma ye-JPEG ngo-{{ui:Engeza izithombe…}}.

**Ukuskrola kwe-trackpad yami kusondeza kunokunyakazisa idwebo.**
Yindlela imidwebo esebenza ngayo ku-BimCraft. Chofoza udonse ukuze ushelelise.
