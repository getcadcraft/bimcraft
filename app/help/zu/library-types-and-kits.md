## Okuzokwenza

Thola okudingayo emtapweni ngokuthi yini, engeza uhlobo kuphrojekthi oyidweba, qala iphrojekthi entsha kusuka ethempulethini umuntu ayibelanile, bese wabelana ngezifanekiso zakho, izinhlobo nezikhithi.

## Izinhlobo ezintathu zezinto zomtapo

- **Izimpawu** yizinkomba zokudweba: ze-2D kuphela, zidwetshwe ngosayizi wokuchaza, zihlelwe ngomkhakha.
- **Izinhlobo** zitshela ukuthi into yakhiwe ngani: izendlalelo zayo ngobukhulu bazo bangempela, izinga lokuthuthuka layo (LOD) nekhodi yokuhlelwa kwayo. Uhlobo lungezwa kuphrojekthi, akudwetshwa.
- **Izinto ezibonakalayo** zinto zangempela njengeminyango, amawindi, izimpahla, izixhobo zamapayipi nemishini, zinombono wepulani nomzimba we-3D ngosayizi wangempela.

Ku-ithebhu ethi {{ui:Library}}, sebenzisa **Uhlobo lwento** ukubonisa Konke, Izimpawu, Izinhlobo noma Izinto ezibonakalayo.

## Engeza uhlobo kuphrojekthi yakho

1. Khetha **Izinhlobo** ngaphansi kwe-**Uhlobo lwento** bese uvula enye.
2. Hlola isakhiwo sayo, i-LOD nokuhlelwa ngaphansi kwesithombe, bese ukhetha {{ui:Add to this project}}.
3. Ingezwa njengohlobo lodonga, lwephansi, lophahla noma lwesiling oyolidweba ngalo. Uma uhlobo olunegama elifanayo sekukhona, olusha lungezwa njengekhophi futhi okwakho kuhlala njengakwakunjalo. I-Undo eyodwa iyasisusa.

## Amathempulethi ephrojekthi nama-Quick Kit

Khetha {{ui:Kits}} ukubona okwabelwane ngakho abanye.

- **Ithempulethi yephrojekthi** yifayela le-IFC elinamazinga, amagridi, izinhlobo nezilungiselelo. {{ui:Save to my templates}} ilibeka ku-**Isakhiwo esisha** ngaphansi kwe-**Qala kusuka ku**.
- **I-Quick Kit** uhlu lwezinto zomtapo. {{ui:Show the kit's items}} ziveza njengamakhadi ongawabeka.
- Ikhithi ngayinye inenguqulo nelogi yokushintsha, eyokuqala kuqala. Inguqulo eqala nge-0 ilungele ukuxoxisana; 1.0.0 ilungele okwamukelwe ngumnikazi womugqa kuphela.

## Yabelana ngezakho

- **Ithempulethi yephrojekthi:** {{ui:Save as template}}, thikha {{ui:Also make a file to share in the Library}}, engeza okuqukethwe kuyo nenguqulo. Uthola ifayela le-`.json`.
- **I-Quick Kit:** ku-{{ui:Kits}}, khetha {{ui:Make a Quick Kit from this project}}. Ibala izinto zomtapo esetshenziswa yiphrojekthi.
- **Uhlobo:** ku-{{ui:Type packs}}, thikha uhlobo olulodwa bese ukhetha {{ui:Save as a Library item…}}.

Layisha ifayela ekhasini lokulayisha lomtapo. Lidlula ukubuyekezwa okufanayo njanoma yisiphi isinto, futhi ukhetha ilayisensi lapho: yonke into imemezela ilayisensi yayo. Izinto zicelwa i-LOD yazo, ikilasi le-IFC nekhodi yokuhlelwa (ikhodi, hhayi igama kuphela).

## Amanothi asensimini

Ekhasini lanoma yiyiphi into yomtapo, amalungu angenile angangeza inothi: isetshenzisiwe ephrojekthini yangempela, izanywe ephrojekthini yesampula, noma ithole inkinga. Chaza uhlobo lweprojekthi ngamagama ambalwa bese ugwema amagama amakhasimende. Inothi lisobala futhi aliyona ukubuyekezwa: alishintshi into.

## Okuwusizo ukukwazi

- Akukho okuthunyelwa ohlelweni. Amafayela akhiqizwa ekhompyutheni yakho futhi wena uwalayisha ngokwakho.
- Ikhithi eyenziwe kusuka ethempulethini ithwala umbhalo we-IFC njengoba wagcinwa. Hlola ukuthi awukho okuyimfihlo ngaphambi kokulayisha.
- I-CadCraft ibonisa izimpawu namazinto abonakalayo ku-ithebhu yayo yomtapo; izinhlobo nezithempulethi zamaphrojekthi e-BimCraft kuphela.
