## Okuzokwenza

Vula imodeli ye-IFC, uhambe kuyo nge-3D bese ufinyelela izinhlobo zokubuka ezijwayelekile. Cishe imizuzu emihlanu.

Udinga ifayela le-`.ifc`. Iningi lezinhlelo ze-BIM liyakhipha elilodwa; ku-Revit kungu-**File > Export > IFC**. Bheka [Thola ifayela lephrojekthi libe ku-3D nge-IFC](topic:rvt-to-ifc).

## Izinyathelo

1. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha ifayela}} (noma indawo yokuwisa engenhla kwesobunxele).
2. Khetha ifayela lakho le-`.ifc` bese uqinisekisa.
3. Linda umugqa wesimo uqede. Ubonisa "Iqalisa injini ye-IFC…" okokuqala, bese "Ifunda imodeli…" no-"Yakha ijometri: … kwezingu-… zezinto".
4. Bheka imodeli: ivulwa ngombono wamaqhwa amathathu, ilingane nesikrini.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Phezulu}} ethuluzini phezu komodeli ukuze ubheke phansi ngqo.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Phambili}} ukubheka phambili, bese {{ui:Uhlangothi}} ukubheka ohlangothini.
7. Chofoza {{ui:Lwanele}}, noma cindezela {{key:F}}, ukuze ubuyele ekubukeni kwamaqhwa amathathu kwimodeli yonke.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Imodeli phakathi, nemigqa emnyama emincane ukuze kubonakale njengomdwebo.
- Ipaneli ye-{{ui:Ifayela}} ngakwesobunxele: igama lefayela, usayizi, uhlelo lwe-IFC (njengesibonelo IFC2X3 noma IFC4), igama lephrojekthi, isigaba, uhlelo olukhishwe kulo, umbhali, usuku lokukhishwa, nenani lezinto nonxantathu.
- Izinkomba ze-{{ui:Izitezi}} ne-{{ui:Izinto}} ngaphansi kwayo.
- Umugqa wesimo: "Ivulwe *igama lefayela*: *n* izinto ngo-*s* s".

## Ukuhamba

:::mouse
| Ukuzungeza | Hudula ngenkinobho yokhiye wokunxele |
| Shelelisa | Hudula ngenkinobho yokhiye wokudla, noma bamba {{key:Shift}} (noma {{key:Mod}}) bese uhudula ngokhiye wokunxele |
| Sondeza | Skrola ngevili, noma hudula ngenkinobho ephakathi |
| Bona konke | Cindezela {{key:F}} noma chofoza {{ui:Lwanele}} |
:::

:::mac mouse
Ku-trackpad: hudula ngomunwe owodwa ucindezelwe ukuze uzungeze; bamba {{key:Shift}} noma {{key:Mod}} bese uhudula ukuze ushelelise; skrolela ngeminwe emibili noma ukhuhlise ukusondeza. Ukuchofoza ngeminwe emibili kuyisichofozo sokudla, futhi ungahudula nakho ukuze ushelelise, kodwa {{key:Shift}} nokuhudula kulula kakhulu.
:::

:::touch
| Ukuzungeza | Hudula ngomunwe owodwa |
| Sondeza | Khuhlisa ngeminwe emibili |
| Shelelisa | Hambisa iminwe emibili ndawonye |
| Bona konke | Thepha {{ui:Lwanele}} |
:::

Ukuzungeza kuhlala kuzungeza indawo ephakathi kwemodeli. Ukushelelisa kuyishintsha leyo ndawo, ngakho ngemva kokushelelisa isikhathi eside ukuzungeza kungase kuzwakale kungalingani: chofoza {{ui:Lwanele}} ukuyisetha kabusha.

## Eshadiwe, nemiphetho noma uhlaka lwemigqa

Izinkinobho ezintathu ezincane ngaphezu kwesikhwama sombono zikhetha indlela imodeli edwetshwa ngayo: {{ui:Okushadiwe}}, {{ui:Okushadiwe nemiphetho}} (okuzenzakalelayo) no-{{ui:Uhlaka lwemigqa}}. Zikwa imiyalo nazo (kumenyu yombono nasohlwini lokwenza ngokwakho), ngakho zingaba nezikhiye zazo. {{ui:Ipulani}}, {{ui:Hlukanisa}} no-{{ui:3D}} zisethulalini {{ui:Ukubuka}} sethuluzi.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Ukulindela imibono enamagama.** I-BimCraft inemibono emine esetshiwe ({{ui:Lwanele}}, {{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Uhlangothi}}), ikhyubhu yombono ekhoneni eliphansi kwesokudla nokuzungeza okukhululekile. Ayigcini imibono.
- **Ukufuna amapulani nezigaba.** Akukho mapulani angu-2D noma amabhokisi esigaba kulolu hlelo. Sika imodeli ngendiza eyodwa ye-{{ui:Isigaba}} ([Sika imodeli ye-IFC ngendiza yesigaba](topic:section-plane)), noma vala izitezi: [Bonisa nofihle izitezi, izindawo nemiphetho](topic:storeys-and-spaces).
- **Ukuvula ifayela lephrojekthi ulindele i-3D.** Ifayela lephrojekthi livula njengekhadi lefayela. Khipha i-IFC kuqala.
- **Ukuvula i-`.ifczip`.** Hlobosha, noma khipha `.ifc` ngokuvamile.
- **Imodeli enkulu yenza ithebhu ibe nzima noma iwe.** Bheka [Iziphequluli, amadivayisi nenkumbulo](topic:platforms).
