## Okuzokwenza

Rekhoda inkinga: yimaphi amashidi aphumile, ngusuku luni, ngenjongo yani, ngesiguquko ngasinye. Kamuva, buza okushintshile kusukela kuleyo nkinga. Gcina imiyalelo yesayithi nemidwebo yomkhiqizi ebuyiswe ngayo ngenkinga.

## Izinyathelo

1. Ku-panel yesobunxele vula **Izinkinga nezinguquko**.
2. Khetha **Rekhoda inkinga**. Isiguquko siphakanyiswa (A, bese B; okunombolo efana no-P1 kukhuphuka). Khetha inhloso yokukhishwa, ukuthi ithunyelwa kubani, usuku namashidi. Uma isigaba sephrojekthi sisethiwe, inhloso ilandela sona. Uma iphakheji lemvume linohlu lwemibhalo esekelayo, amashidi axhunyiwe kuleso hlu ayatshekwa ngokuzenzakalelayo.
3. Shiya **Futhi londoloza ikhophi yemodeli njengoba ikhishiwe** itshekiwe ukuze imodeli ihlale njengoba iphumile. Ikhophi iyalandwa; igama, usayizi kanye ne-SHA-256 yayo kugcinwa nenkinga.
4. Khetha **Rekhoda le nkinga**. Ishidi elishintshile kusukela ekukhishweni kwalo kokugcina lithola isiguquko esisha. Ishidi elingashintshanga ligcina isiguquko lalo. Ibhulokhi yesihloko yeshidi ngalinye iveza isiguquko salo.
5. Ukuze ubone okushintshile, khetha **Izinguquko kusukela** eduze kwenkinga. Ishidi ngalinye lithi Ifanayo noma Ishintshile, kanye nezakhi ezengeziwe, eziguqukile nezisusiwe kulo. Chofoza inani ukuze ukhethe lezo zakhi.
6. Khetha **Engeza irekhodi** ngemiyalelo yesayithi, umdwebo womkhiqizi, isethi yokumaka, imodeli exhunyiwe noma inothi. Khetha inkinga okuyo. Ifayela oliKhethayo lirekhodwa ngegama, usayizi kanye ne-SHA-256; ifayela lihlala lapho lisekhona.
7. **Logi yokushintsha komdwebo** ikhombisa umugqa oyedwa ngeshidi ngalinye nekholomu eyodwa ngenkinga ngayinye. **Landa logi (CSV)** iyigcina ngezinkinga namarekhodi.
8. **Gcina umlando wezinkinga** ibhala ifayela le-`.issues.json`. I-MarkupCraft, i-TakeoffCraft ne-DetailCraft ziyalifunda nge-**Funda umlando wenkinga**.

## Okuwusizo ukukwazi

- Umlando ugcinwa ngaphakathi kwefayela le-IFC (ipropathi set `CadCraft_Issues` kwiphrojekthi). Ezinye izinhlelo ziyawunaka.
- Ishidi libalwa njengeshintshile uma ukusethwa kwalo kushintshile, noma uma into ebonakala kulo ishintshile: ipulani ibonisa izinga layo, umbono ongaphezulu noma isigaba ibonisa imodeli yonke, ishejuli yomnyango noma yewindi ibonisa iminyango noma amawindi ayo.
- Isakhi sibalwa njengeshintshile uma ipropathi, indawo yaso, usayizi noma isimo sashintsha.
- Ukuze ubone ngqo ipropathi eshintshile, vula ikhophi egciniwe yemodeli ekhishiwe nge-**Qhathanisa izinguqulo**.
- Ukususa inkinga kugcina amarekhodi ayo, kodwa ngaphandle kwenkinga. Isinyathelo ngasinye yi-Undo eyodwa.
- Imodeli enezakhi ezingaphezu kuka-60,000 ayigcini irekhodi lalo lonke elakhi ngalinye nenkinga. Amashidi asaqhathaniswa.

## Akukakwakhiwa

- Amashidi awagcinwa njenge-PDF nenkinga.
- Igu lokushintsha alidwetshwa emashidini e-BimCraft. Rekhoda amagu ku-MarkupCraft ngokumelene nenkinga.
