## Okuzokwenza

Thola ukuthi indawo yakho idinga ni, bese wenza i-BimCraft ikusebenzisele lokho: izinyathelo zokuthola imvume yokwakha, okufanele unikeze nefomethi yakho, indlela amadwebo okufanele abukeke ngayo, namanothi ajwayelekile okufanele afakwe kuwo. Iphakheji eyodwa ibamba lokho, eduze kwemithetho yokwakha, futhi uyikhetha kanye.

Iphakheji iyisiqondisi. Umphathi wokuvuma nengcweti ebhalisiwe banquma okudingekayo. Iphakheji ngayinye ichaza ukuthi ingathenjwa kangakanani: **akuhlolwanga** (akukho muntu owayiqinisekisile), **uhlaka** (ibhalwe kusuka emakhasini ashicilelwe, kodwa ingakabhekwa ngongcweti) noma **ibuyekeziwe**. Iphakheji yokufundisa eyenziwe ngamanga ibhala MADE-UP kukho konke.

## Izinyathelo

1. Engeza i-add-on ethi Building regulations bese uvula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:Imithetho yokwakha}}.
2. Thikha iphakheji yendawo yakho. Yivule: {{ui:Iphinde iqukethe}} ohlwini lokukhona.
3. Ephakhejini yokuvuma khetha uhlu lwayo: izinyathelo, ifomethi namadwebo okufanele unikeze avela. Bheka [Iphakheji yokuvuma](topic:permit-package).
4. Esheethini, {{ui:Amanothi ajwayelekile}} ifaka amanothi ephakheji. Bheka [Amashidi](topic:sheets).
5. Ku-{{ui:Izinkomba zosayizi}}, {{ui:Sebenzisa usayizi wephakheji yemithetho}} uthatha ukuphakama kombhalo ephakhejini. Bheka [Dweba ngosayizi ogcwele, uchaze ngesikali](topic:annotation-sizes).

## Okungabanjwa yiphakheji yemithetho

- **Inqubo yokuvunywa:** izinyathelo ngokulandelana, ubani owenza ngayinye, okufakwayo, nemibhalo. Imali noma isikhathi sokuqeda kuvela kuphela nekhasi elafundwa kulo nosuku lokufunda.
- **Ifomethi yokuthumela:** usayizi weshidi, ukuma kwalo, amafomethi amafayela, izikhombisa, amagama amafayela, izinkundla zebhulokhi lesihloko, namadwebo (nezikali) namaphepha okuthunyelwa kudingeka. Into engekho ilokhu ibhalwa ku-Problems, hhayi ukuyima.
- **Izivumelwano zokudweba:** ukuphakama kombhalo ephepheni, ukuminyana komugqa, ithebhu yamapeni, namarekhodi afana nophawu lwasenyakatho noma ibha yesikali. Ezinye zihlolwa njengemithetho.
- **Amanothi ajwayelekile:** amanothi ngokwezifundo, ngamagama ephakheji uqobo, ngalinye nesigatshana sawo.

## Okwaziwayo okuhle

- Iphakheji yedolobha yakha phezu kweyezwe lalo: izinyathelo, amadwebo namanothi ayo azinqoba, kanti eyezwe lihlala.
- Lokho okungafundwanga kwikhasi elisemthethweni kumakwa njengesampula noma okungaqinisekisiwe ephakhejini nasendaweni lapho kubonakala khona.
- Ungabhala iphakheji yendawo yakho bese uyingeza ku-{{ui:Layisha ifayela lemithetho}}; ihlala ikhonjwa njengeyakho, hhayi njengeyabuyekeziwe.
