## Umndeni ngokuwukuthi

Umndeni uhlobo olulodwa lwento olukhona ngosayizi abaningi: umnyango owodwa ovulekayo ofika ububanzi be-32, 36 no-42 amabhodlela, noma itafula elifika lide nelifushane. Ku-BimCraft ubona umndeni kanye, nosayizi bawo ngaphansi kwawo, esikhundleni sohlu olude lwezinhlobo.

I-IFC alinayo imindeni. Kwifayela ngayinye usayizi uyisinhlobo esijwayelekile (uhlobo lomnyango, uhlobo lwewindi, uhlobo lwempahla…), futhi umndeni uyiqembu elidwebela osayizi balo. Ezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM zifunda izinhlobo neqembu njengoba zinjalo; i-BimCraft ibuyisela umndeni kuzo.

## Lapho uwathola khona

- Isilawuli sephrojekthi, iqembu le-**Families**, sidwebela imindeni kulo modeli, nokuthi ngamuva ngamunye unosayizi abangaki nokuthi bangaki asetshenzisiwe. Ngaphansi kwazo kukhona i-**Starter families**: imisebenzi yomnyango newindi kumathuluzi omnyango newindi, nethebula, iyunithi yeshelufu, nekhabhinethi esezansi.
- {{ui:Build}} › {{ui:Families…}} ibonisa uhlu olufanayo ewindini.
- Chofoza umndeni ukuze uwuvule: osayizi bawo, izinkomba zosayizi ngamunye, nokuthi zingaki izinto ezisebenzisa ngayinye.

## Iminyango namawindi

Amathuluzi omnyango newindi adwebela imindeni yalolo hlobo, bese kuseduze kwawo kukhona **uhlu lwosayizi**. Khetha usayizi, noma ubhale **W** no-**H**: ububanzi nobude obungekho kosayizi bomndeni obuba usayizi omusha womndeni lapho umnyango umiswa. Iminyango yosayizi ofanayo yabelana ngohlobo olulodwa.

Ku-{{ui:Properties}}, umnyango noma iwindi okhethiwe lubonisa **Umndeni** kanye **Usayizi** wawo. Khetha olunye usayizi oluvela ohlwini, noma ubhale ububanzi noma ubude obusha. {{ui:Open the family}} ivula umndeni walo.

Uhlaka lomnyango noma lewindi lulandela ukuqina kodonga lwalo, njengakuqala kwemindeni. Uhlobo losayizi lugcina nomnyango odwetshwe odongeni olungu-140 mm, ukuze izinhlelo ezifunda izinhlobo zikwazi.

## Usayizi omusha kulo mndeni

1. Vula umndeni.
2. Ngaphansi kwe-**New size from this family**, bhala izinkomba zawo (Width, Height nezinye zamapharamitha womndeni) futhi, uma uthanda, igama. Uma ungawunikezi igama, lizoqanjwa ngokwezinkomba zalo.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Make the size}}.

Usayizi ababili abangabelana ngezinkomba ezifanayo. **Qamba kabusha** ishintsha igama losayizi; **Susa** isusa usayizi ongasetshenziswa muntu; **Khetha** ikhetha izinto zalowo sayizi.

## Faka impahla

1. {{ui:Build}} › {{ui:Place family}}, noma {{ui:Place}} ewindini lomndeni.
2. Ebhalweni, khetha umndeni nosayizi wawo.
3. Chofoza kupulani ukuze uyibeke esitezini. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuze uyiphendule ngama-90° ngaphambi kokuchofoza. {{key:Esc}} iyayima.

I-{{ui:Properties}} yendawo emisiwe ibonisa usayizi wayo, lapho ikhona nokuthi iphendulwe kangakanani; {{key:Delete}} iyisusa. Yonke into yosayizi ofanayo yabelana ngesimo salowo sayizi, ngakho ukushintsha umndeni kushintsha zonke.

## Usayizi kusuka ethebhulini

{{ui:Sizes from a table…}} ewindini lomndeni ifunda ifayela le-CSV (ikhoma, isemikholoni noma i-tab). Umugqa wokuqala uqamba amakholomu: igama noma ilebula lephrameter, nokukhetha `Name`. Ubude buyamamilimitha uma ungabhali iyunithi (`1.2 m`, `36 in`); amakholomu e-Yes / No amukela yes, no, true, false, 1 noma 0, nephrameter engenaKholomu igcina okuzenzakalelayo kwayo. Umugqa ngamunye uba usayizi, ngesinyathelo esisodwa osingabuyiselwa emuva; usayizi osekhona uyadlulwa. Umugqa owodwa ongafundeki, noma umndeni ongawudweba, uyala ithebhu yonke bese ubonisa umugqa wawo. Okungenani imigqa engu-200 nobukhulu obungu-1 MB. {{ui:Save the sizes as a table}} ibhala ithebhu efanayo ibuyele ngaphandle.

## Setha kusinto ngayinye

Iphrameter emaka ngokuthi **Ngesinto ngasinye** ohlelweni (i-Yes / No yengeza i-transom, ilebula, impahla) akuyona ingxenye yosayizi: iminyango ababili yosayizi ofanayo ingahluka. Khetha into bese i-**Izici** ibonisa lezi zamapharamitha; shintsha enye bese leyo nto idwetshwa kabusha ngesimo sayo (impahla nezinto ezilungiselelwe zithola isimo sazo, iminyango namawindi yenziwa kabusha odongeni lwazo). Zibuyisele zonke ezizenzakalelayo, ibuyela esimweni sosayizi wayo.

## Izinga lemininingwane

{{ui:Build}} › {{ui:Families…}} › **Izinga lemininingwane** isetha, kumndeni ngamunye emodelini, okugcinwa ezinhlanganweni nokudwetshwa ezimbonakalweni zepulani: **Fine** igcina konke, **Medium** ne-**Coarse** zisusa izingxenye ezihlelwe njengezinhle kakhulu (amafayela amancane, ukubuka okusheshayo). **Automatic** igcina konke futhi ivumela ukubuka kweshidi ukukhetha ngesikali sayo: u-1:50 nokukhulu fine, u-1:100 medium, u-1:200 nokuncane coarse. Isinyathelo esisodwa ongasihlehlisa.

## Phinda umndeni

Akudingeki uqale ngaphandle kwalutho. {{ui:Duplicate}} ikopisha umndeni namapharamitha, izinhlanganyelo nosayizi ngaphansi kwegama elisha. Shintsha ikhophi kumhleli; umndeni wangempela uhlala njengoba ubunjalo.

## Amafayela emindeni

- {{ui:Save the family file}} igcina i-`.ccfamily.json`: amapharamitha womndeni, izinhlanganyelo nosayizi. Yivule kwenye imodeli nge-{{ui:Open a family file…}} eqenjini le-Families.
- {{ui:Save as IFC}} igcina umndeni njengefayela elincane le-IFC: izinhlobo zosayizi bawo neqembu lawo, okumenyezelwa yithala lephrojekthi le-IFC. Ezinye izinhlelo ziyavula njengesethi yezinhlobo; i-BimCraft iyavula njengomndeni futhi.

Ifayela lomndeni elivulwe lapha lifundwa njengedatha bese lihlolwa ngaphambi kokubhalwa noma yini. Umndeni onokhiye osekhona kumodeli ufika njengomndeni ohlukile. Umndeni oqukethe abanye (bheka Families inside, ku-usizo lomhleli) uletha wona: ifayela libala wona, nemodeli igcina abasekhona.

## Imindeni ku-Library

Vula {{ui:Families…}} bese ukhetha {{ui:From the Library…}} ukuze ubone imindeni eyabelwana ku-Library. {{ui:Add to this project}} iyifunda njengefayela lomndeni: i-BimCraft ihlola yonke insimu ngaphambi kokuthi yenze noma yini, umndeni ufika njengomndeni wawo, bese uvuleka kumhleli. Ukuyengeza kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa. Ukwabelana ngowakho, sebenzisa {{ui:Save all as a library}} bese uthumela ifayela ku-Library (abasebenzi be-Library bashicilela ifayela lomndeni ngomyalo, futhi ngamunye umndeni uyinto yawo nosayizi bawo). Uma i-Library ingafinyeleleki, uhlu luyashoyo futhi akukho okushintsha.

## Izinhlobo zezinye izinhlelo

Izinhlobo ezenziwe yizinye izinhlelo nazo zikhombisa eqenjini le-Families, zihlanganiswe ngokuqagela: ngengxenye yegama lezinto zazo ngaphambi kolunye usizo, noma ngamagama ezinhlobo ahlukahlukana kuphela ngezinombolo zazo. Iwindi lomndeni lisho ukuthi liqagele ngani. Ziyadwetshwa futhi ungakhetha izinto zazo, kodwa azihleliwe lapha: azinayo iresiphi yomndeni.

:::note
Iminyango namawindi okuqala adwetshwa ngokuphelele njengangaphambi kwemindeni. Amafayela akhiwe ngaphambi kwemindeni avula ngokungashintshi: iminyango namawindi awo abonakala ngaphansi komndeni wawo ngosayizi asetshenzisiwe, bese anyakazela aye kusayizi wawo ngokwawo lapho ushintsha okokuqala.
:::
