## Okuzokwenza

I-PCF ingumbhalo wombhalo owufunda futhi ubhale ohlelweni lwesitres lwepayipi namaisometric. Uhlu ngalinye lwezingxenye zomugqa wepayipi ngamaphoyinti alo okuphela nobubanzi balo.

## Hambisa

1. Khetha ithuluzi le-Pipe. Umugqa wezinketho ubonisa **Hambisa i-PCF**.
2. Chofoza kulo. Konke umugqa wepayipi kumodeli uyabhalwa: ipayipi eliqondile, ama-elbow (ukujika okungu-90 degrees, kungenjalo i-bend), ama-tee nephoyinti lawo legatsha, ama-reducer namavalvu.
3. Ifayela lisebenzisa amamillimitha. Ama-bore ngobubanzi obubizwa ngamamillimitha. Iphoyinti elimaphakathi le-elbow yikhoni lalo, lapho amagatsha awo amabili ahlangana khona.

I-PCF ibhalelwa ipayipi lensimbi lebutt-weld. Umugqa wempahla ehlukile nawo uyabhalwa, ngamakhodi wezingxenye zensimbi, futhi uyaphawulwa kwiphaneli Yezinkinga.

## Ngenisa

1. Khetha ithuluzi le-Pipe bese uchofoza **Ngenisa i-PCF…**, bese ukhetha ifayela.
2. Ipayipi eliqondile kuya kuma-elbow, ama-bend, amavalvu kanye namaflange liba umugqa owodwa esigabeni samanje. Ushintsho losayizi luqala umugqa omusha. Igatsha le-tee liba umugqa walo siqu oqala ephoyinteni le-tee.
3. Imodeli yakha izixhumi zayo. Ama-reducer namatee awakopishwa: avela lapho imigqa yosayizi abahlukile, noma igatsha, lihlangana khona.

:::note
Amaphakama efayeleni afundwa njengamaphakama ngaphezu kwesigaba samanje. Imithwalo, izibambi kanye nokuhlaziya kwesitres ngokwako akusefayeleni le-PCF futhi akufiki.
:::

## Hlola okufikile

- Ubude bonke bepayipi ngomugqa ngamunye kufanele bufane nomthombo.
- Iphaneli Yezinkinga ibhala, kanye kanye ngayinye: i-elbow ephoyinti lawo lesiqu elingafani ngobude kusuka kukho kokubili okuphela, ipayipi elingenabude, nebore elingeyona usayizi ojwayelekile.

## Ipayipi elisesifakiwe ku-faela ye-IFC

Uma ifayela le-IFC livuliwe, **Hambisa i-PCF** futhi ibhala izingxenye zalo zepayipi, izixhumi namavalvu, eceleni kwemigqa oyidwetshile. Ingxenye ithola ukuphela kwayo kumaphothi ayo, noma kumaphethelo omzimba wayo. Ingxenye engenayo amaphothi iyashiywa ngaphandle futhi iphaneli Yezinkinga iyasho lokho. Ungahambisa kusuka ku-IFC kuphela uma imodeli ingenayo imigqa edwetshiwe.

Lapho ungenisa, ama-supports, ama-olet, ama-flange, ama-gasket kanye nama-cap kufayela akudwetshwa. Iphaneli Yezinkinga ibala ukuthi kwashiywa kangaki kwngayinye, ukuze lutho lunganyamalali ngokungazelelwe.
