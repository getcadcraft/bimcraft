{{ui:VR}} kwithuluzi lokusebenza lapho uzothola ukuthi ungena kumodeli nge-VR headset, ngobukhulu obuphelele. Umi lapho kamera yombono we-3D ikhona, phansi kwayo, ubheke ohlangothini olufanayo. Ibuka okubonisa imbono ye-3D: izinga, izinto kanye namamodeli afihliwe akusalokothi avele, nezinto ezipendiwe ziyaphinde zifike. Akukho lutho oluthunyelwayo: isiphequluli sidweba lokhu kulesi sikhombisi (noma kusiphequluli se-headset).

## Okudingekayo

- I-headset esiphequluli singayilawula (WebXR): isiphequluli se-headset eyimeleyo, noma i-Chrome noma i-Edge kwi-PC enohlobo oluxhunyiwe ku-headset.
- Imodeli ye-IFC evuliwe, kwikhasi eliphephile (https), njengoba kunjalo kule sayithi.

Uma kungekho headset, {{ui:VR}} ayisebenzi futhi iyakutshela ukuthi kungani.

## Qala

1. Lungiselela imbono ye-3D lapho ofuna ukuqala khona: egumbini, noma ngaphandle ubheka isakhiwo.
2. Chofoza {{ui:VR}} kwithuluzi lokusebenza.
3. Gqoka i-headset.

Imenyu isenhlanganweni yangasekhohlo. Yibheke ngesilawuli sangakwesokunxele bese udonsa isiqalo ukukhetha inkinobho. (Uma kuyisandla esisodwa kuphela, imenyu iyaphaphama phambi kwendawo owaqala kuyo.)

## Hamba uzungeze

- **Dlula:** ku-{{ui:Move}}, bheka phansi bese udonsa isiqalo; uzofika lapho. Noma sunda induku phambili ukuze ulawule, bese uyiyeka ukuze udlule. Ungafika kuphansi, kumashaftha, nasemhlabeni kuphela (izindawo ezibheke phezulu), hhayi kudonga.
- **Phendukela:** sunda induku ngakwesokunxele noma ngakwesokudla: uphendukela ngama-30° njalo lapho.
- **Hamba:** endaweni yakho yokudlala, hamba nje.

## Linganisa

Khetha {{ui:Measure}}, bese udonsa isiqalo kumaphuzu amabili. Ibanga liboniswa phakathi kwawo, ngamamitha. Iphuzu eliseduze nekhona liyabambelela ekhoneni.

## Yisike uyivule

Khetha {{ui:Section}} bese udonsa isiqalo endaweni:

- kudonga (noma kunoma yini engayisilo phansi): konke okuphakathi kwakho nalendawo kuyasikwa, ukuze ubone ngaphakathi. Lokhu kusebenza kangcono uma ubheka ngaphandle, noma ngaphesheya kwegumbi;
- phezu kwesilele, usuka phezulu: ipulani esikwe ngama-1.2 m ngaphezu kwalesi silele, ukuze izindonga zibonise ingxenye yazo ephansi.

{{ui:Clear}} isusa ukusika kanye nemilinganiso. Uma i-[section plane](topic:section-plane) isika imodeli lapho uqala, i-headset iqala nayo.

## Beka inkinga

Khetha {{ui:Issue}} bese udonsa isiqalo lapho kukhona inkinga. Ukhonkolo onenombolo uyibonisa, inkinga ifika kuthebhu ye-{{ui:Issues}} njengesihloko se-BCF, nesithombe sakho ekubonile kanye nekhamera lapho wayemi khona, ezixhumanisweni zemodeli ngokwayo. Zigcine kanye nezinye [izinkinga](topic:issues-bcf) zakho njengefayela le-BCF; ezinye izinsiza zokuhlela ziyovula zibonise lo mbono ofanayo.

Engeza ikhomenti enkingeni ngemva kokuphuma ku-VR, kuthebhu ye-Issues: ukubhala ku-headset akukakwakhiwa.

## Yibone njengemodeli etafuleni

{{ui:Table top}} yenza imodeli ilingane etafuleni phambi kwakho; zungeza kuyo, noma uyiphendule ngenduku. {{ui:Full size}} ikubuyisela lapho wawukhona khona.

## Nabanye, ngesikhathi esiphilayo

Ku-[live session](topic:work-live), wonke umuntu ku-VR ubona abanye njengekhanda nezandla ezimbili ngombala wabo, negama labo, nedonsa lapho ngalinye libheka khona. Izinguquko zanoma ubani zifika ku-headset yakho ngokushesha. Abantu edeskini abakabonakali ku-headset okwamanje.

## Phuma

Khetha {{ui:Leave VR}} (nomaususe i-headset uphele ngaleyo ndlela). Imbono ye-3D esikrinini ihamba iye lapho ikhanda lakho lase likhona.

:::note
Kuhlolwe nge-headset emulator, hhayi kuzo zonke izinhlobo ze-headset okwamanje. Imodeli enkulu ingenza i-headset ibe nemvamisa: fihla izitezi noma amamodeli ongawadingi kuqala ([hide and isolate](topic:hide-and-isolate)).
:::
