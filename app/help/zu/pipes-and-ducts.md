## Okuzokwenza

Dweba amapayipi namapayipi omoya esakhiweni njengoba udweba izindonga: chofoza amakhoni emgqeni wasembindini ngobude obusethiwe. I-BimCraft yenza izixhumi, ibeka ingxenye ngayinye esistimini bese iyixhuma, ngakho ezinye izinhlelo zifunda umugqa njengesistimu eyodwa exhunyiwe.

## Dweba ipayipi

1. Vula {{ui:Dweba}}, vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Ipayipi}}.
2. Ebhareni, khetha **isistimu** (amanzi abandayo, amanzi ashisayo, udaka lwezempilo, igesi…). Iyasetha impahla evamile: ethusi lamanzi, i-PVC yodaka nemimoya, insimbi yokushisa, amanzi apholile, igesi kanye nokuvikela umlilo. Shintsha **impahla**, **ikilasi** kanye **nosayizi** uma udinga. Osayizi nodonga bavela ezindinganisweni zomphakathi: ipayipi lensimbi ASME B36.10M, ithubhu lethusi ASTM B88, i-PVC ASTM D1785 kanye ne-PEX ASTM F876. Amaphrojekthi aseBrazil angasebenzisa **ipayipi lamanzi e-PVC (NBR 5648)**, i-PVC ebotshelwa ngesolvent yamanzi abandayo, kanye **nepayipi lodaka le-PVC (NBR 5688)**, i-série normal noma reforçada, lezodaka nemimoya: osayizi babo babizwa ngo-DN (DN 25, DN 100). Uma usekhethe enye yazo, ukukhetha esinye isistimu kugcina amapayipi aseBrazil (ipayipi lodaka le-PVC lodaka lwezempilo ngemuva kwepayipi lamanzi e-PVC).
3. Setha **At**: ukuphakama komugqa wepayipi ngaphezu kwesitezi.
4. Chofoza lapho ipayipi liqala, bese kwiKhoni ngalinye. Chofoza kabili, noma cindezela {{key:Enter}}, endaweni yokugcina. {{key:Backspace}} ibuyisela ikhoni elidlule; {{key:Esc}} ilahla umugqa.

Ipayipi liyambambelela njengezindonga, nakwezinye imigqa: qala ekugcineni kwepayipi ukuze uqhubeke kulo, noma uphele emgqeni wepayipi ukuze uhlukanise. Umugqa omusha uthatha ukuphakama kwepayipi ambambelela kulo.

Umugqa oqala noma ophele ephothini lensiza uthatha usayizi nesistimu yalo futhi uhlala ubambelelwe kulo: bona [Izinsiza ze-MEP ezinamaphothi](topic:mep-equipment) kanye [Xhuma amapayipi namapayipi omoya kwezinsiza](topic:mep-connections).

## Setha inqubomgomo yepulumbing yephrojekthi

Uhlu lokuqala ebhareni lepayipi yi-**inqubomgomo yepulumbing** yemodeli: **US (IPC, inches)** noma **Brazil (ABNT, DN)**. Igcinwa kumodeli, ngakho wonke umuntu ovula ifayela uqala imigqa ngendlela efanayo. Ngaphansi kwe-**US**, umugqa omusha uqala ethusini (amanzi) noma i-PVC Schedule 40 (udaka), ngamayintshi, nokuhlamuka kwe-International Plumbing Code. Ngaphansi kwe-**Brazil**, amanzi abandayo aqala njenge-PVC soldável DN 25, udaka lwezempilo nelwemvula kanye nemimoya njenge-PVC esgoto DN 100 nokuhlamuka kwe-NBR 8160, namanzi ashisayo ethusini. Iphinde isebenze nakumugqa oqala ku-connector yesisetshenziswa noma ephothini lensiza. Amapayipi asevele edwetshiwe agcina impahla yawo nezithebula zawo; uhlu lwezimpahla luyahlala luvuliwe, ngakho ungadweba omunye umugqa waseBrazil kumodeli yase-US ngamabomu. Ifayela elingenakukhetho liziphatha njengangaphambili: umugqa omusha ulandela impahla esetshenziswayo.

## Dweba idaktho

1. Khetha {{ui:Idaktho}}. Khetha isistimu (ukunikeza, ukubuya, ukukhipha noma umoya wangaphandle) kanye nesimo: sizungezile, sinekhona noma sinemifantu eyisicaba.
2. Thayipha ububanzi (ububanzi bedaktho ozungezile buyidayamitha yalo) kanye **At** yokuphakama komugqa wasembindini.
3. Ukuze uyisayize ngokwomoya, thayipha **Airflow** kanye nezinga le-**Friction** bese uchofoza {{ui:Linganisa usayizi}}. Idaktho isayizwa ngokulingana kokuhwaqeka, indlela esencwadini yedizayini yedaktho ye-ASHRAE Handbook, futhi ijikelezwa ibe usayizi otholakalayo, ngakho ukuhwaqeka kuphuma kulo noma ngaphansi kwalelo zinga oliithaye. Uma kungathayiwe zinga, kusebenzisa i-0.08 in. of water ngamafidi ayi-100 (0.8 Pa/m). Idaktho enekhona ligcina ukuphakama oluthayiwe futhi lithola ububanzi; i-BimCraft iyatshela uma izinhlangothi zalo ziyi-4 kuya ku-1.
4. Chofoza amakhoni njengakwepayipi.

## Izisetshenziswa zepulumbing

{{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} › {{ui:Faka umndeni}} unikeza izisetshenziswa ezinhlanu zokuqala, ngayinye nedrenishi nokuxhumana kwamanzi abandayo: i-**Water closet**, i-**Lavatory**, i-**Kitchen sink**, i-**Shower tray** kanye ne-**Laundry tub**. Ubuso bazo bubheke wena ngenkathi ubeka, nemuva lazo lishaywe odongeni. Udrenishi wazo unosayizi omncane kakhulu wedrenishi yesisetshenziswa ye-NBR 8160 (DN 100 ye-water closet, DN 50 ye-kitchen sink, DN 40 kwezinye); amanzi ngu-DN 20. Shintsha osayizi bazo, noma uzihlele njengomndeni noma yiluphi: bona [Imindeni nosayizi bayo](topic:families).

Umndeni utshela ukuthi yisiphi isisetshenziswa ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Isisetshenziswa samanzi}} ethebhini le-{{ui:Izixhumi}} kumhleli womndeni. Udrenishi wayo ke ubalwa ngamayunithi wesisetshenziswa: 6 UHC ye-water closet, 1 ye-lavatory, 3 ye-kitchen sink noma i-laundry tub, 2 ye-shower (NBR 8160), noma amayunithi edrenishi e-IPC Table 709.1.

## Kusukela kuconnector yesisetshenziswa

Izinto ezibekwe kusuka emndenini onama-connector (udrenishi nethephu yebhesini, nentamo yesiphephisi somoya: bona [Hlela umndeni](topic:family-editor)) zikhombisa ama-connector abo akhululekile njengamaphoyinti lapho i-{{ui:Ipayipi}} noma i-{{ui:Idaktho}} sivuliwe, futhi iphoyinta iyambambelela kuwo.

- **Qala ku-connector**: umugqa uthatha isistimu nosayizi wawo (ipayipi lempahla yebha elingana noma libe nkulu kancane kusayizi we-connector; idaktho elizungezile lalo ububanzi balo). Uma i-connector ibheke phansi noma phezulu, njengedrenishi ngaphansi kwebhesini, umugqa uqala uhamba uye phansi noma phezulu ukuya ekuphakameni kwe-**At**, bese uqhubeka ngokuchofoza kwakho.
- **Phela ku-connector**: umugqa wehla noma uphakama uye kuyo, bese uphela lapho.

Umugqa uxhunyiwe kulokhu okwesisetshenziswa ku-faela ye-IFC, ngakho ezinye izinhlelo zibona isisetshenziswa njengengxenye yesistimu yaso. Into ebekwe kamuva nge-connector ekugcineni komugqa nayo iyaxhunywa kuwo. Umugqa osuswa ku-connector awusaxhunyiwe kuyo.

## Izixhumi ezenziwe ngawe

| Lapho | Okwenziwa yi-BimCraft |
|---|---|
| Umugqa uyajika | I-elbow. Ipayipi lensimbi ngomsakazo omude we-ASME B16.9 (1.5 × usayizi), amanye amapayipi ngobubanzi bangaphandle obubodwa, idaktho elizungezile ngama-diameter angu-1.5, idaktho elinesikhwama ngobubanzi obubodwa (noma ukuphakama okubodwa uma kujikwa phansi noma phezulu) |
| Umugqa uphela emgqeni womunye umugqa | I-tee; omunye umugqa uyahlukaniswa lapho |
| Imigqa emibili ihlangana ekugcineni ngosayizi abahlukile | I-reducer (amapayipi) noma i-transition (amapayipi omoya) |
| Imigqa emibili yosayizi ofanayo ihlangana ekugcineni nge-engeli | I-elbow |

Uma ushintsha, uhambise noma usule umugqa, yonke ixhumanisi kuleli zinga yenziwa kabusha. Uma umlenze ufushane kakhulu kumaxhumanisi asezinhlangothini zawo, i-BimCraft iyatshela futhi ayishintshi lutho: hambisa ikhoni kude.

:::note
Izixhumi namavalvu ayizinto ezijwayelekile: zidwetshwe kusuka osayizini wepayipi noma wedaktho, hhayi kukhathalogi yomkhiqizi. Izixhumi zokuxhuma, zokucindezela nezegrooved, imizimba yamavalvu kanye nezinsiza zinosayizi womkhiqizi wazo; zihlole nomhlinzeki wakho ngaphambi kokuoda.
:::

## Amarisa

Lapho udweba, thayipha ukuphakama okusha ku-**At** bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: umugqa uhamba uye phezulu noma phansi ekhoneni lokugcina, nge-elbow ekugcineni ngakunye. Irisa lingakhuphuka lingene kwesitezi esingaphezulu. Ilevili ngalinye likhombisa izingxenye zomugqa ibubanzi bayo bokubuka obuzifinyelelayo, nge-**UP** noma **DN** kurisa.

## Imifantu nokuhlamuka kwayo

Izindawo zodaka nezemvula ziqala ngokuhlamuka okuncane okuvunyelwe yi-International Plumbing Code ngosayizi wazo: 1/4 in. ngefidi kuze kube ngu-2-1/2 in., 1/8 in. ngefidi kusuka ku-3 kuya ku-6 in., 1/16 in. ngefidi kusuka ku-8 in. Udaka lwePVC lwaseBrazil luqala ngokuhlamuka kwe-NBR 8160: 2 % kuze kube ngu-DN 75, 1 % kusuka ku-DN 100. Thayipha okunye ukuhlamuka ebhareni noma ku-{{ui:Izici}}: `1/4"/ft`, `2 %` noma `1:50`. Umugqa wehla kusukela phuthuma lawo lokuqala, ngakho dweba izindawo zodaka ngendlela egeleza ngayo. Ipulani likhombisa ukwehla kanye nomcibisholo. Izici ziyakhomba uma udaka lulele kunokuhlamuka okuncane kwe-code.

## Linganisa izindawo zodaka ngamayunithi wesisetshenziswa

Khetha udaka lwezempilo: **Fixture units** ku-Properties itshela ukuthi ithwala amayunithi amangaki nosayizi omncane kakhulu awadingayo. Yonke isisetshenziswa sepulumbing enedrenishi ye-connector yayo emgqeni iyabalwa, nodaka lwonke oluphuma kulo luyabalwa: dweba izindawo zodaka ngendlela ezigeleza ngayo, ngayinye iphela esindaweni yodaka elingena kuyo.

- Udaka lwePVC lwaseBrazil lubala **UHC** futhi lulandela i-NBR 8160; ezinye zibala **DFU** futhi zilandela i-International Plumbing Code.
- **Drain**: i-**Branch** isayizwa ngethebula le-code lamagatsha wezisetshenziswa; i-**Building drain** ngethebula layo ngokuhlamuka, ngakho i-**Slope** ibalulekile.
- Udaka alukaze lube mncane kunodaka wezisetshenziswa aluphakelayo (i-water closet idinga DN 100, noma 3 in.), futhi kunodaka olungena kulo. Ngaphansi kwe-NBR 8160 i-building drain ingu-DN 100 okungenani.

{{ui:Linganisa ubukhulu bokukhipha}} yenza wonke udaka lwezempilo lwemodeli olumncane kakhulu ngamayunithi wezisetshenziswa lube usayizi odingekayo, njengesinyathelo esisodwa osingasihlehlisa. Udaka olwenziwe bukhulu kunalokho okudingekayo ushiywa njengoba kunjalo. Uma udaka lwethwala ngaphezu kwalokho usayizi omkhulu kakhulu wepayipi lwawo ungakwenza ngalo ukuhlamuka kwalo, i-BimCraft iyatshela: yinike ukuhlamuka okunamandla noma ipayipi elinye.

:::note
Amathebula ngawezindawo zodaka ezinengama. Izitaki, iziventilishi kanye nodaka lwemvula alisayizwa lapha: zihlole, kanye nodizayini wonke, ngokuhambisana nekhodi esebenza kwiphrojekthi.
:::

## Amavalvu

1. Khetha {{ui:Ivalvu}} bese ukhetha uhlobo: isango, ibhola, iglobe noma i-check.
2. Chofoza epayipini lapho okuya khona. Ipayipi liyasikwa lizungeze lona futhi ivalvu iyaxhunywa ohlangothini lwombili.

Ezicini zomugqa zibala amavalvu awo, ngayinye nge-{{ui:Susa}}.

Izinhlobo eziningi zamavalvu, isihlungi, amadampha emidwebo, izinkombandlela zepayipi zakho kanye nemindeni yabakhiqizi: bona [Izinkombandlela zepayipi, amavalvu, amadampha nemindeni yabakhiqizi](topic:pipe-specs).

## Shintsha umugqa

Khetha noma iyiphi ingxenye yomugqa, epulanini noma ku-3D view. Ku-Properties:

- **System**, **Material**, **Class** kanye **Size** (amapayipi), noma **Shape**, **Width**, **Height** (amapayipi omoya).
- **Height above the level** ihambisa umugqa wonke phezulu noma phansi.
- **Insulation**: ububanzi bayo, bugcinwa ukuhlola ukukhululeka nohlu lwezimbobo (ayidwetshwa lutho ngayo): bona [Hlola amapayipi namapayipi omoya ngokumelene nesakhiwo](topic:mep-against-structure).
- Ipayipi lensimbi: **Sika ibe amaspuli**: bona [Amaspuli epayipi lensimbi namashidi amaspuli](topic:pipe-spools).
- **Slope** (amapayipi).
- **Flow** nge-{{ui:Linganisa usayizi}}: kumanzi, osayizi omncane kakhulu ogcina amanzi ku-8 ft/s (2.4 m/s), noma ku-5 ft/s (1.5 m/s) kumanzi ashisayo; ku-3 m/s ngepayipi lamanzi e-PVC laseBrazil (NBR 5626). Kumapayipi omoya, ijubane kanye nezinga lokuhwaqeka kwedaktho njengoba lidwetshiwe. Udaka lwezempilo lusayizwa ngamayunithi wesisetshenziswa esikhundleni salokho: bona **Linganisa ubukhulu bodaka ngamayunithi wezisetshenziswa** ngezansi.

Ushintsho ngalunye lungesinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}. {{key:Delete}} isusa umugqa wonke.

## Amapulani

- Amapayipi amancane kuna-150 mm (cishe 6 in.) adwetshwa njengomugqa owodwa emugqeni wasembindini, amapayipi amakhulu kanye nawo wonke amapayipi omoya njengemigqa emibili ngobubanzi bawo.
- Isistimu ngayinye inombala wayo; i-PDF yepulani iwaphrinta emnyama.
- Umugqa ngamunye umakwe ngosayizi nesistimu yawo (isibonelo `2" CW` noma `16×10 SA`).
- Uhla lombono wepulani lunquma imigqa okubonisa, futhi {{ui:Okuboniswa kule pulani}} ingashiya **Izinkombandlela** noma **Idaktho** ngaphandle: bona [Imibono, uhla lombono namashidi akho](topic:views-and-sheets).

## Amathebula nokubalwa kwezinani

Ipaneli ye-{{ui:Uhlu}} yengeza **Amapayipi** (ngesistimu, impahla kanye nosayizi, nobude bawo), **Amapayipi omoya** (ngesistimu, isimo kanye nosayizi, nobude nendawo yobuso bangaphandle) kanye **Izixhumi namavalvu** (ngohlobo nosayizi, ibalwa). Chofoza umugqa ukukhetha okuhlwini, {{ui:Landa i-CSV}} iyalilanda.

{{ui:Ukubala amanani}} luthatha imigqa iye ku-TakeoffCraft: ipayipi namapayipi omoya ngosayizi njengemigqa edwetshwe epulanini (udaka olunokuhlamuka kulinganiswa ngokuhamba kwalo), izixhumi namavalvu zibalwa lapho zikhona: bona [Thumela izinani zemodeli ku-TakeoffCraft](topic:takeoff). Imirisa ishiywa ngaphandle kwalelo shidi, ngoba ipulani alikwazi ukuyilinganisa; ithebula lamapayipi linobude bayo.

## Ku-faela ye-IFC

Umugqa ngamunye uyi-assembly equkethe izingxenye zawo: izingxenye zepayipi nedaktho, izixhumi namavalvu, ngayinye nohlobo lwayo, impahla, umbala wesistimu yayo kanye namaphothi ayo. Amaphothi ahlanganayo axhunyiwe, futhi yonke ingxenye ingeyesistimu yayo yokusabalalisa (amafayela e-IFC2X3 athola okufanayo njengezigaba zokugeleza, izixhumi nezilawuli esistimini). Ezinye izinhlelo ezifunda izistimu ze-IFC zibona umugqa oyodwa oxhunyiwe ngesistimu ngayinye. Incazelo yomugqa wawo ilondolozwa nawo, ngakho i-BimCraft iyawenza kabusha ngendlela efanayo lapho uwushintsha.
