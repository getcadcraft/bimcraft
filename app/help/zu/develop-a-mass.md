Imbono yesayithi ye-CivilCraft ingadweba ubuningi bezakhiwo emhlabeni futhi iwuhlole ngomhlaba, imikhawulo oyibhalayo nelanga. **Thuthukela ku-BimCraft** ivula i-BimCraft kwithebhu entsha ngobuningi; **Ubuningi njenge-IFC** bulondoloza njengefayela ovula lapha ngendlela ejwayelekile. Kwenzeke kanjani noma kanjani, ubuningi ngabunye bufika njengesayithi, isakhiwo esinesitezi esisodwa ngasinye sendawo, nebhulokhi eyodwa (ubuningi ngokwabo) inezinombolo zalo.

## Thuthukisa ubuningi

1. Khetha ubuningi ku-3D view. Izimfanelo zibonisa ukusetshenziswa kwabo, izitezi nobude besitezi, isisekelo, indlela okuhlala ngayo esimeni, nephaseli labo.
2. Cofa **Develop this mass**.

Ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa, lonke izinga lalesi sakhiwo lithola izindonga ezungeza isisekelo (isisekelo siyisiphetho sangaphandle sezindonga; ngayinye yazo iphakama iye esitezini esingenhla, nowaphezulu uphakama isitezi esisodwa) kanye ne-floor ngaphansi kwakhiwo lonke. Ibhulokhi iyashintshwa, futhi isakhiwo sigcina izinombolo zocwaningo esethini lempahla `CadCraft_SiteMass`, siphawulwe njengesithuthukisiwe. Akukho uphahla: uphahla lwncike kuklama.

## Indawo yesayithi

Ifayela elivela ku-CivilCraft libhalwe ngeyunithi yobude yeprojekthi ngokwayo, futhi uma iprojekthi ye-CivilCraft inohlelo lokuhlela, liyakuthwala njengokuguqulwa kwemephu yeprojekthi. Ukuthuthukisa ubuningi akulushintshi lolo lwazi, ngakho isakhiwo sihlala lapho imephu ithi kunjalo.
