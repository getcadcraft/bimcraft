## Okufanele ukwenze

Dweba amapayipi, imisele yomoya, amatreyi wamakhebula nokhondo njengejwayelekile, bese uvula ibhokisi **Izibalo**. Ithebhu ngayinye ifunda imodeli, ibonise ithebula, futhi ibhale okungalungile njengezinkinga ephaneli ye-⚠. Lapho usayizi ozolungisa inkinga, **Faka usayizi** uguqula izintambo ngesinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

Lezi zisiza zokudweba. Yonke into ezenzakalelayo ibhalwe kuskrini, futhi akukho lapho olungu ithebula lekhodi: thayipha izinombolo ezisuka kumqondo oyiklama kuwo, bese umphumela uhlolwa yinjiniyela ephendula ngawo.

## Amapayipi

1. Khetha ukhiye we-**system** (amanzi abandayo ngu-`cw`, amanzi ashisayo ngu-`hw`).
2. Setha ijubane eliphezulu kakhulu, futhi uma ufuna ingcindezi ihlolwe, ingcindezi emthonjeni nengcindezi encane isifaki esiyidingayo.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Hlola}}.

Ipayipi ngalinye lithwala isidingo sezinto zokuhlinzeka ezingaphansi kalo. Isidingo sivela kwigrafu yezinto zokuhlinzeka ngokuya kokugeleza, ngendlela uHunter ayichaza ngayo. **Igrafu eyakhelwe ngaphakathi iyisibonelo, hhayi ithebula lekhodi**; Izinkinga ziyakutshela lokho kuze kube yilapho uthayipha eyakho. Ukumelana kuyi-Hazen–Williams, izingxenye zokuxhuma yizilinganiso zokulahlekelwa, nokukhuphuka kuba ngu-9.8 kPa ngemitha. Izinto zokuhlinzeka zesifaki sihlala kuhlobo lwaso, noma kunani elisetha ngethuluzi losizo `loads_set`.

## Imisele yomoya

Setha isistimu, izinga lokumelana (ukumelana okulinganayo) noma ijubane, nanoma yikuphi ukulahlekelwa kwekhoyili, kwesihlungi noma kwesifaki somsindo. Izindawo zokuphela zithatha umoya wazo ku-`loads_set` kwi-diffuser. Ithebula likhombisa ijubane nokumelana ngesiqephu ngasinye, **ingcindezi yefeni** endleleni ebalulekile, nengcindezi okudingeka idampa lesinye isiphetho ngasinye lithathe. Ukulahlekelwa kwezingxenye zokuxhuma kusebenzisa izilinganiso ezizenzakalelayo ezijikelezile, hhayi idatabeyisi yezingxenye.

## Amatreyi wamakhebula nokhondo

Dweba ngethuluzi le-Duct: izistimu **Cable tray** ne-**Conduit** zigcinwa njengabathwali bamakhebula, ngakho ezinye izinhlelo zizifunda njengamatreyi nokhondo. Kuthebhu le-Cables, namathisela uhlu lwamakhebula (i-tag, ububanzi bangaphandle ngamamillimitha, inani, kusuka, kuya). `from` no `to` kuba yi-tag yesiqephu sezinsiza noma iphoyinti `x,y,z` ngamamillimitha. Amakhebula alandela indlela emfushane engaphazamisekile ngamatreyi nokhondo; isintambo ngasinye sikhombisa ukugcwala kwaso ngokumelene nemikhawulo oyithayipha, futhi uhlu luyagcinwa njenge-CSV nobude obufaka iphesenti lesikhala nezinhlangothi.

## Ukukhanya

Nikeza ama-luminaire ngamunye ifayela layo le-IES (LM-63) noma le-EULUMDAT ngethuluzi losizo `photometry_set`. Thayipha izinhloso, ukuhlukana kwegridi nokuphakama kwendawo yokusebenza. Umphumela **ukukhanya okuqondile** kuphela: akukho ukubuyiselwa phakathi nokuba khona kwezithunzi, ngakho uyisilinganiso esiphansi ekamelweni.

## Izinkinobho zomlilo

I-add-on **Fire sprinklers** inethebhu yayo; bheka usizo lwayo.
