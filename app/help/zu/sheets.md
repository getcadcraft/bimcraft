## Okuzokwenza

Yenza isethi yemidwebo kusuka emodelini: ipulani yesitezi ngasinye, ukubukeka okune kohlangothi (enyakatho, empumalanga, eningizimu nasentshonalanga), isigaba sesakhiwo kumugqa wesigaba ngamunye, amahlu eminyango, amawindi, amakamelo nezinkuni, nombukiso wohlaka lwendawo yodonga ngalunye oluhlaliswe. Isethi ilungiswa kabusha kusuka emodelini njalo lapho uyivula, ngakho ihambisana nakho konke okwakhile.

## Dweba umugqa wesigaba

1. Esitezini, vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Umugqa wesigaba}}.
2. Chofoza lapho umugqa uqala khona nalapho uphela khona. Umugqa omncane esiphakathi kwawo ukhombisa uhlangothi lokusika olubukeka kulo: kwesobunxele komugqa, uhamba kusuka ekuchofozeni kokuqala kuya kokwesibili.
3. Umugqa ubonakala epulanini nezinhloko zawo nochungechunge lwawo (A, bese B, njalo).

Khetha umugqa ukuze uwushintshe ku-**Izakhiwo**: uchungechunge lwawo, **Phendula umbono** ukubuka ngakolunye uhlangothi, no-**Bheka kuze kube** ukuze umise ibanga oqondile ngemuva kokusika (0 ibonisa konke okungemuva kwawo). Cindezela {{key:Delete}} ukuze uwususe.

## Vula amashidi

1. Chofoza {{ui:Amashidi}} ebhalini yemodeli (eduze kwe-{{ui:Phrinta}}).
2. Amashidi ohlwini ngakwesobunxele: **A-101** nokunye kumapulani, **A-201** wokubukeka kohlangothi, **A-301** wezigaba, **A-401** wamahlu, kanye **S-101** wokwakheka kodonga. Chofoza elilodwa ukuze uliboneke.
3. Khetha **Iphepha**: Letter, Tabloid (11 × 17), Arch D (24 × 36), A3 noma A1. Iphepha ligcina igama lalo elijwayelekile, nosayizi walo ubhalwa ngama-{{ui:Amayunithi}} okhethile, isibonelo *Letter · 8.5 × 11 in* noma *A3 · 297 × 420 mm*.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Landa isethi njenge-PDF}}: ikhasi elilodwa ngeshidi ngalinye.

## Amanothi ajwayelekile avela esethini yemithetho

Kushidi, {{ui:Amanothi ajwayelekile}} ikuvumela ukuthi ubeke amanothi avela esethini yemithetho (ngomkhakha) ekholomini yesokudla yeshidi. Inothi ngalinye ligcina isethi nesigatshana okuvela kuso, futhi ishidi eliphrintiwe lithi lapho isethi ingaqinisekisiwe. Amanothi ayisisekelo nje: umuntu obhekele umdwebo unquma ukuthi athini. Adinga okwengeziwe kwemithetho yokwakha.

## Okusemashidini

- **Amapulani** akhombisa udonga, iminyango, amawindi, amakamelo ngezindawo zawo, izilinganiso nemigqa yezigaba, ngesikali esikhulu esijwayelekile esilingana nawo: 1/4" = 1'-0" noma esincane (1:50 noma esincane ngamamilimitha).
- **Ukubukeka kohlangothi** kudwetshwa emodelini ye-3D ngemigqa efihliwe esusiwe, ababili ngeshidi, bonke ngesikali esifanayo, nomugqa womhlaba ngaphansi kwabo. Uphahla, izisekelo nomhlaba kubonakala njengoba kwenziwe.
- **Izigaba** zigcwalisa lokho okudlula kukho ukusika (izindonga, amaphansi, uphahla) futhi zidwebe okubonakala ngaphesheya kwakho.
- **Ukubukeka nezigaba kunamanothi azo:** ukumaka kwesinye nesinye isitezi ngegama nobude bakhe ngakwesokudla, ubude phakathi kwamazinga ngakwesobunxele, nokubukeka kohlangothi ububanzi besakhiwo ngaphansi kwabo nethegi (D1, W1) kunoma yimuphi umnyango nawo wonke amawindi odongeni oluhlangene nawe. Ngezindonga zegumbi ngalinye bona [Ukubukeka kwangaphakathi](topic:interior-elevations).
- **Amahlu** afana nalawo {{ui:Uhlu}} ebhalini yemodeli.
- **Ukwakheka** kukhombisa imisumpa, amapuleti, izinhloko nezinsimbi zezindonga ezakhiwe njengoba zibonakala ngaphandle, ukuze iqembu likwazi ukuzibeka phansi. Bona [Ukwakha udonga, amaphansi nophahla](topic:framing).

Ishidi ngalinye linebhulokhi lesihloko elinenombolo yalo, isihloko salo, igama lefayela nosuku.

:::tip
Amashidi avela emodelini: ukuze ushintshe umdwebo, shintsha imodeli bese uvula {{ui:Amashidi}} futhi.
:::

:::note
Ukubukeka nezigaba kubonisa imigqa nokugcwaliswa kuphela: izilinganiso, amamaki wamazinga nezinkomba phezu kwawo abikho okwamanje.
:::
