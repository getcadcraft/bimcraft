## Okufanele ukwenze

Xhuma izintambo kuzinsiza ukuze imodeli ibe yisistimu eyodwa exhunyiwe: okuphuma kwepompo kuye epayipini elishiya kuyo, nentamo yediffuser kumsele wegatsha lakhe. Bese uvumela i-BimCraft ibhale lokho okungaxhunyiwe.

## Dweba usuka ephoyinteni

1. Khetha {{ui:Ipayipi}} noma {{ui:Ipayipi lomoya}}.
2. Hambisa isikhombi phezu kwephoyinti lesinsiza (izindilinga ezincane; iphoyinti elivulekile lidwetshwa ngombala wesexwayiso). Isikhombi siyanamathela kulo bese sithi **ephoyinteni**.
3. Chofoza: intambo iqala ngobude bephoyinti **futhi ithatha usayizi nesistimu yalo** (ipayipi nalo lithatha izinto ezijwayelekile zesistimu). Umugqa wesimo utshela okuthathile.
4. Dweba uqhubeke njengejwayelekile. Ukuphela kwentambo kwephoyinti elinye nakho kuyaxhuma lolo luhlangothi.

Isiphetho sentambo ephoyinteni sihlala **sisekelwe** kulo: hambisa noma ujikise isinsiza (ku-Izici) bese isiphetho sentambo sihamba nakho; hlela intambo bese lolo luhlangothi lihlala ephoyinteni. Amakhoveni wentambo ayo amanye ahlala lapho ayekhona, ngakho ngemva kokuhamba okukhulu, umlenze wokuqala ungase uphume ephoyinteni ngokuthambeka: hambisa nekhoveni elilandelayo futhi.

## Hlola ukuxhumana

Vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese uchofoza {{ui:Hlola ukuxhumana}}. Uhlu olungaphansi kwebha luwubonisa:

| Umugqa | Okusho |
|---|---|
| Iphoyinti alixhunyiwe | Isinsiza sinokuxhuma okungadwetshwanga lutho kuso |
| Akukho lutho esiphethweni | Intambo iphela emoyeni |
| Amaphoyinti axhunyiwe anosayizi abahlukile | Intambo yosayizi oyodwa ihlangana nephoyinti noma nentambo yosayizi omunye ngaphandle kwenciphisi (usayizi ojwayelekile wepayipi, isimo nezinhlangothi zomsele) |
| Amaphoyinti axhunyiwe ezistimu ezahlukile | Isibonelo: umsele wokubuyisela esigedeni sokunikeza |
| Ipayipi lixhunywe emsele womoya | Izixhumi ezimbili zezinhlobo ezihlukile ziyahlangana |
| Akukho sinsiza esinethiwekhi yaso esinikezayo | Akukho okuxhunyiwe entanjeni, noma kude kangakanani, okunomthombo. Imifudlana, amanzi esiphepho nezimbobo zokuphefumula azibuzwa lokhu: amanzi azo avela ezinsizeni |
| Ixhunywe ephoyinteni lesinsiza esingasasekho kumodeli | Isinsiza sasusiwe; dweba kabusha isiphetho noma susa intambo |

Amaphutha avela kuqala. Chofoza umugqa ukukhetha okuchazwa yiwo. {{ui:Vala}} ifihla uhlu. Ukuhlola kufunda imodeli njengoba idwetshiwe: kuhlola ukuxhumana nosayizi, hhayi okugeleza, ingcindezi noma ukulinganisa.
