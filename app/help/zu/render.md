{{ui:Renda}} ebhareni lamathuluzi yenza isithombe sombono we-3D esibukeka njengosuku: isibhakabhaka, ilanga lapho indawo yesayithi libeka khona, izithunzi ezithambile, ukukhanya okuvela esibhakabhakeni sonke okufiphaza amakhona, nemibala engabukeka njengengilazi noma insimbi. Imodeli nefayela akushintshwa.

## Renda umbono

1. Lungisa umbono we-3D ngendlela ofuna ngayo isithombe: zungeza futhi usondeze, fihla izitezi noma izinto ongazidingi, futhi setha [isigaba](topic:section-plane) uma isithombe kufanele sibonise ukusika.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Renda}} ebhareni lamathuluzi.
3. Khetha {{ui:Isimo}} uma eyayicindezelwe ingeyona ofuna (bona [Izilungiselelo zesimo](topic:render#scene-presets-a-good-picture-in-one-click)), khetha {{ui:Usayizi}}, bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}} engxoxweni.

   Isithombe sigcwala sisarenderwa futhi sibushelelezi ngayinye ukudlula. {{ui:Misa}} (noma {{key:Esc}}) iyiphelisa ngaphambi kwesikhathi igcine isithombe sanamuhla.

4. Chofoza {{ui:Londoloza}} ukulanda njenge-PNG, noma {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} ukuze ukhethe ifomethi enye (bona [Londoloza isithombe](topic:render#save-the-picture)). Siqanjwa ngegama lefayela lemodeli.

:::tip
Qala ngo-{{ui:Uhlaka}} ku-{{ui:Usayizi wombono}} ukuze uhlole ikhamera, bese urenda {{ui:Okuhle}} noma {{ui:Okuhle kakhulu}} ngosayizi omkhulu wesithombe sokugcina.
:::

## Izilungiselelo zesimo: isithombe esihle ngokuchofoza kanye

Phezulu kwengxoxo kukhona izinkinobho ezine ze-{{ui:Isimo}}. Ngayinye iseta isibhakabhaka, ukukhanya, umhlaba, imigqa yemiphetho, ikhwalithi kanye nelanga ngokuchofoza kanye, ngakho akukho okudingeka ukufunde ngaphambi kwesithombe sokuqala. Chofoza esinye, bese {{ui:Renda}}.

| Isimo | Okusethiwe | Okusetshenziselwa |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Phandle}} | Isibhakabhaka esicacile, ilanga lemodeli, umhlaba uvuliwe | Isakhiwo ngaphandle |
| {{ui:Phakathi}} | Izulu elimpunga (ukukhanya okulinganayo, akukho amabala elanga anzima), ukukhanya okukhanya ngesigamu sesitobhi | Umbono ngaphakathi kwegumbi |
| {{ui:Ukubuka ngokusikwa}} | Istudiyo esimhlophe, akukho mhlaba, imigqa yemiphetho ekurendeni okusheshayo | Imodeli esikwe yisigaba |
| {{ui:Ubusuku}} | Ilanga lingaphansi (ubusuku embusika kuhhafu wendawo), ukukhanya ngesitobhi esisodwa | Izibani ezikhanyisa imodeli. Ngaphandle kwezibani isithombe simnyama |

- {{ui:Khetha ngalo mbono}} ikukhethela: {{ui:Ukubuka ngokusikwa}} uma kukhona ukusika, {{ui:Phakathi}} uma ikhamera isemodeleni, kungenjalo {{ui:Phandle}}. Okokuqala lapho uvula ingxoxo iqala ngale ndlela ngokwayo.
- Wonke amasethingi asalele ngezansi futhi angashintshwa. Uma ushintsha okukodwa, isimo asisasethwa, akukho nkinobho ecindezelwe, futhi umugqa ongaphansi kwezinkinobho uthi amasethingi ngawakho. Chofoza isimo futhi ukuze ubuyisele amanani aso.
- Isimo asithinti injini, {{ui:Indlela}}, usayizi, izimpahla, ikhamera noma izibani.

:::note
Izimo zinika amanani afanayo ku-ModelCraft nasku-BimCraft. Isithombe sokugcina sincike emodelini yakho nasekhadini lakho lezithombe.
:::

## Okubonakala esithombeni

Okubonakala ku-3D view njengokuqinile kuyafakwa esithombeni: izinto ezifihliwe nezibonakala ngokukhanyayo, izitezi ezifihliwe namamodeli afihliwe ayashiywa, namagumbi abonakala kuphela uma {{ui:Izindawo}} ivuliwe. Ideleyi yesigaba nebhokisi lokusika zisika isithombe njengoba zisika umbono. Izinto zibonakala ngemibala yazo: ukudweba ngempahla (ithebhu ye-{{ui:Umbala}}) akusetshenziswa.

## Izibani

Yonke impahla yokukhanyisa emodelini iyisibani esithombeni: izinto zokukhanyisa kufayela le-IFC (IfcLightFixture, noma into ehlelwe njengempahla yokukhanyisa), kuhlanganise nalezo ozibeka kusuka kulabhulari. Ngayinye ikhanya kusuka ngaphansi kancane kwempahla. Uma ifayela linikeza amandla kawathi wempahla, ukukhanya kwayo kulandela lokho; kungenjalo kungu-800 lumens, ukukhanya okumhlophe okufudumele.

Ingxoxo ibhala izibani ohlwini {{ui:Izibani}}: khipha esinye ukuze usivale, noma uthayipha ukukhanya kwaso ngama-lumens. Lezi zinguquko zilondolozwa ku-IFC (isethi yempahla `CadCraft_Light`, lapho ungazishintsha khona nakwezakhiwo), futhi {{key:Ctrl+Z}} iyazihlehlisa.

Uma ifayela lingasho lutho ngapho isayithi yalo ikhona, ilanga libekwa ku-40° enyakatho, futhi ingxoxo iyasho lokho. Isikhathi yisikhathi sewashi lesayithi, esibalwa ngolongitudi walo, ngakho kungaba ngehora elilodwa endaweni lapho amawashi esetshenziswa ngokwehlukile.

Lezi zilungiselelo zigcinwa kulesi siphequluli sesithombe esilandelayo. Azilondolozwa kufayela le-IFC.

## Iglasi nezinye izimpahla

Ohlwini {{ui:Izimpahla zokwakha}}, yonke imibala ekuskrini inombono: {{ui:Engakhazimuli}}, {{ui:Ukukhanya okuthambile}}, {{ui:Okukhazimulayo}}, {{ui:Insimbi}}, {{ui:Insimbi ecwebezelayo}}, {{ui:Ingilazi}} noma {{ui:Ingilazi eqhwabile}}. Imibala efayela le-IFC eyenza ibonakale (njengengilazi yewindi, isibonelo) iqala njenge-{{ui:Ingilazi}}.

:::note
Ngendlela {{ui:Ngokushesha}}, iglasi nensimbi zibonisa isibhakabhaka, hhayi enye imodeli, futhi ukukhanya akugobeki ngeglasi. Imodi {{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} engezansi ikwenza kokubili.
:::

## Ikhwalithi ephezulu

Khetha {{ui:Indlela}} › {{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} ukuze uthole isithombe esilandela ukukhanya ngendlela ikhamera ayibona ngayo:

- ukukhanya kugxumayo phakathi kwezindawo, ngakho igumbi elikhanyiswa ngewindi, noma ngesibani, likhanyiswa lonke, nemibala iyagudluka ezindaweni eziseduze;
- ingilazi iyingilazi yangempela: ukukhanya kuyagoba kuyo futhi ibonisa imodeli;
- insimbi nezindawo ezicwebezelayo zibonisa ingxenye esalayo yemodeli;
- amapanelo okukhanya akhanya njengamapanelo, nezithunzi ezithambile.

Ilinde kakhulu kune-{{ui:Ngokushesha}}: iphikseli ngalinye lisanda ukukhanya kwalo futhi. Isithombe siqala sinensali bese siyahlanzeka njengoba amasampula evolozwa, ngakho qala ngosayizi omncane ({{ui:Usayizi wombono}}, {{ui:Uhlaka}}) bese ukhetha {{ui:Umkhawulo wesikhathi}}: lapho isikhathi siphela, isithombe sakuleso sikhathi siyagcinwa. {{ui:Misa}} nayo iyagcina. Ukuqala kokudlula kokuqala kulinda isiphequluli silungise isilandeli sendlela.

Lapho isiphequluli sinaye-WebGPU, isilandeli sendlela sayo siyeCadCraft futhi siqhuba ekhadini lezithombe; umugqa ongaphansi kwesithombe uyakusho lokho. Ngemva kwe-{{ui:Misa}} noma umkhawulo wesikhathi, {{ui:Qhubeka}} ithatha isithombe esifanayo iye kumasampula aso wonke; ukushintsha isilungiselelo noma ukucindezela futhi {{ui:Renda}} kuqala kusha. Ngaphandle kwe-WebGPU isithombe sishintshwa ngokulandelela indlela nge-WebGL (umugqa uyakusho lokho) futhi {{ui:Qhubeka}} ayikhonjiswa.

:::linux
I-Firefox nezimo eziningi ze-Chrome ku-Linux azinayo i-WebGPU okwamanje, ngakho ikhwalithi ephezulu isebenzisa i-WebGL lapho.
:::

Kule modi imiphetho ayidwetshwa, izinkombandlela zibonisa umbala nokuqina kodwa hhayi amabhampu, futhi ukusika kwenziwa emodelini ngaphambi kokuqala kwesithombe.

{{ui:Ukususa umsindo (kususa okusele ngemva kwamasampula)}} ivulwe ngokuzenzakalelayo: lapho isithombe siqediwe, noma simisiwe, umsindo osele uyasuswa yinethiwekhi yamanyuroni eyenzelwe lokhu (i-Intel Open Image Denoise eshicilelwe, eqhutshwa kukhompyutha yakho: ekhadini lezithombe, noma kuprosesa uma kungenjalo). {{ui:Bonisa}} › {{ui:Isithombe ngaphambi kokususa umsindo}} sikhombisa njengoba senziwe.

## Izinsa nemaphrofayili wokukhanya ekhadini lezithombe

Nge-WebGPU, ikhwalithi ephezulu inezinsa ezimbili ezengeziwe:

- {{ui:Isibhakabhaka sefiziksi}} sisebenza ukukhanya kwemini ngokuphakama kwelanga: ukuluhlaza okujulile phezulu, ibhendi elikhanyayo eduze nesibhakabhaka, nelanga elishisa kancane futhi likhanye kancane lapho liphansi. {{ui:Umusi}} uhamba kusuku olucacile kakhulu (2) kuya kolunomhwamuko (10).
- {{ui:Isithombe sesibhakabhaka (HDRI)}} sikhanyisa imodeli ngesithombe sepanorama sendawo yangempela. {{ui:Khetha isithombe…}} ithatha ifayela le-`.hdr` noma le-`.exr` le-equirectangular; {{ui:Jikeleza}} iyijikeleza imodeli futhi {{ui:Ukukhanya kwesithombe}} iyenza ikhanye noma imnyame. Isithombe sinelanga layo, ngakho ilanga lemodeli alengezwa. Sigcinwa ngesikhathi ikhasi livulekile kuphela: ngemva kokuvula kabusha, khetha kabusha.

Isibani ngasinye ohlwini {{ui:Izibani}} singathatha iphrofayili yokukhanya yomenzi: {{ui:Iphrofayili ye-IES…}} ifunda ifayela le-IES (LM-63) bese isibani sichitha ukukhanya kwaso njengokuthi impahla leyo yenza (isibani esinomsakazo omncane, esigeza udonga). Zonke izibani ezinalelo gama zisebenzisa leyo, futhi isibani silondoloza ukukhanya kwaso. Chofoza igama leprofayili ukuze uyeke ukuyisebenzisa.

Amawindi engilazi ecacile asiza ukukhanya kwesibhakabhaka kuthole indlela yakho ngene ekamelweni.

Indlela {{ui:Ngokushesha}} nesilandeli sendlela se-WebGL zibonisa {{ui:Isibhakabhaka sefiziksi}} kanye nesithombe sesibhakabhaka njengesibhakabhaka esicacile futhi zinganaki amaphrofayili wokukhanya.

## Ikhamera

{{ui:Ikhamera}} isetha isithombe njengesithombe esithathwe ngekhamera:

- {{ui:Ukuvezwa}}: gcina {{ui:Isilayidi sokukhanya}}, noma khetha {{ui:Izilungiselelo zekhamera}} usethe {{ui:Ukuvulwa kwembobo (f-stop)}}, {{ui:Isivinini sesibambi}} ne-{{ui:ISO}} njengakukhamera; i-EV eseduze kwazo iyisilinganiso sokuvezwa esijwayelekile. f/16 ku-1/100 s ne-ISO 100 ifana nosuku olunelanga; isikhala esimnyama sidinga ukuvulwa okubanzi, isivinini esiphansi noma i-ISO ephezulu. Lokhu kusebenza kuzo zonke izindlela.
- {{ui:Ukujula kokugxila}} kushaqisa okusondele noma okude kunobude be-{{ui:Ibanga lokugxila (m)}} njengoba ilensi yalobo f-stop ingenza (shiya kungenalutho ukuze ugxile kokuphakathi kombono); {{ui:Ukwakheka kwembobo}} kunika izinkanyiso ezingagxilile isimo esijikelezile noma esinezimpaphe.
- {{ui:Ukubonakala kwamaphuzu amabili (imigqa emile ihlala imile)}} ilinganisa ikhamera futhi ihambisa isithombe, njengalensi yesakhiwo ye-tilt-shift, ukuze izindonga zingaphenduleki uma ubheka phezulu noma phansi ebhilidini.
- {{ui:Ibhalansi yombala}} isetha umbala wokukhanya okubonakala njengomhlophe: ophansi (isibonelo 3000 K) ngokukhanya kwezibani okufudumele, ophakeme ngomthunzi nezinsuku ezinamafu.
- {{ui:Ukuprojekta}}: {{ui:Ipanorama ye-360°}} yenza isithombe esingu-2:1 sakho konke okuzungeze ikhamera, sezibukeli ze-panorama; {{ui:Ipanorama yesterio ye-360°}} ibeka ndawonye ipanorama yeso lesokunxele nelesokudla zezingilazi zekhanda; {{ui:Imephu yekyubhu (izinhlangothi eziyi-6)}} yenza imibono eyisikwele eyisithupha ngegridi ye-3 × 2. Ububanzi buvela ku-{{ui:Usayizi}}.

Ukuprojekta, ukujula kokugxila, umbono wamaphuzu amabili nebhalansi yombala kudinga isilandeli sendlela ekhadini lezithombe (WebGPU), ngakho zibonakala kuphela ku-{{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}}.

## Qeda isithombe

{{ui:Isithombe}} silungisa isithombe sekhwalithi ephezulu njengesithombe sefoto, futhi isilawuli ngasinye sishintsha isithombe esiqediwe ngesikhathi esifanayo, ngaphandle kokurenda futhi:

- {{ui:Ukubukeka (ukuguqulwa kokubuka)}}: {{ui:ACES filmic}} (isithombe njengakuqala), {{ui:AgX}} (esithambile, sigcina umbala ekukhanyeni okukhanya kakhulu), {{ui:Filmic}} (ijika lefilimu elithambile) noma {{ui:Ejwayelekile}} (okujwayelekile, ukukhanya okukhanya kakhulu kuguqukela kumhlophe).
- {{ui:Ukuphambana}} kanye {{ui:Ukucindezelwa kokukhanya okukhulu}} (ibuyisela imininingwane ezindaweni ezikhanya kakhulu).
- {{ui:Bloom}} (ukukhanya okuthambile okuzungeze ukukhanya okukhanyayo), {{ui:Glare}} kanye ne-{{ui:Imigqa ye-glare}} (imigqa yezinkanyezi kumaphuzu akhanya kakhulu) ne-{{ui:Vignette}} (imikhawulo emnyama).
- {{ui:Ithebula lemibala (.cube)…}} lisebenzisa ukuhlelwa kombala kusuka kufayela le-`.cube`, ngamandla {{ui:Amandla ethebula lemibala}}; njengesithombe sesibhakabhaka, ligcinwa kuphela ngesikhathi ikhasi livulekile.

## Londoloza isithombe

Eceleni kwe-{{ui:Londoloza}}, {{ui:Londoloza njenge}} ikhetha ifayela: {{ui:PNG}}, {{ui:JPEG}} noma {{ui:WebP}} ukwabelana (i-JPEG ne-WebP kuphela lapho isiphequluli singazibhala khona), futhi emva kokurenda kwekhwalithi ephezulu ekhadini lezithombe nalezi:

- {{ui:PNG, amabhithi 16}}: isithombe esihlelwe ngezinyathelo ezinembe phakathi kwezithunzi, sokuphrinta noma ukuhlelwa okwengeziwe.
- {{ui:EXR, zonke izendlalelo (float ye-32-bit)}}: isithombe njengoba sirenderiwe (ukukhanya okuqondile, akukho ukuhlelwa) nezakhi zokurenda zonke kanye neqembu ngalinye lokukhanya njengezendlalelo ezizimele, zezinhlelo zokuhlanganisa.
- {{ui:Ifayela lokurenda (.ccrender, ukuze ulungise futhi kamuva)}}: konke ingxoxo edinga ukuxuba nokuhlela isithombe futhi. {{ui:Vula ifayela lokurenda…}} liyabuyisa, nokuxuba kwalo nokuhlelwa kwalo, ngaphandle kokurenda.

## Xuba izibani ngemva kokurenda

Vula {{ui:Ukuxuba ukukhanya (shintsha umbala nokukhanya kwesibani ngasinye ngemva kokurenda)}} ngaphambi kokucindezela {{ui:Renda}}. Lapho isithombe siqediwe (noma simisiwe), {{ui:Xuba isithombe}} ikhombisa izibani zaso ngamaqembu: isibhakabhaka, ilanga, nezibani ngegama (sonke isibani esinegama elifanayo siyiqembu elilodwa). Vala iqembu, linike umbala, noma yenza likhanye noma libe mnyama, futhi isithombe siyashintsha ngokushesha, ngaphandle kokurenda futhi. {{ui:Londoloza ukuxuba}} igcina ukuxuba ngegama ukuze ukhethe futhi kamuva; {{ui:Njengoba kurenderiwe}} ibuyela esithombeni njengoba senziwa. {{ui:Londoloza}} ilondoloza isithombe esixubile.

{{ui:Izakhi zokurenda (albedo, normals, depth, diffuse, specular, transmission, emission, IDs)}} ilondoloza izingxenye zesithombe nazo. {{ui:Bonisa}} iguqula ukubuka ibe enye yazo: imibala yephezu (albedo), izinormali, ukujula, ukukhanya okusabalele, okuqondile nokudlula (ngayinye eqondile nengqondile), okukhanyayo nesibhakabhaka, nombala ngento noma ngempahla ukuze kwenziwe amamaski.

Zombili zigcina izendlalelo ezengeziwe ekhadini lezithombe. Ezinkulwini ikhadi lezithombe lingenawo indawo; ingxoxo iyakushoya futhi usayizi omncane uyasebenza.

## Ukhohlo, intuthu, umsindo nokukhanya okugoqekile

Lokhu kukhona ku-{{ui:Ukhohlo, intuthu nomsindo}} futhi kusebenza ku-{{ui:Ikhwalithi ephezulu (ukulandelela indlela)}} ekhadini lezithombe; ezinye izinjini ziyazishiya.

- {{ui:Ukhohlo nomhwamuko}} ugcwalisa umoya kuze kufike ebudeni nomsi onokukhanya kwelanga, kwesibhakabhaka nezibani. {{ui:Ukubona kukude kangakanani, ngamamitha}} kusetha ukuthi kujiya kangakanani (cishe ama-100 m ukuba umhwamuko omncane, ama-20 m ukhohlo oluqinile), {{ui:Ukuphakama kokhohlo ngaphezu komhlaba, ngamamitha}} kuyinhla lapho liphela khona, futhi {{ui:Imisebe yokukhanya}} ukuthi ukhohlo luyakhanya kangakanani ngasekukhanyeni. Ukukhanya kwelanga kushiya ukhohlo ngaphezulu kwalo, ngakho lapho ilanga lingena ngewindi noma phakathi kwezakhiwo ubona njengemisebe yokukhanya.
- {{ui:Intuthu noma ifu}} yengeza ivolumu eyodwa yentuthu noma ifu elikhanyiswa njengokhohlo. {{ui:Uhlobo}} luba {{ui:Ifu}}, {{ui:Ikholomu yentuthu}} noma {{ui:Kusuka kufayela levolumu (.ccvol)}}, ifomethi yegridi yeCadCraft. {{ui:Imininingwane}} no {{ui:Ukuhlukahluka}} zishintsha ifu noma ikholomu eyenziwe, {{ui:Ubude obude kakhulu ohlangothini olulodwa, ngamamitha (0 = usayizi wemodeli)}}, {{ui:Hambisa X (m)}}, {{ui:Hambisa Y (m)}}, {{ui:Hambisa Z (m)}} ne-{{ui:Jikeleza}} zibeka kona (iqala phakathi kwemodeli), {{ui:Ukujiya}} yenza kube nzima kakhulu, {{ui:Ukukhanya phambili}} yenza kukhanye kakhulu ngasekukhanyeni, futhi {{ui:Umbala}} uyidaya. Ifayela levolumu ligcinwa kuphela ngesikhathi ikhasi livulekile, njengesithombe sesibhakabhaka.
- {{ui:Yima ngokushesha lapho isithombe sihlanzekile (ukusampula okuzivumelanisayo)}} ayinikezi amasampula amaningi amaphikseli asezinzile. {{ui:Umsindo okwamukelekayo}} uthi umsindo osele kangakanani lapho iphikseli liyima. Umugqa ongaphansi kwesithombe ukhomba ukuthi iphikseli ngalinye lithole amasampula angaki kangakanani ngokuvamile.
- {{ui:I-caustics (ukukhanya okugoqwa ngeglasi noma okubuyiselwa yizibuko)}} ilandela ukukhanya okuvela ezibanini nelanga, ngakho isiza ingxenye ekhanyayo ngaphansi kwebhola leglasi noma ukukhanya kwesibuko odongeni kuvele kuhlanzekile kunokuba kube nensali. Ivaliwe lapho ukhohlo, intuthu, ukuxuba ukukhanya noma izakhi zokurenda zivuliwe, futhi yenzelwe izithombe, hhayi izithombe ze-360°.
