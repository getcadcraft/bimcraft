## Okuzokwenza

Khetha uhlelo odweba kuwo, ubone lapho lukhona, futhi udwebe kuhlelo oluma phezulu.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula **Dweba** bese uvula imenyu **Akha** (kuledi yesitayela se-ribbon: ithebhu ye-Architecture, Uhlelo Lokusebenza).
2. Khetha **Setha Uhlelo Lokusebenza**. Bese wenza okulodwa kwalokhu:
   - khetha izinga ku-**Setha ku**;
   - khetha uhlelo lwesethenjwa oluqanjiwe ohlwini oluseduze kwawo;
   - chofoza umugqa ku-plan: udonga, umugqa wegridi, uhlelo lwesethenjwa, umugqa wemodeli noma uhlelo lwe-curtain. Uhlelo lokusebenza luba uhlelo oluma phezu kwalowo mugqa;
   - chofoza ubuso obuthe ku-3D view. Uhlelo lokusebenza lulala phezu kwaleso busa.
3. Khetha **Bonisa Uhlelo Lokusebenza** ukuze ulubone: ishidi elinombala ocwebile negridi yamamitha ku-3D view, umugqa noma umbandela ocwebezelwe ku-plan, nombandela ocwebezelwe ku-ipaneli yokubukeka kohlangothi nesigaba esinqunyiwe. Khetha futhi ukuze uyifihle.
4. Khetha **Isibuki Sohlelo Lokusebenza** ukuze ubheke ngqo kuhlelo. Imigqa emnyama imodeli eyanqunywa uhlelo. Sebenzisa amathuluzi aso ukudweba umugqa wemodeli, umbhalo wemodeli noma uhlelo lwesethenjwa kuhlelo. **Bheka kuyo ku-3D** uphendula ku-3D view ukuze ibhekane nohlelo.

## Uhlelo lwesethenjwa

Uhlelo lwesethenjwa uhlelo oluqanjiwe oludwetshwa njengomugqa.

1. Khetha **Uhlelo Lwesethenjwa**. Thayipha igama emgqeni wawo uma ufuna elilodwa (kuzoqanjwa ngokuzenzakalelayo uma ungenzi lutho) nobude.
2. Chofoza lapho iqala khona nalapho iphela khona. Ku-plan lokhu kwenza uhlelo oluma. Ku-Isibuki Sohlelo Lokusebenza kwenza uhlelo olumile ngokuqondile kuhlelo lokusebenza.
3. Lubonakala njengomugqa ocwebezelwe oluhlaza negama lawo ku-plan, nombandela ocwebezelwe ku-3D view nasepanelini lokubukeka kohlangothi nesigaba esinqunyiwe.
4. Khetha ukuze uyiqamba kabusha, ushintshe ubude, ukuphakama nesisekelo sawo, noma ucindezele **Sebenzisa njengohlelo lokusebenza**.

Uhlelo lwesethenjwa olumbili alukwazi ukuba negama elifanayo, ngoba igama yindlela yokukhetha olulodwa.

## Okuwusizo

- Imodeli engenalo uhlelo lokusebenza isebenza ezingeni laleyo elisezansi kakhulu.
- Uhlelo lokusebenza lulondolozwa efayelini. Ukubonisa noma ukufihla kwalo kukhunjulwa kulesi siphequluli, hhayi efayelini.
- Uma uhlelo lokusebenza lulandela uhlelo lwesethenjwa futhi ulunyakazisa, uhlelo lokusebenza nalo luyanyakaza.
- Ohlelweni lokusebenza oluma, amathuluzi Womugqa Womodeli nombhalo Womodeli avula isibuki, ngoba iplan ingakwazi ukubhekisa ebudeni.
- Amathuluzi okudweba abamba iziphetho zohlelo lwesethenjwa oludwetshwe ku-plan ngendlela efanayo abamba ngayo iziphetho zemigqa yabanye ku-viewer.

## Ku-fayela le-IFC

- Uhlelo lokusebenza yisethi yezici (CadCraft_WorkPlane) kuphrojekthi.
- Uhlelo lwesethenjwa yi-IfcAnnotation enohlobo lwento ReferencePlane negama layo. Isimo sayo ingqikithi evaliwe yohlelo, ukuze enye inhlelo ibonise lapho lukhona. Isethi yayo CadCraft_ReferencePlane iqukethe umthombo, isiqondiso, i-normal nobubanzi. I-IFC ayinayo ikilasi lohlelo lwesethenjwa; i-annotation ayibalwa njengento yesakhiwo futhi ayibophi amagumbi.
