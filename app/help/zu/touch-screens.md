I-BimCraft isebenza ngeminwe njengakho ngemouse. Ukuthinta kokuqala kwesikrini, izinkinobho, amathebhu nemigqa yohlu kukhula kube ngosayizi womunwe.

## Imodeli ye-3D

| Ukwenza | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Zungeza | Hudula umunwe owodwa |
| Sondeza | Hlanganisa iminwe emibili noma uyihlukanise |
| Shelelisa | Hudula iminwe emibili ndawonye |
| Khetha into | Thinta kuyo |
| Engeza into ekukhetheni, noma uyisuse | Cindezela uphinde ubambe kuyo isigamu sesekhondi (njengo {{key:Ctrl}}+click) |
| Khetha lutho | Thinta isikhala esingenalutho |

Ukuhlanganisa noma ukudonsa ngeminwe emibili akukaze kukhethe lutho, nanoma umunwe uphakama phezu kwento.

## Amadwebo (DWG ne-DXF)

| Ukwenza | Yenza lokhu |
|---|---|
| Shelelisa | Hudula umunwe owodwa |
| Sondeza | Hlanganisa; indawo phakathi kweminwe yakho ihlala ivele |
| Bonisa ishidi lonke | Thinta kabili, noma uthinte {{ui:Lwanele}} |

Ku-{{ui:3D}} view yedwebo, izenzo zemodeli ziyasebenza: umunwe owodwa uzungeza, iminwe emibili ihlanganise futhi ishelelise, ukuthinta kukhetha, ukucindezela isikhathi eside kungeza ekukhetheni.

## Hamba

Thinta {{ui:Hamba}}, bese uthinta iflowa lapho ufuna ukuma khona. Umugqa wamanikinobho womcibisholo uvela embarini yokuhamba:

- Bamba umcibisholo ukuhamba phambili, emuva, noma uye ohlangothini.
- Uma {{ui:Ukudonsela phansi}} evaliwe, imicibisholo emibili iyaphakama iphinde yehle.
- Hudula umunwe owodwa emodelini ukuze ubheke ngakho konke.
- Thinta {{ui:Kuqediwe}} ukuze uyeke.

## Ukubeka izinto zomtapo

Thinta lapho into ibekwa khona. {{ui:Phendula 90°}} embarini yokubeka iyayiphendula (ukhiye {{key:R}} ekhibhodini), {{ui:Ukubopha kodonga}} iyayibopha ngemuva kodonga oluseduze, bese {{ui:Yeka ukubeka}} iyaphela.

## Linganisa

Thinta iphoyinti lokuqala, bese iphoyinti lesibili. Amaphoyinti abopha emakhoneni nasemiphethweni eseduze nokuthinta kwakho.

:::tip
Uma ikhibhodi ixhunywe ku-ithebhulethi, zonke izinqamuleli zisebenza njengakukhompyutha. Bona [Izinqamuleli zekhibhodi](topic:keyboard).
:::
