## Okuzokwenza

Qala imodeli yesakhiwo kusukela ekuqaleni esikhundleni sokuvula enye. I-BimCraft yenza imodeli ye-IFC engenalutho enephrojekthi, isayithi, isakhiwo namazinga walo. Ungabeka izinsiza nezinto zasendlini kumazinga usuka emtapweni bese uyigcina njengefayela le-IFC.

:::note
Izindonga zingadwetshwa manje: bona [Dweba izindonga](topic:draw-walls). Izimnyango, amawindi, amaphansi nophahla kusakhiwa ngokulandelayo.
:::

## Izinyathelo

1. Chofoza {{ui:Isakhiwo esisha}} ngaphansi kwe-**Vula ifayela noma ifolda**, noma ekhasini lokuqala.
2. Bhala **Igama lesakhiwo**.
3. Khetha ukuthi sinamazinga angaki nge-**Amazinga** (1 kuya ku-200).
4. Bhala **Ukuphakama kwezinga**, kusukela phansi kuya phansi: isibonelo `10'-0"`, `10'6"` noma `3000 mm`. Inani elingenayo iyunithi libe yizinyawo e-US, kwezinye izindawo libe ngamamilimitha.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Dala}}. Amazinga ayakhiwa ngokulandelana kusukela ku-0, ngalinye liphakeme ngokuphakama kwezinga kulelo elingaphambilini, futhi abizwa ngokuthi Izinga 1, Izinga 2 njalo njalo. Abonakala emthini wemodeli, nomdwebo wezinga 1 uvula ngasembonweni we-3D nge-{{ui:Dweba}} ivuliwe.
6. Dweba izindonga: bona [Dweba izindonga](topic:draw-walls). Qamba kabusha amazinga noma shintsha ukuphakama kwawo kupaneli ethi **Amazinga**.
7. Faka izinto: bona [Beka izinsiza nezinto zasendlini emtapweni](topic:place-from-library). Ibha yokubeka ihlu lonke amazinga.
8. Chofoza {{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}}. Ifayela libizwa ngegama lesakhiwo, isibonelo `Smith's house.ifc`.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Imodeli igcinwa ngamamilimitha, njenge-IFC4. Ezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM ziyayivula.
- **Izitezi** ngakwesokunxele zidwala kuphela amazinga anokuthile kuwo; umthi wemodeli uwadwala wonke.
- Kuze kube yilapho ulondoloza, isakhiwo esisha sibalwa njengomsebenzi ongakalondolozwa. I-BimCraft iyabuza ngaphambi kokuvula enye ifayela noma ukuphuma, futhi igcina ikhophi kulesi siphequluli uma ithebhu ivalwa.
