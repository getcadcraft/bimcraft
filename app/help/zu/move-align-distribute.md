## Okuzokwenza

Hambisa udonga, ifulowa, insika noma into yethala ebekiwe iye endaweni entsha, yenze amakhophi ayo, noma uhlele izinto eziningi, ungazidwebanga futhi.

## Amagama nokhiye

Imiyalo ibizwa ngokuthi {{ui:Hambisa}}, {{ui:Linganisa}} no-{{ui:Sabalalisa}}, ungathayipha `MOVE`, `ALIGN` no-`DISTRIBUTE` komugqa womyalo, njengoba kwenziwa ku-drafting app ye-CadCraft. Umehluko owodwa: ku-BimCraft {{key:M}} ingu-Linganisa (Measure), ngakho u-Hambisa unokhiye wakhe, {{key:Shift+M}}. Amathuluzi atholakala kumenyu {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}}.

## Hambisa

1. Khetha okuzohanjiswa, epulanini noma ku-3D view. Izindonga, amafulowa, insika nezinto zethala ezibekiwe zonke zingakhethwa ngokukhethwa okukodwa.
2. Cindezela {{key:Shift+M}} noma khetha {{ui:Hambisa}}.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti lesisekelo, bese iphoyinti lesibili. Noma, emva kwephoyinti lesisekelo, thayipha ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}: liyohamba ngendlela ophawula ngayo ukusuka ephoyinteni lesisekelo. Ungathayipha futhi `dx,dy` (noma `@dx,dy`), noma `ubude<i-engeli`.
4. Cindezela {{key:C}} ukuvula {{ui:Kopisha}}: yonke ukuhamba kuzokwenza amakhophi futhi kulungele okulandelayo. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuyima.

Kokubili amaphoyinti angachofozwa nasku-3D view: nge-Hambisa ivuliwe, isikhombi sihlangana nobude befulowa lezinga, ngakho ukuchofoza lapho kuyiphoyinti lepulani. Bamba {{key:Shift}} uchofoze ukukhetha into enye. Sebenzisa ipulani, phezulu, phambili noma uhlangothi njengoba ufisa: amaphoyinti ahlala esezingeni.

Udonga luhamba nemisebe yalo namafasitela. Ifulowa elidwetshwe lihamba namavulo alo. Amagumbi, amafulowa nezilingi ezilandela izindonga ayavuselelwa. Izindonga ezixhumene nodonga oluhanjisiwe zihlala lapho zazikhona.

Ukuhanjiswa ngakunye kuyinguquko eyodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} eyodwa iyayihlehlisa, futhi iyafika kubo bonke ku-Live TeamWork session. Into yethala ebekiwe ayikwazi ukukopishwa ngale ndlela; sebenzisa {{ui:Hlela}} › {{ui:Kopisha}} no-{{ui:Namathisela}}.

## Linganisa

Khetha izinto ezimbili noma ngaphezulu, khetha {{ui:Linganisa}}, bese uhlangothi (kwesobunxele, esiphakathi, kwesokudla, phezulu, phakathi noma ngaphansi) kanye nalokho okuzolinganiswa kukho: isethi ekhethiwe, okokuqala okukhethiwe, okokugcina okukhethiwe, noma iphoyinti oliphephe. Cindezela {{ui:Linganisa}} kwibha.

## Sabalalisa

Khetha izinto ezintathu noma ngaphezulu, khetha {{ui:Sabalalisa}}, bese uqondisa (ngokuhambisana noma phansi) kanye nokusabalala: amagebe alinganayo phakathi kwazo noma amaphakathi alinganayo. Izinto ezisemaphethelweni amabili zihlala lapho zazikhona.

Izindonga ezilinganiswa noma ezisabalaliswa ziyawagcina amajoyini azo, njengoba kwenziwa ku-Hambisa: udonga oluxhunywe nodonga oluhanjisiwe luhamba ngomkhawulo waluhle ngomugqa walo ukuze luhlale luxhunyiwe, izindonga zokuhlukanisa ezigxumela kulo ziyahamba nalo, nezindonga ezimbili ezixhunyiwe ezombili ezihanjisiwe zigcina ikhona lazo lapho imigqa emisha ihlangana khona. Amagumbi ahlala evaliwe. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa ku-**Izinguquko**.
