## Okuzokwenza

Dweba imigqa nombhalo okuyingxenye yemodeli, hhayi yombono owodwa.

## Izinyathelo

1. Setha indawo yokusebenza kuqala (bheka **Indawo yokusebenza**). Indiza yezinga idwetshwa epulanini; indiza emile idwetshwa ku-Work Plane Viewer.
2. Ngomugqa khetha **Umugqa wemodeli** kumenyu **Ukwakhiwa** bese ukhetha **Umugqa**, **Iphola-layini** noma **Umnsalo (amaphoyinti amathathu)**:
   - umugqa ngochofo ababili;
   - iphola-layini ngochofo lwephoyinti ngalinye, bese {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze kabili. Ukuchofoza iphoyinti lokuqala kuyivala;
   - umnsalo ngochofo ezintathu: ukuqala kwawo, iphoyinti edlula kulo, nokuphela kwawo.
3. Ombhalweni khetha **Umbhalo wemodeli**. Thayipha amagama, setha ukuphakama kwamakhaphithali nokujula, bese uchofoza lapho umbhalo uqala khona (uhlangothi lwesobunxele lwesisekelo sawo).
4. Khetha umugqa noma umbhalo ukuze uwubone ku-**Izici**. Amagama, ukuphakama nokujula kombhalo kungashintshwa lapho. Susa kususa kona.

## Lapho ziboniswa khona

- Umugqa wemodeli ubonakala epulanini yezinga lawo, ku-3D view, ekubukeni kohlangothi nakupanele yesigaba, nakwi-Work Plane Viewer uma uwuthwele endaweni yokusebenza. Umugqa wokudweba (Chaza, Umugqa) ungowombono owodwa kuphela.
- Umbhalo wemodeli uyijometri eqinile: ubonakala nakuphi lapho imodeli ibonakala khona, epulanini, ku-3D, ezinhlangothini nasezigabeni, nasemashidini.
- Imigqa yemodeli ayikadwetshwa emashidini aphrintiwe. Umbhalo wemodeli uyadwetshwa.

## Ifonti

Umbhalo wemodeli usebenzisa ifonti efanayo namashidi kanye nama-PDF: Liberation Sans, ngaphansi kwe-SIL Open Font License. Uhlamvu ifonti angalunaki lubonakala njengophawu lombuzo futhi uyatshelwa ukuthi yiluphi. Kukhona ifonti eyodwa nobukhulu obubodwa. Izinhlamvu zibekwa ngobubanzi bifonti yazo, ngaphandle kwe-kerning.

## Kwifayela le-IFC

- Umugqa wemodeli ungu-IfcAnnotation onohlobo lwento ModelLine. Isimo sawo siyijometri ye-3D: iphola-layini, noma umjikelezo osikiwe womnsalo.
- Umbhalo wemodeli ungu-IfcBuildingElementProxy onohlobo lwento ModelText. Isimo sawo siyisisekelo esisodwa esikhishiwe ngakunye kwesiqephu sohlamvu, namaholo ezinhlamvu ezifana no-O no-B. Izinga lawo liyindawo yesiphimbo nevolumu (indawo yesiphimbo ephindwe ngojuqu).

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Noma yiliphi usayizi lenziwa njengoba ucela. Kuphela umugqa ongenabude, umnsalo onamaphoyinti amathathu emugqeni owodwa, noma umbhalo ongenazinhlamvu ayenqatshelwa, ngoba akukho okokwenza.
- Umugqa ngamunye, nombhalo noma ushintsho kuwo, kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuHlehlisa.
