## Okuzokwenza

Dweba izindonga zesakhiwo epulanini lalo lezinga ngalinye. Chofoza lapho udonga luqala khona, bese uchofoza ikhona ngalinye; BimCraft ijoyina amakhona ngokwayo, inikeze udonga ngalunye izingqimba zalo (ukugqoka, ukumboza, izinsika, udonga lwebhodi …) futhi ilikhuphule kuze kufike esitezini esingaphezulu. Ukubuka kwe-3D kubonisa izindonga njengoba udweba.

## Vula ipulani

1. Qala isakhiwo esisha ([Qala isakhiwo esisha](topic:start-a-new-building)) noma vula imodeli ye-IFC.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Draw}} kwithuluzi. Isakhiwo esisha sivulwa sesivulekile ngokwaso.
3. Ipulani yezinga ibonakala ngakwesokunxele, nokubuka kwe-3D ngakwesokudla. Khetha izinga ohlwini oluphezulu kwipulani, noma ngenkinobho eyindilinga eseceleni kwalo kuphaneli **Amazinga**.
4. {{ui:Plan}}, {{ui:Split}} no-{{ui:3D}} ngamathuluzi aseqenjini {{ui:View}} ethulusini (imiyalo PLAN, SPLIT nezinkinobho zisasebenza njengakuqala). Sebenzisa {{ui:Plan}} ukubona ipulani yodwa, {{ui:Split}} ukubona kokubili, {{ui:3D}} ukuvala ipulani. Uma iwindi linqunyiwe (noma usayizi wesixhumanisi mkhulu), {{ui:Split}} ibeka ipulani phezulu kokubuka kwe-3D esikhundleni sokuba eceleni kwakho.

Ipulani inquma izinga ngo-4'-0" (1200 mm) ngaphezulu kwesakhiwo salo, njengoba kwenziwa ipulani yokwakha. Skrola ukuze usondeze; hudula ngokuchofoza inkinobho ephakathi noma yesokudla (noma ubambe {{key:Space}} uhudule) ukuze ushelelise; uma {{ui:Select}} ivulekile, ukuhudula okujwayelekile nakho kuyashelelisa. {{key:F}} ngesikhombi phezu kwepulani kuyayilinganisa.

## Dweba igumbi

1. Chofoza {{ui:Wall}} (noma cindezela {{key:W}}).
2. Khetha uhlobo lodonga ohlwini lokuqala, isibonelo **Exterior 2×6** noma **Interior 2×4**.
3. Khetha ukuphakama kwalo: **Kuze kube esitezini esingaphezulu** (udonga luyalandela uma leso sitezi sinyakaza), **Kuze kube** izinga eliphakeme kakhulu ngodonga oludlula ezingqimbeni eziningi (indawo yokwehla, igumbi elinobude obuphindwe kabili, igable ephakeme), noma **Ukuphakama okumisiwe** bese uthayipha kona. Udonga olunjalo luyingxenye yesitezi owadwebe kuso; amapulani ezinga adlula kuwo awubonisa ngokukhanya okuncane, amagumbi lapho avalelana nawo, futhi ungachofoza izimnyango namawindi ngawo kulawo mapulani (umnyango umi phezu kwephansi lalelo zinga). Ukuze uhambise noma ushintshe ushebe walo udonga ngokwalo, iya esitezini oqala kulo.
4. Chofoza lapho udonga lokuqala luqala khona.
5. Chofoza ikhona ngalinye. Ukuchofoza ngakunye kwenza udonga; udonga olulandelayo luqala lapho owedlule ephela khona. Ubude nokuguquka kwe-engeli kubonakala eduze kwesikhombi.
6. Chofoza iphoyinti lokuqala futhi ukuvala igumbi. Umugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi igumbi linezindonga ezingaki, futhi ngemodi yomugqa wephakathi, udonga ngalunye luthola ungqimba lwalo lwangaphandle ngaphandle kwegumbi.
7. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} (noma chofoza ngesokudla, noma chofoza kabili) ukuze umise ngaphandle kokuvala.

Amakhona ajoyina ngokwawo: izindonga ezimbili zihlangana ngomthamo we-mitre, udonga oluphela kolunye lumi ngobuso balo (i-T), nezindonga ezintathu noma ngaphezulu endaweni eyodwa zabelana ngekhona. Izindonga ezidlula ngaphakathi kwezinye ziyashiywa njengoba zinjalo.

Igumbi lonke ngasikhathi sinye: vula {{ui:Shapes}} ukuze uthole irektangela, irektangela ejikiyo, ipholigoni, imijikelezo, izindonga emigqeni ekhethiwe noma emkhawulweni ojikijelwe (bona [Izimo zodonga, ukuhlukanisa, ukubeka phezu, ukweluleka nokukhiya](topic:wall-tools)).

## Khetha isithombe sokudweba

Khetha **Centerline**, **Inside face** noma **Outside face** ngaphambi kokuchofoza kokuqala. Uma kukhethwe ubuso, ukuchofoza kwakho nobude othayipha kuchaza lobo buso; BimCraft ishintsha umugqa wephakathi wodonga ngesigamu sobukhulu obuphelele futhi ijoyina amakhona alandelanayo. Khetha **Outside on left** noma **Outside on right** ngokuhambisana nendlela odweba ngayo. Ukuze uhlaka oluvaliwe udwetshwe ngokuphambene nokushintsha kwewashi, ingaphandle lisesokudla; ngokushintsha kwewashi, lisekhohlo. Ngo-**Inside face** umzimba wodonga uya ohlangothini lwangaphandle lomugqa wakho; nge-**Outside face**, ngakolunye uhlangothi. Ukudweba ngobuso kugcina uhlangothi lwangaphandle oluzikhethele uma uvala umjikelezo. Emugqeni wephakathi, izindonga ezingavali igumbi zithatha uhlangothi lwangaphandle olukhethiwe; ukuvala igumbi kubeka wonke udonga ngaphandle kwegumbi ngokwawo, noma udwebe ngayiphi indlela.

Ukushintsha uhlobo lodonga, isithombe noma uhlangothi lwangaphandle kuqeda ucingo lwamanje. Eduze kwekhona elibuyela emuva, ama-mitre amade amiswa ngokushiya izimbobo ezisikwe ngesikwele.

Ngenkathi udweba, iphoyinti eliphelayo endaweni engenalutho lingalinganiswa ngokuthi lilingane ngokuvundlile noma ngokuthi lilingane nesiphetho sodonga noma nekhona langaphambili. Umugqa onamachashaza uxhumanisa leliphoyinti nesikhombi esinamathelisiwe. Shift igcina indlela yokudweba ilingana; ukulinganisa kuyeka ngaleyo ndlela. Amaphoyinti aseduze, izimaphakathi nezinqamuleli zezindonga zithatha ukubaluleka kunokulinganisa okukude.

## Umugqa wendawo

Uma **Reference** isethelwe ku-**Outside face** noma **Inside face**, udonga ugcina lobo buso njengomugqa wendawo walo. Kamuva, uma udonga lunqenqema noma uhlangothi lwalo lwangaphandle luphenduliwe, lobo buso luhlala lapho bukhona futhi udonga lukhula noma luphenduke ngalo. Udonga oluqondile oluhlangana nalo ekhoneni luyalandela, ngakho ikhona liyavaleka. Ungashintsha umugqa wendawo kamuva ku-**Properties**: bona [Shintsha udonga, umnyango noma iwindi](topic:change-a-wall-or-door).

## Dweba udonga oluqhathaniswe

1. Chofoza {{ui:Wall}}, bese ukhetha **Curved** ohlwini **Shape** somugqa wezinketho.
2. Chofoza lapho udonga luqala khona, bese lapho luphela khona.
3. Chofoza iphoyinti emjikelezweni walo. Noma thayipha ukuphakama kwejika bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.
4. Uchungechunge luqhubeka emagameni alo udonga. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze uliqede.

Udonga oluqhathaniswe lujoyina izindonga ezikuphelo zalo (amakhona nokujoyina okuyi-T kuyasebenza), lenza amagumbi, futhi lubambe izimnyango namawindi eduze komjikelezo walo. Ayisekho ngokuqondile, ilingana nodonga emaphakathini ayo, futhi ibanga layo lilinganiswa emjikelezweni. Udonga lungaphenduka okungenani amakota amathathu omjikelezo. Lulingana neziphetho zalo nephakathi lomjikelezo wayo.

Okungakakhiwa ngodonga oluqhathaniswe: {{ui:Trim / extend}} no-{{ui:Corner}}, ukwakha ifreyimu, izilinganiso zepulani ezizenzakalelayo nokulinganisa emjikelezweni. Bona [Okungakakhiwa](topic:not-built-yet).

## Thayipha ubude esikhundleni sokuchofoza

Ngemva kokuchofoza kokuqala, thayipha bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}:

| Thayipha | Idweba |
|---|---|
| `12'6"` noma `3810` | udonga olunobude obufanayo, ngendlela isikhombi esiyibonisa |
| `12'6"<90` | obude obufanayo, ngale ngu-engeli (amadigri, kuya ngakwesobunxele ngokushintsha kwewashi kusuka kwesokudla) |
| `@3000,0` | kuleliphoyinti elikude kangaka nelokugcina, ngokuqondile nangaphezulu |
| `1200,3400` | kuleliphoyinti epulanini |

Ukuthayipha inombolo ngenkathi ithuluzi lika-Wall livulekile kuya ngqo ebhokisini le-{{ui:Length}}. Ngamafithi namainshi, inombolo engenalutho iyinshi; ngamamilimitha iyamamilimitha. Ungaphawula noma yiyiphi iyunithi: `3.5 m`, `10 ft`, `6 1/2"`.

## Izinamathisi

- Iziphetho zodonga, amakhona ezinhlangothi zalo nezimaphakathi zodonga ziyanamathela lapho isikhombi sisondele kuzo (isikwele, unxantathu).
- Isikhombi siyanamathela komugqa wephakathi wodonga noma kunoma yiluphi uhlangothi (isiphambano).
- Kusukela kwiphoyinti lokugcina, indlela ibamba njalo ngama-15° uma iseduze; bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze ibambe ku-0°, 90°, 180° noma 270°.
- Izindlela ezikhululekile zizungeza ubude ku-1/2" (noma ku-10 mm).

## Shintsha, hlehlisa, susa

- Udonga ngalunye lulinyathelo elilodwa ku-**Izinguquko**: {{ui:Undo}} no-{{ui:Redo}} buyisela izindonga emuva bese ziphinde ziyabuyela.
- Uma {{ui:Select}} ivulekile, chofoza udonga epulanini ukuze ukhethe; ukubuka kwe-3D no-**Properties** kulandela. {{key:Delete}} isusa izindonga ezikhethiwe, bese izindonga abazijoyine zihlanzwa futhi.

## Amazinga

Ipaneli ye-**Amazinga** ibala onke amazinga kanye nokuphakama kwawo nobude bawo.

- Thayipha igama elisha, ukuphakama noma ubude bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ukushintsha ukuphakama (noma ubude) kwezinga kuhambisa amazinga aphezulu ngalo mbala, nezindonga ezikhuphuka kuze kufike ezingeni ziyalandela.
- {{ui:Add a level on top}} yengeza izinga phezu kwelisezingeni eliphezulu, elifana nalo ngobude.
- {{ui:Add a level below}} yengeza izinga ngaphansi kwelisezingeni eliphansi (iseli), elifana nalo ngobude: ukuphakama kwalo kuba negative uma izinga eliphansi lisesingeni le-0, futhi awekho amanye amazinga ahamba. Undo iyasisusa.
- {{ui:Copy this level…}} idweba kabusha okusesingeni olibukayo ezingeni elinye, noma esingeni elisha phezulu: izindonga zalo nezimnyango namawindi azo, amaphansi nezingqimba (hhayi okhuni). Izindonga ezikhuphuka kuze kufike esingeni elingaphezulu ziyakhuphuka kuze kufike esingeni elingaphezulu kwelisha; amagumbi avela ngokwawo. Eyodwa i-{{ui:Undo}} iyabuyisela konke emuva. Uma elinye izinga selinezindonga, ikhophi iyengezwa kuzo.
- {{ui:Units}} ethulusini ikhetha iyunithi (amafithi namainshi, amainshi, amafithi ayizinkomba, amamilimitha, amasentimitha noma amamitha) kukho konke ithuluzi lokudweba elikubonisayo nalifunda.

## Gcina

Chofoza {{ui:Save as IFC}}. Udonga ngalunye lutholakala njenge-IFC wall ngomugqa wawo wephakathi, ukwakheka kwawo okujoyiniwe, uhlobo lwawo nezingqimba zawo, `Pset_WallCommon` nezilinganiso eziyisisekelo (ubude, ukuphakama, izindawo nevolumu), ukuze ezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM zivule futhi zihlele.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Izindonga zidwetshwa emugqeni wazo wephakathi. Ukuqina kuvela ohlotsheni lodonga.
- Ipulani ibonisa izindonga ezidwetshwe lapha zigcwele, nakho konke okunye (izinto zethala, amamodeli avela kwezinye izinhlelo) kunqunywe indiza efanayo, nalokho okusezansi kokusikwa kubonakala ngemigqa emincane.
- Izindonga ezenziwe ezinye izinhlelo zibonakala epulanini kodwa azikwazi ukushintshwa ngalawa mathuluzi okwamanje.
