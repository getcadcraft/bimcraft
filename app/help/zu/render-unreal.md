Ku-CadCraft Studio uhlu lwe-Injini lungaba nesixhumanisi kuphrojekthi ye-Unreal Engine. Indlela eyodwa kuphela: kusuka ku-CadCraft iye ku-Unreal.

## Thumela isimo

1. Khetha inkombandlela ye-Unreal ohlwini lwe-Injini. Okokuqala, i-Studio icela ukuthi ukhethe ifolda yephrojekthi yakho (enefayela le-`.uproject`); iyayikhumbula.
2. Chofoza {{ui:Thumela}}. I-Studio ibhala `CadCraft/<igama lefayela>/` ngaphakathi kwephrojekthi: isimo njengefayela le-USD, ifayela lezilungiselelo, amaphrofayili okukhanya, namafayela amabili amancane esendlalelo.
3. Shintsha imodeli, londoloza bese uthumela futhi. Kuphela isimo nefayela lezilungiselelo ziyashintshwa.

## Okwakho kuphephile

Isiteji evulwa yi-Unreal iyisendlalelo esincane esenziwa kanye, nezendlalelo ezimbili ngaphansi kwayo: **eyakho** phezulu (`<igama>.user.usda`, ingenalutho ekuqaleni, ayikaze ibhalwe kabusha yi-CadCraft) nesimo se-CadCraft ngaphansi. Ukuhlelwa okulondolozela esendlalelweni yakho kuhlala uma isimo sithunyelwa futhi. Izibani nezinye izinto ozibeka kuleveli ye-Unreal ziphandle kwesiteji futhi azithintwa nazo.

## Ngaphandle kwe-Studio

Kuwebhu, noma kukhompyutha lapho i-Studio ingathola i-Unreal, khetha **Ifolda yephrojekthi ye-Unreal (.zip)** ohlwini lwe-Injini bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}}. Ibhala ifolda efanayo njenge-zip: yiyiphathe ifolda ye-`CadCraft` yephrojekthi yakho. Yishiye ivuliwe **Faka amafayela esiteji** okokuqala; yicime ekulondolozeni okulandelayo, ukuze i-zip iqukathe isimo nefayela lezilungiselelo kuphela, nesendlalelo sakho singabhalwa kabusha.

:::note
Ipulaginini ye-Unreal evula isiteji, isebenzisa ilanga, izibani, amakhamera nokuvezwa, ilandela ukuthunyelwa ngakunye futhi ikhiphe ikhamera **ayikakhishwa**, futhi ayikaze iqhutshwe ku-Unreal. Kuze kube yilapho, Thumela ifaka amafayela ephrojekthini futhi amathuluzi we-USD e-Unreal angawavula. I-Windows yesistimu yokuqala; i-macOS iyalandela.
:::
