## Okuzokwenza

Sula umbono ufike kulokho obukela kuwo: fihla izinto ezikuvimbayo, bonisa ezikhethile kuphela, noma gcina konke okunye kusekuboneni njengomugqa oyisithunzi ukuze usazi lapho ukhona.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC. Bona [Vula imodeli ye-IFC ubheke ngo-3D](topic:open-ifc-model).
2. Chofoza into ukuze uyikhethe. Ukuze ukhethe eminye, bamba {{key:Mod}} noma {{key:Shift}} bese uchofoza ngayinye. Ukuchofoza into ekhethiwe ngalendlela iyayisusa.
3. Vula {{ui:Show}} kumbhalo wamathuluzi bese ukhetha okufanele ukwenziwe, noma cindezela ukhiye waso:
   - {{ui:Hide}} ({{key:H}}) ifihla izinto ezikhethiwe.
   - {{ui:Isolate}} ({{key:I}}) ibonisa izinto ezikhethiwe kuphela.
   - {{ui:See-through}} ({{key:T}}) ibonisa izinto ezikhethiwe njengoba zinjalo, nakho konke okunye ngokusobala.
4. Chofoza {{ui:Show all}} kumenyu efanayo, noma cindezela {{key:Shift+H}}, ukuze ubonise zonke izinto futhi.
5. Ukuze usike konke okuzungeze izinto ezikhethiwe esikhundleni salokho, chofoza {{ui:Section box round the pick}} kumenyu efanayo, noma cindezela {{key:Shift+X}}. Bona [Sika imodeli ye-IFC ngomkhawulo wesigaba](topic:section-plane).

## Okufanele ukubone

- Uma kukhethwe izinto eziningi, {{ui:Properties}} ibala ukuthi zingaki futhi ziziluhla ngohlobo. Chofoza enye yodwa ukuze ubone izici zayo.
- Izinto ezifihliwe azikwazi ukukhethwa: ukuchofoza kufinyelela lokho okungemuva kwazo.
- Izinto ezibonakala ngokusobala nazo azikwazi ukukhethwa, ngakho ungachofoza uye ngqo ngodonga uye kokuhlukanisile.
- Imiphetho ifihlwa nento yayo, futhi ibe yisithunzi nayo.
- Ukufihla, ukuhlukanisa nokubona ngokusobala kuhlala njengoba kunjalo uma uhlela, wengeza into ethonjeni noma usika imodeli ngomkhawulo wesigaba. Ukuvula enye imodeli kubonisa konke futhi.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Izinketho zemenyu azisebenzi (zifiphele).** Akukho okukhethiwe. Chofoza into kuqala; beka isikhombisi kunto ukuze ufunde ukuthi kungani ingenza.
- **Ukuhlukanisa kubonise udonga owawufihlile.** Ukuhlukanisa kubonisa kuphela okukhethile, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi kufihliwe yini.
- **Isitezi sonke sisweleka.** Lokho kuluhlu lwe-{{ui:Storeys}}, hhayi {{ui:Show all}}: faka itiki kusitezi futhi.
