## Okuzokwenza

Beka ibanga phakathi kwemigqa yegridi kanye nosayizi jikelele wegridi kumapulani, ngaphandle kwamabhamuza, ukuze amashidi akhiwe kuwo aphathe izilinganiso. Udinga igridi kuqala: bheka [Igridi nezinsika](topic:grids-and-columns).

## Izinyathelo

1. Bonisa ipulani lezinga bese uchofoza {{ui:Grid}} ukuze uvule ithuluzi legridi. Umugqa wezinketho zegridi uyavela.
2. Faka itiki ku-{{ui:Dimensions}}. Ibanga phakathi kwemigqa eseduze ebalwe ngezinombolo nephakathi kwemigqa eseduze ebalwe ngezinhlamvu, kanye nosayizi jikelele ngayo yombili imiqondo, kudwetshwa kupulani.
3. Khetha elinye izinga: izilinganiso ezifanayo ziyabonakala kupulani lalo.
4. Hambisa umugqa, uqamba kabusha noma wengeze elinye. Izilinganiso ziyalandela igridi.
5. Susa itiki ku-{{ui:Dimensions}} ukuze uzisuse futhi.

## Amanothi

- Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyasihlehlisa.
- Isilungiselelo sigcinwa ku-IFC kanye negridi, namapulani abekwe emashidini aphatha izilinganiso.
- Uma imodeli ingenayo igridi ongayishintsha (igridi eyafika kufayela elivela kolunye uhlelo ayishintshwa lapha), umugqa wesimo uthi "Ayikho igridi yokushintsha."
