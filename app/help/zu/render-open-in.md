Khetha okuthi **Vula ku…** ohlwini lwe-Injini engxoxweni {{ui:Renda}} ukuthatha isimo siye kolunye uhlelo. Akukho okubuyela kumodeli.

## Ifayela lanoma yiluphi uhlelo

- **Ifayela le-USD** lilondoloza isimo njenge-`.usdc` (noma `.usdz` uma imodeli inezithombe): [ifayela elifanayo njengalelo Londoloza njenge-USD](topic:usd), nezinto zokwakha, izibani namakhamera. Ligcina ukuphindeka nezinani eliqondile zezinto zokwakha. Iningi lezinjini zokurenda, izinjini zemidlalo nezibukeli lifunda i-USD.
- **Ifayela le-glTF (.glb)** lilondoloza ifayela elikhanyayo le-glTF 2.0 ngezinto zokwakha, izithombe zokwakha nezibani; bona [Thumela imodeli kuhlelo lokurenda](topic:model-for-rendering) ukuthola okulifakayo nokungalifaki.
- **Ifolda yephrojekthi ye-Unreal (.zip)** lilondoloza ifolda [Thumela isimo kwinjini yomdlalo](topic:render-unreal) ibhala, yephrojekthi kunoma iyiphi ikhompyutha.

Zombili zisebenza kuwebhu nakuStudio.

## Ku-Studio: vula kuhlelo

Studio ibhala uhlu lwezinhlelo ezitholakala kule khompyutha ezingavula isimo: i-usdview uma isendleleni yakho yokusesha, nohlelo lwekhompyutha lwalolo hlobo lwefayela. Khetha olulodwa, khetha i-USD noma i-glTF lapho lufunda zombili, bese uchofoza {{ui:Renda}}. Studio ibhala ipakethe efolda entsha ngaphansi kwe-Amadokhumenti (`CadCraft hand-off`; akukho okubhalwa phezu kwakho, ukuthumela ngakunye kuthola efolda yakho), bese iqala uhlelo kuyo, bese ikhomba lapho amafayela akhona.

| Uhlelo | Lufunda kangcono |
|---|---|
| usdview | USD |
| Uhlelo lwekhompyutha | Noma yini ikhompyutha inayo ngohlobo lwefayela |

Studio iqala kuphela izinhlelo kulolu hlu oluqinile; ayikwenzi lutho okucelwa yikhasi ngegama, futhi ayiqali uhlelo olunelayisensi ye-GNU GPL (bona [Dweba isithombe ngolunye uhlelo](topic:render-other-program#programs-cadcraft-never-runs)). Noma yiluphi uhlelo olufunda i-USD noma i-glTF luvula ifayela elisuka ku-**Ifayela lanoma yiluphi uhlelo** lapha phezulu.

:::note
I-Twinmotion, i-Enscape nezinye izinhlelo zokubonisa azikho kulolu hlu: i-CadCraft ayikahlolwa nazo futhi ayithi lutho ngazo. Ifayela le-glTF noma le-USD kusuka lapha yindlela yokungena; bona izinkomba ku-[izinjini zokurenda ze-plug-in](topic:model-for-rendering#enscape-and-other-plug-in-renderers).
:::
