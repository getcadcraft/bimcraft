## Okuzokwenza

Vula umdwebo we-CAD, ubone ngo-3D noma ngo-2D, uhambe kuwo, uguqule phakathi kwendawo yemodeli namashidi awo, bese ufunda amakhoordinethi. I-BimCraft ibonisa imidwebo ye-DWG ne-DXF; ayihleli noma ilondoloze. Ukuze ushintshe, chofoza {{ui:Hlela ku-CadCraft}}: umdwebo uvulwa ohlelweni lokudweba lwe-CadCraft ethebhini elisha.

## Izinyathelo

1. Chofoza {{ui:Khetha ifayela}}, noma wehlise umdwebo ekhasini.
2. Khetha ifayela `.dwg` noma `.dxf`.
3. Linda kuze umugqa wesimo uthi "Ifunda umdwebo…". Imidwebo emikhulu ithatha imizuzwana. Bese uthi kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani ukufunda, "Kuyarenda…" uma kudwetshwa isithombe sokuqala, nokuthi kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani lokho.
4. Umdwebo uvula ngo-3D, indlela esheshayo yokubonisa umdwebo omkhulu (bheka [Bona umdwebo we-DWG noma we-DXF nge-3D](topic:dwg-in-3d)). Chofoza {{ui:Imodeli}} ukuze uthole ukubuka okulinganayo kwe-2D. Ukuze imidwebo ivule njalo nge-2D, setha {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} › {{ui:BimCraft}} › **Imidwebo ivulwa ku-** ibe {{ui:Imodeli (2D)}}. Umdwebo ongenalutho endaweni yemodeli, noma isiphequluli esingeke sibonise i-3D, uvula nge-2D.
5. Bheka amathebhu aphezulu womdwebo: {{ui:Imodeli}} kuqala, bese ithebhu eyodwa ngeshidi ngalinye, ngokulandelana komdwebo ngokwawo.
6. Chofoza ithebhu leshidi ukuze ubone leshidi: iphepha, nemodeli ebonakala ngamawindi okubuka ayo.
7. Chofoza {{ui:Imodeli}} ukuze ubuyele endaweni yemodeli.
8. Hambisa ipoyinta phezu komdwebo ufunde ama-X no-Y ekhoneni.

## Okufanele ubone

- Umdwebo, ulingana newindi. Imibala ilandela umdwebo; imigqa emhlophe (umbala 7) idwetshwa ngombala wombhalo wetimu, nemibala ekhanyayo iyagqokisiswa ethimini elikhanyayo ukuze ihlale ifundeka.
- Ipaneli {{ui:Umdwebo}} ngakwesobunxele: ifayela, usayizi, ifomethi (isibonelo "AutoCAD 2018 (AC1032)"), izinyunithi, inani lezinto nezingqimba, inombolo yamashidi, nezidwebo ezixhunyiwe nezinsiza uma zikhona.
- Uhlu {{ui:Izingqimba}} ngakwesobunxele.
- Ngakwesokunene: indlela yokuhamba, imidwebo nezithombe ezixhunyiwe, no-**Akukadwetshwa**.

## Amablokhi wamandla

Ibhlokhi lamandla lidwetshwa njengoba umdwebo uyibonisa: ibhlokhi ngalinye elibekiwe esimweni sokubonakala umdwebo owagcina ngaso ("Plan" noma "Front", "Single bowl" noma "Double bowl"). Ngakwesokunene, **Amablokhi wamandla** ibala zonke ezibekwe endaweni yemodeli: zingaki ezibekiwe, izimo ezibonisa, nezimo zonke ibhlokhi linazo. Ku-{{ui:3D}}, chofoza ibhlokhi elibekiwe ukuze ubone **Ibhlokhi lamandla**, **Isimo esibonisiwe** nezinye izimo kuphaneli {{ui:Izici}}. I-BimCraft ayishintshi izimo.

## Akukadwetshwa

Ezinye izinhlobo zezinto azidwetshwa kule nguqulo. Zibalwa ngaphansi kwe-**Akukadwetshwa** ngakwesokunene ("Lezi zingxenye zomdwebo zidlulwa okwamanje, azicatshangwa."), ukuze wazi ukuthi kukhona okushiyiwe. Imigqa, iphola-layini, umnsalo, indilinga, i-ellipse, i-spline, imigqa yokufiphaza nokugcwaliswa okuqinile, umbhalo nombhalo wemigqa eminingi, amablokhi anezici, izilinganiso, imigqa yokuholela nemigqa eminingi yokuholela, amatafula, izithombe namawindi okubuka kuyadwetshwa. Ama-3D faces, ama-polyface mesh ne-polygon mesh adwetshwa njengemiphetho yawo ebonakala ukusuka phezulu; shintsha ku-{{ui:3D}} ukuze uwabone njengezindwangu.

I-DXF eyinari ayivuli: i-BimCraft iyasho lokho. Yilondoloze futhi njenge-ASCII DXF, noma njenge-DWG, kusuka ohlelweni olwayenza.

## Izinkinga ezijwayelekile

- **Umdwebo ubukeka ungenalutho.** Ezinye izingqimba zingase zivaliwe noma zikhenyiwe emdwebeni ngokwawo; zifika zingakhethiwe ku-{{ui:Izingqimba}}. Chofoza {{ui:Konke kuvuliwe}}. Uma isimo sithi "*igama* ngumdwebo ongenalutho: awunayo imigqa, umbhalo noma amablokhi.", kayikho lutho kuwo.
- **Ishidi libonisa uhlaka olungenalutho.** Ishidi lingase lincike emidwebeni exhunyiwe. Bheka [Thola imidwebo nezithombe ezixhunyiwe](topic:dwg-linked-files).
- **Umbhalo ubukeka umehluka kancane.** I-BimCraft udweba umbhalo ngefonti elincane elingenazo izibambo, hhayi amafonti omdwebo, ngakho umbhalo ungase ube mkhulu noma mncane kancane kunasohlelweni olwawenza.
- **"*igama* alikwazanga ukufundwa. Lingase lonakale, noma lilondolozwe ngefomethi umfundi ongakasekeli."** Ibhulaketi ekugcineni liqamba ikhodi yefomethi yefayela. Yilondoloze ohlelweni olwayenza njenge-DWG endala noma njenge-DXF, bese uzama futhi.
- **Ulindele ukukala, ukudweba noma ukuhlela.** I-BimCraft ibukela imidwebo kuphela. Chofoza {{ui:Hlela ku-CadCraft}} ukuze uvule ifayela elifanayo ohlelweni lokudweba lwe-CadCraft, olihlela futhi lilondoloze ikhophi njenge-DWG noma i-DXF. Ifayela lakho lokuqala alishintshwa, nemidwebo exhunyiwe ihlala ku-BimCraft. Inkinobho ikhombisa kuphela lapho i-CadCraft ivulekile kuwe.
