## Okuzokwenziwa

Thegisa amagumbi nezindawo ngayinye noma zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa, bese uhambisa ithegi lapho lifundeka kahle.

## Lapho kukhona amathuluzi

- Izakhiwo ze-Ribbon: {{ui:Architecture}} › **Igumbi nendawo** › {{ui:Tag Room}} ne-{{ui:Tag Area}}.
- Isakhiwo sakudala: ibha ye-Draw › imenyu ye-{{ui:Rooms}}.
- Igumbi noma indawo ekhethiwe: Properties › {{ui:Add a tag}}.

## Izinyathelo

1. Khetha {{ui:Tag Room}} bese uchofoza egumbini, lapho ithegi kufanele libe khona.
2. Noma cindezela {{ui:Tag all untagged}} ohlwini: wonke amagumbi aleli zinga angenalo ithegi athola elilodwa, emaphakathini awo. Yisinyathelo esisodwa sokuHlehlisa.
3. Ukuze uhambise ithegi,liyikhethe ngo-Khetha bese uliphonsa. I-Properties inawo {{ui:Put it back in the middle}}.
4. Okufanayo kusebenza ku-{{ui:Tag Area}} yezindawo.

## Okwenziwa yithegi

- Ikhomba inombolo, igama kanye nendawo, ngamayunithi wokubonisa akho.
- Ilandela igumbi lalo: qamba kabusha igumbi, hambisa udonga, namazwi nendawo yethegi kushintsha ngesinyathelo esifanayo.
- Ilinganiswa ephepheni: {{ui:Text height on paper}} ikalwa ngamamilimitha (2.5 ukuqala). Ekubukeni kwe-1:50 ithegi elingu-2.5 mm libe ngu-125 mm ebukeni lemodeli; ku-1:100 libe ngu-250 mm.
- Igumbi elinethegi likhombisa ithegi esikhundleni selebhili layo elijwayelekile.
- Uma igumbi noma indawo yalo inyamalala, ithegi liyanyamalala nalo. I-Hlehlisa ibuyisela kokubili.

## Ku-IFC

Ithegi liyi-IfcAnnotation (ObjectType ROOMTAG noma AREATAG) nombhalo walo njengombhalo oqondile, ne-GlobalId yalokho okubizayo. Umbhalo ofayeleni unikeza indawo ngamamitha ayisikwele.

## Akukakwakhiwa okwamanje

- Izimo zamathegi ozenzela wena, nomugqa oholayo.
- Amathegi emasangweni, emawindini nasezindongeni yithuluzi elihlukile (bheka izitayela zamathegi zamashidi).
