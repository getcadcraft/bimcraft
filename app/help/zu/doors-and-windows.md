## Okuzokwenza

Beka iminyango namafasiteli ezindongeni ozidwebile ([Dweba izindonga](topic:draw-walls)). Phonsa udonga bese umnyango noma ifasiteli kukhombisa lapho kungahle kuhlale khona, lapho kuvuleka khona nokuthi kukhashelwe kangakanani kuwo wonke amakhoni. Cofa, bese ukuvuleka kusika kuzo udonga kwipulani nakwi-3D.

## Beka umnyango

1. Ngo-{{ui:Dweba}} svuliwe, cofa {{ui:Umnyango}} (noma cindezela {{key:D}}).
2. Khetha uhlobo: **Single swing door**, **Double swing door**, **Sliding door**, **Pocket door**, **Garage door**, **Bifold closet door**, **French doors**, **Barn door (on the wall)** noma **Entry door with sidelites**. Usayizi wawo ojwayelekile ugcwalisa **W** no-**H**; khetha esinye kuhlu lwezinhlobo eduze kwawo, noma uthayipha omunye, umzekelo `2'-8"` noma `800`. Uhlu lunikeza nemindeni yeminyango yalo modeli (bona [Imindeni nezinhlobo zayo](topic:families)).
3. Phonsa udonga. Isimo sommnyango silandela iphoyinti ngomugqa wodonga, nebanga kusuka kwelinye lamaphethelo alobo buso libonakala kupulani.
4. Umnyango uvulwa ngohlangothi lodonga iphoyinti elikulo. Cindezela {{key:H}} (noma cofa {{ui:Phendula}}) ukuze ubeke amakhanda ekugcineni kwelinye.
5. Cofa. Umnyango ubekiwe kanti ithuluzi lihlala livuliwe okulandelayo. Phonsa phakathi kodonga umnyango uzizungeze.

Ukuze ubeke ngebanga elinembile, phonsa udonga eduze nekhona olilinganisa kulo, uthayiphe ibanga (umzekelo `2'` noma `600`) bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}. Ibanga liphuma endaweni eseduze yobuso bodonga kuya emphethelweni womnyango.

## Beka ifasiteli

1. Cofa {{ui:Iwindi}} (noma cindezela {{key:N}}).
2. Khetha uhlobo: **Fixed window**, **Single hung window**, **Double hung window**, **Casement window**, **Sliding window**, **Awning**, **Picture window** noma **Transom**, kanye ne-**W**, **H** kanye ne-**Sill** (ukuphakama kwaphansi kwefasiteli ngaphezu kwefloor).
3. Phonsa udonga bese ucofa, noma uthayiphe ibanga bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}} njengamaminyango. Ifasiteli lihlala ngaphandle kodonga.

## Hambisa, phendula, susa

- Ngo-{{ui:Khetha}} svuliwe, hudula umnyango noma ifasiteli ngodonga lwalo. Hudula kudonga olunye ukuze uwuhambise lapho.
- {{key:Delete}} isusa iminyango namafasiteli akhethiwe; udonga luyavaleka.
- Ukuhambisa noma ukususa udonga kuhambisa noma kususe okusemkhatsini walo. Uma ukuqala kodonga kuhamba ngomugqa walo, iminyango namafasiteli ayo kugcina indawo yawo.
- {{ui:Hlehlisa}} no-{{ui:Phinda wenze}} bathatha ngayinye ukubeka, ukuhamba noma ukususa emuva nasembhalo.

## Okwenqatshiwe

- **Akulingani kulowo donga.** Umnyango noma ifasiteli kubanzi kunodonga, noma kuzophuma ekugcineni kwalo. Zama ububanzi obuncane noma udonga olude.
- **Iphakeme kunodonga.** I-sill nobude kudlula phezulu kodonga.

## Okulondolozwayo

Umnyango noma ifasiteli ngalinye liyi-IFC door noma window enosayizi wayo, ukusebenza kwayo (umzekelo single swing left), uhlobo lwosayizi wayo (uhlobo olulodwa ngosayizi ngamunye womndeni wawo), `Pset_DoorCommon` noma `Pset_WindowCommon` nezinani zesisekelo, ugcwalisa ukuvuleka kwe-IFC kodonga lwalo. Indawo yesisekelo nevolumu yodonga kuyishiya ukuvuleka. Ezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM ziyivula njengomnyango odongeni.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ipulani ibonisa iminyango ivulekile ngama-90° nendlela yokuvula kwayo, iminyango esongwayo njengamaphaneli amabili, iminyango edonsekayo ingena odongeni, iminyango yegalaji njengomugqa wedashi phezulu, namafasiteli nemigqa yawo yefreyimu nengilazi.
- Ububanzi bungububanzi bonke befreyimu; ilimu lomnyango liyababanzi obuyisishiyagalombili kubhedu ngalinye.
- Ukukopisha iminyango namafasiteli adwetshwe akuqedile; beka elinye.

## Ifasiteli lokuphuma kwesisekelo kanye nomgodi

Khetha **Basement egress with well** ohlwini lwezinhlobo zamafasiteli. Iqala njenge-casement engu-36 × 48 inch enesill engu-36 inch ngaphezu kwezinga. Umgodi usecaleni langaphandle kodonga, nobubanzi bokucacile obungaphansi kwe-48 inch, ukuphuma kwe-36 inch, nesisekelo esingu-6 inch ngaphansi kwe-sill. Ubonakala njengemigqa emincane ku-plan nanjengezinhlangothi ezintathu ezivulekile phezulu ku-3D. Umgodi uyingxenye ye-IFC body yefasiteli, ngakho ukugcina, ukuhambisa, ukushintsha usayizi noma ukususa ifasiteli kuyawuhambisa nawo. Ukushintsha usayizi kugcina okungenani i-6 inch ecaleni kwe-jamb ngayinye.

Lezi yizinombolo zokwakha ezizenzakalelayo, hhayi ukuqinisekiswa kokuthobela ukuphuma kwesisekelo. Hlola ngokwahlukana ukuvuleka okuchazwe ngumkhiqizi, izimo zendawo, ukuphansi komhlabathi, ukudonswa kwamanzi nanoma yiliphi iladi elidingekayo. Ukujula nobude womgodi kuyizilinganiso ezisetshenziswe ngaphambili; azilandeli ubuso bomhlaba.
