## Okuzokwenza

Hlela amakhishi, izindlu zokugezela namagumbi asebenzisa isethi yasekhaya: amakhabethe, amatafula okugezela, izinsiza, izixhobo zamapayipi nempahla eyenziwe yi-CadCraft futhi ekhululekile ukusetshenziswa (CC0). Zivela emtapweni futhi zihambisana nezindonga; amakhawunta bese ebekwa phezu kwamakhabethe esisekelo ngokuchofoza kanye.

## Beka amakhabethe nezinsiza

1. Vula ithebhu ethi **Umtapo** bese ukhetha isigaba: **Amakhabethe** (amakhabethe esisekelo, odonga namakhabethe amade), **Izinsiza zasekhaya**, **Ukuxhumana kwamanzi**, **Impahla**. Ukusesha `base cabinet`, `B24`, `W3030` noma `toilet` nakho kuyazithola.
2. Khetha into bese ukhetha **Beka**. Khetha izinga.
3. Sondela odongeni bese ucindezela {{key:Tab}} ukuze ulibambe: ingemuva lento libamba ubuso bodonga. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuyiphendula.
4. Chofoza ukuyibeka. Beka eyilandelayo eduze kwayo; uchungechunge lwamakhabethe ngumane amakhabethe ecaleni kwawo.

Amakhabethe odonga, i-microwave ephezu kwesitofu, amahhudi esitofu namasinki asekhishini anobude bawo: abeke phansi njengazo zonke izinto, azilenga noma ahlale lapho kufanele khona — amakhabethe odonga aphakama ngama-54", isinki esikhawunteni esiphakeme nge-36". Amakhabethe ekhoneni angena ekhoneni elingaphakathi, ngemuva kwawo kumi odonga olulodwa, uhlangothi lwawo kumi odonga olunye.

## Amakhawunta

1. Ezingeni, vula {{ui:Build}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Countertop}}. Setha **Ukuphuma kwangaphambili** ne-**Ubukhulu** uma zingeyona i-1" ne-1½".
2. Chofoza phezu kochungechunge lwamakhabethe esisekelo: ikhawunta eyenziwa izobonakala phezu kwawo wonke amakhabethe ahlangene, nasezikhonweni.
3. Chofoza.

Ikhawunta yenziwa kumakhabethe angaphansi kwayo. Uma wengeza noma uke wasusa ikhabethe, ikhawunta idwetshwa ngomugqa ophukile epulanini kuze ilingane futhi: chofoza uchungechunge ngo-{{ui:Countertop}}, noma khetha ikhawunta bese ukhetha **Lwanele kumakhabethe** ku-**Izici**. Noma yiluphi ushintsho odongeni kuleso zinga lulinganisa amakhawunta ngokuzenzakalelayo.

## Iminyango namawindi

Amathuluzi e-{{ui:Door}} ne-{{ui:Window}} nawo anezinhlobo zasekhaya: **Bifold closet door**, **French doors**, **Barn door (on the wall)**, **Entry door with sidelites**, **Awning**, **Picture window** kanye ne-**Transom** (bona [Iminyango namawindi](topic:doors-and-windows)).

## Okuwusizo ukukwazi

- Amakhawunta alondolozwa njengempahla ("Countertop") nendawo yawo, ububanzi bawo nomphetho wangaphambili, alungele ukubalwa kwamanani.
- Ukusikwa kwesinki kumakhawunta nasezidonga zokuvikela akukakwenziwa.
- Usayizi ungosayizi ovamile abamiwe, hhayi umkhiqizi othile; hlola usayizi womkhiqizi wangempela ngaphambi kokuoda.
