Imiyalo ye-BimCraft ifana noma ngabe uyifinyelela ngayiphi indlela. **Isitayela semenyu** sinquma kuphela lapho zikhona esikrinini, ukuthi zibizwa ngani lapho, nokuthi ziqalwa yiziphi izinkinobho, ngakho ungagcina izinkambiso ozijwayele. Khetha esinye ku-{{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ({{key:Mod+,}}) › {{ui:BimCraft}} › {{ui:Imenyu, okhiye nemouse}}; ushintsho luyasebenza ngokushesha, futhi lesi sisekhi siyakukhumbula.

## Izitayela ezinhlanu

| Isitayela | Okuzothola |
|---|---|
| {{ui:CadCraft}} | Ithuluzi lika-BimCraft nokhiye bayo ({{key:W}} udonga, {{key:D}} umnyango, {{key:N}} iwindi, {{key:X}} isigaba, {{key:F}} lingana). Okokuqala. |
| {{ui:Idizayini yekhaya}} | Ibha yemenyu (Ifayela, Hlela, Ukwakhiwa, Umtapo, 3D, CAD, Amathuluzi, Ukubuka) phezu komugqa wamathuluzi okwakha, njengoba izinhlelo zokuklama amakhaya zizihlela ngayo. Okhiye abafanayo abayodwa, kanye {{key:Mod+L}} ye-library, {{key:F9}} yombono we-3D, {{key:F10}} yeplani kanye {{key:F11}} kokubili. |
| {{ui:BIM ribbon}} | Amathebhu ezinkinobho ezinkulu (Ukwakha, Faka, Chaza, Guqula, Ukubuka, Okuphumayo) nemenyu ye-Ifayela namankinobho asheshayo ngakwesokudla. Okhiye ababili: {{key:W}} bese {{key:A}} kudonga, {{key:D}} {{key:R}} umnyango, {{key:W}} {{key:N}} iwindi, {{key:S}} {{key:C}} isigaba, {{key:Z}} {{key:F}} lingana. | {{ui:Isibukeli}} | Ukuvula nokuhlola amamodeli hhayi ukuwadweba: imenyu {{ui:Ifayela}}, {{ui:Ukubuka}}, {{ui:Bonisa}}, {{ui:Hlola}} kanye {{ui:Hlela}} phezu komugqa wamathuluzi okubuka (lingana, phezulu, phambili, eceleni, isigaba, linganisa, fihla, hlukanisa, okubonakalayo, bonisa konke, hamba). Amathuluzi okwakha alindele kumenyu ye-{{ui:Imodeli}} ekugcineni. Okhiye afanayo nalawo esitayela sika-BimCraft. |

Imenyu namabhoni kuvela uma imodeli ivuliwe noma uqala isakhiwo esisha; ngaphambi kwalokho, iphaneli echibini lesokunxele ivula amafayela iqale izakhiwo.

:::tip
Ukhiye ongowe-BimCraft ngokwayo kodwa hhayi wesitayela osikhethile awusebenzi, futhi umugqa wesimo utshela ukuthi umyalo wawo waya kuphi ("Ku-BIM ribbon, Section ingu-SC"). {{key:Delete}}, {{key:Esc}}, {{key:Enter}}, {{key:Tab}}, {{key:Space}} kanye nezitshalo zihlala zinencazelo yazo, nethuluzi elisebenzayo ligcina okhiye balo: {{key:R}} kanye {{key:Tab}} lapho ubeka, {{key:W}} {{key:A}} {{key:S}} {{key:D}} lapho uhamba, {{key:H}} ngamathuluzi omnyango newindi, izinombolo lapho uthayipha ubude.
:::

## Yenze eyakho

1. Khetha isitayela esiseduze kakhulu nalokho ofuna, bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa…}}.
2. Ku-{{ui:Okhiye nemenyu}}, thola umyalo (ibhokisi lokusesha liyasiza). Chofoza {{ui:Engeza ukhiye}} bese ucindezela ukhiye, noma okhiye ababili ngokulandelana ukuze uthole ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili. Chofoza ukhiye ukuze uwususe.
3. Ngomugqa wemenyu noma ribhoni, {{ui:Lapho ihlala khona}} ihambisa umyalo kwenye imenyu noma iphaneli, noma ku-{{ui:Okhiye kuphela}}.
4. Ku-{{ui:Imouse}}, setha isondo, izinkinobho ezishelelisa iplani, nokuthi yiliphi umsebenzi nganoma iyiphi inkinobho ezibonweni ze-3D.
5. Nikeza igama bese uchofoza {{ui:Londoloza}}.

Ukulondoloza isitayela esakhelwe ngaphakathi kwenza ikhophi yakho ("Idizayini yekhaya yami"); esakhelwe ngaphakathi asikaze siguquke. Ukhiye onikezwe imiyalo emibili, noma olandelana nokhiye ababili ngaphambi kokuthayipha ukhiye wezinhlamvu ezimbili, ubonakala ngombala obomvu futhi kudingeka ulungiswe ngaphambi kokuthi ulondoloze.

## Imouse

- **Isondo lemouse**: {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphakathi}} (okokuqala) noma {{ui:Ukuskrola phezulu kusondeza ngaphandle}}, nokuthi kushesha kangakanani. Ukuhlanganisa iminwe ku-trackpad kusondeza njalo ngendlela iminwe yakho enyakaza ngayo.
- **Hambisa iplani nemidwebo ngo**: noma yiziphi izinkinobho zesobunxele, eziphakathi nezesokudla. {{key:Space}} nokuhudula nakho kuyashelelisa; ukuhudula ngesobunxele kushelelisa kuphela lapho ukuchofoza kungeke kukhethe noma kudwebe.
- **Hudula emibonweni ye-3D**: {{ui:Eyombono ngamunye}} igcina izinkinobho ze-BimCraft (isobunxele sizungeza, isokudla sishelelisa). Noma setha inkinobho ngayinye ukuthi izungeze, ishelelise, isondeze noma ingenzi lutho; {{key:Shift}} nenkinobho yenza ukuguqula phakathi kokuzungeza nokushelelisa. Ukuchofoza ngaphandle kokuhudula kusakhetha.

## Yithathe nawe

{{ui:Thumela}} ilondoloza isitayela esisetshenziswayo njengefayela elincane le-`.ccinput.json`; {{ui:Ngenisa…}} lingeza elinye, kusuka kwenye ikhompyutha noma kumngane wakho. Ifayela elingeniswe lingabhekisela kumiyalo ka-BimCraft kuphela: noma yini engeyona kuwo (umyalo lolu weqiniso alunawo, noma ukhiye ongasebenzi lapha) iyasuswa futhi ibalwe kumugqa wesimo. {{ui:Susa lesi sitayela}} isusa eyakho.

:::note
Lezi zilungiselelo zihlala kulesi sisekhi. {{ui:Sebenzisa leli sitayela kuzo zonke izinhlelo}} ngaphansi kwamakhadi esitayela yenza zonke izinhlelo zivule esitayelweni esihambisanayo (ithuluzi layo elingokwayo, ibha yemenyu, ribhoni noma {{ui:Ukudweba kwakudala}}; {{ui:Isibukeli}} ingeyesi-BimCraft kuphela futhi ayinaso esihambisanayo kwezinye), ngoshintsho owenze kwiyo ngayinye; uhlelo olungenaso isitayela esihambisanayo lugcina owalo. Ukukhetha esinye isitayela kwesinye isisetshenziswa kushintsha lowo kuphela.
:::
