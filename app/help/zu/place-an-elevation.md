## Okuzokwenza

Yenza ukubukeka kohlangothi lwakho: umdwebo wesakhiwo obonwa usuka kuphoyinti okhethile, ubheke ngendlela okhetha ngayo. Kuthatha ukuchofoza kabili epulanini lomgangatho. Umdwebo ubhalwa ohlwini lokubukeka kohlangothi lwesakhiwo.

:::note
Izinhlangothi ezine sezikhona. Sonke isakhiwo sinokubukeka Okusenyakatho, Okusempumalanga, Okusemaphakathini nokusentshonalanga ku-{{ui:Isiphequluli sephrojekthi}} ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}} ngaphandle kokubeka lutho. Beka eyakho uma ufuna ukubuka kwenye indawo: ngaqondile kwesiphambano esigxiwe, usuka ngaphakathi kwesikhala esingaphakathi, noma ubuke udonga oyedwa ngaphakathi kwegumbi.
:::

## Lapho ithuluzi likhona

- Amalayawudi eribhoni: {{ui:Ukubuka}} › **Dala** › {{ui:Ukuphakama}}.
- Ilayawudi yakudala: vula {{ui:Dweba}}, bese {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} › {{ui:Ukuphakama}}.
- Noma yisiphi isitayela semenyu: umyalo ubizwa ngokuthi {{ui:Ukuphakama}}; ipharetha yomyalo iyawuthola, futhi umugqa womyalo uwubiza ngokuthi ELEVATION.

## Izinyathelo

1. Khetha {{ui:Ukuphakama}}. Ukubukeka kohlangothi kubekwa epulanini lomgangatho, ngakho uma wawusembonweni we-3D i-BimCraft ikhombisa ipulani lesitezi owawusesitezini. Umcibisholo uthi **Chofoza lapho ukubukeka kuma khona**.
2. Chofoza lapho umbono uthathwa khona. Ukubukeka kwangaphandle, chofoza ngaphandle kwesakhiwo, kancane kude nodonga ofuna ukulubona.
3. Umcibisholo manje uthi **Chofoza indlela okubukeka ngayo**. Sondela esakhiweni: umcibisholo ukhombisa indlela, nomugqa omfishane ophambi kokuchofoza kwakho kokuqala ungumphetho okubukwa kuwo. Chofoza.
4. Ipharetha enenombolo nentonga ivela epulanini. Leyo ngophawu lokubukeka kohlangothi. Okokuqala ngu-1, okulandelayo ngu-2.
5. Cindezela Esc ukuqeda, noma uqhubeke ubeke elinye.

Ukubeka uphawu kuyinyathelo elilodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyasihlehlisa.

## Vula umdwebo

Ipulani lisahlala phambi kwakho ngemva kokuchofoza kwesibili. Umdwebo usesi-{{ui:Isiphequluli sephrojekthi}}:

1. Vula {{ui:Ukubukeka kohlangothi}}. Okwakho kubhalwa ngemuva kwezinhlangothi ezine njenge-**Elevation 1**.
2. Chofoza inkinobho yepeni {{ui:Hlela kulokhu kubuka}} emgqeni wayo ukuze ubone umdwebo usebenze kuwo: hambisa izindonga, iminyango, amawindi namazinga, engeze amanothi nezilinganiso. Bona [Hlela kumabukeko kohlangothi nakuma-section](topic:edit-in-elevations).
3. Chofoza umgqa uqobo ukuze uvule isethi yomdwebo, noma engeze umbono esheetini yakho. Bona [Imibono namashidi](topic:views-and-sheets).

Kwelayawudi yakudala, i-project browser isendaweni yangakwesobunxele uma {{ui:Dweba}} livuliwe. Kumalayawudi eribhoni, yithebhu ethi {{ui:Isiphequluli sephrojekthi}} ngaphansi kwe-Izici.

## Okubonisa ukubuka

- Konke okuphambi kophawu, kuze kube sekufinyeleleni kwesakhiwo. Akukho okungemuva kwalo.
- Amazinga, amagama wawo nokuphakama kwawo.
- Uma uphawu lumi phakathi kwesakhiwo, noma yini ilapho imi ikhishwa khona, njengoba i-section iyisika. Ukuze uthole ukubukeka okucacile kwangaphandle, mi ngaphandle.

## Shintsha kona

Khetha uphawu epulanini (chofoza isiyingi salo nge-{{ui:Khetha}}). Ku-Properties kukhona:

- {{ui:Ilebula}}: inombolo yalo noma igama lakho, kuze kube izinhlamvu ezingu-8.
- {{ui:Guqula ukubuka}}: bheka ngendlela ephambene kusuka endaweni efanayo.
- {{ui:Bheka kuze kube}}: ukubuka kufinyelela kangakanani phambi kophawu. 0 ibonisa konke.

Cindezela Delete ukususa uphawu nomdwebo walo. {{ui:Hlehlisa}} ibuyisela kokubili.

:::tip
Ukuze ubone ipulani kanye nombono we-3D ngesikhathi esisodwa uma usebenza, sebenzisa {{ui:Ukubuka}} › **Amawindi** › {{ui:Ukwabelana ngombono}} emalayawudini eribhoni, noma {{ui:Hlukanisa}} kwelayawudi yakudala. Ukuchofoza ithebhu yombono kubuyisela kombono oyedwa.
:::

## Imikhawulo

- Uphawu alukakwazi ukudonswa noma lujikeleze epulanini okwamanje. Lisuse bese ulibeka futhi, noma sebenzisa {{ui:Guqula ukubuka}}.
- Ohlwini lwemibono ongayengeza esheetini, ukubukeka kohlangothi lwakho luhlelwa kanye namasekhishini.
- Amathegi wemisindo nowezifasiteli adwetshwa ezinhlangothini ezine zesakhiwo, hhayi kolunye ohlangothi olubekiwe.

## Ku-IFC

Uphawu lugcinwa efayeleni njenge-IfcAnnotation enegama elithi "Elevation 1" nohlobo lwento "Elevation marker", esitezini lapho lwabekwa khona. Olunye uhlelo lubonisa njengesichazi; umdwebo ngokwawo wenziwa yi-BimCraft kusukela emodelini njalo.
