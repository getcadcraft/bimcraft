## Okuzokwenza

Dweba ngokumelene nemodeli yekhonsathi (isakhiwo, izinsiza, isakhiwo esikhona) ngaphandle kokukopisha lutho kufayela lakho.

**Imodeli exhunyiwe** iboniswa ku-3D view futhi inqunywa emaplanini akho, kodwa ayishintshwa futhi ayilondolozwa emodelini yakho. Imodeli yakho igcina kuphela isixhumanisi: igama lefayela nendawo lapho liya khona.

## Xhuma imodeli

1. Esigabeni se-{{ui:Amamodeli axhunyiwe}} sepaneli esingakhashana kwesokunxele, chofoza {{ui:Xhuma imodeli…}} bese ukhetha ifayela le-IFC. Liboniswa masinyane, futhi umugqa walo uthi **Ibonisiwe**.
2. Khetha lapho iyakhona ku-{{ui:Ukubekwa}}:
   - **Izindawo ezabelwanayo** (ukuqala): izindawo zayo zingezakho, njengalapho zombili amamodeli ebekwe ngomsuka ofanayo.
   - **Izindawo zemephu**: izindawo zemephu zamafayela womabili zizihlanganisa (zombili kudingeka ukuguqulwa kwemephu; i-BimCraft iyakusho uma enye ingenayo).
   - **Ngamaphoyinti amabili…**: chofoza iphoyinti emodelini exhunyiwe, bese lapho lingena khona kwakho; bese kuba khona ipheya yesibili. Ixhumanisi liyahanjiswa futhi liphendulwe ngokuzungeza ngokuthi lime, ukuze lifane.
3. Ushintsho ngalunye lwesixhumanisi nokubekwa kwaso kuyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyasibuyisela emuva.

## Ukusebenza nakho

- Amaplani abonisa imodeli exhunyiwe njengemigqa emincane esemkhawulweni wokunqunywa kwezinga, futhi ithuluzi {{ui:Udonga}} namanye amathuluzi okudweba ayambelela ezikhonweni nasemaphethelweni ayo.
- Khetha izinto zayo ku-3D view ukuze ufunde izimpahla zazo; imodeli exhunyiwe akuyona eyohlelwa nguwe, ngakho inkinobho yokukhetha yayo ku-{{ui:Amamodeli}} ayikhethiwe.
- **Ifayela lishintshile?** Uma ibhrawuza ingakwazi ukubheka ifayela (ifayela elivulwe ngomkhethi walo wamafayela, noma ku-Studio), i-BimCraft iyayilayisha kabusha ngokuzenzakalela amasekhondi ambalwa ngemva kokuthi ifayela lilondolozwe futhi, iyakusho lokho. Ngaphandle kwalokho chofoza {{ui:Layisha kabusha}}.
- {{ui:Khipha}} iyayifihla futhi ikhulule inkumbulo; ixhumanisi lihlala emodelini yakho. {{ui:Layisha…}} iyayibonisa futhi (ukhetha ifayela: ibhrawuza alikwazi ukuvula ifayela ngegama lalo lodwa).
- Ukuthi × ususa ixhumanisi emodelini yakho.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Kufayela lakho le-IFC: i-IfcDocumentReference yalo lonke ixhumanisi (igama lefayela), ehlobene neprojekthi, nendawo yalo ezethulweni zempahla yeprojekthi ethi CadCraft_Links. Ukuvula kabusha imodeli yakho kuzodwetsha izixhumanisi zayo njengo-**Ayilayishiwe** size uzilayishe.
