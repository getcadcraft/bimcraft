Isigaba {{ui:Umbono wempilo yonke}} esingakhashana kwesokunxele sidweba okuqukethwe imodeli okuguguka nokuthi kushintshwa nini phakathi nesikhathi sokufunda (iminyaka engama-50 ekuqaleni). Akukho okushintshwa emodelini.

## Ukuyifunda

Ingxenye ngayinye ibonisa inani layo (elilinganiswe emodelini: indawo yewindi, iminyango yangaphandle nangaphakathi, ukuqeda udonga lwangaphandle, upende wangaphakathi, ukuqeda iphansi nesilingo, indawo yophahla, izitebhisi), **impilo ngeminyaka** kanye **nendleko ngeyunithi**. Ingxenye ishintshwa ngemva kwempilo yayo, ngemva kwezikhathi ezimbili zempilo njalo njalo uma lokho kusengaphambi kokuphela kwesifundo, ngakho iwindi elinempilo yeminyaka engama-30 kusifundo seminyaka engama-50 lishintshwa kanye, eminyakeni engama-30. Izikhathi zempilo ezisetshenziswa ngokuvamile yizibalo zokuhlela ezijwayelekile: zishintshe ukuze zifanele imikhiqizo yakho nesimo sezulu sakho. Akukho sindleko esithathwa njengesivamile; thayipha esisodwa ukuze ubone imali ngonyaka, isamba nenani layo lamanje. Engeza izinga lokunciphisa nezinga lokwanda kwentengo uma uzifuna.

{{ui:Londoloza uhlelo (CSV)}} ibhala izingxenye neminyaka. Amapayipi, amapayipi omoya, ukuwiringa nemishini nakho kufakwa oluhlu (bheka ngezansi).

## Ipayipi, ipayipi lomoya, ukuwiringa nemishini

- **Amapayipi namapayipi omoya** ayizinmitha zawo wonke umugqa odwetshiwe. **Imishini** (iziphampu, amafeni, amavalvu, amatheminali, izinto ezifakiwe, amasokhethi, amaswishi, amabhodi nezinye izinto ethrekisa ithebhu ye-Assets) ibalwa ngohlobo, ngalunye lunempilo yokuhlela. Into esethe *impilo yensiza* ethebhuleni le-Assets inomugqa wayo nalowo mpilo, futhi uma inedethi yokufakwa iqala ngeminyaka yayo: iphampu eneminyaka eyi-16 enempilo engu-10 isifanele manje, bese kusho ngayo yonke iminyaka eyi-10.
- **Ukuwiringa** kuyisilinganiso: ibanga eliseplanini ukusuka ebhodini liye kumadivayisi ngamadivayisi esekethe ngayinye, eduze kakhulu kuqala,liphindwe nge-1.25 ngenxa yokuhamba kwamakhebuli. Imodeli ayidweshi ikhebuli, ngakho hlola lokhu nohlu lwakho lokubala amanani.
- Izikhathi zempilo ziyizibalo zokuhlela okufanele ziphinde zibhekwe, hhayi umthombo.

## Ikhabhoni ngokushintshwa ngakunye

Khetha {{ui:Faka ikhabhoni eyakhiwe yokushintshwa ngakunye}}. Iwindi elisha, umnyango, isitebhisi, uphahla, ipayipi noma isiqephu semishini ibalwa ngekhabhoni elinalo izinto zemodeli zalolo hlobo (izinsiza zethebhu ye-Carbon, ngeyunithi). Ingxenye engenayo into noma insiza, njengopende, izinto zokuqeda nokuwiringa, ibonisa idashi futhi iyafakwa oluhlu. Impilo yokugcina yengxenye endala nomsebenzi wokuyishintsha akubalwa.
