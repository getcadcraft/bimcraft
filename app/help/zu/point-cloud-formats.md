## Okuvulayo lapha

Vula lezi nge-{{ui:Ivulekile}} noma ngokuzikhipha ekhasini (bheka isihloko sokubonisa ifu lamaphoyinti ecaleni kwemodeli):

| Ifomethi | Isandiso | Okufundwayo |
|---|---|---|
| LAS, LAZ | `.las`, `.laz` | LAS 1.0–1.4: amaphoyinti, imibala, amandla okukhanya, amakilasi e-LiDAR, uhlelo lokugonya. LAZ kuya ku-1 GB. |
| E57 | `.e57` | Konke ukuskena kuleli fayela, ngakunye kubekwe ngendawo yakho; imibala, noma amandla okukhanya uma ifayela lingenayo imibala. Izithombe kulo fayela ziyashiywa (kuyashiwo). Kuya kumaphoyinti ayizigidi ezi-6 nama-512 MB njengamanje: thumela ukuskena okukhulu njenge-LAS noma i-LAZ. |
| PLY | `.ply` | Okubhaliwe nokunamabhithi (zombili izindlela zama-byte): i-x y z yama-vertex, imibala namandla okukhanya. Izinhlangothi ziyashiywa (kuyashiwo). |
| PCD | `.pcd` | Okubhaliwe nokunamabhithi: x y z, imibala ehlanganisiwe noma ehlukene, amandla okukhanya. Amafayela e-PCD acindezelwe awafundwa okwamanje: gcina njengabhithi noma njengombhalo. |
| PTX | `.ptx` | Ukuskena okukodwa noma okuningi, ngakunye kubekwe ngoshintsho lwakho; amaphoyinti umskena ongawalinganisanga ayashiywa. Ngamamitha. |
| PTS, XYZ | `.pts`, `.xyz` | Okubhaliwe: x y z, namandla okukhanya nemibala uma amakholomu enawo. |

## Amafayela asetshenziswa yisofthiwe yomskena kuphela

Amanye amafayela okuskena ayifomethi yomkhiqizi womshini wokuskena, angenayo incazelo eshicilelwe nomfundi ovulekile. I-BimCraft ayikwazi ukuwafunda, futhi ukuvula elilodwa kubonisa ikhadi elisho ukuthi yini nokuthi yenzeni. Thumela ukuskena kusofthiwe womkhiqizi njenge-E57 noma i-LAS (noma i-PTX, i-PTS), bese uvula leyo fayela ethunyelwe.

| Ifayela | Isandiso | Thumela kusuka ku |
|---|---|---|
| ReCap project | `.rcp` | ReCap: vula iphrojekthi bese uyithumela. Iphrojekthi ikhomba kuskena kuphela, ngakho ukukopisha i-`.rcp` yodwa akunike amaphoyinti. |
| ReCap scan | `.rcs` | ReCap: vula iphrojekthi uskeni olungene kuyo bese uyithumela. |
| FARO scan | `.fls` | FARO SCENE. |
| Leica scan | `.lgs` | Leica Cyclone REGISTER 360. |
| Trimble scan | `.tzf` | Trimble RealWorks noma Trimble Business Center. |
| Z+F scan | `.zfs` | Z+F LaserControl. |

:::tip
Lapho ukuthumela kubuza ifomethi, i-LAS noma i-LAZ igcina konke i-BimCraft ekhombisayo (imibala, amandla okukhanya, amakilasi nohlelo lokugonya) efayeleni elincane kakhulu.
:::

## Kungani lawa mafayela angafundwa

Lezi zifomethi azishicilelwe, futhi indlela kuphela yokuzifunda iyilayibrari yesofthiwe yomkhiqizi ngamunye, ngaphansi kwemigomo yayo. I-BimCraft ifunda kuphela ifomethi engayifunda ngekhodi yayo kukhompyutha yakho, ngakho ukuskena akudingi ukuthunyelwa noma kuphi ukuze kuvule.
