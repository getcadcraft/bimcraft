## Okuzokwenza

Khetha izinto kumodeli ye-IFC bese ufunda konke ifayela elikushoyo ngazo: izimpawu, amasethi wezici, izici zohlobo nezilinganiso.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC.
2. Chofoza into, isibonelo umnyango. Chofoza ungadonsi: ukudonsa okungaphezu kwamapikseli ambalwa kuzungeza esikhundleni sokukhetha.
3. Bheka into: iqanjiswa ngoluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka.
4. Bheka iphaneli ethi {{ui:Properties}} ngakwesokudla. Phezulu kukhona uhlobo lwento (njengo "Door" noma "WallStandardCase") negama layo.
5. Funda ibhlokhi yokuqala: **Igama**, **Uhlobo**, **Uhlobo lwento**, **Ithegi**, **Incazelo**, **GlobalId** kanye **Umugqa**.
6. Skrola phansi kumasethi wezici. Ayo amathathu okuqala avulekile; chofoza igama lanoma yiliphi elinye ukuze uvule.
7. Chofoza enye into ukuze uyishintshele kuyo.
8. Cindezela {{key:Esc}}, noma chofoza indawo engenalutho, ukuze ususe ukukhethwa.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Into egqanjiswe nedatha yayo ngakwesokudla.
- Amasethi wezici ahlelwe ngokwenhlamvu. Amasethi avela ku**hlobo** lwento aba nelebula " (type)" ngemva kwegama lawo kanye nesaziso ethi "Type property: a change here applies to every element of this type."
- Amasethi ezilinganiso (njengobude, izindawo nevolumu) ohlwini lwamanye amasethi. Izilinganiso azishintshwa.
- Amanani owakwazi ukushintsha abonakala njengamaconso. [Shintsha izici ze-IFC ugcine ikhophi eshintshiwe](topic:edit-ifc-properties) ikhombisa indlela.

[Izinkambu zephaneli Yezici](topic:properties-panel) zichaza ngayinye yazo.

## Ukufinyelela izinto ezifihliwe

1. Ukuze ufinyelele okuthile ngaphakathi esakhiweni, susa ukukhethwa kwezitezi ezisezingeni eliphezulu kuluhlu lwe-{{ui:Storeys}}.
2. Ukuze ufinyelele udonga ongemuva kwevolumu yegumbi, vala {{ui:Spaces}}.
3. Chofoza into.

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Ukudonsa kancane ngenkathi uchofoza.** I-BimCraft iyakuthatha njengokujikeleza futhi ayikhethi. Bamba imbewu inganyakazi.
- **Ukulindele amayunithi ngasemuva kwamanani.** Amanani abonakala njengoba agcinwe kufayela, azungezwe kuma-decimal amane, ngaphandle kophawu lweyunithi. Ubude kokuthunyelwa kwe-metric kuvame ukuba ngamamilimitha noma amamitha; hlola izilungiselelo zomthumeli.
- **Ukulindele ipharamitha ngegama oliziyo.** Abathumeli baqamba kabusha futhi bahlele kabusha amapharamitha ube amasethi wezici we-IFC afana ne-`Pset_WallCommon`. Hlola yonke isethi; amapharamitha ngokwezifiso ahlala kwisethi eqanjwe ngohlelo olwathumela noma ngenkampani yakho.
- **Ukulindele ibhokisi lokusesha.** Chofoza ukuze ukhethe into; {{key:Mod}}+chofoza noma {{key:Shift}}+chofoza zikhetha okuningi, bese {{key:Mod+A}} ikhetha zonke izinto ezibonakalayo. Uhlu lwe-{{ui:Elements}} oluseceleni lokunxele lubala izinto ngohlobo kuphela; alukwazi ukuchofozwa.
- **Ukukhetha ifayela leprojekthi.** Ikhadi lefayela alinazinto zokukhetha: "Izici zento zidinga imodeli ye-3D. Vula i-IFC export yalelo fayela ukuze uzibone."
