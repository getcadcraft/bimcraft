## Okuzokwenza

Yenza udwebo ugcwele: bonisa amadwebo awuxhumanisa nawo (ukuthenjwa kwangaphandle, noma i-xrefs) namaphiksha asiwasebenzisayo, njengelogo kwibhulokhi yesihloko.

## Indlela elula: vula ifolda

1. Chofoza {{ui:Open a folder}} bese ukhetha ifolda equkethe udwebo namaxhumanisi alo, isibonelo ifolda yonke ethi `Cad` yephrojekthi.
2. Kumbiko wefolda, skrola uye ku-**CAD drawings**.
3. Chofoza udwebo.
4. Bheka iphaneli ye-{{ui:Properties}} ngakwesokudla, ngaphansi kwe-**Linked drawings** ne-**Images**.

I-BimCraft ifuna ngalinye idwebo elixhunyiwe ngegama lalo ("*igama*.dwg"), kuqala efolda elifanayo nodwebo, bese noma kuphi kwifolda oyivulile. Izithombe ziyatholakala ngendlela efanayo, futhi nangegama elifanayo: i-eTransmit noma ukuthumela kwe-Revit kuvame ukungeza igama lephrojekthi phambili, ngakho i-`logo.png` itholakala njenge-`Tower-logo-5425051.png`.

## Indlela enye: engeza amafayela ngesandla

1. Vula udwebo wodwa.
2. Ngakwesokudla, ngaphansi kwe-**Linked drawings**, chofoza {{ui:Add linked drawings…}}. Ngaphansi kwe-**Images**, inkinobho ithi {{ui:Add pictures…}}; zombili zivula isikhethi esifanayo.
3. Khetha amafayela e-`.dwg` axhunyiwe namafayela ezithombe. Ungakhetha amaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa.
4. Linda ngenkathi i-BimCraft iphinde ivula udwebo nawo.

## Okufanele ubone

Idwebo ngalinye elixhunyiwe nesithombe ngasinye kuhlwiswe ngelebula:

| Ilebula | Isho ukuthi |
|---|---|
| **Kuboniswa** | Itholakele futhi idwetshwe. Indlela ngaphansi kwayo ibonisa lapho ivela khona, noma "engezwe nguwe". |
| **Kuboniswa, igama elifanayo** | Isithombe esitholakele ngegama elifanayo. Hlola ukuthi siyilo elifanele. |
| **Akutholakali** | Akukho efolda noma kumafayela owengezile. Isithombe esingatholakali sibonakala njengefreyimu engenalutho. |
| **I-TIFF ayikwazi ukuboniswa kusiphequluli** | Isithombe iyifayela le-TIFF, amasiphequluli angayidwetshi. Gcina njenge-PNG ngegama elifanayo bese uyengeza. |
| **Ayikwazanga ukufundwa** | Ifayela litholakele kodwa alikwazanga ukuvulwa njengesithombe. |

Ifulelo le-{{ui:Drawing}} kwesokunxele libonisa "*n* kwe-*m* kulayishiwe" wamadwebo axhunyiwe. Izingqimba zamadwebo axhunyiwe zivela ku-{{ui:Layers}} njenge-"*igama lexhumanisi*|*ingqimba*".

## Amaphutha ajwayelekile

- **Ukuvula udwebo kuphela.** "Lo mdwebo ubonisa okuqukethwe kwawo kuphela uze ungezwe amadwebo axhunyiwe. Ukuvula wonke ifolda yephrojekthi kuwatholela ngokuzenzakalelayo."
- **Ukuvula ifolda encane kakhulu.** Uma amaxhumanisi ahlala kwifolda engxenye, vula ifolda yomzali.
- **Amaxhumanisi aqanjiwe kabusha.** I-BimCraft ifanisa amadwebo axhunyiwe ngegama. Ixhumanisi eliqanjwe kabusha kudiski alitholakali; yengeze ngesandla.
- **Amaxhumanisi amaxhumanisi.** I-BimCraft ilayisha amadwebo odwebo ovulekile ahlanganiswe nawo. Amadwebo axhunyiwe ngaphakathi kwalawo awafuneki, ngakho ngokuvamile awabonisi kule nguqulo.
