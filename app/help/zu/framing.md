## Okuzokwenza

Akha uhlaka lomakhiwo kusuka kokudwetshiwe. BimCraft ibeka izitendi, amapuleti nehedha ezindongeni, amajoyisi emaphansini namarafa (noma amatrasi) ophahleni, ibakhethe ngamathebula amanani ahlukile, ibagcina nemodeli futhi iwakha kabusha noma nini lapho okubakhayo kushintsha. **Lumber** ohlwini lulona luhlu lokusika.

## Yakha

1. Esitezini, vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Uhlaka lokwakha}}.
2. Khetha **ukuhlukana** (16" noma 24" on center; 400 noma 600 mm), **amakhona** (izitendi ezintathu noma i-California), **Amarafa** noma **Amatrasi** ophahleni, nokuthi izindonga zithola yini umugqa wokuvinjwa maphakathi nobude. Amaphansi khetha **Ukhuni olusikiwe** noma **TJI (ngokwesihlahla)** bese usetha ukuqondisa kwamajoyisi kube ngokuzenzekelayo, i-plan X, noma i-plan Y.
3. Chofoza **Akha wonke udonga**, noma chofoza udonga oluodwa, iphansi noma uphahla (esitezini uphahla oluhlala kulo) ukuze wakhe lowo kuphela.

Vala **Bonisa uhlaka** ukuze ufihle zonke izingcezu zohlaka, njengengqimba; **See through** ifihla izindonga, amaphansi noma uphahla oluhlakiwe ukuze ubone uhlaka lodwa.

## Okuzotholakala

- **Izindonga:** ipuleti eliphansi (lisikwe ezindaweni zezivalo), ipuleti eliphezulu eliphindwe kabili, izitendi ezilayini kusuka esiqalweni sodonga, ama-king stud nama-jack stud nehedha ngayo yonke imbobo nefasitele, ama-cripple phezu kwehedha nangaphansi kwamafasitele, i-sill engaphelele ngaphansi kwamafasitele, amakhona lapho izindonga zihlangana (udonga oludlula lunikeza izitendi zekhona), izitendi ezimbili zokusekela lapho enye indonga ihlangana khona, nokuvinjwa phakathi kwezitendi.
- **Amaphansi:** amajoyisi aphambene nendlela emfushane yephansi ngokuzenzekelayo, noma ecaleni kwe-axis yepulani oyikhethile; ama-rim joist azungeze umphetho, nama-trimmer nehedha aphindwe kabili azungeze isitezi, nama-joist adlula kuso asikwe amfushane (ama-tail joist). Imodi ye-TJI ikhetha uhlobo oluncane oluhlu nojulile olufinyelela isikhala esicacile esilinganiswe ngokuhlukana okukhethiwe.
- **Uphahla:** amarafa ajwayelekile asikwa ngesikwele kuyo yonke i-eave, ama-jack rafter lapho ama-hip namavalley esika khona, amarafa e-hip nevalley, amabhodi e-ridge, amajoyisi kasilingi ezipuletini nama-collar tie — noma amatrasi alula ekuhlukaneni, ingxenye eyodwa ngayinye.

## Shintsha

Khetha noma yiliphi uhlaka ku-3D view noma kumuthi wemodeli. Ku-**Izici** kubonisa uhlaka lwayo: shintsha ukuhlukana, uhlobo lwamajoyisi aphansi nokuqondiswa, amakhona, ukuvinjwa noma uhlaka lophahla. Ukuhamba kwezindonga, izivalo nezifasitele, noma ukushintsha iphansi noma uphahla, kwenza uhlaka kabusha ngokwalo.

Ukuze ugcine ingxenye owayibeka ngamabomu uhlaka lapho lwenziwa kabusha, khetha kuyo bese ukhetha **Gcina le ngxenye lapho uhlaka lwenziwa kabusha**. Ingxenye egciniwe ihlala lapho ikhona; uma uhlaka oluyizungezile lushintsha, iphawulwa ukuze uhlole.

## Amaphansi e-TJI ngethebula oyilayishayo

BimCraft ayinawo amathebula wesikhala samajoyisi womenzi (ngoba ngabenzi, futhi aguqukela kokuhlobo ngakunye). Khetha uhlaka lwephansi, setha **Amajoyisi aphansi** ku-**TJI (ngokwesihlahla)** bese ukhetha **{{ui:Layisha ithebula…}}** ku-**Izici**: ifayela lefomethi `cadcraft-tji-table`, JSON noma CSV, owalenza kusuka kumathebula wesikhala amanje womenzi. Libiza umenzi, umkhiqizo nohlobo, nokuthi amanani avela kuphi, futhi libala kucala lomthwalo ngamunye (umthwalo ophilayo, umthwalo wesisindo, umkhawulo wokuphambuka, ubude bokuphatha) isikhala esikhulu esicacile somhlaka ngamunye nokujula ngakunye ngesikhala ngasinye.

- BimCraft ithatha **isikhala esicacile** sephansi, ikhethe ukujula okuncane kakhulu, phakathi kwalokho uhlu, olufinyelela kwesikhala esikhonjiswe ngesikhala nomthwalo okhethiwe, bese uhlaka liyafakwa ngalo. Uma isikhala noma isikhala esiphakathi singaphandle kwethebula, isho lokho futhi ayishintshi lutho.
- Imithwalo, umkhawulo wokuphambuka, ubude bokuphatha, ithebula nemishwana ethi "Isiqondiso sokuhlela sokuqala; ukukhethwa kokugcina kudinga amathebula womenzi nemithwalo yephrojekthi" kubhalwa kwi-Lumber schedule, kubhalwa kwimodeli nohlaka, futhi kudwetshwa ohlwini lwe-**Izinkinga** njengesaziso. Ithebula elimaka njengesibonelo lisho lokho ngesexwayiso.
- Ngenkathi i-add-on ye-**Building regulations** ivulwe, umthetho wayo wamajoyisi aphansi nawo ufunda ithebula elilayishiwe; ngaphandle kwethebula amajoyisi e-TJI ahlala ngaphandle kwalowo mhlolo, njengakuqala.
- Ithebula lihlala kule msebenzisi kuze kube yilapho ulisusa noma ulayisha elinye. Ifayela: `format` (`cadcraft-tji-table`), `version` 1, `manufacturer`, `product`, `edition`, `source`, `units` (`imperial`: inches, feet, psf; noma `metric`: mm, metres, kPa), `cases` (`id`, `live`, `dead`, `deflection`, `bearing`) kanye `rows` (`series`, `depth`, `spans`: ngalowo cala ngamunye, ukuhlukana nesikhala esikhulu esicacile, njengo `"16": "15-6"`). Ku-CSV, lezi zinto zifana kuzo `# key = value`, bese `series,depth,case,spacing,span`.
- Uma kungalayishwanga ithebula, uhlelo lwe-TJI luyenqatshwa futhi akukho okushintsha: **Layisha ithebula le-TJI: amathebula womenzi ngawabo futhi awakhiwe ngaphakathi.** Imodeli eyakhelwe ngethebula elakhelwe ngaphakathi le-TJI (ngaphambi kwalolu shintsho) igcina yonke amajoyisi ayo; Izici nezinkinga zithi ithebula liyadingeka, futhi iphansi lingakhiwa kabusha uma ulayisha elinye. Amajoyisi ensimbi ahlala njengakuqala.

Lokhu kuyisiqondiso sohlelo. Ukukhethwa kokugcina kwamajoyisi kudinga amathebula womenzi, imithwalo yephrojekthi nonjiniyela.

## Uhlu lokusika

Ku-**Ipaneli yezinhlu** khetha **Lumber**: yonke ingcezu yohlaka ihlelwe ngosayizi nangobude bemvelo ongawuthenga (izinde ezipheleleyo ze-2' kusuka ku-8'; izinyathelo ze-600 mm kusuka ku-2400 uma kumamilimitha), ngenani lezingcezu nama-board feet. **CSV** iyilanda.

:::warning
Usayizi wehedha, wamajoyisi, wamarafa nowesilingi usuka kumathebula amanani ajwayelekile wendlu eyodwa noma ezimbili, hhayi ukuklama. **Izici** zitshela lapho isikhala noma imbobo idlula ithebula laso. Umklami noma unjiniyela uhlola izikhala, imithwalo, nezixhumanisi.

Ukuhlelwa kwe-TJI kusebenzisa ithebula olilayishayo kuphela, olilungisayo kumathebula amanje womenzi; i-BimCraft ayinalo nelodwa. Lesi siphakamiso akihloli ukubamba, imithwalo yamaphoyinti, ama-cantilever, izimbobo, amahedha esitezi noma izixhumanisi, nesigaba se-I semodeli sisemgogodla.
:::

## Okugciniwe

Uhlaka ngalunye lodonga, iphansi noma uphahla kuyi-IFC element assembly ("Framing") yezinto ze-IFC (izitendi, amapuleti, amarafa, ama-collar tie, ukuvinjwa, amatrasi) namabhimu (amahedha, amajoyisi, amarimu, amabhodi e-ridge), ngayinye inosayizi wayo, ubude nama-board feet.
