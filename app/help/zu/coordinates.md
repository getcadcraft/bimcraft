## Okuzokwenza

Ukwazi ngokushesha lapho usemodelini: izinga olidweba kulo, ukuthi isikhombi sikude kangakanani emsukeni wephrojekthi, nokuthi iphoyinti lokuhlola lesiza lilaphi.

## Lapho izinombolo zibonakala

- **Phezu kwombono we-3D** (ngaphansi kwesokudla, eduze kwekhyubhu lokubuka): hambisa isikhombi phezu kwemodeli. Ibhokisi likhombisa izinga iphoyinti elisulapha kulo nokuthi liphakeme kangakanani phezu kwe-floor yalelo zinga, bese kuba **X, Y no-Z**. Phezu kwendawo engenalutho ifunda iphoyinti ku-floor yezinga eliphansi kakhulu.
- **Ku-plani** (ngaphansi kwesokudla, uma {{ui:Dweba}} ivuliwe): izinga eliboniswa yiplani nokuphakama kwe-floor yalo, nka **X** no-**Y** wesikhombi uma sisePlani.
- Uma ifayela linokuguqulwa kwemephu (lalithunyelwe namakhoordinethi emephu), zombili amabhokisi engeza **empumalanga nasenyakatho** kugridi yemephu, ngakwi-{{ui:Amayunithi}} oyikhethile (ngayo yonke imvamisa ibhalwe eduze kwawo; izinyawo nezintshi zibonakala njengezinyawo zedesimali). Umdwebo (DWG noma DXF) ugcina amayunithi wawo: ifunda ikhombisa amagama awo, futhi ayiguqulwa ngakunye.

Zonke ama-X, Y no-Z zilinganiswa kusukela **emsukeni wephrojekthi**, ngokwe-{{ui:Amayunithi}} akhethwe ebhareni lamathuluzi (izinyawo nezintshi, izintshi, izinyawo zedesimali, amamilimitha, amasentimitha noma amamitha). Y ehlelekile ibheka enyakatho yemodeli, engahlali ibe yinyakatho yeqiniso. Empumalanga nasenyakatho zemephu zilandela ukukhetha okufanayo (izinyawo nezintshi zibonakala njengezinyawo zedesimali); uma iyunithi yesistimu yemephu ingafani nelo oyikhethile, ibhalwa ngaphakathi kwamabhulaketi ngemuva kwezinombolo, isibonelo *(US survey feet)*, ukuze umsinyi abone ukuthi isistimu uqobo ibhalwe ngani.

## Amamakha amabili

- **Umsuka wephrojekthi** (isiyingi esibomvu esinesiphambano): iphoyinti 0, 0, 0 lapho zonke izikhombisi zemodeli zilinganiswa khona.
- **Iphoyinti lokuhlola** (isiyingi eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka esinonxantathu): iphoyinti lesiza elinikezwa ububanzi, ubude nokuphakama kwalo, noma amakhoordinethi emephu. Ku-3D, ligxile kulo ukuze ufunde ukuthi lilaphi.

Uma isayithi lisemsukeni wephrojekthi, njengesakhiwo esisha esidwetshwe lapha, kukhona okukodwa okubonisa kokubili. Chofoza inkinobho yesiphambano kunoma yiliphi ibhokisi ukuze ufihle noma ubonise amamakha; i-BimCraft ikhumbula ukukhetha kwakho kulesi siphequluli.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Izinombolo ziyashintsha njengoba isikhombi sihamba, nebhokisi leplani liqamba izinga oliklame ohlwini lwezinga.
- Amamakha ahlala endaweni lapho uzungeza, uhambisa nosondeza.
