## Vula amaphaneli

Khetha {{ui:Imiklamo yamaphaneli}} kumenyu {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} (noma thayipha {{cmd:PANELS}}). Uhlu lunawo wonke udonga owudwetshiwe, wonke amaphansi nawo wonke amaphahla emodeli, ngayinye ngethikhi. Udonga uza nezicabha nezikhenkethi njengezimbobo. Iphansi lakhiwa njengesikwele esizungeze umjikelezo waso, nama-joist ahamba ngendlela emfushane. Iphahla lakhiwa njenge-gable phezu komjikelezo wodonga lalo, nge-pitch yophahla; uhlu lukusho lokho, ngoba i-hip noma iphahla eyinkimbinkimbi akuyona i-gable.

## Impahla yokwakha, usayizi nokuhlukana

Khetha {{ui:Impahla yokwakha}}: **Ihlaza** noma **Insimbi ekhanyayo**. Izingcezu zensimbi ziyi-C-stud ezibizwa ngokujula kwazo nokuqina kwazo (isibonelo C92-1.2), nethrekhi lama-plate. {{ui:Usayizi wengxenye}} ukhetha usayizi oyodwa kumaphaneli wonke athikiwe, noma uyishiye ku-Default ukuze uthathe okuzenzakalelayo kwendlela ngayinye yephaneli. {{ui:Spacing}} kuwukuhlukana phakathi kwama-stud, ama-joist noma ama-rafter. Lezi yizinani zokuqala ozokusetha. Azikho ukuhlola kwekhodi: ukuthi i-stud ekuhlukana kwayo ithwele lolo donga, nokuthi i-header inde kangakanani, kuncike kumathebula ekhodi (i-Building regulations add-on inawo we-IRC) nakunjiniyela wakho.

## Okutholayo

Iphaneli lokuqala elithikiwe lidwetshwa ngesikali kuwindi, namabanga. {{ui:Uhlu lokusika (CSV)}} ligcina zonke izingcezu zephaneli ngamaki, usayizi nobude, nobude bezitoko okufanele uzithenge. {{ui:Imiklamo yeshobhu (PDF)}} igcina ikhasi elilodwa le-A3 ngalinye iphaneli, zonke izingcezu zimakwe nohlu lwazo lokusika. {{ui:Umdwebo (SVG)}} ugcina iphaneli lokuqala njengomdwebo. Noma yiyiphi into ekhodi yokwakha ingayikwazi ukuyakha (ukuvulwa okusondele kakhulu ekugcineni kodonga, i-header engalingani ngaphansi kwama-plate, uchungechunge olude kunesitoko eside kakhulu) iyalulwa ohlwini lwezinkinga njengoba ikhona nakuwindi.

## Akwenzi

Amaphaneli ayimidwebo eyenziwa emodelini uma uyicela: akukho okubhalwa emodelini, ngakho ahlala efana nawo. Ukuze ufake ukwakheka emodelini njengezinto ezizimele, sebenzisa {{ui:Uhlaka lokwakha}} wodonga, amaphansi namaphahla ehlaza.
