## Okuzofunda

Yini eyenzeka ku-modeli ye-IFC uma abantu abaningi behlela ngesikhathi esifanayo. I-CadCraft Studio iqala futhi ijoyine iseshini ye-Live TeamWork (Yabelana, Joyina); leli khasi likhuluma ngemodeli ngokwayo.

## Bonke bahlela ngesikhathi esisodwa

Imodeli yomphathi ifika kubo bonke abajoyina. Qamba kabusha izinto, shintsha amanani ezici zazo, faka, shintsha noma susa amathuluzi nezinto njengoba kujwayelekile. Ukuhlela kwakho kubonakala masinyane; ukuhlela kwabanye kuvela kancane ngemva kokuthi bakwenze. Uma ukuhlela kushintsha into oyibonayo (into efakiwe noma esusiwe), i-BimCraft iphinde idwebe imodeli futhi igcine ukubuka kwakho.

- **Abantu ababili beshintsha into eyodwa:** ushintsho olwenziwe kamuva emugqeni ofanayo wemodeli luyanqoba. Isethi yezici eyabelwana ngezinto eziningi ikhopishelwa into oyihlelayo, njengakuqala, ukuze ezinye zigcine amanani azo.
- **Into abanye abantu eyisusile sekuthe:** ayisakwazi ukuhlelwa. Ukuhlela kwakho kuyahlehliswa, futhi ibha yesimo ichaza ukuthi kungani.
- Imigqa emisha yomuntu ngamunye kumodeli ithola izinombolo zayo, ukuze abantu ababili bangalibali ngenombolo efanayo.

## Babonane

Wonke umuntu oseshini unombala wakhe. Ku-ukubuka kwe-3D ubona isikhombi somuntu ngamunye njengomcibisholo ngombala wakhe negama lakhe eduze kwawo, esinqumeni asikhomba kuso, kanye nebhokisi elimile ngombala wakhe elizungeze ngayinye yezinto abayikhethile.

Izikhombi, amagama nokukhethwa kuseshini kuphela: akusoze kulondolozwe efayelini futhi akusoze kungene ekuhlehliseni komuntu. Ziyashelela zingene lapho kukhona ngayinye yabantu, ngakho zihlala zibushelelezi nakuxhumo oluhamba kancane.

## Ukuhlehlisa kuhlehlisa umsebenzi wakho

{{ui:Hlehlisa}} uhlehlisa ukuhlela kwakho kokugcina futhi ushiye ukwabanye bonke. Uma othile eshintshe into efanayo kusukela lapho, akukho okuhlehliswayo futhi ibha yesimo itshela ukuthi ngubani.

## Joyina usebenzisa isiphequluli

Ilungu lethimba elingenayo i-Studio lingaskena isithombe ngaphansi kwekhodi lomphathi ngefoni, ithebhulethi noma ikhompyutha kunethiwekhi efanayo. I-BimCraft ivula esiphequluli futhi ivule imodeli yomphathi uma umphathi evumela ukungena. Isixhumanisi sokumema sivumela umuntu ukuthi ajoyine kwenye inethiwekhi: umphathi udinga isihlalo se-Team Cloud Plan sale-app, abanye bonke bajoyina mahhala, futhi isixhumanisi sisebenza kuphela uma iseshini yomphathi ivulekile.

## Lapho iseshini iphela

Umphathi kuphela ulondoloza imodeli. Abanye bonke bagcina ikhophi engalondolozwanga: sebenzisa Londoloza njenge-IFC uma ufuna ukuyigcina.

:::note
Imodeli yokuqala kuphela esigcemeni ihlanganyelwa. Ukuvula enye ifayela ngesikhathi seshini kushiya iseshini kulowo iwindi.
:::
