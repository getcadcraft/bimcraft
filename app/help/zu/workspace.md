Isikrini se-BimCraft sinebha phezulu nezindawo ezintathu ngaphansi kwayo: ipaneli ngakwesokunxele, isiteji maphakathi nepaneli ethi {{ui:Izici}} ngakwesokudla. Okubonakala kuyo ngayinye kuncike kulokho ovule: imodeli ye-IFC, ifayela lephrojekthi noma lomndeni, umdwebo noma ifolda.

## Ibha yohlelo

Ibha encane phezulu iyeyesayithi ye-CadCraft, hhayi ye-BimCraft ngokwayo. Kusukela kwesokunxele kuya kwesokudla:

- Uphawu lwe-CadCraft: iya ekhasini lokuqala le-CadCraft.
- Igama lohlelo nemenyu yenguqulo. Ku-build yasesikhungweni ikhombisa ukuthi yi-build yini oyisebenzisayo futhi ikuvumela ukushintsha ibe inguqulo ekhishiwe.
- {{ui:Mayelana ne-BimCraft}}: ikhasi lohlelo kusayithi.
- {{ui:Izilungiselelo}}: izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft nezabelwa zonke izinhlelo. Bona [Izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft](topic:settings).
- {{ui:Isikrini esigcwele}}: ishintsha izimo zesikrini. Bona [Izimo zesikrini](topic:workspace#screen-modes) ngezansi.
- {{ui:Iphepha}} ne-{{ui:Imodeli}}: izindikimba ezikhanyayo nezimnyama. Umbono we-3D namadwebo we-2D alandela indikimba oyikhethile.

## Ipaneli yangakwesokunxele

Ipaneli yangakwesokunxele ihlala iqala nge-**Vula ifayela noma ifolda**:

- Indawo yokuwisa, {{ui:Khetha ifayela, noma lahla ifayela noma ifolda lapha}}. Chofoza ukuze ukhethe ifayela elilodwa, noma lahla ifolda noma ifayela phezu kwayo (noma kunoma yiluphi uhlangothi lwekhasi).
- {{ui:Vula ifolda}}: khetha ifolda yephrojekthi yonke. Bona [Vula ifolda yephrojekthi yonke](topic:open-project-folder).
- {{ui:Amafolda akamuva}}: ivela ku-Chrome ne-Edge ngemuva kokuvula ifolda ngesikhethi sefolda noma ngokulahla. Chofoza {{ui:Vula futhi}} negama lefolda ukuze uyivule futhi, noma × encane ukuze uyikhohlwe.

Ngaphansi kwayo, izigaba zivela njengoba zisebenza:

| Isigaba | Lapho sibonakala | Okukuso |
|---|---|---|
| {{ui:Izinguquko}} | Imodeli ye-IFC ivuliwe | Izinguquko zakho ezingakalondolozwa, eziqala ezintsha, nge-{{ui:Hlehlisa}} ne-{{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}} |
| {{ui:Ifayela}} | Imodeli noma ikhadi lefayela livuliwe | Izici zefayela: usayizi, ischema noma ukukhishwa, iphrojekthi, umlobi, inani lezinto |
| {{ui:Umdwebo}} | I-DWG noma i-DXF ivuliwe | Ifomethi, amayunithi, inani lezinto nezingqimba, amashidi, amadwebo axhunyiwe, izinsiza |
| {{ui:Ifolda}} | Ifolda ivuliwe | Inani lamafayela namamodeli, usayizi, izinkinga ezitholakele |
| {{ui:Buyela kwifolda}} | Uvule ifayela kusuka efolda | Ibuyela embikweni wefolda |
| {{ui:Izingqimba}} | Umdwebo uvuliwe | Ibhokisi lokukhetha ngalinye lengqimba, nge-{{ui:Konke kuvuliwe}} ne-{{ui:Konke kuvaliwe}} |
| {{ui:Izitezi}} | Imodeli ye-IFC ivuliwe | Ibhokisi lokukhetha ngalinye lesitezi, eliphezulu kuqala |
| {{ui:Izinto}} | Imodeli ye-IFC ivuliwe | Inani lezinto zohlobo ngalunye emodelini (ezingu-30 ezivame kakhulu) |

## Isiteji

Maphakathi nesikrini kubonisa ifayela ovulile.

- **Okungenalutho**: "Vula imodeli yesakhiwo." nge-{{ui:Khetha ifayela}} ne-{{ui:Vula ifolda}}, nencazelo emfushane yefomethi ngayinye.
- **Imodeli ye-IFC**: umbono we-3D nebha yamathuluzi phezulu kwawo, ngamaqembu aqanjiwe, afana nawesicelo sokudweba se-CadCraft, isithonjana sethuluzi ngasinye phezulu kwegama lalo: {{ui:Ukubuka}} ({{ui:Lwanele}}, {{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Uhlangothi}}, {{ui:Imiphetho}}, {{ui:Izindawo}}); {{ui:Hlela}} ({{ui:Hlehlisa}}, {{ui:Phinda wenze}}, {{ui:Kopisha}}, {{ui:Namathisela}} kanye {{ui:Okuningi}}, bheka [Kopisha, namathisela nephinda izinto zomtapo](topic:copy-paste)); {{ui:Imodeli}} ({{ui:Amayunithi}} ne-{{ui:Dweba}}); {{ui:Umtapo}} ({{ui:Umtapo}}, {{ui:Izinsiza}}, {{ui:Izibuyekezo}}, {{ui:Izikweletu}}); {{ui:Buyekeza}} ({{ui:Bonisa}}, {{ui:Isigaba}}, {{ui:Hamba}}, {{ui:Linganisa}}; bona [Sika imodeli ye-IFC ngendiza yesigaba](topic:section-plane)); kanye {{ui:Okukhiphayo}} (ukurenda, umbono ophilayo, i-VR, ukuthumela, ukupenda nemibono elondoloziwe). Uma {{ui:Dweba}} ivuliwe, ibha yokudweba ngaphansi kwayo inombala ofanayo: {{ui:Ukubuka}} (izinga kanye nohlobo lweplani), {{ui:Dweba}}, {{ui:Hlela}} kanye {{ui:Umtapo}}. Ikhyubhu lokubuka lihlala ekhoneni elingakwesokudla ngezansi. Bona [Vula imodeli ye-IFC ubuke ngo-3D](topic:open-ifc-model). {{ui:Izilungiselelo}}, {{ui:Okujwayelekile}} ikhetha ukuthi izithonjana, amalebula noma kokubili kulezi zinkinobho, ibonise noma ifihle amagama amaqembu, futhi chofoza ngesokudla ebhareni lamathuluzi ukuhlela amathuluzi namaqembu alo. Esikrinini esincane ibha yamathuluzi ilahla amagama angaphansi kwezithonjana zayo.
- **Ifayela lephrojekthi noma lomndeni**: ikhadi lefayela, nesithombe sokubuka, ulwazi lwephrojekthi namafayela axhunyiwe. Bona [Funda ifayela lephrojekthi noma lomndeni](topic:read-rvt-file).
- **DWG noma DXF**: umugqa wamathebhu ({{ui:Imodeli}}, bese kunye ngeshidi ngalinye, bese {{ui:Izinsiza}} uma umdwebo unezinsiza ezinamathegi, {{ui:3D}}, {{ui:Lwanele}} kanye ne-{{ui:Ukubuka kokuphrinta}}), umdwebo, nezixhumanisi ze-X ne-Y ezibonakalayo ekhoneni. Bona [Buka umdwebo we-DWG noma DXF](topic:view-dwg) ne-[Bona umdwebo we-DWG noma DXF ku-3D](topic:dwg-in-3d).
- **Ifolda**: umbiko wefolda, nebhokisi ethi {{ui:Qala lapha}} namathebhu wamamodeli, izixhumanisi, amamodeli e-IFC nemidwebo. Bona [Vula ifolda yephrojekthi yonke](topic:open-project-folder).

Umugqa wesimo ongaphansi kwesiteji ukutshela ukuthi i-BimCraft yenzani ("Ifunda imodeli…", "Yakha ijiyomethri: izinto ezingu-1200 kwezingu-5400") nokuthi yini eyahluleka, ebomvu, uma kukhona okuhlulekile. Imiyalezo yempumelelo inyamalala ngemva kwemizuzwana embalwa.

## Ipaneli yezici

Ipaneli yangakwesokudla ibonisa:

- Ngemodeli ye-IFC: into owe-chofoze, negama, uhlobo, ithegi, i-GlobalId kanye nalo lonke isethi yezici. Izinani ongazishintsha zibonakala njengezinkinobho. Bona [Izinkundla zepaneli yezici](topic:properties-panel).
- Ngomdwebo: ukuthi ungahamba kanjani, imidwebo nezithombe ezixhunyiwe nokuthi zatholakala yini ngayinye, nohlu lwalokho okuthi **Akukadwetshwa okwamanje**.
- Ngekhadi lefayela noma ifolda: inothi elifushane ngalokho okufanele ukuvule okulandelayo.

## Esikrinini esincane

Uma iwindi lincane kunesikrini selaptop, i-BimCraft ihlela izindawo zayo phezu kwazo: isiteji phezulu, bese izipaneli ngaphansi. Skrola ikhasi ukuze ufinyelele izipaneli. Kufoni zakha ikholomu eyodwa; kuthebulethi, ezimbili.

## Izimo zesikrini

Inkinobho ethi {{ui:Isikrini esigcwele}} ebhareni lohlelo, noma {{key:Ctrl+Shift+F}} kuzo zonke izinhlelo, ishintsha phakathi kwezimo ezintathu:

1. Ejwayelekile.
2. Isikrini esigcwele, namabha asabonakala.
3. Isikrini esigcwele nebha yohlelo ifihliwe. Hambisa isikhombi uye emaphethelweni aphezulu, noma thepha lapho, ukuze uyibuyisele.

Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukubuyela kwejwayelekile. Izisiphequluli ezingasekeli isikrini esigcwele (njengakwi-iPhone) zishintsha phakathi kwejwayelekile namabha afihliwe kuphela.
