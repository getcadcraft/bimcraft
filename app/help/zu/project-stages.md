## Okuzokwenziwa nguwe

Hamba ngezigaba zephrojekthi ngephaneli eyodwa: bhala isidingo somkhthi njengamagumbi nezindawo ezihlosiwe, qhathanisa izinketho zekhonsepthi ngakho, gcina isichazi nemininingwane yokwakha ihlobene nezinhlobo ezifanele kuzo, buyekeza amadrowing okukhiqiza njengoba ebuya kubahwebi, bese urekhoda imiyalelo yesayithi ngezakhi ezishintshwa yiyo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Kwiphaneli ekhohlo vula **Izigaba zephrojekthi**, bese **Isidingo**.
2. Namathisela amagumbi, igumbi elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye: `Living; 1; 30` isho igumbi elilodwa elingu-30 m². Inani lingasuswa. Khetha **Engeza amagumbi**. Gcwalisa iklayenti, indawo, isabelomali, ukubekezela nohlelo, bese **Londoloza isidingo**.
3. Dweba izindonga. Igumbi ngalinye elenziwe yizindonga elinegama legumbi lesidingo liyabalwa (inombolo elandela igama, njengo "Bedroom 2", iyanganakwa). **Ishedyuli yezindawo** ithi **Ifinyelelwe**, **Imfushane**, **Idlule** noma **Ayidwetshwanga**, nomehluko ku-m².
4. Ukuqala iphrojekthi entsha ngale ndlela, khetha **Qala iphrojekthi entsha ngesidingo**. Ivula imodeli entsha, evela kwesinye sezifanekiso ozigcinile uma ukhetha esisodwa, bese ibeka isidingo njengesinyathelo sayo sokuqala.
5. Vula **Izinketho ngokumelene nesidingo**. Yonke inketho yobukhulu bomumo egcinwe kumodeli idwetshwe ngesikali esisodwa nendawo yayo ephelele, okucelwa yisidingo, kanye nokuthi amagumbi esidingo angaki angena kangakanani. **Beka isidingo lapha** ifaka amagumbi ezitezi zenketho, eyiphansi phezulu, bese ikhomba okungangenwa kukho. Izindleko zivela etafuleni lamanani ephrojekthi kuphela; uma kungekho mugqa ohambisanayo ithi "Akukho izinga".
6. Vula **Izinhlobo, izichazi nemininingwane**. Ngayinye yezindonga, izivalo, amawindi nezinhlobo zophahla nephansi ibonisa ukuthi inombhalo wesichazi yini, imininingwane exhunyiwe kuyo, nokuthi isichazi, amashedyuli namashidi ayahlukana kuphi. **Xhuma umininingwane** yengeza ifayela le-DetailCraft noma le-CadCraft, ikheli, noma ishidi lalo modeli.
7. Vula **Amadrowing okukhiqiza**. Khetha izakhi idrowing yokukhiqiza eyimbozayo kumodeli, bese **Yamukela**. Sebenzisa **Vumela**, **Njengoba kuphawuliwe** noma **Buyekeza** negama nenothi yomhloli. **Ithunyelwe kabusha** yenza inguqulo elandelayo yalo rekhodi futhi igcine umlando wokubuyekeza. Isimo siphinde sibhalwe kuzakhi ngayinye.
8. Vula **Imiyalelo yesayithi**. Khetha izakhi, nikeza imbangela nokuthi iyithinta kanjani izindleko nesikhathi ngamazwi akho, bese **Yirekhoda**. Ngemva kokurekhoda udaba, **Chaza izinguquko ezenzeke kusukela kulolu daba** ithi yiziphi izakhi ezishintshile eziyimbozwa yimiyalelo nezishintshile ngaphandle kokuthi kurekhodwe lutho.

## Okuwusizo ukwazi

- Isinyathelo ngasinye yi-**Hlehlisa** eyodwa. Akukho okwenqatshwayo: isidingo esingafinyelelwe, uhlobo olungavumelani nombhalo walo, noma idrowing yokukhiqiza ephuzile kuyisexwayiso noma inothi ku-**Izinkinga**.
- Amagumbi esidingo ayizindawo ezingenazo izindonga. Anesilinganiso esihlosiwe, hhayi indawo edwetshiwe, ngakho amashedyuli amagumbi kanye ne-COBie abawabali njengamagumbi adwetshiwe.
- Isakhiwo sibeka amagumbi ezitezi ngokwendawo kuphela. Asidwebi izindonga.
- Umbhalo wesichazi ubhalwa ku-**Imibhalo** kusuka kumodeli. Lapha uqhathaniswa nosayizi wemodeli kuphela: akukho sigaba sesitandade esibhalelwa wena.
- Amadrowing okukhiqiza nemiyalelo yesayithi ngamarekhodi ngomlando ofanayo nephaneli ethi **Izindaba nezinguqulo**, ngakho zombili iziphaneli ziwabonisa.
- Isimo sedrowing yokukhiqiza siyimpahla yakho ngayinye (`CadCraft_ShopDrawing`), kanti imiyalelo yesayithi yi-`CadCraft_SiteInstruction`, ngakho **Thola** kanye **Shaya ngempahla** kungazisebenzisa.
- Izimpawu ezihlolwe endaweni (Isigaba 7) zisezici ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuqinisekiswa**.
