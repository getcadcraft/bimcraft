## Okuzokwenza

Hlanganisa ibhe evamile, uyibeke izikhathi eziningana, bese ushintsha yonke ikhophi ngokushintsha eyodwa.

## Lapho ithuluzi likhona

- Izakhiwo ze-ribbon: {{ui:Ukwakheka}} › **Imodeli** › {{ui:Iqembu lemodeli}}.
- Isakhiwo esidala: ibha yokudweba › imenyu {{ui:Amagumbi}} › Iqembu lemodeli.

## Izinyathelo

1. Ngokusebenzisa Khetha, khetha izinto: chofoza eyodwa, {{key:Ctrl}}+chofoza noma {{key:Shift}}+chofoza ezinye.
2. Khetha {{ui:Iqembu lemodeli}}. Zishintsha zibe yiqembu ngokushesha. Thayipha {{ui:Igama leqembu}} kumugqa ngaphambili ukuze uliqambe igama, noma thayipha elinye kamuva ukuze uliqamise kabusha.
3. Cindezela {{ui:Beka elinye}} bese uchofoza lapho ikhophi kufanele imi khona: ikhona yayo engezansi kwesobunxele iya endaweni ochofoze kuyo.
4. Shintsha noma iyiphi ikhophi: hambisa uhlangothi lodonga, lungisa usayizi wesivalo, susa insika. Zonke ezinye zekhophi zeqembu ziyashintsha ngesinyathelo esisodwa, nokuhlehlisa okukodwa kubuyisa konke.
5. Uma {{ui:Iqembu lemodeli}} sekusesandleni sakho, ukuchofoza into kukhetha iqembu layo lonke.

## Ezinye izinto ongazenza

- {{ui:Engeza ezikhethiwe}}: engeza izinto eziningi eqenjini okhethe kulo ekugcineni.
- {{ui:Susa iqembu}}: lale khophi ibe izinto ezijwayelekile futhi. Ezinye zekhophi zihlala ziyiqembu.
- Hambisa ikhophi yonke ngo-{{ui:Hambisa}}: iyiqembu elifanayo endaweni entsha; ezinye azihambi.
- Susa izinto zekhophi bese leyo khophi iyanyamalala.

## Okungaba seqenjini

Izindonga nemisebe yazo namafasitela, insika namafulowa adwetshwe zingabekwa futhi zigcinwe zifana. Iqembu lingabamba nezinye izinto, kodwa khona-ke alikwazi ukubekwa futhi; umugqa uchaza ngani.

Amakhophi ayahanjiswa, akagujwa noma abonakaliswe.

## Ku-IFC

Iqembu liyi-IfcGroup ene-IfcRelAssignsToGroup kwizinto zalo, ezihlala ziyizindonga, insika namafulowa ajwayelekile. Yonke ikhophi yeqembu elilodwa ithwele i-id yencazelo efanayo.

## Akukakwakhiwa

- Ukujikeleza noma ukubonakalisa ikhophi.
- Izitebhisi, uphahla, amafulowa amagumbi nezinto zethala lemitapo eqenjini elibekwa futhi.
- Iphrofayili yodonga, ukuthambeka kwalo nezimbobo zodonga akuyi kumakhophi.
- Amaqembu ngaphakathi kwamaqembu.
