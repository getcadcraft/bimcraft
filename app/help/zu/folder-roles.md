## Isimo sekhophi

Yonke ikhophi yemodeli, ifayela elikhulu namakhophi okusekela anamanani, konke kuthola ukuhlolwa kwekhanda.

| Incwadi | Okusho kwayo |
|---|---|
| **Eqediwe** | Ikhanda lefayela nendondo yalo kulingana ngaphakathi kwefayela |
| **Akuqediwe** | Ifayela lifushane kunenkomba yalo: lanqunyiwe lapho likopishelwa, lilayishelwa noma lilungiswa, noma kuyifayela lefu elisemakhasini "online only" |
| **Akufundeki** | Ifayela alisiyo i-compound file esemthethweni, noma alikwazanga ukufundwa |

Kwikholomu **Isimo** imodeli ibonisa **Eqediwe**, i-*n* akuqediwe ngombala we-amber uma okungenani ikhophi eyodwa imbi kodwa enye ilungile, noma **Akuqediwe** obomvu uma zingekho ikhophi elungile.

## Amakhophi okusekela

Amafayela anegama elinamanani njengo `Name.0001.rvt`, `Name.0002.rvt` njalo njalo ayizikhophi zokusekela ezinamanani ka `Name.rvt`. Ahlelwa ngaphansi kwemodeli yawo, amasha kuqala, ku-**Zonke … amakhophi**. Ikhadi lifundwa kusuka kufayela elikhulu uma liphelele, noma kusuka kokusekela okusha okuphelele ("newest complete").

## Izindima

Zikhonjiswa ngamagama aphansi egama lemodeli.

| Indima | Okusho kwayo |
|---|---|
| Oxhunywe *yenye imodeli*: ingemuva | Enye imodeli kule folda iyixhumanisa, ngakho kungenzeka kakhulu ukuthi ingemuva labanye, njengemodeli yomeluleki |
| Ifayela lesisekelo noma elingenagama | Igama njengo-Blank, Template, Default, Untitled noma Project1: indawo yokuqala, noma ifayela elingazange lithiywe ngokufanele |
| Imodeli emaphakathi | Imodeli emaphakathi esetshenziswa ngabaningi. Umsebenzi wenziwa kwikhophi yasendaweni, hhayi lapha |
| Umndeni (ingxenye) | Ifayela `.rfa` noma `.rft`: ingxenye, hhayi iphrojekthi |
| Izikhwama kuphela | Ifayela elikhulu seliphelile, kusele izikhwama zokusekela ezinamanani kuphela |

## Ukuthi "Qala lapha" ikhethwa kanjani

BimCraft isebenzisa ukucabanga okungenziwa umsebenzisi onolwazi:

1. Abaviwe bayiphrojekthi abafundekayo amafayela (`.rvt`, `.rte`) angaxhunywanga yimodeli enye futhi angesona isisekelo.
2. Amaphakathi namamodeli okusekela kuphela kubalwa uma kungekho okungcono.
3. Phakathi kwabaviwe, eyolongwe kamuva kakhulu iyanqoba.

Ibhokisi lidweba izizathu zalo, futhi lixwayisa uma ifayela elibonakala njengokukhetha okuvelele lonakele, uma ezinye izimodeli ziyingemuva, nalapho ukukhethwa kuyimodeli emaphakathi. Kuyisiqagelo; buza lowo owenza umsebenzi wokugcina wephrojekthi.

## Isimo sokuxhuma

| Incwadi | Okusho kwayo |
|---|---|
| **Kule folda** | Itholakale endleleni ehlobene egciniwe, noma ekugcineni kwendlela egcwele egciniwe ngaphakathi kwefolda |
| **Igama elifanayo litholakele** | Akusendleleni yalo, kodwa ifayela elinegama elifanayo lisefolda. Hlola ukuthi liyilelo elifanele |
| **Ifayela le-Library** | Ithebula lezinkomba noma lekhodi lomhlangano. Lezi zifika nalolu hlelo kwikhompyutha ngayinye |
| **Akulona kule folda** | Alitholakali noma kuphi kule folda |

Ikholomu **Amaxhumanisi atholakele** kwi-**Amamodeli** ibonisa ukuthi kwatholakala amaxhumanisi emodeli angaki, phakathi kwalawo alindelwe kule folda (amafayela e-library akabalwa).

## Okubekwe eceleni

| Umugqa | Okusho kwayo |
|---|---|
| Amafolda okugcina i-Revit (awafundwa) | Amafolda `*_backup`, `*_log` ne-`Revit_temp`, kanye namafolda afihliwe ne-`__MACOSX` |
| Okusalayo kokukhiya, ilogi nokusekela | Amafayela `.dwl`, `.dwl2`, `.bak`, `.err`, `.log`, `.tmp`, `.sv$`, `.ac$`, `.lck` ne-`.slog` |
| Amanye amafayela | Konke okunye (okokuqala angu-40 kuqanjwe amagama) |
