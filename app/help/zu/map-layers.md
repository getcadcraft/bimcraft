## Okuzokwenza

Bona imodeli endaweni yayo: imephu yesitaladi noma isithombe sendiza sesayithi emhlabeni ngaphansi kwayo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC ethwele indawo yayo emephini (ukuguqulwa kwemephu, IfcMapConversion, nohlelo lokuhlela oluhlelekile). Abathumeli bayibhala lapho izindawo zeprojekthi (iphoyinti lokuhlola, izindawo ezabelwanayo) sezisethiwe.
2. Khetha {{ui:Bonisa}} › {{ui:Izingqimba zemephu…}}. Ingxoxo ichaza uhlelo lwemodeli, njengo-EPSG:28356. Imodeli engenayo ukuguqulwa kwemephu ayikwazi ukuba nezingqimba zemephu: ingxoxo iyakusho lokho.
3. Vula {{ui:Izingqimba zemephu ezivela ku-inthanethi}}. Kuze kube yilapho, akukho okucelwa noyiphi iseva; uma livuliwe, amaseva onezengeza abona indawo ezungeze imodeli nekheli lakho le-inthanethi, hhayi imodeli yakho.
4. Ku-{{ui:Engeza iqoqo}} khetha amathayela (ikheli elinezi-`{z}`, `{x}` kanye `{y}`), isevisi ye-WMS, WMTS noma ArcGIS ({{ui:Thola izingqimba}}, bese ukhetha eyodwa), noma isithombe nefayela lomhlaba laso.
5. Vala ingxoxo. Imephu ilele emhlabeni ngaphansi kwephoyinti eliphansi kakhulu lemodeli, ngaphezu kwendawo imodeli emi kuyo, nokuphinde kube kude kangaka kuyo yonke indawo ezungezile.

:::note
Imephu ibonakala esikrinini kuphela; ayilondolozwa efayelini le-IFC. Uhlu lwezingqimba ligcinwa kule bhrawuza. Sebenzisa kuphela amasevisi emephu okuvunyelwe imigomo yawo.
:::
