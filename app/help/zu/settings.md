Vula {{ui:Izilungiselelo}} ngegiya elisebhalini lohlelo noma nge-{{key:Mod+,}}. Ithebhu yokuqala, {{ui:BimCraft}}, ibamba izilungiselelo ezibhekelela lolu hlelo; {{ui:Jikelele}} ibamba ezabelwa zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft (itimu, ithuluzi, amapaneli). Izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft zikhona kuphela kulolu hlelo: uzishintsha lapha, futhi leli shiphini liyazikhumbula. {{ui:Setha kabusha izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft}} ngaphansi kwengxoxo iyazibuyisela ekuqaleni, kanye nezabelwanayo.

## Imenyu, okhiye nemouse

Isitayela semenyu (ithuluzi lakwa-BimCraft, imenyu yokuklama ikhaya, ribhoni ye-BIM noma umugqa womyalo womdwebo), okhiye nemenyu yakho, nokuthi ivili nezinkinobho zemouse zenzani. Bona [Imenyu, okhiye nemouse](topic:menus-keys-and-mouse).

## Lapho imodeli ivulwa

- **Bonisa amakamelo nezindawo** (kuvaliwe ekuqaleni): amakamelo nezindawo ziyizibhokisi ezibonakalayo ezingavimba izindonga ezisemuva kwazo, ngakho ziqala zifihliwe. Inkinobho ethi {{ui:Izindawo}} phezu kwemodeli isavula futhi icime kumodeli evuliwe.
- **Imiphetho**: {{ui:Imiphetho ivulekile}} (ukuqala) idweba imigqa yemiphetho enikeza ukubukeka kokudweba. {{ui:Amamodeli amancane kuphela}} idweba kuphela emamodelini angaphansi kwezinxantathu ezingu-300 000, okugcina amamodeli amakhulu eshesha ukuguqulwa. {{ui:Imiphetho ivaliwe}} iqala yonke imodeli ingenayo. Inkinobho ethi {{ui:Imiphetho}} isashintsha kumodeli evuliwe. Ngaphezu kwezinxantathu eziyizigidi ezingu-1.5 imiphetho ivalwa njalo.

## Imidwebo ivulwa ku-

Ukuthi umdwebo we-DWG noma we-DXF uqala ukubonakala kanjani: {{ui:3D (eshesha kakhulu)}} (ukuqala) udweba nomdwebo omkhulu ngokushesha, noma {{ui:Imodeli (2D)}} ivula umbono wesikhala semodeli obalele. Amathebhu ngaphezulu komdwebo ashintsha phakathi kwe-{{ui:Imodeli}}, amashidi kanye ne-{{ui:3D}} noma nini. Umdwebo ongenalutho esikhaleni semodeli, noma isiphequluli esingakwazi ukubonisa i-3D, uvula njalo ku-2D.

## Ukubuka ekuvulweni

Ikhamera imodeli evulwa ngayo: {{ui:Lwanele}} (ukuqala, imodeli yonke isuka ekhoneni), {{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phambili}} noma {{ui:Uhlangothi}}. Izinkinobho ezingaphezulu kwemodeli zishintsha ukubuka noma nini.

**Isakhiwo esiphendukile endaweni yaso**: amamodeli amaningi afika nesakhiwo ngokuthambeka ezinhlelweni zefayela (ekuhloleni noma kuzixhumanisi ezabelanwayo). {{ui:Sisesikwele nesakhiwo}} (ukuqala) ikhombisa isakhiwo esinjalo siqondile: {{ui:Lwanele}}, {{ui:Phezulu}}, {{ui:Phambili}}, {{ui:Uhlangothi}}, izinhlangothi zekhyubhu lokubuka nazo zonke izigaba ezintsha zilandela izindonga zakhe, futhi ikhampasi lekhyubhu lokubuka lisaqondisa enyakatho yemodeli. {{ui:Njengoba sibekwe esizeni}} igcina ama-axis efayela. Isakhiwo esinezindonga ezigijima ngama-axis sibukeka ngendlela efanayo kunoma iyiphi indlela.

## Ukubeka izinto zemitapo

**Qala ngokubopha kodonga kuvuliwe** (kuvaliwe ekuqaleni): lapho kuqala ukubeka noma ukunamathisela into yemitapo, ingemuva layo libophela edongeni eliseduze, libheke ikamelo. {{key:Tab}} noma {{ui:Ukubopha kodonga}} ebhalini yokubeka iyalicima okwamanje. Isitezi esingenazo izindonga siqala ngaphandle kwalo.

## Ibhokisi lesigaba

{{ui:Indawo ezungeze okukhethiwe}} yisikhala esishiywa yi-{{ui:Ibhokisi lesigaba elizungeze okukhethiwe}} ezinhlangothini zonke zezinto ozikhethile, ngamamilimitha: 300 ukuqala, kuze kube ngu-5000. Bona [Sika imodeli ye-IFC ngendiza yesigaba](topic:section-plane).

## Indawo

{{ui:Ububanzi nobude kusetshenziswa}} ukhetha uhlelo {{ui:Linganisa}} › {{ui:Indawo}} anika ububanzi nobude kulo: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326, ukuqala, njengoba i-GPS namamephu awebhu esebenzisa), {{ui:Idatha yefayela ngokwalo}}, noma enye yalezi: ETRS89, NAD83, GDA94, GDA2020, NZGD2000, JGD2011 neCGCS2000. Uhlelo lwamamephu nedatha yefayela lwalo nokho luyabonakala njalo. Bona [Linganisa amabanga, izindawo zobuso nezindawo nge-3D](topic:measure).

## Isikhombisi sekhona

Okuhlezi ekhoneni elingezansi kwesokudla lwemibono ye-3D (imodeli ye-IFC, nomdwebo oshintshelwe ku-3D): {{ui:Ikhyubhu lokubuka}} (ukuqala), {{ui:Isithonjana se-axis}} (i-X, Y no-Z ezincane) noma {{ui:Akukho}}. Kushintsha kanye. Umbono womdwebo we-2D awukaze ubonise lutho.

Ekhyubhini lokubuka chofoza ubuso, umphetho noma ikhona ukuze ubuke kuleyo ndlela, donsa ikhyubhu ukuze uligibe, donsa indandatho ukuze uyiphendule, bese uchofoza indlu ukuze uthole umbono wokuqala.

## Igama lakho kuzihloko

Uma ungene ngemvume, izihloko zithwala igama le-akhawunti yakho futhi i-BimCraft ayicelani lutho: lesi sigaba sikhombisa {{ui:Ukusuka ku-akhawunti yakho (<name>)}}. {{ui:Sebenzisa elinye igama}} ivula ibhokisi lelinye igama, elinqoba kuze kube yilapho ukhetha {{ui:Sebenzisa igama le-akhawunti yami}}. Uma ungene ngaphandle, ifomu lesihloko licela igama njengakuqala. Bona [Izihloko namafayela e-BCF](topic:issues-bcf).

## Abasizi

**Vumela abasizi be-AI nezikripthi ukusebenzisa i-BimCraft** (kuvaliwe ekuqaleni) livumela umsizi okukhompyutha yakho ukufinyelela imodeli evuliwe nge-CadCraft Studio: **Funda** ithola izinto ifunde izakhiwo zazo, **Shintsha ukubuka** iyazihlukanisa, iyazifihla futhi iyazibonisa. **Umsebenzi** uhlu lwezinkomba zawo. Bona [Vumela umsizi we-AI asebenze nemodeli yakho](topic:assistants).

:::note
Ezinye izilungiselelo zisebenza lapho imodeli ivulwa noma ukubeka kuqala. Imodeli evele ivuliwe igcina ukubukeka kwayo kuze usebenzise izinkinobho zethuluzi noma uyivule futhi.
:::
