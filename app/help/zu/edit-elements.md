## Okuzokwenza

Shintsha okusemodelini ye-IFC: susa izakhi ongazidingi, kopisha udonga noma isethi yempahla uyithumele endaweni enye, noma uhambise izakhi ngokusika nokunamathisela. Yonke inguquko iyisinyathelo esisodwa osikwazi ukusihlehlisa, nokulondoloza kudala ifayela elisha, ngakho umthombo wakho uhlala njengoba wawunjalo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC. Bona [Vula imodeli ye-IFC ubheke ku-3D](topic:open-ifc-model).
2. Khetha okufuna ukushintsha: chofoza isakhi, bamba {{key:Mod}} noma {{key:Shift}} uchofoze ukuze ukhethe okwengeziwe, noma cindezela {{key:Mod+A}} ({{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Khetha konke}}) ukuze ukhethe zonke izakhi ezibonakalayo.
3. Bese:
   - **Susa:** cindezela {{key:Delete}}, noma {{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Susa}}.
   - **Kopisha:** cindezela {{key:Mod+C}} noma chofoza {{ui:Kopisha}}.
   - **Sika:** cindezela {{key:Mod+X}}, noma {{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Sika}}. Izakhi ziyanyamalala, futhi ungazinamathisela.
4. Ukuze unamathisele, cindezela {{key:Mod+V}} noma chofoza {{ui:Namathisela}}. Ibhokisi elibonakala kancane, elifana nobukhulu balokho okukopishile, lilandela isikhombi esitezini elikhethwe kubha yokubeka. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuze uyiphendulele ngama-90°, bese uchofoza lapho okufanele ibe khona. Ukuchofoza ngakunye kubeka ikhophi entsha; cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuze uyeke.
5. Noma cindezela {{key:Mod+Shift+V}} ({{ui:Okuningi}}, {{ui:Namathisela endaweni}}): emva kokusika izakhi zibuyela endaweni ezazikuyo ngqo; emva kokukopisha ikhophi ifika eduze kwezakhi zoqobo.
6. Chofoza {{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}} ukuze ulande imodeli eshintshiwe.

## Okuhamba nesakhi

- **Izingxenye:** isitebhisi sithatha izinqwaba zaso, udonga lwekhathini lithatha amaphaneli alo, noma yini eyenziwe ngezingxenye ithatha zonke.
- **Ukuvuleka:** udonga luthatha ukuvuleka okusikwe kulo. Ukususa umnyango noma iwindi kususa nokuvuleka okwakugcwalisa, ukuze udonga luvale, njengoba kwenzeka kuhlelo lokudweba. Ukususa udonga odwetswe ku-BimCraft kususe nemiyango namawindi alo, bese izindonga ezisele ziyaxhunyiwa. Ukususa udonga kufayela elenziwe kwenye indawo kushiya iminyango namawindi alo lapho ayekhona.
- **Idatha:** ikhophi inohlobo, izinto, amasethi ezici kanye nezinani njengomthombo, futhi inesihlonzi esisha se-GlobalId. Ukushintsha inani ekhophini kamuva akushintshi umthombo.
- **Izinto zelabhulari** zigcina idatha yazo yelabhulari; ikhophi ithola inombolo yesinto elandelayo, nento esikiwe ebuyiselwe ngemuva igcina eyayo.

## Okufanele ukubone

- "Kususiwe: …" noma "Kunamathisiwe: …" ohlwini {{ui:Izinguquko}}. {{key:Mod+Z}} ibuyisela inguquko yokugcina; {{key:Mod+Y}} iyibuyisela futhi.
- Emva kokususa, ulayini wesimo utshela ukuthi izingxenye nezivuleko ezingaki nazo zisusiwe.
- Izakhi ezinamathisiwe zikhethiwe, ngakho ungazinamathisela endaweni yazo futhi ukwenze umugqa, noma uzisuse.

## Okufanele wazi

- Amakhophi asebenzisa ijometri efanayo nomthombo (i-IFC ivumela izakhi ukwabelana ngayo), ngakho ifayela likhula kancane kakhulu.
- Izakhi zesakhiwo ezinamathisiwe zingena kumodeli ezikopishwe kuyo, kuthebhu efanayo. Ukusebenzisa izinto zelabhulari ezibekiwe kwenye imodeli, kopisha into eyodwa ngesikhathi: bona [Kopisha, namathisela nophinde ukhiphe izinto zelabhulari](topic:copy-paste).
- Izitezi, izikhala, izakhiwo nendawo yesakhiwo azikwazi ukususwa noma ukukopishwa. Khetha izakhi ezikuzo kunalokho. Igumbi elidwetswe ku-BimCraft livela ezindongeni ezilizungezile: susa udonga ukuze uhlanganise amagumbi amabili.
- Izindonga, iminyango namawindi edwetswe ngamathuluzi wakhe e-BimCraft awakwazi ukukopishwa, ukusikwa noma ukuphindwa okwamanje; ulayini wesimo uyatshela lokho. Zidwebe futhi. Ukususa kuyasebenza.
- Ukukopisha kubuye kubeke amagama nezici zento okukopishwe yona ebhodini lakho lokopisha njengombhalo, ukuze unamathisele kuspredshithi noma ku-i-imeyili.

## Izinkinga ezivamile

- **{{ui:Susa}} ishaywe ngegrey.** Akukho okukhethiwe, noma kukhethwe izitezi noma izikhala kuphela. Yibheke ukuze ufunde ukuthi kungani.
- **Ukunamathisela kuthi izakhi zivela kwenye imodeli.** Uvule enye imodeli kusukela lapho uzikopishe khona. Vula imodeli yokuqala futhi uzikopishe lapho.
- **Ikhophi enamathisiwe isesitezini esihlukile.** Ibha yokubeka ye-{{ui:Isitezi}} inquma lapho ifika khona; khetha isitezi ngaphambi kokuchofoza.
