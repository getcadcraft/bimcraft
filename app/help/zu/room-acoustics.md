Isigaba se-{{ui:Room acoustics}} sesobunxele sibala isikhathi somsindo ohlala egumbini ngalinye. Sifunda ubukhulu bendawo yegumbi ngalinye, ukuphakama kwalo, izindonga, iminyango neziwindi kusuka kumodeli ye-IFC evuliwe. Akukho okushintshwa kumodeli.

## Khetha ukuqedwa

Lapho imodeli isho ukuthi yini esembozweni, igumbi liyayifunda: ungqimba wangaphakathi wohlobo ngalunye lodonga (i-gypsum, ipulasta, udaka lwebhricks, ukhonkolo nebhulokhi kuyaziwa), uhlobo lwephansi (izinkuni noma ukhonkolo) nokuthi isilingi sinamathayili yini. Ilebula eseduze nokukhetha ngakunye likhombisa ukuthi ivela emodelini, kuyisisetho esizenzakalelayo noma ukukhetha kwakho; khetha ukuqedwa okuhlukile ukuze ushintshe. Konke imodeli engakushongo kuhlala kusetho esizenzakalelayo. Izinkomba ziyamanani ajwayelekile ashicilelwe; zishintshe ngedatha yokuhlola yomkhiqizo uma uyinayo. Thayipha i-**target** (ukuphindaphinda okude kakhulu kwesilinganiso esiphakathi ofuna) futhi noma yiliphi igumbi elidlula kulo lizoba yisexwayiso, nokumunca okuzolehlisa.

## Funda ithebula

Ukuphindaphinda komsindo kunikezwa kusukela ku-125 Hz kuya ku-4 kHz. I-Sabine ifanele amagumbi aphilile, neyi-Eyring amagumbi afile; umugqa omaka ngokuthi *Used* uthatha i-Eyring lapho ukumunca okuphakathi kukhulu kuno-0.2. Ukumunca komoya kufakiwe (kunciphisa amabhendi aphezulu emagumbini amakhulu). Kuyimodeli yezibalo: iqagela ukuthi umsindo usakazeka ngokulinganayo, ngakho amagumbi amade noma abanzi kakhulu namagumbi amancane kakhulu ayisiqondiso esiyinkimbinkimbi, futhi impahla, abantu namasango avulekile akubalwa.

## Lalela

{{ui:Play it in this room}} idlala izwi (elenziwe lapha), ukushaya kwezandla, amanothi acutshungulwayo noma **ifayela lakho lomsindo** (lihlala kukhompyutha yakho) ngokusebenzisa i-impulse response ebalwe yegumbi: umsindo oqondile, izibuyiselo zokuqala zebhokisi losayizi walo, nomsila ohlakazeka ngesikhathi somphindaphindo wegumbi ngalinye ebhendini. {{ui:Play it dry}} idlala umsindo ofanayo ngelinga elifanayo ngaphandle kwegumbi. Ungalondoloza i-impulse response noma umsindo osegumbini njengamafayela e-WAV. Uzwakala njengegumbi losayizi walo nokuqedwa kwalo, hhayi njengegumbi langempela elithile.

## Abantu nezinto

Thayipha inani le-**people in this room** ukuze wengeze ukumunca kwabo. Impahla imodeli inayo (amasofa, amabhedi, izihlalo, amatafula, amakhabethe, ngegama) yengeza ukumunca kwayo. Izibalo ziyizinkomba zokuhlela ezinembile, zizungeziwe; zishintshe ngezilinganiso uma igumbi libalulekile.

## Umthombo nomlaleli

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Source and listener}}, cindezela {{ui:Place the source}} noma {{ui:Place the listener}} bese uchofoza ipulani legumbi. Umsindo odlalwayo uzwakala kusukela lapho: umsindo oqondile ufika ngemuva kwebanga, nengxenye ephindaphindayo isemazingeni alingana nalelo banga. Susa kokubili ukuze ubuyele ebhokisini lebanga.

## Ukungafani kokumunca nezindlela zegumbi

Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Uneven absorption (box room)}}, thayipha igumbi njengebhokisi bese ukhetha ukuqedwa kwalo kwephansi, kwesilingi nezindonga. Ithebula lengeza i-Fitzroy ne-Arau-Puchades ku-Sabine ne-Eyring: zibheka amapheya amathathu ezindawo ezibhekene ngokwahlukana, ngakho igumbi elinekhaphethi nesilingi esiqinile alifundwa njengelifushane kakhulu. Lapho amapheya ehluka kakhulu, inothi lisho ukuthi yiliphi okufanele lethenjwe. Ngaphansi kwethebula, **Room modes** idwedwesa amagagasi amancane amile webhokisi nefrikhwensi ngaphansi kwayo igumbi linezindlela ezihlukile esikhundleni somsindo olinganayo. Lezi yizibikezelo zebhokisi legumbi, isisetshenziswa sokuklama hhayi ukulinganisa.

## Amayunithi

Izindawo zamagumbi, ukuphakama, amavolumu nezibalo zikhonjiswa ngama-{{ui:Units}} okhethile (izikwele nezikhubo zamafidi, ama-inch, amamilimitha, amasentimitha noma amamitha). Ukubalwa ngokwakho kuhlala ku-SI; kushintsha kuphela ukubonakala. Izikhathi (amasekhondi), amafrikhwensi nemazinga akushintshi.
