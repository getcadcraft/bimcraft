## Okuzokwenza

Hlanza izindonga oziDwebile: sika odongeni olugijima olunye, elula odongeni olufushane, noma uvale ikhona elivulekile.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula {{ui:Dweba}} bese ukhetha izinga ohlwini lwezinga lephlani.
2. **Sika noma elula kudonga:** khetha {{ui:Lungisa}} › {{ui:Sika / eleza}}. Chofoza udonga okuzosikwa kuwo noma okuzoelulwa kuwo (umphetho), bese uchofoza yonke indonga ofuna ukuyishintsha. Udonga oludlula emphethweni luyasikwa lubuyiselwe kuwo, futhi ingxenye oyichofozile yiyo ehlala; udonga oluma lufinyeleli luyelulwa kuze kufike emphethweni. Umphetho uhlala ukhethiwe, ngakho ungachofoza izindonga eziningi ngolandelana. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukukhetha omunye umphetho.
3. **Xhuma izindonga ezimbili ekhonweni:** khetha {{ui:Lungisa}} › {{ui:Ikhona}}, chofoza udonga lokuqala, bese olwesibili. Zombili ziyafika endaweni lapho zihlangana, futhi i-BimCraft iyahlanza ikhona. Lapho udonga lunqamula olunye, ingxenye oyichofozile iyahlala.
4. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuqala kabusha, noma {{key:V}} ukubuyela ku-{{ui:Khetha}}.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Lapho ukhomba udonga, iphlani likhombisa lapho lingayoya khona, selihlanganiswe kakade nezindonga ezihlangana nalo, nobude balo obusha.
- Ushintsho ngalunye luyisinyathelo esisodwa: {{key:Mod+Z}} iyalihlehlisa.
- Amagumbi, amaphansi, amasilingi, amaphahla nakho konke okulandela izindonga kwenziwa kabusha, njengemva kwanoma yiluphi ushintsho odongeni. Igumbi elivalwe yikhona libonakala nenombolo nendawo yalo.
- Umnyango noma iwindi endaweni esikiwe liyasuswa nayo.

## Okufanele wazi

- Izindonga zidwetshwa ngomugqa wazo maphakathi, ngakho ziyasikwa ziphinde zelulwe ngawo: udonga luhlangana nomugqa maphakathi womunye, nesixhumanisi sihlanzwa sibe yi-T noma i-L.
- I-BimCraft iyasho ukuthi kungani ingakwazi: izindonga zimbili ziyafana, umugqa wodonga udlula eduze komunye, umnyango noma iwindi liyisithiyo (lihambise noma lisuse kuqala), noma kungeke kushiye lutho lodonga (ngaphansi kuka-1 mm). Udonga oshiywe mfushane kuno-50 mm luyakhiwa, nesexwayiso ku-Izinkinga: bona [Iseluleko sokudweba](topic:drawing-advice).
- Izindonga ezidwetshwe ku-BimCraft kuphela ziyasikwa noma ziyelulwa.

Bona futhi [Dweba izindonga](topic:draw-walls) kanye [Shintsha udonga, umnyango noma iwindi](topic:change-a-wall-or-door).
