## Okuzokwenza

Tshela i-BimCraft ukuthi yini isakhiwo esisha okufanele siqale ngayo, ukuze akekho ehhovisi oyodinga ukushintsha izinto ezifanayo njalo. Lesi yi-add-on: ivaliwe size usivule, futhi inqubomgomo ongayisethi ishiya izinga le-BimCraft njengoba linjalo.

:::note
Lezi zinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela zezakhiwo **ezisha** nezinto **ezisha**. Iphrojekthi osuke uyiqalile ayishintshwa.
:::

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula Izilungiselelo, uye ku-{{ui:Okwengeziwe}} bese uvula {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}}.
2. Ku-Izilungiselelo, vula ithebhu ye-**BimCraft** bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}} ohlwini oluseceleni.
3. Setha izinqubomgomo ofuna. Ngayinye iqala ku-**eyeBimCraft**, nalokho okukuphakathi kwamabhulaketi. Inqubomgomo enebhokisi elithi **Metric** nelithi **Imperial** ithatha inani elilodwa ngohlobo ngalunye lweprojekthi; gcwalisa ezidingekayo kuphela.
4. Ayikho inkinobho yokugcina: ushintsho ngalunye lugcinwa njengoba ulenza. Inani elingasetshenziswa liyenqatshwa ngamazwi futhi elidala lihlala.
5. Cofa {{ui:Isakhiwo esisha}}. Ifomu igcwaliswa ngezinqubomgomo zakho, futhi ngayinye inqubomgomo oyisethile ithi **kusuka kuzinqubomgomo zakho zokuzenzakalela** eduze kwayo. Ungazishintsha noma yiziphi ngaphambi kwe-{{ui:Dala}}.

## Izinqubomgomo

- **Iyunithi yesakhiwo esisha**: iyunithi ishintshela kulokhu lapho ucofa u-Dala.
- **Inani lezitezi**, **Amagama ezitezi** kanye **Ukuphakama kwesitezi**. Egameni, `{n}` ibala 1, 2, 3; `{n0}` ibala 0, 1, 2; `{nn}` no-`{nn0}` benza okufanayo ngezinombolo ezimbili. Ngakho `Isitezi {nn0}` sinikeza Isitezi 00, Isitezi 01, Isitezi 02.
- **Uhlobo lokuqala lodonga**: okudwetshwa yithuluzi lodonga kuqala. Ihhovisi le-metric lingaqala nge-Exterior masonry 300 mm, kanti amaphrojekthi e-imperial agcine i-Exterior 2×6.
- **Ithegi le-LOD kuzinhlobo zodonga zokuqala**: izinga lokuthuthukiswa (LOD 100 kuya ku-500) uhlobo lwezakhiwo lwesishiyagalolunye olubonisa noma yikuphi lapho uhlobo lubhalwe khona. Bona [Akha izinhlanganisela zodonga](topic:wall-assemblies).
- **LOD yohlobo lodonga olusha olwenziwe**: ithegi uhlobo olusha oluqala ngalo kumhleli wohlobo. Ungayishintsha lapho.
- **Ishidi lokuqala**: iphepha lalo, isikali, inombolo yeshidi negama (sebenzisa `{level}` ukuthola igama lesitezi esiphansi kakhulu), noma akukho shidi lokuqala ngokuphelele.
- **Usayizi wezichasiso ephepheni** (Izilungiselelo › Global › Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela): ukuphakama kwehhovisi, ngamamillimitha njengoba kushicilelwe, kwamanothi, umbhalo wesilinganiso, amathegi, amagama amagumbi, izinhlamvu zamabhamuza egridi, amagama emibono nombhalo wohlu. Isakhiwo esisha siyawathatha ngoku-Dala futhi siwagcina efayelini yaso. Bona [Dweba ngosayizi ogcwele, uchaze ngesikali](topic:annotation-sizes).
- **Ithuluzi elisebenzayo ngemva kuka-Dala**: Khetha, njengoba i-BimCraft ikwenza yodwa, noma Udonga, ukuze ukucofa okubili kokuqala kupulani kudwebe udonga ngokuqondile.

## Ukuhlelwa kohlu: umgomo wehhovisi

Ku-**Izilungiselelo › Global › Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela**, ngaphansi kwe-**Ukuhlelwa kohlu (umgomo wehhovisi)**, setha ukuthi yonke uhlu luhlelwa kanjani, ukuthi luneziqephu nesamba yini, isihloko sawo nokuthi amakholomu anezinhloko zethe kanjani. Yonke uhlu olungachazi lwayo luyawuthatha, noma ngubani ohlela. Bona [Hlela, gcina futhi uthumele uhlu](topic:schedule-layouts).

## Yabelana ngazo ehhovisi

1. Ku-Izilungiselelo, vula ithebhu ye-**Global** bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinqubomgomo zokuzenzakalela}}.
2. Bhala **Igama lehhovisi** uma uthanda. Libhalwa efayeleni.
3. Cofa {{ui:Gcina efayeleni}}. Uthola ifayela elincane elibiza ngokuthi `.ccdefaults.json`.
4. Kwenye ikhompyutha, vula i-add-on, vula indawo efanayo bese ucofa {{ui:Layisha kusuka efayeleni}}. Izinqubomgomo zefayela ziyathatha indawo yezalapho kulelo khompyutha.

Ithebhu ye-Global nayo inohlelo lwamayunithi weprojekthi emisha, olusebenza kuzo zonke izinhlelo ezinalo i-add-on, kanye ne-{{ui:Setha kabusha zonke izinqubomgomo}}. I-{{ui:Setha kabusha kuzenzakalelo lwe-BimCraft}} kuthebhu ye-BimCraft isula ezeBimCraft kuphela.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ifayela liyi-JSON evulekile, elula: uhlu lwamagama enqubomgomo namanani. Inqubomgomo ifayela elayichaza engaziwa yile nguqulo ishiywa ngaphandle futhi ichazwe emyalezweni; ezinye zona ziyalayisha.
- Izinqubomgomo zigcinwa kulesi siphequluli, kuzo zonke izinhlelo ze-CadCraft kuleli khompyutha. Azigcinwa efayeleni lesakhiwo, futhi azihlanganiswa phakathi kwamakhompyutha: sebenzisa ifayela.
- Ithegi le-LOD kuhlobo lokuqala livela kunqubomgomo yalelikhompyutha. Uhlobo oyenzayo njengekhophi lokuqala lithwala ithegi layo efayeleni lesakhiwo.
- I-BimCraft ayinayo i-precision setting, ngakho akukho inqubomgomo yokunemba lapha.
- Isakhiwo esiqalwe kusuka kwethempulethi sithatha izitezi nezishidi zethempulethi. Inqubomgomo yohlobo lodonga neyeyunithi isasebenza.
- Vala i-add-on bese konke kubuyela kokuzenzakalelayo kwe-BimCraft. Izinqubomgomo zakho ziyagcinwa uyivula futhi.
