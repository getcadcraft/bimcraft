Isithombe ku-{{ui:Renda}} sidwetshwa **yizinjini zethu**: Ngokushesha nesilandeli sendlela, kwiwebhu nasku-CadCraft Studio. Olunye uhlelo lungadweba kuphela uma kuletha **i-add-on yenjini yokurenda**: bona [Engeza injini yokurenda nge-add-on](topic:render-engine-addons). Ukuthatha isimo siye kolunye uhlelo nokuqhubeka usebenza kulo, sebenzisa [Vula isimo kolunye uhlelo](topic:render-open-in) noma [Thumela isimo kwinjini yomdlalo](topic:render-unreal).

## Dweba isithombe ngenjini ye-add-on

1. Vula i-add-on ku-Izilungiselelo › Okwengeziwe. Injini yayo ivela ohlwini lwe-Injini engxoxweni {{ui:Renda}}.
2. Yikhethe. Izilungiselelo zayo zivela ngaphansi kohlu lwe-Injini.
3. Chofoza {{ui:Renda}}. Ku-Studio, okokuqala lapho i-add-on iqhuba uhlelo, i-Studio ikubuza engxoxweni yayo. Inqubekelaphambili ibonakala ngenkathi idweba; {{ui:Misa}} iyiphelisa. Isithombe sivela sibhalwe ngenjini eyidwebile.

Injini ebhalwe nge-JavaScript iqhuba nakuwebhu. Injini eqhuba uhlelo kule khompyutha iqhuba kuStudio kuphela; kuwebhu ibonakala njengefayela okufanele silondolozwe.

## Okudluliselwayo

Injini ithola **ipakethe**: imodeli njengefayela le-USD (nefayela le-glTF uma icela), nefayela lezilungiselelo elinezinani zezinto zokwakha ngokunembile, ilanga nesibhakabhaka, kanye nezibani ngazinye ezivulekile ngamandla nephrofayili yazo yokukhanya, ikhamera nokuvezwa. Uhlelo ngalunye luyakhanyisa, luhlele amanani emibala futhi lube ngamasampula ngendlela yalo, ngakho izithombe zifana ngekhamera, ilanga, izibani nokuvezwa, kodwa hhayi ngepikseli.

## Izinhlelo i-CadCraft ingakaze iziqhube

I-CadCraft ayikaze iqale uhlelo olunelayisensi ye-GNU GPL, futhi ayikaze iluhlanganise njengenjini noma njengento ye-Vula ku. I-Blender yenye yazo, ngakho ayikho ohlwini lwe-Injini. Ifayela le-USD noma le-glTF elilondoloziwe kusuka ku-{{ui:Renda}} livula kuyo njengakunoma yiluphi uhlelo olufunda lawo mafayela.

:::note
Amakhamera epanorama nesterio nesithombe sesibhakabhaka (imibala yesibhakabhaka isetshenziswa esikhundleni salo) akudluliselwa kolunye uhlelo. Akukho okwenziwa kolunye uhlelo okubuyela emodelini.
:::
