## Okuzokwenza

Dweba isakhiwo esikhona ngale-laser scan yaso: izindonga lapho iskena izikhombisa khona, ezingqeni ezifanele, zibhekene ngqo nakwezinye.

## Izinyathelo

1. **Isakhiwo lapho ozodweba khona.** {{ui:Ifayela}} › {{ui:Isakhiwo esisha}}, nezitezi zakhona ngokwenani lezitezi esinazo (ungazengeza ezinye kamuva, bheka [Isayithi, isakhiwo esisesayithini nezitezi zaso](topic:site-building-and-levels)). {{ui:Dweba}} iyavuleka.
2. **Iskena.** {{ui:Bonisa}} › {{ui:Ifu lamaphoyinti}} bese ukhetha ifayela (LAS, LAZ, E57, PLY, PCD, PTX noma XYZ: bheka [Bonisa ifu lamaphoyinti eduze kwemodeli](topic:point-clouds)). Ifu likhombisa ekubukeni kwe-3D, nepulani likhombisa **isendlalelo** salo: amaphoyinti eskenwe angu-1200 mm (4 feet) phezu kwezinga, njengamachashaza. Yile ndawo lapho izindonga, iminyango namafasitela kufundeka khona kangcono.
3. **Liqondanise nezinga.** Vula imenyu {{ui:Skena}} kwibha yemodeli bese uchofoza {{ui:Lungisa iphansi lalo libe kulelizinga}}: ifu liyanyuka noma lehle, kuze iphansi elaskenwa lihlale phezu kwezinga elikhonjisiwe. Hlola ukuthi isendlalelo manje sihlikihla izindonga.
4. **Qondanisa nesakhiwo.** {{ui:Skena}} › {{ui:Yenza ibe ngqo nesakhiwo}} ijikeleza ifu ukuze izindonga esendlalelweni yalo zihambisane nama-axis epulani, ngakho izindonga ozidweba ngokubamba {{key:Shift}} ziqondane nskena. Ijikeleza eduze kwephakathi lezindonga ezitholiwe, ngakho izihlahla noma udonga lomakhelwane olubanjwe esendlalelweni alungeli ifu ngaphandle. Qala ngokulungisa iphansi kuqala: isendlalelo bese sihlikihla izindonga.
5. **Landela izindonga.** Khetha {{ui:Udonga}} bese uchofoza ngasemachasheni: ithuluzi libamba amaphoyinti eskenwe (uphawu lokubamba luyidayimane elincane) kanye nezindonga ozidwebile. Udonga ludwetshwa ngomugqa walo ophakathi; chofoza phakathi kwemigqa emibili yamachashaza udonga oluhamba kuwo; thayipha ubukhulu balo ngokukhetha uhlobo lodonga kuqala. Bese wengeza iminyango namafasitela lapho isendlalelo sikhombisa khona izikhala.
6. **Izitezi ezilandelayo.** Khetha izinga eliphezulu ohlwini lwezinga lwepulani: isendlalelo silandela ukuphakama kokusikwa kwezinga. Qondanisa iphansi nalelo zinga futhi kuphela uma amaphansi eskenwe engakahlali lapho amazinga akho ewakhona.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Isendlalelo siyashintsha uma ushintsha izinga, nesendlalelo esikhulu ({{ui:Skena}} › {{ui:Ubukhulu besendlalelo}}) sikhombisa amaphoyinti amaningi lapho iskena ilula khona.
- {{ui:Skena}} › {{ui:Hlehlisa ukulungisa}} ibuyisela ifu endaweni yalo lapho livulwa khona. Ukulungisa kuhambisa ifu ekubukeni kuphela: alugcinwa efayelweni le-IFC, izindonga ozidwebile zihlala lapho zazikhona. Ukulungisa kuhamba nofu uma ushintsha {{ui:Misa}} noma {{ui:Amayunithi}}, noma uvule enye imodeli.
- {{ui:Skena}} › {{ui:Bonisa isendlalelo epulanini}} iyafihla noma ibonise amachashaza.

:::tip
Amaskena amakhulu: ipulani lidweba kuze kube ngamaphoyinti angu-60,000 esendlalelweni, nombono we-3D udweba amaphoyinti ambalwa ezigidi zefu lonke, kokubili akhethwe ngokulinganayo kuskena, ngakho ifayela elikhulu lihlala lisheshe. Ukuze ulandele ku-drafting app, sebenzisa {{ui:Landela ku-CadCraft}} kubha yefu.
:::
