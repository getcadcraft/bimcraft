## Okuzokwenza

Shintsha ukwakheka kodonga phezulu nangaphansi kwepulani yalo: phezulu kwalo kungalandela uphahla, iphansi lesilele noma ulwelwesi, lunganikwa umugqa walo wohlangothi, lungathambeka, futhi lunezimbobo ezingenamnyango noma iwindi kuzo. Ipulani yodonga, amakhona nomugqa wendawo kuhlala njengoba kwakunjalo.

## Lapho ungenza khona

- **Properties**: ngo-{{ui:Draw}} ovulekile no-{{ui:Select}} ovulekile, chofoza udonga oludweyiwe. **Properties** ibonisa imigqa yodonga, bese **Shape**, lapho konke kuthayiphwa khona.
- **Ukubukeka kohlangothi noma isigaba**: ipensela lomsizi wephrojekthi ({{ui:Edit in this view}}) livula umbono; khetha udonga lapho bese udonsa izibambi zalo. Bheka "Esigabeni nasekubukekeni kohlangothi" ngezansi.
- **Umbono wobuso bodonga**: {{ui:Walls}} › {{ui:Edit profile}}, bese uchofoza udonga kwipulani. Lokhu kusebenza kunoma yiliphi udonga, nedonga elingakaze libukwe ngqo yinoma yiluphi ukubukeka kohlangothi, nodonga olugobile (olubonakala lusongwe).
- **Ipulani nombono we-3D**: imiyalo emine ku-{{ui:Walls}} (iqembu le-Edit): {{ui:Attach top / base}}, {{ui:Edit profile}}, {{ui:Slant}} kanye {{ui:Wall opening}}. Bheka "Epulanini nasemibonweni ye-3D" ngezansi.

Noma ikuphi okukhethile, kuyinguquko efanayo: isinyathelo esisodwa ku-**Changes**, futhi {{ui:Undo}} iyasuthula.

Izindonga ezigobile nezingagobile zamukela zonke izithuluzi zokwakheka. Kudonga olugobile, izinkulungwane zihamba eduze kwalo ugobo.

## Hlanganisa phezulu noma isisekelo

- **Attach top**: khetha uphahla, iphansi noma ulwelwesi. Phezulu kodonga kulandela ingaphansi kwalokho okukhethile, ngqo phezu kwalo ikhona ngakunye ikhona yodonga. Ngaphansi kophahla olunamagumbi, udonga luthola phezulu oluthambekile, futhi lapho uphahla lushintsha ubuso (uphethe noma i-hip), phezulu kodonga luyagoba nalo.
- **Attach base**: khetha iphansi noma ulwelwesi. Isisekelo sodonga silandela ubuso bayo obuphezulu.
- Lapho kungekho lutho phezulu (noma phansi) kwengxenye yodonga, leyo ngxenye igcina ukuphakama kwayo.
- Ukuxhumana kuyahlala: shintsha ukuthambeka noma ukukhishwa kophahla, noma ukuphakama kolwelwesi, bese izindonga ezixhunyiwe ziyalandela kwisinyathelo esifanayo.
- Khetha **Its own height** ukuze ukhulule.

## Hlela iphrofayili

{{ui:Edit profile…}} ivula umugqa wodonga obonwa uvela ohlangothini lwawo:

- **Top line**: imigqa ye-**Along** (ibanga kusukela esiqalweni sodonga) ne-**Height** (ngaphezu kwesisekelo sodonga). Imigqa emithathu 0 / 3000, 2500 / 4500, 5000 / 3000 yenza umphetho we-gable edongeni elingu-5 m.
- **Bottom line**: yishiye ingenalutho ukuze kube isisekelo sezinga, noma ufake imigqa yodonga oluphezu kwesisekelo esiyiqhwa.
- {{ui:Add a point}} yengeza umugqa, **Remove** uwususe. {{ui:Use this profile}} iyisebenzisa; {{ui:Back to straight}} iyasusa.

Umugqa ngamunye uhamba kudonga kanye, kusukela esiqalweni sawo kuya ekugcineni; umugqa obuyela emuva kuwona, noma phezulu oyehlela esisekelweni, awamukelwa. Uma kukhona nophezulu oxhunyiwe, okuphansi kwalo kwamukelwa.

## Ukuthambekisa udonga

**Slant**: **Upright**, **Slanted** (zombili izinhlangothi zithambekela ohlangothini olufanayo, ukuqina kuhlala) noma **Double slant** (uhlangothi ngalunye lunekona yalo: udonga oluba luncane noma lukhule lapho lukhuphuka). Thayiphu amadigri ukusuka emaqondini; i-engeli elihle lithambekela ngakwesobunxele kodonga, libukwa esiqalweni salo. Udonga luhlala lapho lidweywe khona esisekelweni salo; izindonga ezixhunyiwe kuyo azithambeki nayo.

## Izimbobo ngaphandle komnyango noma iwindi

{{ui:Add opening}} isika umgodi ububanzi obungu-900 phakathi kodonga. Umugqa wayo ku-**Openings** usetha **From start**, **Width**, **Sill** (kusukela ezingeni) ne-**Height**; **Remove** iyasusa. Imbobo idlula kodonga lonke, izinto zodonga azibalwa kuyo, futhi ipulani iveza igebe lapho ifinyelela ekuphakamisweni kwesigaba sepulani. Uma isiqalo sodonga sihamba eduze komugqa walo, imbobo ihlala endaweni yayo; ushintsho olungayishiya ngaphandle kodonga luyenqatshelwa.

## Esigabeni nasekubukekeni kohlangothi

Vula umbono ngepensela lomsizi wephrojekthi. Ngo-{{ui:Select and move}} ovulekile:

- **Profile**: chofoza udonga obhekene nombono. Imigqa yawo ephezulu nephansi ibe luhlaza, nezibambi ezinesikwele kuyo yonke iphuzu nedonsa phakathi kwayo yonke ingxenye.
  - Donsa isikwele ukuze uhambise leliphuzu (amaphuzu amabili aphelayo anyuka aphansi kuphela).
  - Donsa idonsa ukuze uhambise ingxenye yonke phezulu noma phansi.
  - Chofoza kabili umugqa (noma uwucindezele ngokudla) ukuze wengeze iphuzu lapho; {{ui:Add a point (top)}} kanye {{ui:Add a point (bottom)}} zengeza ngaphakathi kwengxenye ende kakhulu.
  - Chofoza isikwele bese ucindezela Delete (noma uwucindezele ngokudla) ukuze ususe leliphuzu. {{ui:Back to straight}} isusa iphrofayili.
  - Uma kukhethwe iphuzu, thayipha i-**Along** nethi **Height** kwisitrip ngaphansi kwethuluzi lokusebenza.
- **Attach**: isitrip sinohlu **Attach top** kanye **Attach base** lodonga olukhethiwe. Noma sebenzisa ithuluzi {{ui:Attach top}} noma {{ui:Attach base}}: chofoza izindonga, bese uphahla, iphansi noma ulwelwesi, okuboniswa njengamabhokisi amadashi. Zonke izindonga ezikhethiwe zixhunyiwe kwisinyathelo esisodwa.
- **Wall openings**: ngethuluzi le-{{ui:Wall opening}}, donsa unxantathu kubuso bodonga. Ubuyele ku-{{ui:Select and move}}, donsa imbobo ukuze uyihambise, donsa esinye sezibambi zayo eziyisishiyagalombili ukuze uyilungise usayizi, thayipha **From start**, **Width**, **Sill** ne-**Height** esitripini, noma cindezela Delete ukuze uyisuse.
- **Slant**: udonga umbono obheka ngalo (obonakala uma ubheka ngasemuphethweni, njengesigaba ngalo noma kubukeko lohlangothi ngasemuphethweni) nalo ungachofozwa. Donsa isibambi esijikelele esiphezulu kuso ukuze uthambekise, noma khetha **Upright**, **Slanted** noma **Double slant** esitripini bese uthayipha ama-engeli.

Usayizi ulinganiswa ku-10 mm; thayipha inani lanoma yini engaphansi kwalokho. Udonga oluyigobo lungahlelwa ekubukekeni kohlangothi uma lubheka indlela eyodwa phezu kombono; umbono wobuso bodonga wona usebenza njalo.

## Epulanini nasemibonweni ye-3D

Vula {{ui:Walls}} eqenjini le-Edit:

- {{ui:Attach top / base}}: khetha **Attach top** noma **Attach base** emugqeni wethuluzi. Chofoza izindonga (epulanini noma kumbono we-3D), bese kuthi yini okuyixhumanisa kuyo: ku-3D chofoza uphahla, iphansi noma ulwelwesi ngqo; epulanini chofoza iphuzu ngaphansi kwalo (uma kukhona okungaphezu kwesisodwa, uhlu luyabuza).
- {{ui:Edit profile}}: epulanini, chofoza udonga ukuze uvule umbono wobuso bawo. Ku-3D, chofoza udonga: amaphuzu eprofayili alo athola izibambi; donsa elilodwa endizeni yodonga.
- {{ui:Slant}}: chofoza udonga (epulanini noma ku-3D) bese uthayipha ukuthi lunyakazela kangakanani.
- {{ui:Wall opening}}: epulanini, chofoza udonga lapho imbobo izophuma khona; i-**Width**, i-**Height** kanye ne-**Sill** zisemugqeni wethuluzi. Ku-3D, chofoza ikhona elilodwa ebusweni bodonga, bese ikhona elingaphambili. Uma kukhethwe udonga ku-3D, izimbobo zalo zinezibambi: amakhona alungisa usayizi, ummaphakathi uyahamba.

Izibambi ze-3D ziyizibambi zombono we-3D ngokwawo, ngakho zilinda lapho ibhokisi lesigaba libonisa izibambi zalo.

## Epulanini

Udonga othambekile udweywa lapho ipulani iwusika khona (1200 ngaphezu kwezinga, ngaphandle uma umbono usho ngenye indlela): umphetho walo uyahanjiswa ngokuthi uthambekele kangakanani kulowo mkhawulo, futhi i-slant ephindwe kabili iba banzi noma incane lapho. Umphetho walo esitezi udwetshwa ngemigqa emincane yalokho okuphansi kwesigaba. Izikhona zodonga, izibambi kanye nezilinganiso zihlala esisekelweni salo.

## Amakhophi

{{ui:Copy this level…}} kanye Hambisa ngo-**Copy** zithatha iphrofayili yodonga, ukuthambeka kwalo kanye nezimbobo zodonga kanye nazo. Udonga oxhunyiwe eniphansi noma kulwelwesi uxhunyiwa eniphansini elikopishiwe noma kulwelwesi lezinga elisha; ukuxhumana noluphahla akukopishwa nezinga (uphahla alukopishwa). Ikhophi esitezini esifanayo ihlala ixhunyiwe kuphahla, phansi noma kulwelwesi olufanayo.

## Efayeleni

Udonga oluyibumbe lubhalwa njengento eqinile enezinhlangothi (IfcFacetedBrep), izinhlelo zezinye ziyilonde njengoba ibonakala; izilungiselelo zalo zisedongeni (CadCraft_Wall Profile, AttachTop, AttachBase, Slant). Imbobo iyi-IfcOpeningElement ekodongeni engagcwele lutho.

## Akusakhiwe okwamanje

- Iphrofayili enemiphetho ephuma noma izimbobo esilweni (sebenzisa izimbobo ukwenza imingxunya).
- Ku-3D: ukwengeza noma ukususa amaphuzu eprofayili, nokuthambeka ngesibambi (sebenzisa umbono wobuso bodonga, ukubukeka kohlangothi noma isigaba, noma uthayiphe).
- {{ui:Offset}} no-Copy avikopishi ubumo noma izimbobo zodonga (Hambisa ngo-Copy kuyakwenza).
- Izendlalelo zodonga othambekile azidwetshwa ezipulanini eziphakathi nezincane (kodwa umphetho wawo uyadwetshwa).
- Izimbobo ezingesikwele.
