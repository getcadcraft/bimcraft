## Yini eyenzayo

Uma ukuskena kuvulekile (ifu lamaphoyinti njenge-PLY, E57 noma LAS, noma imesh njenge-OBJ), i-BimCraft iyasika ukuskena ekuphakameni kwepulani, iyikhombise ibe yisikwele ngokubhekene nesakhiwo, bese ifuna imizila ende nebhoshiwe yamaphoyinti: lezo zindonga. Igebe elicishe libe ububanzi bomnyango phakathi kwemizila emibili esemgqeni owodwa lingena udonga olunomnyango. Izindonga ezivala indawo yenza igumbi.

Akukho okudwetshwayo kuze kwamukelwe. Izindonga, iminyango nezindawo ziyafika njengohlu lwezincomo (zingoshintsho bemodi ye-Suggest): gcina ezilungile, nqaba ezingalungile, bese konke okugcinile kudwetshwa ngesinyathelo esisodwa se-Hlehlisa. Amagumbi avela ngokwawo.

## Okuhlolwayo

Izindonga ezingavaliwe, imizila yezindonga emfushane kunobukhulu bodonga, negebe elinobukhulu bomnyango elingenalo udonga ozungeze lona, zibikwa njengezinkinga ngendawo, ukuze ubukele ukuskena lapho.

## Akuqinisekiswanga kuze kuhlolwe

Izindonga, iminyango namagumbi owamukele ekuskeneni amaka njengaqinisekiswanga: ibhokisi elinemigqa nombuzo "?" ephlaneni, uze ukumaka njengabahlolisisiwe. Bheka [Maka izinto njengezihlolisisiwe noma ezaqinisekiswa endaweni](topic:verification).

## Akukwenzi okwamanje

- Ithola izindonga ezigijima ezimbili ezingqamanayo. Udonga oluchwekile noma oluyisangqa ayitholwa.
- Isitezi esisodwa ngesikhathi. Amawindi awatholwa (iwindi liyigebe kuphela uma ukusika kusezingeni lesiyingi).
- Ilinyelwe kuskeno lamagumbi elenziwe nakumesh, kodwa akukaze linyelwe ekuskeneni kwefoni yangempela. Ukuthi lusebenza kanjani ekuskeneni kwangempela (ukuphambuka emgwaqeni omude, impahla eseduze kwezindonga, ingilazi, iminyango evulekile) akwaziwa, futhi ukulungisa ngokwezinkomba zangempela yisinyathelo esilandelayo. Hlola yonke incomo ngokuskena ngaphambi kokuyamukela.
