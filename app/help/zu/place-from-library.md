## Okuzokwenza

Faka into ye-library emodelini ye-IFC: isinki, itafula lokusebenzela, isihlalo. I-BimCraft iyibhala njengento ye-IFC efanele ngohlobo, ngakho zonke izikhobi zento efanayo zabelana ngesethi eyodwa yejiyomethri, nedatha yayo, inombolo yento kanye nelayisensi kuhamba nayo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC.
2. Chofoza ithebhu ethi {{ui:Umtapo}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Izici}} ngakwesokudla, noma {{ui:Umtapo}} ebhareni lamathuluzi ngaphezu kwemodeli.
3. Khetha umthombo:
   - **Izimpawu ezakhelwe ngaphakathi**: izimpawu zepulani zepulumbing ezijwayelekile ezifakwe ku-BimCraft ngaphansi kwe-Apache-2.0, ezitholakala ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Hlola ubukhulu bazo ngaphambi kokwakha. Ngezimpawu zepulani zikagesi kanye nezinsiza zikagesi ze-IFC, sebenzisa **Ukwakhiwa › Ugesi**.
   - **Library**: i-CadCraft library kulesi sayithi. Izinto zomphakathi azidingi ukungena.
   - **Le faela**: amabhulokhi omdwebo ovulekile we-DWG noma we-DXF. Ibhulokhi elinamamodi okubonakala linikezwa kanye ngayinye imodi, libizwe ngokuthi "Sink: Double bowl", ngayinye idwetshwe ngezinto zalowo mo kuphela.
   - **Amafayela…**: ifayela le-DWG, DXF, IFC, glTF/GLB, OBJ, RFA noma CSV kwikhompyutha yakho. Lifundwa kule thebhu; akulayishwa noma kumuphi.
   - **Imithombo yezinye**: enye library esebenzisa ifomethi efanayo yomthombo. Engeza ikheli layo kanye; ligcinwa kulesi siphequluli.
4. Sesha noma khetha isigaba, bese uchofoza ikhadi. Likhombisa ukubukeka kwepulani, igama, umenzi nemodeli, ilayisensi kanye nenguqulo.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Beka epulanini}} (kuMbono wePulani) noma {{ui:Beka emodelini}} (kuMbono we-3D). Ku-Split, lapho ipulani ne-3D zibonakala ndawonye, inkinobho iyinkinobho ehlukaniswayo: isenzo sayo esiyinhloko yi-{{ui:Beka epulanini}}, nomcibisholo wayo ongakwesokudla uvula imenyu enayo {{ui:Beka emodelini}}. Noma hudula ikhadi, noma isithombe kwiminingwane yento, uliyise epulanini noma kumbono we-3D: into iyabekwa lapho uyilahla khona. Ngepeni noma ngomunwe, cindezela ikhadi isikhashana, bese uhudula.
6. Khetha isitezi ebhareni lokubeka. Kumbono we-3D, izitezi eziphezulu kwaso ziyafihlwa ngenkathi ubeka, nombono uphenduka uye phezulu; epulanini, ipulani sekuvele lelo sitezi. Ukukhetha {{ui:Beka emodelini}} kusuka epulanini wedwa kuletha umbono we-3D ecaleni kwayo.
7. Hambisa iphoyinti phezu kwemodeli: ibhokisi elisobala likhombisa lapho into iyokwenza khona.
8. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuze uyiphendulele ngama-90°. Cindezela {{key:Tab}} (noma {{ui:Ukubopha kodonga}}) ukuze ubeke ingemuva layo eduze kodonga oseduze, ibheke igumbi.
9. Chofoza ukuze uyibeke. Noma thayipha isikhundla ku-**X, Y (mm)**, njengo `1200, 3400`, bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

Ukukhanya noma okunye okuphuza kusuka phezulu: setha **Ukufaka** ibe **Esilingini**. Phezulu kwento kuhlala ngaphansi kwesiling ngalowo ndawo (noma ngaphansi kwephezulu lesitezi lapho kungekho siling). Uma kukhona ipulani yesiling epulanini lombono, **Ukufaka** iqala ku-**Esilingini**.

10. Phendula umbuzo owodwa: **inombolo yento** (inombolo elandelayo etholakalayo igcwaliswa), inani kanye nenothi. Cindezela {{key:Enter}}.
11. Beka okunye, noma cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuyeka.
12. Chofoza {{ui:Gcina njenge-IFC}} ukulanda ikhophi eshintshiwe.

## Ipulani noma imodeli

Inkinobho yokubeka ye-Library ilandela umbono onawo, futhi iyashintsha uma ushintsha umbono (izinkinobho {{ui:Ipulani}}, {{ui:Hlukanisa}} kanye {{ui:3D}}, ikhibhodi, ukuvula ifayela). Ipulani likhombisa inkinobho eyodwa, {{ui:Beka epulanini}}; i-3D ikhombisa eyodwa, {{ui:Beka emodelini}}; Split ikhombisa inkinobho ehlukaniswayo, ne-{{ui:Beka epulanini}} njengesenzo esiyinhloko ne-{{ui:Beka emodelini}} emenyuini yayo. Zombili izindlela zisebenzisa ibha efanayo yokubeka futhi zibhala into efanayo ye-IFC: umlobi usebenza kumbono wepulani kwesinye, futhi kumbono we-3D kwesinye. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} noma nini ukuyeka.

Ukuhudula ikhadi akunakhiye yalo: inkinobho ehlukaniswayo, noma eyodwa, yenza okufanayo ngekhibhodi.

## Hambisa noma uphendulele into ebekiwe

Ku-{{ui:Dweba}}, khetha into epulanini. Igridi eyisikwele ibonakala endaweni yokubeka, nesiyingi esiseduze kwayo.

- Hudula into, noma igridi yayo eyisikwele, ukuze uyihambise. Iyambambelela njengethuluzi lodonga (imiphetho nobuso bodonga); {{key:Shift}} iyigcina iqondile kwendawo ayeseyo.
- Hudula isiyingi ukuze uyiphendulele ngezinyathelo ezingu-15°. Bamba {{key:Shift}} ngezinyathelo ezingu-1°.
- Cindezela {{key:R}} ukuze uyiphendulele ngama-90°.
- Ku-**Izici** (futhi ku-3D view), thayipha **X**, **Y** kanye **Rotation (°)**, noma chofoza **Turn 90°**.

Ukuhamba ngakunye yinyathelo elilodwa le-undo futhi kuhamba ku-Live TeamWork. Into igcina inombolo yayo, idatha yayo ye-library kanye nokuphakama kwayo ngaphezu kwesitezi. Akukakhiwa okwamanje: ukuhudula kumbono we-3D.

## Okufanele ukubone

- Into emodelini, ikhethiwe, nezici zayo ngakwesokudla: ibhokisi elithi **Library item** nelayisensi nokubonga kwayo, kanye nezethi `CadCraft_Placement`, `CadCraft_LibraryItem`, `CadCraft_Equipment` kanye, kwizinto ezivela kumadrowing athegiwe, `CadCraft_SourceAttributes`.
- "Placed: *item* (item *n*)" kuhlu lwezinguquko. {{key:Mod+Z}} iyayihlehlisa.
- Umugqa wesimo: "Placed *item* as item *n*."

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Ezinye izinto zifika ngosayizi noma ngokukhetha okuningi: ikhadi lapho linokukhetha **Variant**, futhi eyodwa oyikhethayo ibekwa. Bona [Beka futhi ushintshe into ye-library enokuhlukahluka](topic:library-variants).
- Into engenayo umzimba we-3D (isimpawu se-2D) ibekwa ngebhokisi elibambela; unikeza ukuphakama kwayo lapho ibuzwa.
- Okwengeziwe ngokufanayo: khetha into ebekiwe usebenzise {{ui:Kopisha}} no-{{ui:Namathisela}}, noma {{key:Mod+D}}. Bona [Kopisha, namathisela futhi uphinde uthathe izinto ze-library](topic:copy-paste).
- Ukwenza ukubopha kodonga kuvulwe ekubekeni ngakunye, vula **Start with wall snap on** ku-[Izilungiselelo ze-BimCraft](topic:settings).
- Ubude bungaguqulwa bube ezingeni lemodeli, noma yiziphi ezinye.
- **Entourage** (izihlahla, izihlahlana, abantu, izimoto) nayo itholakala kwi-library: sesha "tree", "person" noma "car". Lezi zidwetshwe yi-CadCraft (CC0) futhi zigcina imibala yazo lapho zibekwa (isiqu sibe nsundu, amaqabunga aluhlaza, amawindi emoto akwazi ukubonakala), kuBimCraft nakwezinye izinhlelo ze-BIM. Zibonakala ku-[izithombe ezinikeziwe](topic:render).
- Ku-IFC2X3 umzimba ubhalwa njenge-faceted solid; ku-IFC4 njenge-triangulated face set. Zombili zivulwa ngamanye amathuluzi e-IFC.
- Umdwebo (DWG noma DXF) awukwazi ukugcinwa lapha, ngakho izinto zibekwa emamodelini e-IFC kuphela. Sebenzisa **Le faela** kumdwebo ukubona ukuthi amabhulokhi awo angaba yini.

## Izinto ngolimi lwakho

Into ingaba negama, incazelo nohlobo ngezinye izilimi. Uma inakho ngolimi lwe-BimCraft olusethiwe (Izilungiselelo › Okujwayelekile › Ulimi), ikhasi lento libonisa lezo, negama umenzi wayo alinikeze lingaphansi kwazo, futhi **Beka** ibhala igama ngolimi lwakho emodelini, ukuze uhlu lwezinsiza lulibonise ngalo. Into ihlala iyinto efanayo: ukuhlola izibuyekezo kanye nokushintshwa kwenziwa ngento uqobo, hhayi ngegama layo. Izinto ezingenayo ukuhunyushwa zigcina amagama awo.

## Iziphazamiso ezivamile

- **Inkinobho yokubeka ayikho.** Akukho modeli ye-IFC evuliwe. Vula eyodwa kuqala.
- **Isithombe esingaqinile asilandeli iphoyinti.** Iphoyinti liphume endaweni yemodeli, noma indiza yesitezi ayikho ebonakalayo. Chofoza {{ui:Phezulu}}.
- **{{ui:Ukubopha kodonga}} kuthi akukho zindonga.** Isitezi asinazo izindonga ku-IFC (ezinye izinhlelo zokuthumela zibeka izindonga esitezini esihlukile). Khetha lelo sitezi, noma thayipha X no-Y.
- **Izinto zefayela zinosayizi ongalungile.** Ifayela alinayo iyunithi, noma lisho eyingalungile. Ku-{{ui:Amafayela…}} khetha iyunithi bese ulifunda futhi.

:::tip
Ukubeka into efanayo kabili kwabelana ngohlobo olulodwa lwe-IFC, ngakho imodeli enezihlalo ezingu-40 igcina isihlalo kanye.
:::
