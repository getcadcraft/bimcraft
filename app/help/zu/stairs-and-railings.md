## Okuzokwenza

Engeza izinyathelo phakathi kwamazinga namarelingi lapho abantu bedinga khona. Isitebhisi siqamba izinyathelo zaso ngokwaso ngokuphakama phakathi kwamazinga, sihlale ngaphakathi kwemikhawulo ejwayelekile yokuhlala, sibeke amarelingi okubamba ezinhlangothini zaso ezivulekile, futhi sisike isikhala sesitezi esitezini esingaphezulu.

## Dweba isitebhisi

1. Esitezini isitebhisi esiqala kuso, vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Izinyathelo}}.
2. Khetha uhlobo — **Eqondile**, **L nebhalansi**, **U nebhalansi** noma **L nezinyathelo ezigoqayo** — ukuthi kuphi **kugoqa**, **ububanzi** bayo, nokuthi **amarelingi okubamba** azoya kuphi.
3. Chofoza lapho isitebhisi siqala khona: maphakathi nomphetho wangaphambili wenyathelo lokuqala.
4. Hambisa ukhesho ngendlela isitebhisi esiya ngayo phezulu; izinyathelo ziyalandela, nenombolo yezinyathelo ezinokuphakama nezinyathelo zokuhamba. Sibambelela ezindongeni nasezinyathelweni ezingu-15°; bamba {{key:Shift}} ukuze sihlale sisikwele.
5. Chofoza. {{key:Esc}} iqala kabusha.

## Dweba isitebhisi esigoqayo

1. Vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}}, khetha {{ui:Izinyathelo}}, bese ukhetha **Isigoqo** njengohlobo.
2. Setha **Ukugoqa**, ukuthi kugoqa kanjani siya phezulu (kwesobunxele kuwukuphambene nowashi).
3. Chofoza maphakathi nesigxobo sawo.
4. Chofoza indlela inyathelo lokuqala elibheka ngayo.

Siqala ngobubanzi obungu-30" (inyathelo, kusukela esigxobeni kuya ngaphandle), nesigxobo esingu-4½". Izinyathelo zibalwa ngokuphakama phakathi kwezitezi, zifike ku-9½" ngokuzenzakalelayo. Izinyathelo zijule ngu-7½" emgwaqweni wokuhamba, ngu-12" kusuka esigxobeni. Irelingi lokubamba lisebenza ngaphandle.

Kuma-**Izici** isitebhisi esigoqayo sengeza **Isigxobo esiphakathi**, ububanzi baso. Inkambu ye-**Inyathelo** ilinganiswa emgwaqweni wokuhamba. Izinhlolo zaso ziyizinkomba ezibizwa ngokuthi okuzenzakalelayo kwezitebhisi ezigoqayo: ububanzi obuvulekile obungu-26", ukujula kwenyathelo emgwaqweni wokuhamba okungenani ngu-6¾", izinyathelo ezingeqi ku-9½" kanye namarelingi okubamba angu-34"–38". Azikona izibalo zobunjiniyela; umphathi wokwakha uyanquma.

## Indlela izinyathelo ezibalwa ngayo

Ibanga phakathi kwephansi lezinga nezinga elingaphezulu lihlukaniswa libe ukuphakama okulinganayo okungeqi ku-7¾", kanti inyathelo ngalinye lijule ngu-10" (zombili lezi zingashintsha ku-**Izici**). Ibhalansi ibalwa njengenyathelo elilodwa; izinyathelo ezigoqayo ziyizinyathelo ezintathu ezigoqwe ezungeze ikhona langaphakathi. Shintsha ukuphakama kwezinga, bese zonke izitebhisi esitezini salowo zibalwa kabusha.

:::warning
Ukuphakama kwenyathelo nge-7¾", izinyathelo nge-10", ububanzi nge-36", ikhanda nge-6'-8", nezinsimbi zokubamba ezingu-34"–38" kuyizinkomba ezizenzakalelayo ngemva kwekhodi evamile yokuhlala, hhayi ubunjiniyela. **Izici** ibonisa ezihlangabezwayo; umphathi wokwakha unquma ukuthi yimuphi umthetho osebenza.
:::

## Shintsha isitebhisi

Khetha (chofoza kusona ku-plan noma ku-3D) bese ushintsha ku-**Izici**: uhlobo nokugoqa kwaso, ububanzi, ukuphakama okuphezulu kwenyathelo, ukujula kwenyathelo, amarelingi okubamba nokuphakama kwawo, kanye ne-**Sika isitezi esingaphezulu** sesikhala sesitezi. Izinyathelo nokuphakama kwazo okuphele ngakho kubonakala ngaphansi.

## Isikhala sesitezi

Uma isitezi esingaphezulu sinofloro, isitebhisi siyisika lapho izinyathelo zingaba nekhanda elingaphansi kwe-6'-8". Ukusikwa kuhamba nesitebhisi futhi kunyamalala uma isitebhisi sisusiwe.

## Dweba irelingi

1. Vula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Irelingi}}.
2. Khetha **Irelingi lokuvikela** (36" ukuphakama) noma **Irelingi lokubamba** nokuphakama kwalo.
3. Chofoza eduze komphetho — ideki, ibhalkoni, ozungeze isikhala sesitezi — bese uchofoza kabili noma ucindezele {{key:Enter}} ukuze uqede.

Amarelingi athola amaposti emaphethelweni nasezikhonweni namabhodlela aneziqhephu ezingaphansi kwe-4". Khetha elilodwa ukuze ushintshe uhlobo lwalo, ukuphakama kwalo, noma ukuthi liqala kangakanani ngaphezu kwephansi.

## Dweba ifenisi

Khetha **Ifenisi** kwithuluzi elifanayo lerelingi, setha ukuphakama kwayo (1800 mm ukuqala), chofoza eduze komugqa wefenisi bese ucindezela {{key:Enter}}.

- Ifenisi ithola isigxobo emaphethelweni nasezikhonweni, nesigxobo esingaphezu kwe-2.4 m phakathi kwazo. Ifenisi yamaphaneli inephaneli phakathi kwamaposti; ifenisi yamagxa inamagxa amabili. Lobu bukhulu bungokwesikhethi esivamile, hhayi isidingo sekhodi; imithetho yendawo ikhawulela ukuphakama kwefenisi.
- Iyisakhi esisodwa emodelini (i-IfcRailing ebizwa ngokuthi Fence) enesethi yezici {{ui:CadCraft_Fence}}: isitayela, ukuphakama, amaposti, amaphaneli, amagxa, amasango, ubude nendawo yephaneli. Isinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.
- Ayidwetshwe lapha: amasango (umkhiqizi kwi-CivilCraft uwahlela), izinti zefenisi, umhlaba (ifenisi imi phezu kwezinga), nokukhetha isigxobo esisodwa.

## Okulondolozwayo

Isitebhisi silondolozwa njenge-IFC stair esakhiwe ngezinyathelo zamaflight (nokuphakama nobude bokuhamba kwazo), amabhalansi, izinyathelo ezigoqayo namarelingi okubamba; isikhala sesitezi siyimbobo kuslabhu yefloro esingaphezulu. Amarelingi alondolozwa njenge-IFC railings anobude bawo kanye nenani lamabhodlela namaposti.
