## Okuzokwenza

Dweba ipulani likagesi lendlu kumodeli: amasokhethi, amaswishi nopanele odongeni, izibani nezinzwa esilingeni. Isinsiza ngasinye yinto yangempela yikagesi kufayela le-IFC, enethegi yayo, isekethi nomthwalo wayo, namaswishi akhumbula izibani avula zona.

## Misa izinsiza

1. Esitezini, vula {{ui:Build}} bese ukhetha {{ui:Electrical}}.
2. Khetha insiza: amasokhethi (amandla, idatha, ucingo, i-audio-video), amaswishi nezinzwa (ithemostati nesikhathi nazo), izibani, izinzwa zokutholela nezezwi, ibhokisi lokuhlanganisa, upanele noma upanele omncane.
3. Bhala **Isekethi** uma uyazi (noma yiliphi igama: 1, 2, A-3). Shiya **Ukuphakama kokumisa** ingenalutho ukuze uthole ukuphakama okujwayelekile kalezo zinsiza.
4. Chofoza kupulani. Amasokhethi, amaswishi nopanele kuya ngaphezulu kodonga oluseduze, bheka ngaphakathi kwegumbi; izibani, izinzwa namabhokisi okuhlanganisa kuya esilingeni lapho uchofoza khona.

Izinsiza ziphawulwa ngesikhathi zimiswa: **R** wamasokhethi, **S** wamaswishi, **L** wezibani, **M** wezinzwa zokunyakaza, **J** wamabhokisi okuhlanganisa, **P** wopanele. Izinsiza ezisodongeni zihamba nodonga uma uhambisa lodonga.

Uthanda ukubheka ngezithombe? Vula i-**Symbol palette** kusuka ku-{{ui:Build}} ukuze uthole insiza ngegama, uthande ezisetshenziswa kakhulu bese uyimisa ngocingo olulodwa: bona [Ipharethi yezimbonakalo](topic:symbol-palette).

## Xhumanisa amaswishi nezibani

1. Khetha iswishi (noma inzwa yokunyakaza) bese uchofoza **Xhuma izibani** ku-**Izici**.
2. Chofoza wonke isibani esivulayo. Chofoza futhi isibani esixhumiwe ukuze usixhumule.
3. Cindezela {{key:Enter}} noma uchofoze **Kwenziwe**.

Ipulani lidweba ijika elinamachashaza lisuka ku-iswishi liye kuzo zonke izibani zalo. I-**Izici** yesibani ikhombisa ukuthi yimaphi amaswishi awuvulayo.

## Igumbi insiza lapho imi khona

Yonke insiza yazi igumbi eliyo kulo. Insiza esodongeni isegumbini eliyibheke; insiza esilingeni isegumbini ngaphansi kwayo. I-**Izici** ikhombisa **Igumbi**, nesikhala sohlu **Izinsiza zikagesi** sinekholomu **Igumbi** (inombolo negama, njengo-101 Ikhishi).

Igcinwa ilungile: uma ubiza kabusha noma uvuselele igumbi, uhambise insiza, noma uhambise udonga, igumbi liyalandela ngesinyathelo esifanayo. Insiza esedongeni langaphandle lesakhiwo, noma endaweni engeyona igumbi, ibika ukuthi ayisegumbini.

Igumbi futhi libhalwa kufayela ngaphakathi kwesethi yezici yensiza ethi `CadCraft_Location` (Room, RoomNumber, RoomName), ukuze i-Find, i-Color by property nohlu lwamathegi kukwazi ukulisebenzisa, nezinye izinhlelo nazo zikwazi ukulisebenzisa. Izimbonakalo zepulani nezinto ezixhunywe kwimbonakalo zithola isethi efanayo; bona [Ipharethi yezimbonakalo](topic:symbol-palette).

## Vumela umsizi akumisele zona

Uma umsizi exhunyiwe (Izilungiselelo › Abasizi, nokuhlela kuvunyelwe), ungacela izinsiza ngamazwi: "faka isokhethi elikabili buhlelo lwezindonga zegumbi lokuhlala, ngo-300 mm ukuphakama, esekethini 3", noma "engeza isibani sophahla neswishi yaso eduze komnyango". Umsizi usebenzisa amathuluzi amahlanu: `devices_add`, `device_change`, `lights_set`, `devices_delete` kanye no-`devices_list`. Adala izinsiza ezifanayo nalezo ithuluzi likagesi elizenzayo, ezindongeni nasemathegini, nasezisekethini, nakumthwalo nasezingeni lokuphakama ezifanayo, futhi ukubizwa ngakunye kuyisinyathelo esisodwa sokuhlehlisa.

Insiza ayikaze ithole uhlobo ithuluzi likagesi elingalenzi: uhlobo olungaziwa luyenqatshwa, nomsizi unikezwa uhlu. Isokhethi noma iswishi kufanele libe ngaphakathi kwe-600 mm kuphansi wodonga, njengokuchofoza, uma kungenjalo ukubizwa kuyenqatshwa futhi akukho okumiswayo. Izinkomba zokuphakama zisemamilimitha: 300 wamasokhethi, 1100 wamaswishi; insiza yesilingo imi ngaphansi kwesilingo.

## Shintsha nobhale ohlwini

Khetha insiza ukuze ushintshe ithegi, isekethi, umthwalo (watt) nokuphakama kokumisa. Khetha eziningi ukuze uzifake esekethini eyodwa ngesikhathi esisodwa. Cindezela {{key:Delete}} ukuze usule eyodwa; isibani esisusiwe sisuswa namaswishi aso.

Ku-{{ui:Schedules}} khetha **Izinsiza zikagesi** ukuze uthole yonke insiza nezinga layo, isekethi, umthwalo, ukuphakama nezibani ngalinye iswishi elivula zona, noma **Amasekethi** ukuze uthole izinsiza zesekethi ngayinye, umthwalo wayo wonke nokugeleza kwayo kwamanje. Zombili zisendaweni yohlu lwesethi yemidwebo: bona [Okuphakanyisiwe, izigaba namashidi](topic:sheets).

:::note
Ukuphakama kokumisa nemithwalo kungokuzenzakalelayo kokuhlela (isokhethi ku-16" ngaphezulu no-200 W, iswishi ku-48", isibani sophahla ku-60 W), akusona ukuklama: uthisha kagesi noma unjiniyela uyaziqondisa izisekethi bese ehlola ikhodi.
:::

:::tip
Izimbonakalo ngezifanayo nezokwengeziwe kwe-Electrical kwe-CadCraft ezidweba kumapulani okufakwa, ngakho ipulani kwelinye lezi zinhlelo lifundeka ngendlela efanayo.
:::
