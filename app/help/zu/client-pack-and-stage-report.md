## Okuzokwenza

Yenza iphakheji lekhasimende noma umbiko wesigaba kusuka kumodeli njengoba ikhona manje, bese uqhathanisa izinketho zevolumu ezigciniwe ngendawo nezindleko, ecaleni komunye.

## Izinyathelo

1. Esidlule sohlangothi lwesobunxele vula **Iphakheji lekhasimende nombiko wesigaba**.
2. Khetha **Vula ku-Documents** eduze kuka-Iphakheji lekhasimende noma Umbiko wesigaba. Idokhumenti iyavela ku-Documents isuka kumodeli, lapho ungashintsha umbhalo wayo.
3. Ku-preview, chofoza isibalo ukukhetha izinto ezingemuva kwaso.
4. Khetha **Yakha iphakheji**. Kulondolozwa i-zip eyodwa: idokhumenti njenge-PDF, isithombe sombono we-3D, kanye ne-`figures.csv`, ebala yonke isibalo namaGlobalId ezinto zaso.

## Izinkambu

Lezi zingasetshenziswa kunoma iyiphi idokhumenti.

| Inkambu | Ikhombisa |
|---|---|
| `{{stage}}` | Isigaba sephrojekthi emininingwaneni yomdwebo. |
| `{{options}}` | Izinketho zevolumu ezigciniwe, ecaleni komunye: amafolo, ukuphakama, isiza, indawo yonke yamafolo, isigqoko nezindleko. |
| `{{areas}}` | Indawo yamafolo ngezinga, ivela ezigumbini. |
| `{{sheets}}` | Amashidi nokuguqulwa okumi kuwo nokuthi akhishwe nini ngokugcina. |
| `{{issues}}` | Umlando wokukhishwa nencwadi yezinguquko zemidwebo. |
| `{{changes}}` | Okushintshe kusukela ekukhishweni kokugcina, ishidi ngalinye. |

## Ukubalwa kwezinketho

Izindleko zivela kuphela etafuleni lamanani ephrojekthi yakho (Energy/Cost, izindleko ze-5D). Akukho lokho okulinganiselwa ngokwakho.

- Umugqa wamanani uyabala okukhethwayo kuphela uma izigaba zawo zibiza `IfcBuildingElementProxy`.
- Indawo yamafolo ibalwa ngumugqa onamagama asho ukusetshenziswa kokukhethwayo, njengo-office. Uma kungekho, kusetshenziswa umugqa onamagama athi gross floor area noma building mass. Umugqa nge-m² usebenzisa indawo yonke yamafolo. Umugqa nge-m³ usebenzisa ivolumu.
- Isigqoko (perimitha iphindwe ngokuphakama) sibalwa ngumugqa onegama elithi envelope noma facade.
- Okukhethwayo okungenamugqa ofanayo kubonisa **Akukho izinga**. Inani eliphelele libonakala kuphela uma zonke izingxenye ezicelwe zinezinga.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Gcina izinketho nge-**Gcina njengokukhetha** ohlwini lwevolumu lohlelo lwegumbi. Bona Izinketho zevolumu.
- I-PDF ayikwazi ukukhetha izinto. Ngaphandle kwe-BimCraft, `figures.csv` ithwele ixhumanisi ngayinye yesibalo kuzinto zaso.

## Akwakhiwe okwamanje

- Amashidi nemibono edwetshiwe ayikabekwa ngaphakathi kwedokhumenti. Iphakheji libamba isithombe sombono we-3D kuphela.
