## Okuzokwenza

Thola ukucaciswa okulandela imodeli: isigaba sohlobo ngalunye olusemakhaya ngempela, ngakunye nokwakhiwa kwalo namanani afundwa kumodeli, kanye nombhalo oyibhalayo ngawo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula {{ui:Dweba}}, vula **Uhlu** kumqulu wesobunxele bese uchofoza **Ukucaciswa**.
2. Uhlu lunesigaba ngasinye sohlobo lodonga, uhlobo lomnyango, uhlobo lewindi, iphansi nokuqedela kwegumbi okusetshenziswayo. Ngaphansi kwesihloko ngasinye kukhona **izici** zaso, ezifundwa kumodeli: ukuqina, izendlalelo ngazinye, izinga lokuthuthukiswa, usayizi, inani nobuningi. Isigaba esinamakhodi okuhlelwa ezintweni zaso sibonisa ikhodi esihlokweni saso.
3. Bhala umbhalo wakho wokucaciswa ebhokisini ngaphansi kwesigaba. **Khetha izinto zaso** ibonisa ukuthi isigaba sihilela ini.
4. Chofoza **{{ui:Londoloza}}**. Kuyisinyathelo esisodwa ku-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}, futhi umbhalo ugcinwa efayelini lesakhiwo.
5. Vula **Amadokhumenti kusukela kumodeli**, khetha isifanekiso **Ukucaciswa** bese uchofoza **Buka kuqala**, bese ulondoloza njenge-PDF, Word noma Markdown. Bheka [Amadokhumenti kusukela kumodeli](topic:documents-from-the-model).

## Uhlu lwemidwebo yokwakha njengedokhumenti

Isifanekiso **Uhlu lwemidwebo yokwakha** siphrinta uhlu lweminyango, lwewindi nolwemiphetho lusuka kumodeli ngqo, ngokwesakhiwo sehhovisi lakho. Esifanekisweni sakho sibhale `{{schedule doors}}`, `{{schedule windows}}`, `{{schedule roomfinishes}}`, `{{schedule finishtotals}}`, noma igama lesethi-phansi elonghiwe, isibonelo `{{schedule Door schedule by floor}}`. `{{specification}}` iphrinta ukucaciswa.

## Okufanele wazi

- Izici zingezemodeli. Ziyashintsha uma imodeli ishintsha; umbhalo wakho awushintshi.
- BimCraft ayibhali isigatshana sanoma yiluphi izinga noma umbhalo womkhiqizi. Isigaba esingenambhalo siphrinta "Umbhalo wokucaciswa awukabhalwa."
- Umbhalo obhalelwe uhlobo okungasaphinde kube semodeli uyagcinwa futhi ubhalwe ohlwini lwethi "Umbhalo wezinhlobo ezingasaphinde zibe semodeli".
- Ukushintsha uhlobo jikelele lube yisakhiwo esithile, bheka [Amathuluzi odonga](topic:wall-tools) (Shintsha uhlobo).
