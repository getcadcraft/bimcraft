## Okuzokwenza

Vula iphaneli eyodwa ebhala izimpawu zikagesi nezepulani lepayipi ngezithombe nangamagama azo, thola eyodwa oyifunayo, bese uyibeka epulanini. Akukho okudinga i-inthanethi lapha: izimpawu ziza nohlelo.

## Vula futhi ubeke impawu

1. Vula imodeli bese uvula {{ui:Ukwakhiwa}} › {{ui:Iphalethi yezimpawu}} (umyalo obhaliwe: SYMBOLS). Inkinobho efanayo itholakala nakumenyu we-Tools yebha ye-Draw nanjengo **Symbols** eqenjini le-Library emugqeni wamathuluzi.
2. Khetha isethi: **Electrical** noma **Plumbing**, noma **All sets**. Bhala ebhokisini lokusesha ukuze ufinyeze uhlu. Ungasesha ngegama noma ngesifinyezo (WC, CW, HB).
3. Chofoza impawu. Ipulani selilungele ukuyibeka.
   - Impawu **kagesi** iqala ithuluzi likagesi ngedivayisi ekhethiwe, ngakho konke okusemgqeni we-[The electrical plan](topic:electrical-plan) kuyasebenza: chofoza epulanini, isinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}.
   - Impawu **yepayipi** iqala ithuluzi lezimpawu zepulani. Chofoza epulanini. Ifitshure ehlala odongeni (ibhodwe, ilavatri, ibhavu, isinki, ibhavu lepayipi) ihlanganiswa necala lodonga eliseduze ngesimo sayo sigxile odongeni; idrenishi, amavalvu, ihita lamanzi nezitayela zemigqa zihamba lapho uchofoza khona.
4. Cindezela {{key:R}} ukujikisa impawu ngama-90 degrees ({{key:Shift}}+{{key:R}} ijikisa ngakolunye uhlangothi), noma uthayiphe i-engeli ku-**Rotation** ebhareni yokukhetha. Cindezela {{key:Esc}} ukuyeka.

Ifitshure noma ibhavu lepayipi elihlanganiswe nodonga likhumbula lelo donga: uma udonga lushintsha impawu iyashintsha nalo (ngalolo hlangothi, ngalobo bude odongeni) esinyathelweni sokuhlehlisa sinye. Uma ususa udonga, impawu ihlala lapho ibikhona futhi i-**{{ui:Izinkinga}}** iyabhala ukuthi "ilahlekelwe udonga"; yiyihambise noma uyisuse. Izimpawu ezibekwe kude nodonga aziboshwanga kulutho.

Impawu ngayinye oyibekayo iyisinyathelo esisodwa se-{{ui:Hlehlisa}}. Impawu ebekiwe iyisichasiso sepulani kufayela le-IFC ngethegi yayo (WC1, WC2, ...). Yikhethe ukuze ushintshe ithegi noma ukujika kwayo ku-**{{ui:Izici}}**, noma cindezela {{key:Delete}}.

## Okusethwe isethi yepayipi

Izinto zokusebenza (ibhodwe, urinal, ilavatri, ibhavu, ishawa, isinki sasekhishini, isinki elilodwa), idrenishi yomhlabathi, amahlanzo womhlaba nodonga, ipayipi lamanzi odongeni, i-hose bib, i-water heater, amavalvu (esango, i-globe, ebhola, ye-check, ye-butterfly, ye-pressure reducing) nezitayela zemigqa nezifinyezo zazo: amanzi abandayo (CW), amanzi ashisayo (HW), ukubuyisela kwamanzi ashisayo (HWR), udoti (W), ukuphefumula (V), idrenishi yesiphepho (SD) kanye negesi (G). Imidwebo ilinganiswa nepulani, ngakho igcina usayizi wayo esheethini eliphrintiwe. Isitayela somugqa siyisiphambano esifushane sokubonisa isitayela epulanini; dweba imigqa yangempela yamapayipi ngamathuluzi wamapayipi.

:::note
Lezi yizinkambiso ezijwayelekile zepulani lepayipi, zadwetshwa ngabathi; azinaziqinisekiso ze-ISO 4067-1 noma ASME Y32.4. Zaqhathaniswa nezincazelo zomdwebo ezitholakalayo nemihlahlandlela yokuqeqesha (uhlu luseceleni lomthombo wesethi, olutshela lapho sivumelana khona nalapho sihluke khona), azikopiwe kunoma yiyiphi imikhiqizo, futhi zikhululekile ukusetshenziswa (Apache-2.0). Amasethi emidwebo ayahluka, ngakho hlola inkomba yephrojekthi yakho. Zizimpawu zepulani, hhayi uklelo lwepayipi: azilinganisi amapayipi noma zihlola amakhodi.
:::

## Izintandokazi nekhibhodi

- Chofoza inkanyezi kumpawu (noma cindezela {{key:F}} kuyo) ukuze uyigcine njengentandokazi. Izintandokazi zibekwa kuqala futhi zihlala kulesi sisheshi. **Izintandokazi kuphela** ikhombisa lezo kuphela.
- {{key:Down}} kusuka ebhokisini lokusesha ifika ezimpaweni; izinkinobho zomcibisholo, {{key:Home}} kanye {{key:End}} zihambisa phakathi kwazo; {{key:Enter}} ikhetha eyigxilile; {{key:Esc}} ivala iphalethi.
- Ku-foni iphalethi iba yishidi ngaphansi kwesikrini; liyashintsha uma ukhetha impawu ukuze ubone ipulani. Yivule futhi kumenyu ye-Build noma kunkinobho ethi **Palette** ebhareni yokukhetha.

:::tip
Uma ifayela lisebenzisa isethi yezimpawu le-build lingenayo, ipulani likhombisa ibhokisi lapho impawu ibikhona, futhi i-{{ui:Izinkinga}} iyayibhala.
:::

## Xhumanisa into ebekiwe nempawu

Ifitshure ethathwe emtapweni ingento ye-3D, kodwa uhlelo lukagesi lubonisa njengempawu yayo. Uyibeka **kanye** bese uyixhumanisa:

1. Khetha Build › **Symbol link** (noma thayipha SYMBOLLINK). Isigaba se-Symbol link siyavuleka ngakwesobunxele.
2. Khetha izinto ezibekiwe epulanini noma ku-3D. Isigaba sibonisa ukuthi zingaki ezikhethiwe nokuthi zixhunyene nani.
3. Khetha **Symbol** (kunoma iyiphi isethi yezimpawu: Electrical, Plumbing) bese ukhetha **Link**.

Into isengento eyodwa, futhi wonke amatafula awubala kanye:

- **3D** ihlala ibonisa umzimba wayo.
- **Ipulani** libonisa ifomu layo lepulani, njengakuqala.
- Ipulani lapho i-**This view shows linked elements as** isethelwe ku-**Their symbol (a services layout)** libonisa impawu esikhundleni sayo. Ukuchofoza impawu kukhetha into. Ukukhetha kugcinwa lowo mbono (ipulani lomgangatho welevele, ipulani lesiling, noma i-callout) kufayela.

**Jikisa impawu ngama-90°** iyijikisa ngokuphambene nento yayo. **Susa ixhumanisi** iyasusa ukuxhumana; into ihlala. Itafula le-**Linked symbols** libala ngalinye into exhunyiwe kanye nelevele layo negumbi limi kulo.

Akwakhiwe: ukukhetha komumo esihlokweni kuyiyona yepulani lelevele elibonisa; izinsiza ezibekwe ngethuluzi likagesi zingizimpawu kuzo zonke amapulani, njengakuqala.
