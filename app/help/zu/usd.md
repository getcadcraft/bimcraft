Ebhalini lamathuluzi, {{ui:USD}} ibhala lokho okuvuliwe (zonke izimodeli, ezihlanganisiwe lapho uzibeke khona) njengefayela elilodwa le-USD.

## Hlinzeka

1. Khetha {{ui:USD}} ebhalini lamathuluzi.
2. Khetha {{ui:Umbhalo (.usda)}}, {{ui:Obhayinari (.usdc)}} noma {{ui:Iphakheji (.usdz)}}.
3. Ifayela liyalandwa. Ibha yesimo ikhombisa izinto eziningi ezingeniwe.

## Okusefayelini

| Kumodeli ye-IFC | Kufayela le-USD |
|---|---|
| Indawo, isakhiwo, izitezi | Amaqembu ahlanganisiwe, izitezi ngokulandelana kokuphakama |
| Into ngayinye | Iprim eyodwa ngaphansi kwesitezi nenkambu yayo (Ukwakha, Isakhiwo, MEP, Ukuhlobisa, Indawo, Izikhala, Okunye), nejiyomethri yayo njenge-mesh |
| Ikilasi, i-GlobalId, igama, uhlobo, ithegi | Izici ezibizwa ngokuthi `ifc:class`, `ifc:globalId`, `ifc:name` nokunye |
| Amasethi wezici nezilinganiso | Isici esisodwa ngasinye; ubude, izindawo nevolumu ngamamitha, amamitha esikwele namamitha ekhyubhu |
| Imibala | Izinto zokwakha (nokucaca) |
| Izikhala | Zigcinwa, zimakwe njengezinqondiso ukuze abarenda bazidlule |

Ijiyomethri ihlala ezixhumanisweni zefayela le-IFC. Ukwakheka kulandela imigomo ye-USD ebhalwe yi-CadCraft yezakhiwo.
