## Okuzokwenza

Thola lapho amasethi amabili wezinto ezingenana khona, isibonelo konke esemodelini yezinsizakalo nesakhiwo, bese ubheka ngalinye bese ugcina okubalulekile njengezinkinga.

## Izinyathelo

1. Vula imodeli ye-IFC, bese wengeza amanye ngokuchofoza {{ui:Add a model…}} uma uhlola kumamodeli amaningi. Bona [Bona amamodeli e-IFC amaningi ndawonye](topic:several-models).
2. Vula {{ui:Clash check}} kwipaneli yesobunxele.
3. Khetha amasethi amabili emabhokisini {{ui:Check}} ne-{{ui:Against}}: izinto ozikhethile, isigaba (kuwo wonke amamodeli avuliwe), isethi yokusesha elonga (bona [Thola izinto ngohlobo, igama noma impahla](topic:find)), noma konke.
4. Setha ukuthi ukuhlangana kuyonakaliswa kangakanani, ngamamitha. 0.01 ayinaki izinto ezihlangene nje.
5. Chofoza {{ui:Run}}. Ukuhlola kuqhubeka ngemuva; {{ui:Cancel}} iyakuyeka.
6. Chofoza umphumela ukuwubona: izinto zombili ziyakhethwa futhi zibonakale, konke okunye kubonakala ngokudlula, nombono usondela kuzo.
7. Chofoza {{ui:Make an issue of it}} ngaphansi komphumela ukuwubhala ethebhini le-{{ui:Issues}}. Bona [Izinkinga namafayela e-BCF](topic:issues-bcf).

## Okufanele ukubone

- Ukungqubuzana okuhlu namagama ezinto zombili (namafayela azo, uma kuvulwe amamodeli amaningi) nokuthi zihlangana kangakanani, okujulile kuqala.
- Izinhlangothi ezithintana nje, njengodonga oma phezu kwesilebe noma umnyango emgodini wawo, asiyona ukungqubuzana.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- Lena ukuhlolwa okuyisisekelo kokungqubuzana okuqinile: izinto ezigoqana izinhlangothi. Into efihlekele ngokuphelele kwenye (ipayipi elingcwatshwe odongeni) akutholwa okwamanje.
- Ukujula kuwukuthi imingcele yezinto zombili ihlangana kangakanani kancane kakhulu: umhlahlandlela, hhayi ukukala okunembile.
