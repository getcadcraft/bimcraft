## Okuzokwenza

Uyi-Ana, usebenza ku-Riverside Clinic nomunye umuntu, u-Ben. Amamodeli e-IFC ephrojekthi ahlala kwifolda eyodwa enifinyelela kuyo nobabili (idrayivu eyabelwanwa, ifolda ehlanganisiwe, noma ifolda yefu). Ekugcineni, ifayela lidlule kusukela ekukopisheni kwalo kokuqala liya ekukhishweni okuvunyiwe njenge-**A**, lase lishintshwa njenge-**B.1**; uyibonile ukuthi ubani wenzani, futhi ubone ukuthi u-Ben ubona ini uma ubambe ifayela.

Konke kugcinwa kufolda ecashile ethi `.ccvault` eceleni kwamafayela akho: akukho iseva, akukho okulayishwa (ifolda yefu iyisiphambeko, bona isinyathelo sokugcina). Ukuze uthole zonke izinkinobho nemithetho endaweni eyodwa, bheka isihloko esithi *Amadokhumenti: phuma, izinguqulo nokukhishwa* lapho i-app yakho inaso.

## 1. Setha ifolda yephrojekthi

1. Khetha {{ui:Imibhalo}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Vula ifolda}} esikrinini sokuqala, noma {{ui:Imibhalo…}} kumenyu ye-**File**.
2. Khetha {{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}} bese ukhetha ifolda equkethe amamodeli e-IFC ephrojekthi. (Amafolda ephrojekthi asebenza ku-Studio nasezisekhweni zesiphequluli ezivula ifolda, njengeChrome ne-Edge. Kwezinye izisekhwezi ibhodi iyakutshela.)
3. Khetha {{ui:Ushintsho}} eduze kwe-**Wena:** bese uthayipha igama lakho, `Ana` (uma kubuza, negama lalelikhompyutha). Ibhodi manje ithi *Wena: Ana ku* nekhompyutha yakho. Igama lakho lihamba nakho konke okwenzayo.
4. Khetha {{ui:Qala isitoreji kule folda}} bese uthayipha igama leprojekthi, `Riverside clinic`. Ibhodi ithi *I-vault iqalile i-Riverside clinic.*

## 2. Beka ifayela ngaphansi kolawulo

1. Vula **Amafayela angekho ku-vault** bese ukhetha {{ui:Engeza}} eduze kwe-`models/House.ifc`.
2. Livela ngaphansi kwe-**Amafayela ku-vault** ngenguqulo **A.1**, isimo **Iyaqhubeka**. I-A.1 iyikhophi lokuqala; i-vault igcina ikhophi yenguqulo ngayinye.

## 3. Phuma, shintsha, ngenisa

1. Khetha {{ui:Vula}} eduze kwe-`models/House.ifc`. Imodeli ivulwa ku-BimCraft futhi ibhodi iyavaleka ukuze uyibone. Vula ibhodi futhi kusuka ku-{{ui:Imibhalo}}.
2. Vula ibhodi futhi bese ukhetha {{ui:Phuma}}. Umugqa manje uthi **Iphume kuwe**. Ifayela lokukhiya lenziwe ku-`.ccvault/locks`; akekho ongaphuma kulelifayela aze unengenise.
3. Shintsha imodeli: hambisa udonga, engeza umnyango. Ukugcina kuyenqatshwa uma imodeli ingaphumile kuwe, futhi kukutshela ngani; {{ui:Gcina njenge}} isasebenza njalo ngekhophi yakho.
4. Vula ibhodi, khetha {{ui:Ngenisa}} bese uthayipha okushintshile (umzekelo `Hambise umnyango`). Ibhodi ithi *ingeniswe njenge-A.2*. Ifayela kwifolda yephrojekthi manje ngumodeli owawuvulekile, nokukhiya kuyanyamalala. Ukungenisa ngaphandle koshintsho kugcina inguqulo.

Uma ushintsha umqondo wakho, {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} ibuyisela ifayela ngaphandle kwenguqulo entsha.

## 4. Ukubuyekeza nokukhipha

1. Khetha {{ui:Thumela ukuze ibukwe}}. Isimo sishintsha sibe **Ukuze ibukwe** nefayela lifundeka kuphela.
2. Ngaphambi kokuvuma, khetha {{ui:Izilungiselelo zephrojekthi}} bese uhlu lwalabo abangavuma (igama elilodwa ngomugqa ngamunye), umzekelo `Ben` no-`Carla`, bese ukhomba umthetho wokuthi ovumayo kumele abe omunye kunomlobi. Khetha {{ui:Londoloza izilungiselelo}}. Manje i-vault ngokwayo yenqaba omunye.
3. U-Ben (noma u-Carla) ukhetha {{ui:Vuma}} bese uthayipha igama lomvumayo. Isimo sishintsha sibe **Ikhishiwe** nenguqulo ibe **A**, nokuthi *ivunywe ngu-* negama. Ifayela elikhishiwe lifundeka kuphela. {{ui:Buyisela}} bekuyibuyisela ku-**Iyaqhubeka**, nesizathu.

Igama lomvumayo liwugama olibhalwe ebhodini; liyirekhodi lokuthi ubani usayine, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi ungubani.

## 5. Inguqulo entsha

1. Efayeleni elikhishiwe, khetha {{ui:Inguqulo entsha}}. Uyibamba ngohlamvu olulandelayo.
2. Yishintshe, bese {{ui:Ngenisa}}. Inguqulo ibe **B.1**, iyaqhubeka. Yikhiphe ngendlela efanayo futhi iba **B**. {{ui:Hlehlisa ukuphuma}} ngaphambi kokungenisa kokuqala kushiya ku-**A**.

Amagama enguqulo alandelana A, B … Z, AA, AB. Uma inqubomgomo yakho ishiya amanye amagama, isethingi yephrojekthi *Use the ASME Y14.35 sequence* iyakhipha I, O, Q, S, X no-Z ezinguqulweni ezilandelayo.

## 6. Bona okwenzeke

Khetha igama lefayela ukuze uvule imininingwane yalo:

- **{{ui:Umlando}}**: yonke imicimbi, eyakamuva kuqala, nokuthi ubani, iyiphi ikhompyutha nesikhathi: *Ingeniswe A.2* ngu-Ana, *Ikhishiwe* ngu-Ben, njalo njalo.
- **{{ui:Izinguqulo}}**: ithebula lezinguqulo: zonke izinguqulo ezigciniwe, nosuku lwazo, ubani wazingenisa, inothi, nokuthi ubani wazivuma. I-vault igcina ikhophi yazo ngayinye ku-`.ccvault/revs`.
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa}}**: amadokhumenti imodeli ye-IFC ekhomba kuwo.
- **{{ui:Kusetshenziswa yi}}**: ibala yonke imodeli ku-vault ekhomba kulelifayela.

## Amamodeli akhomba kwamanye amadokhumenti

Imodeli ye-IFC ekhomba kwamanye amadokhumenti esifolda (imidwebo, amahlu) iwabonisa ngaphansi kwe-**Kusetshenziswa**, futhi ngayinye yawo ibonisa imodeli ngaphansi kwe-**Kusetshenziswa yi**. Ungenisa idokhumenti ubone ukuthi yiliphi amamodeli elibhekisela kulo.

## 7. Okubonwa ngu-Ben lapho ubamba ifayela

Manje phuma ifayela futhi, bese ucela u-Ben ukuthi avule ifolda yephrojekthi efanayo ekhompyutheni yakhe bese evula ibhodi. Esihlanganweni salelifayela ubona **Iphume nguAna ku** ikhompyutha yakho **kusukela** isikhathi, futhi akekho inkinobho ye-{{ui:Phuma}}. Uma ezama noma kanjalo i-vault iyanqaba: ukukhiya kuyifayela esistimu engeke iyenze kabili, ngakho abantu ababili abakwazi ukubamba ifayela ngasikhathi sinye, ngisho nangomzuzwana owodwa, uma ifolda ikudiski ku-Studio. (Kusiphequluli ifolda ivumela okungenani okungakwazi, futhi ibhodi iyakutshela ngaphansi kwe-**Ifolda**.)

Uma u-Ana engekho futhi u-Ben edinga ifayela, {{ui:Phula ukuphuma}} liyilikhulula. Kumele athayiphe isizathu; sifakwa kulogi nokuthi ukuphuma kwakubani. Uma iphrojekthi ibala abaphathi, bona kuphela abangakwenza lokho. Ukuphuma okudlule umkhawulo wokuphelelwa isikhathi (izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuzenzakalelayo) kuphawulwa njengesikhathi eside. Ngemva kokuphula, ukungenisa kuka-Ana okuphuzile kuyenqatshwa, ngakho nina nobabili anikwazi ukubhalelana phezu kwezinto.

## 8. Amafolda efu

Ifolda yefu iyifolda yephrojekthi egcinwa esitoreji sefu lethimu yakho, ngakho ithimba alidingi idrayivu eyabelwanwa. Ku-Studio, ngemva kokungena ku-akhawunti yakho ye-CadCraft, ibhodi ibonisa {{ui:Vula ifolda yefu}} eduze kwe-{{ui:Khetha ifolda yephrojekthi}}. Yikhethe bese ukhetha ifolda, ebhalwe njenge-workspace / folder. Umnikazi wesikhala somsebenzi wenza ifolda kuqala, futhi isitoreji sefu sethimu kumele sisebenze. Konke okungenhla kusebenza ngendlela efanayo; iseva yenza ukukhiya, okuqinile phakathi kwabantu. Uma ungangenanga ibhodi iyakutshela ukuthi ungene ngemvume. Kuthebhu evamile yesiphequluli inkinobho yefu ayitholakali; sebenzisa i-Studio.

## Zama

Yenza izinyathelo eziyisishiyagalolunye ezingenhla ngekhophi yefayela langempela. Izinyathelo zabantu ababili zidinga omunye umuntu, noma amawindi amabili namagama amabili. Uma uzama ngefolda eyabelwanwa langempela, ikhasi lokuhlola komphakathi libhala lokhu ngqo futhi lamukela imiphumela.
