## Ikhabhoni elisetshenzisiwe

Vula ipane ethi **Carbon**. Ubukhulu bento ngayinye (ivolumu, indawo noma ubude) buphindwa ngesinto kusuka etafuleni, ngemodyuli yomjikelezo wokuphila: isigaba somkhiqizo siyadingeka; ukuthuthwa nemfucuza kuyazikhethela. Imiphumela iphuma ngezinto, ngezigaba nangesitezi, nangemitha ngayinye yesikwele yophahla.

**Ithebula lezinto ngelakho.** Imigqa eza ne-BimCraft ngamanani okuqala ajwayelekile ngohlobo oluwukuthi ahambisana nobukhulu. Awavela kudathabhesi, aphawulwa njengengahlolwanga, futhi kufanele ashintshwe ngomthombo osetshenziswa yini (i-EPD, idathabhesi kazwelonke, noma izibalo zenkampani yakho). Hlela imigqa kwipane, noma ngenisa noma ukhiphe ithebula njenge-CSV noma i-JSON. Imigqa oyengezayo idlula umugqa wokuqala onalo kanye nokhomba. Ithebulaligcinwa kumodeli.

Izinto ezingenakuhambisana, noma ezingenabukhulu, zidwetshwa njengezinkinga kunokuba zibalwe njengo-zero.

## Izindleko kuhlelo le-4D

Vula ipane ethi **Cost** eduze kohlelo le-4D. Yinike amanani (wawabhale, noma wangenise i-CSV noma i-JSON, noma uletha imigqa ye-TakeoffCraft), bese uhlobanisa ngayinye yamanani nezinto zemodeli ngamagama okhiye, futhi ipane ibiza zonke izinto zomsebenzi ngamunye. Izindleko zomsebenzi ngamunye zisakazwa phakathi kwezinsuku zokuqala kuya ezokuphela (ngokulinganayo, noma ezilayishwe phambili noma emuva), futhi ipane idweba ijika lokugeleza kwemali elibalwe ngosuku, ngeviki noma ngenyanga, nezindleko ngesigaba nangephakheji, nokuthi okuhleliwe kuqhathaniswa kanjani nokwakhiwe ngosuku oluthile. Iphuma njenge-CSV. Akukho okubizwa kuze kube yilapho unikeza amanani: awekho alethwa.

## Imali

Ithebula lamanani lingasho imali anikezwa ngayo (izinhlamvu ezintathu, njengo-USD noma i-EUR). Ipane ye-5D inebhokisi lalokho, igcinwa kufayela kanye namanani, futhi ukuthumela kwe-JSON kuthwala lokho. Ithebula elenziwe ngaphambi kwalokhu, noma elishiywe lingenalutho, alinayo imali: izindleko zalo zikhonjiswa njenge-USD, okuwukuzenzakalela kwe-shell, futhi ipane namanani aphelele ayakusho lokho. Akukho ukuguqulwa kwemali phakathi kwezimali.
