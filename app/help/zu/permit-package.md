## Okuzokwenza

Yakha i-zip eyodwa yokuthumela okusemthethweni (uma uhlu lomphathi luvela kuphakethe lemithetho, ikwathi izinyathelo zokuvuma nefomethi yokuthumela): ikhava, izimvo umhloli angazenza (ezibhalwe ngokuhlolwa, hhayi ezicabangwayo), umbiko wemithetho uma i-Building regulations add-on ivuliwe, imodeli, nohlu lomphathi lwemibhalo esekelayo.

Lesi siqondiso sakhiwe ngemodeli. Umhloli obhalisiwe nomphathi banquma okudingekayo nokwamukelekayo.

## Izinyathelo

1. Ngemodeli evuliwe, vula {{ui:Permit package}} ngaphakathi kuphaneli esekhohlo.
2. Thayipha igama leprojekthi, ikheli nomcelisi, bese ubhala amashidi nezifomu zakho, eyodwa ngomugqa.
3. Ngaphansi kwe-{{ui:Supporting documents}}, khetha i-{{ui:Authority's list}}. Uhlu luvela kumaphakethe emithetho: engeza i-Building regulations add-on bese uthikha iphakethe lendawo yakho ku-Izilungiselelo › Imithetho yokwakha (iphakethe lingabamba imithetho, inqubo yokuvuma, ifomethi yokuthumela, izivumelwano zemidwebo nezinsizakalo ezijwayelekile). Uhlu jikelele usebenza ngaphandle kwalokho.
4. Funda uhlu. Umbhalo ngamunye ungu-{{ui:Linked}} (neshidi nesizathu), {{ui:Supplied by you}}, {{ui:Does not apply}} noma {{ui:Missing}}.
5. Ngephepha onikeza wena (ifomu, isitifiketi somhlaba, irisidi yemali) khetha {{ui:Supplied by me}}. Ngento emakiwe ngokuthi "uma isebenza" engasebenzi, khetha {{ui:Does not apply here}}. Ukuxhumanisa umbhalo weshidi ngokwakho, khetha leshidi.
6. Vula {{ui:Approval process}} kanye {{ui:Submission format}} ngaphansi kohlu ukuze ufunde izinyathelo nokuthi yini okufanele uyinikeze (usayizi wamashidi, amafomethi amafayela, amakhophi). Imali noma isikhathi sivela kuphela uma umthombo wephakethe uyishilo, nekhasi nosuku lapho ifundwe.
7. Chofoza {{ui:Build the permit package (zip)}}. Izinyathelo nefomethi nazo zisekhava.

## Indlela umbhalo oxhunyiwe ngayo

Umbhalo uxhunyiwe kweshidi elibamba uhlobo olufanele lombono (uhlelo lwephansi, ukubukeka kohlangothi, isigaba), kweshidi elibamba uhlobo olufanele lohlu (uhlu lwamasivalo, uhlu lwezinhlobo), noma kweshidi igama layo lithi kanjalo (ishidi elithi "Site plan"). Lapho imodeli inemigqa kodwa akukho shidi elibamba uhlu, noma indawo idwetshiwe kodwa akukho shidi elithi yiphulani yesayithi, into ihlala ingekho futhi ichaze ngani.

## Okufanele ukwazi

- **Uhlu lomphathi ngalinye alubhekwa umuntu wendawo.** Ngalinye lufundwe emthonjeni oshicilelwe alibhekisela kuwo, ngosuku olukhonjisiwe, futhi lubhalwe ngamagama ethu. Izidingo ziyashintsha: hlola uhlu lwamanje lomphathi.
- Okusele okungekho esethini, noma ishidi elingenalo usayizi, ukuqondisa noma isikali esicelwe iphakethe, kubonakala ohlwini lwezinkinga njengesaziso. Izaziso ziyeluleko: azikaze zimise iphakethe.
- Imibhalo elahlekile ayikaze imise iphakethe. Ibhalwa ekhava nakwi-`04-supporting-documents.csv`.
- Uhlu ludatha, hhayi ikhodi, ngaphakathi kumaphakethe emithetho: iNingizimu Afrika (Imithetho Yokwakha Yezwe, umthetho A2), Cape Town (neNingizimu Afrika engezwe kusuka ku-Umtapo), England (izidingo zolwazi lwezwe), Portland e-Oregon, iphakethe lokufundisa elenziwe ngamanga, nohlu oluvamile olungelo lomphathi yisiphi na. Noma ubani angabhala iphakethe lendawo yakhe.
- Izinqumo zakho zigcinwa emodelini, ngakho zikhona lapho uyivula futhi, futhi ngayinye iyisinyathelo esingahlehliswa.
