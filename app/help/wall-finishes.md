## What you'll do

Finish the faces of walls (paint, board, tile) and read how much of each finish the building needs.

## Steps

1. Open {{ui:Draw}} › {{ui:Walls}} › {{ui:Wall finishes…}}. A panel opens over the drawing.
2. Pick a wall. On the {{ui:Face}} tab choose its left or right face, then add a finish: {{ui:Full height}}, {{ui:Splash-back}}, {{ui:Shower wall}} or {{ui:By corners}}.
3. For a tile finish, choose where the set-out starts (a corner, the centre, an opening's edge or a point), the tile direction and a straight or brick pattern. The count of whole and cut tiles shows at once.
4. On {{ui:Finish types}} make or change a type: name, kind, the tile size and joint, waste, and the build-up from the wall outwards (substrate, waterproofing, adhesive, finish).
5. On {{ui:Substrates}} change the substrate layers on their own and read what the wall measures finished.
6. On {{ui:Schedules}} read the wall finishes by zone, by finish and by room.

## What you should see

- The finishes drawn in the plan and as coverings in the model; moving or retyping a wall moves its finishes with it.
- Every change is one undo step.
- Quantities net of openings. Tile counts marked with ≈ are estimates.

## Good to know

- The panel advises and never blocks.
- Wall finishes also appear in {{ui:Schedules}} as Wall finishes by zone, by finish and by room.
