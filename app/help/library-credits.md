## What you'll do

Give credit where a license asks for it, and reach your team's own library items.

## Library credits

1. Open the model.
2. Click {{ui:Credits}} in the toolbar.
3. Read the list: each item used, its version, license, attribution and the item numbers it is placed as.
4. Click {{ui:Copy credits}} to copy the attribution lines, or {{ui:CSV with the schedule}} for a spreadsheet of every placement with its credit.

The license and attribution also show in {{ui:Properties}} when you pick a placed item, and they are saved in the IFC (`CadCraft_LibraryItem`), so they travel with the file.

## The equipment schedule of a model

Click {{ui:Equipment}} in the toolbar. The schedule is built from the placed library items: item number, quantity, name, maker, model, electrical and plumbing data, grouped by storey. It downloads as the same CSV a drawing's schedule does.

## Sign in to the library

1. In the {{ui:Library}} tab, pick **Library**.
2. Click {{ui:Sign in to the library}}. A small window opens on this site.
3. Sign in if asked, then click **Connect**. The window hands BimCraft a library key and you can close it.

## What you should see

- "Signed in to the library" at the top of the Library list. Your team's items appear beside the public ones.
- Items you place are recorded as used, so the library can tell you when they change.

## Good to know

- The key is kept in this tab only and ends when you close it. {{ui:Forget sign-in}} drops it sooner.
- Your account's security page lists the key as "BimCraft library (browser)"; revoke it there.
- Nothing about your model is sent: only which library items you placed.
