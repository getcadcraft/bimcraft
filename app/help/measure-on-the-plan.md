## What you'll do

Check a length on the plan of a level, such as a room width or the distance between two grid lines, without adding anything to the model. To measure in the 3D view instead, see [Measure distances, face areas and locations in 3D](topic:measure).

## Steps

1. Show the plan of the level you want: {{ui:Plan}} for the plan alone, or {{ui:Split}}.
2. In the {{ui:Draw}} group, click {{ui:Measure}}. With only the plan shown you can press {{key:M}} instead.
3. Click the first point. Points snap to walls, grid lines and columns.
4. Click the second point. A dimension line shows the distance, and the status line gives the distance with the X and Y differences.
5. To measure on, click a third point. Each leg is measured and the total is added.
6. Hold {{key:Shift}} while you click to keep the leg straight from the one before.
7. Press {{key:Esc}} to clear the measure, or pick another tool.

## Notes

- Nothing is written to the model, and the dimension is gone when you clear it. For dimensions that stay, see [Wall assemblies and reference dimensions](topic:wall-assemblies).
- With the 3D view on screen, {{key:M}} stays the 3D Measure. {{key:Shift+M}} is Move.
- The distance uses the display unit you chose in {{ui:Settings}}.
