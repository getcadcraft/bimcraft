The pack is made from research done on 2026-11-20, kept in the source as tools/dome/research/us.json: for every rule the value, the code, the section, the page it was read on and the sentence read. No value was typed from memory. Values are in SI units with the inch or psi figure beside them. Researched again on 2026-11-21. Of 35 rules, 14 were read in the code's own text, 15 in documents that quote it, 6 not at all. The load factors (1.4 on own weight alone; 1.2 with other loads and 0.9 where it helps; 1.6 on snow; 1.0 on wind and earthquake) were read in the 2018 building code as one state adopted it, since the 2021 code only points to ASCE 7. Four rules are held back because the only pages found were ones we do not cite (wind and snow on domes, the stiffness formula, the buckling guidance): they say so. The 4,000 psi sprayed concrete strength is California's rule for public schools and hospitals and is shown, never used as a general minimum. Every shell value is from ACI 318.2-14, two editions back: check it against 318.2-19 or the 2025 edition. The sprayed concrete strength is the general floor for structural concrete, not a sprayed-concrete figure. The building code was read as Utah's adoption; your state or county may have another edition or amendments.
