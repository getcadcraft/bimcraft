A rule is used by the generator only when its value is a number in the unit the generator works in. A minimum raises a starting figure; your own figure always stands and is reported when it breaks the rule. Rules that are sentences (bar spacing as the code words it, one layer or two, buckling, analysis, tension steel, sprayed concrete documents and testing, loads, foam and fire, frost) are shown, not applied. Codes: ACI 318.2-14, ACI 318-19, the International Building Code 2021 and 2018 as adopted by one state, ASTM A615, and a state agency's and a trade association's papers where they quote those.
