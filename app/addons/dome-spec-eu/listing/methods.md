A rule is used by the generator only when its value is a number in the unit the generator works in; a strength class such as C20/25 is read as its cylinder strength. A minimum raises a starting figure; your own figure always stands and is reported when it breaks the rule. Rules that are sentences are shown, not applied. Standards: EN 1992-1-1:2004, EN 1992-2:2005 Annex LL, EN 14487-1 and EN 14487-2, EN 1991-1-3 and EN 1991-1-4, EN 13501-1, EN 13165 (which does not cover foam sprayed on site), EN 1997-1.
