The pack is made from research done on 2026-11-20, kept in the source as tools/dome/research/eu.json: for every rule the value, the standard, the clause, the page it was read on and the sentence read. No value was typed from memory. Researched again on 2026-11-21. Of 35 rules, 31 were read in the standard's own text, 2 in documents that quote it, 2 not at all. The load factors are EN 1990's recommended values (1.35 on permanent actions, 1.0 where they help, 1.5 on the leading variable action, 0.75 on snow beside a leading wind). No Eurocode buckling formula for shells exists in the text read; the guidance shown is from a university dissertation describing the published reduction-factor method and the old German safety factor of 5. Every EN 1992-1-1 value is from the 2004 edition, not the 2023 one, and is a recommended value: your national annex may set another. Frost depth and the fire class required are national and were not read for any country. The cover against earth is the figure for prepared ground or blinding; concrete cast straight against soil has a larger one, given in the rule's note.
