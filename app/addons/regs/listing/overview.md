Building regulations checks a model or a drawing against **regulation packs**: files of numbers and clause numbers for a country, state or city. A pack holds limits (a stair riser, a room height, a footing depth), never the text of the standard, and every rule carries the clause it comes from.

In BimCraft it checks stairs, rooms, doors and escape openings, electrical layout, footings, wall framing, floor joists, rafters and wall insulation against the pack you tick. In CadCraft it checks drawing practice (units, levels, sheet series, scales, title block fields), in CivilCraft zoning and crest sight distance, and in DetailCraft the edge distance and spacing of bolts. Findings appear in the shared Problems panel with the pack, the edition and the clause, so you can see why something is flagged.

Packs are separate content: installing a country or state adds its packs, not new code. You tick the packs for a drawing, and the drawing remembers which pack versions it was checked against.
