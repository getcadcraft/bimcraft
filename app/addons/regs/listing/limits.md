This is a guide and a design aid, not an engineering approval or a permit check. The registered professional and the local authority decide what is required. A pack can be out of date or incomplete for your place, and a rule it has not got is not a rule that does not apply.

- The South African packs and the AASHTO crest table are unconfirmed; the zoning sample is made up.
- Roofs and windows against the energy rules are notes, never passes. Rafters are not judged over 20 psf snow.
- The ticks for a CadCraft drawing are kept in the session, not yet in DWG or DXF; a reopened file starts from this computer's Settings.
- A city by-law whose content is zoning has a pack with no rules.
- Wind loads are not checked.
- Only the packs you have installed are checked; nothing is looked up online while you draw.
