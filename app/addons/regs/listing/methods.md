A **pack** is a JSON file in a published format (`cadcraft-regulation-pack`): a jurisdiction, an authority level (adopted law, government, model code, professional or community), a source and a date, and rules. Each rule has an id, a limit with its unit, and the clause. Packs can build on another (a state on a model code, a city on its state), so a city pack holds only what differs.

**Judging.** The engine compares measured values from the model with the limit in force: stair risers and goings, room area and height, hall width, egress door and bedroom escape openings, outlet spacing and counts, footing depth against the rule and the place's frost depth, wall stud spacing and header sizes against span tables, floor joist and rafter spans (horizontal projection of the sloped length), and wall insulation against the cavity and continuous R-value options of the climate zone. Spans and table values come from the tables of the packs' own model-code bases, quoted by clause; the add-on only reads them.

**Honest gaps.** A check that needs a value the model does not hold is a note, never a pass. Roofs and windows against the energy rules are notes. Rafters are not judged over 20 psf snow.
