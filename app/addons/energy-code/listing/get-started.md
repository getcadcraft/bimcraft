1. Switch the add-on on in Settings, Add-ons, then open the Energy code check tab.
2. Type the site's ZIP code. The climate zone appears.
3. For each exterior wall type, choose the stud size, spacing, the insulation between the studs and any continuous insulation.
4. Choose the ceiling, its insulation and whether there is a radiant barrier, then the floor.
5. Type the windows' rated U-factor and SHGC.
6. Read the result: what meets the code's figure, what does not, and what could not be checked.
