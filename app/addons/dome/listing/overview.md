The Dome generator makes a thin concrete shell dome from two numbers: the diameter and the height. It is made for domes built by spraying: an inflated form, foam sprayed on its inside, steel hung from the foam and concrete sprayed over it, on a ring beam. Choose part of a sphere (up to a half sphere) or half an ellipse, flatter or taller, and add a straight stem wall under the curve if the dome should start above the ground. Set doors and windows round it, with a frame of thicker concrete if you want one. It builds the ring beam, the floor slab, the stem wall, the shell that thickens toward its base, the foam and the form's membrane, and works out the quantities, a bar count and the room you get inside. Work in millimetres or in feet and inches.

For your engineer it lays out the forces: membrane forces under own weight and snow, what wind and earthquake add and whether they lift the base, the bending at the ring beam and at the top of a stem wall from a real shell solution, the classical buckling pressure, and what each opening interrupts. Load factors come from the spec you pick.

In BimCraft the dome is placed in your model as real elements. In CadCraft its plan and section are drawn as ordinary entities. It also runs inside SuperCad Studio through either. You can save it as an IFC file. Norm values are not built in: they come from spec side add-ons, one per set of norms (United States, Europe), and every value says which standard and clause it is from and whether we read it in the standard itself. It is a drawing, take-off and first-analysis aid: a licensed engineer must design and check the dome.
