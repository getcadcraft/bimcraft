The generator ships no norm data. Its starting figures (shell thickness, cover, concrete strength, steel ratio) are round numbers that are not from any standard, and the panel and every report say so beside each one. The foam, membrane, ring beam and slab sizes it opens with are plain defaults for you to change, not anyone's recommended practice. Norm values come from the spec side add-ons, which list their sources value by value. No maker's form or dome system data is included.
