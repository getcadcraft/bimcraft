Writes an IFC4 file of the dome (ring beam as a footing, slab, stem wall, shell as a dome roof, foam and membrane as coverings, each a revolved solid with colours, a CadCraft_Dome property set, base quantities and materials, the design figures and first forces on the building), and a CSV report of the figures, quantities, forces and spec rules with where each figure came from. In BimCraft it writes elements into the open model, saved with it. In CadCraft it writes circles, arcs, polylines, text and dimensions on DOME- layers, which export to DXF and DWG. It reads nothing but what you type and the spec packs of the side add-ons you switched on.
