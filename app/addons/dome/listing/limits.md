It is a design aid, not an engineering approval, and it does not design the dome: we accept no liability for what is built from it, and a licensed engineer must design and check the dome. The forces are linear elastic theory for uncracked concrete under simple load patterns. The wind is a textbook pattern, not your code's coefficients, which put more suction over the crown; the earthquake is a static sideways force with no dynamic analysis. Not calculated: bending under wind or earthquake, uneven snow, temperature, shrinkage, cracking, creep, and the bending round an opening. The buckling figure is the classical one for a perfect shell; the reduction is your engineer's. The bar count is one mat with no laps, no ring beam cage and no bars round openings, and no bars are drawn. Shapes are a spherical segment up to a half sphere and half an ellipse on a round plan: no oval plans, no joined domes. An opening has radial sides, so it narrows a little above its sill, and no door or window is put in it. Nothing checks the site, the soil, frost depth or your local code. Panel wording added in version 0.11.1 is English until the next language round. No engineer has reviewed it and nothing has been built from it.
