A rule is used by the generator only when its value is a number in the unit the generator works in. A minimum raises a starting figure; a maximum lowers it; your own figure always stands and is reported when it breaks the rule. A rule that the source wrote for private or for public pools is applied to that use only. Rules that are labels, ranges or sentences are shown, not applied. Standards: ACI 318 and ACI 350 (cover, strength, water to cement ratio, shrinkage steel, bar spacing), the American Shotcrete Association's pool position statements, the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code 2021 (turnover, pipe velocity, skimmers, inlets, suction outlets, barrier, deck slope), and the CDC Model Aquatic Health Code 2024 (filter rate, deck width, slip resistance).
