Read only: nothing here writes to a device, raises an alarm or acts as a safety function.

Open the **Plant** tab and the section **Connect to a device**. It is the same panel as in the P&ID add-on: add devices, type the tag map, press **Check the map**, then **Show these readings** with a CSV of `tag,time,value,quality`.

Each reading tints the element of the equipment table that has that **Tag**: normal readings one colour, high, low and bad quality others, with the value as its label. **Read the devices** needs CadCraft Studio; in a browser tab use the CSV.
