A rule is used by the generator only when its value is a number in the unit the generator works in. A minimum raises a starting figure; a maximum lowers it; your own figure always stands and is reported when it breaks the rule. A rule that the source wrote for private or for public pools is applied to that use only. Rules that are labels, ranges or sentences are shown, not applied. Standards: EN 1992-1-1 and EN 1992-3 (the concrete shell: exposure class, concrete class, cover, crack width, wall thickness, bar spacing), EN 206 (water to cement ratio), EN 16582 and EN 16713 (domestic pools: leak test, turnover, filter rate, suction outlets), EN 15288 (public pools), and national rules where there is no European one: the German accident insurers' slip classes, DIN 19643 for public pool flow, and French rules for barriers and skimmers.
