This is the European spec for the Pool generator: a data pack of the values that norms set for a concrete pool shell and its water circulation, taken from EN 1992-1-1 and EN 1992-3 (the concrete shell: exposure class, concrete class, cover, crack width, wall thickness, bar spacing), EN 206 (water to cement ratio), EN 16582 and EN 16713 (domestic pools: leak test, turnover, filter rate, suction outlets), EN 15288 (public pools), and national rules where there is no European one: the German accident insurers' slip classes, DIN 19643 for public pool flow, and French rules for barriers and skimmers. Switch it on and pick it in the Pool generator's Spec section: its values fill the boxes you left empty, and the design is checked against them.

Every value names its standard and clause, and says whether we read it in the standard's own text, in a document that quotes the standard, or could not read it at all (then there is no value). The Eurocode values are the recommended ones; each country's National Annex may change them. EN 1992-1-1:2004 and EN 1992-3:2006 were read; the 2023 second-generation Eurocode 2 that replaces them was not. The skimmer value is from a French decree for public pools whose text is marked abrogated. The barrier height is French and was read in guides, not in the standard. It is not a substitute for the standards, and a licensed engineer must design and check the pool.
