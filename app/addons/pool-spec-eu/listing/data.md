The pack is made from research done on 2026-11-19, kept in the source as tools/pool/research/eu.json: for every rule the value, the standard, the clause, the page it was read on and the sentence read. No value was typed from memory. Values are in SI units; the pack says so where a value was converted. The Eurocode values are the recommended ones; each country's National Annex may change them. EN 1992-1-1:2004 and EN 1992-3:2006 were read; the 2023 second-generation Eurocode 2 that replaces them was not. The skimmer value is from a French decree for public pools whose text is marked abrogated. The barrier height is French and was read in guides, not in the standard.
