The shell is plain geometry: a floor slab that follows the slope, four walls on it, steps cut to equal risers, each an extruded solid whose volume is its profile area times its length. The water volume is the prism under the water line less the steps. The circulation flow is the water volume divided by the turnover time. The filter area is the flow divided by the filter rate, and the smallest filter step that gives it is chosen (two to four filters when one is too small). Pump power is density times gravity times flow times the head, divided by the efficiency; the head is your figure, or the loss in the routed pipes (Hazen-Williams friction and bends) plus your equipment figure when you choose that. A pipe is the smallest size step whose bore keeps the flow under the velocity limit. Pipes are routed square-cornered round the pool by the shortest way that does not pass under it, except the drain line, which runs under the slab.

No norm value is in the generator. A spec side add-on carries them as data, and each value records its standard, clause, the page it was read on and whether that was the standard's own text. The generator reads only values it can use as a number in the right unit, and applies a value written for private or public pools only to that use.
