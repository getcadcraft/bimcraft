The Pool generator makes a swimming pool or a fountain from three numbers: length, width and depth. From them it builds the reinforced concrete shell with its sloping floor and entry steps, the tile finish, the coping and paving, skimmers or an overflow channel all round, the main drains and inlets, the filter and pump in a machine room, and the pipes that join them. It works out the water volume, the circulation flow, the filter, pump and pipe sizes, and the quantities to price the job.

In BimCraft the pool is placed in your model as real elements. In CadCraft its plan and long section are drawn as ordinary entities. Either way you can save it as an IFC file. Norm values are not built in: they come from spec side add-ons, one per set of norms (Europe, United States), and every value says which standard and clause it is from and whether we read it in the standard itself. It is a drawing and take-off aid: a licensed engineer must design and check the pool.
