The generator ships no norm data. Its starting figures (wall and floor thickness, cover, steel ratio, turnover time, filter rate, velocities, skimmer spacing) are round numbers that are not from any standard, and the panel and every report say so beside each one. Filter diameters, motor ratings and pipe sizes are generic size steps marked unverified: they are not any maker's catalogue, and no maker's equipment data is included. Norm values come from the spec side add-ons, which list their sources value by value.
