A three-dimensional lattice Boltzmann method written for CadCraft (D3Q19 for the air, with the temperature and the humidity ratio carried on the same links), with buoyancy from the temperature (Boussinesq) and a Smagorinsky eddy viscosity for turbulence, plus a viscosity floor that keeps a coarse grid stable. Relative humidity comes from the humidity ratio and the temperature with the psychrometric equations CadCraft's weather tools use (ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals, Psychrometrics, as written there). The solver's closed-box heat transfer was checked against a published numerical benchmark (the differentially heated air cube of Tric, Labrosse and Betrouni, 2000) in 0.11. No comfort index (draught rating, PMV) is worked out.
