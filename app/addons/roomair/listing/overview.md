Room air works out how the air moves in one room: the air speed, temperature and humidity everywhere in it, from the room's supply diffusers, return grilles and fan coils and the heat and moisture of the people and equipment in it. It reads the room and BimCraft's own equipment (with the airflow the duct calculation keeps) from the model, runs CadCraft's own airflow solver in the page, and shows the numbers for the occupied zone, the air in and out, and a plan or section coloured by speed, temperature or humidity. It writes nothing into the model. It has not yet been compared with air measured in a real test room: a design aid, not a certificate.
