Not yet compared with measured room air: the comparison waits on measured velocity and temperature profiles of a published test room, and every result says so. Turbulence is an eddy viscosity with a floor, so supply jets spread faster and fade sooner than real ones. Walls, floor and ceiling are smooth and pass no heat; there is no radiation, sun or air leakage. A terminal is as big as the grid cells it covers and a diffuser blows straight down. Terminals must sit on the ceiling or the floor; wall grilles are left out. The quick grid is about 12,000 cells (15 to 20 cm in an office), so anything smaller than about two cells is not resolved.
